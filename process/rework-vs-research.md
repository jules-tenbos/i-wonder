# Rework vs research — distinct phases, distinct pipelines

Reworking existing site material is one kind of work: restructuring, voice passes, cross-link cleanup. That work has an end. Topics that need genuine new research (deep-dives into thinkers, new cross-cutting themes, positioning work that requires fresh analysis) are a different kind of thing entirely.

## Why

Conflating the two inflates the rework scope indefinitely and skips the conversation pipeline that gives new topics proper treatment. Research topics deserve a submission → blog conversation → ref lib absorption flow, not direct injection into the ref lib via a rework plan.

## How to apply

- Keep `ref-lib-rewrite.md` focused on structural rework of existing material.
- When a new research topic surfaces (a thinker lineage not yet covered, a new principle-themed positioning page, a cross-principle theme), capture it as a future submission candidate — not as a rework task.
- Once the active rework is done, these topics flow into `submissions/` to start the normal conversation pipeline.
- Don't pre-commit to ref lib structure for topics that haven't been developed in conversation yet. Let the conversation shape them first.
