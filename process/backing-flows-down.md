# Backing flows down

When resource material expands on any site page — a subject entry, a facet page, a post — make sure the associated person and subject pages are up to date: the primary source must explicitly carry what the higher layer says about it.

The layers order the flow. Person and subject pages are the primary sources; meta subjects, facet pages and posts say only what the layer below explicitly backs. When a higher page needs something the backing page doesn't yet say, flow the material down into the backing page first, then state it above.

Never resolve a backing gap in the other direction — trimming the better text to match a weaker backing page loses good content and leaves the primary source incomplete. The gap is the backing page's to close.

The same direction of reasoning governs inclusion: importance decides whether something belongs; inclusion creates the obligation to build the backing — being unbacked is never itself a reason to keep something out.
