# A-Z structure — guidelines

Structural conventions for entries in the A-Z sections (Language, Vocabulary, Tools).

## Flat lookup, hierarchical groups

The A-Z is a flat alphabetical lookup. Every entry lives at its letter: `a-z/{letter}/{slug}/`.

Some entries are **hierarchical groups** — a group page with internal structure (sections, sub-entries). Members of a group appear in the flat A-Z at their own letter, pointing back to their position in the group hierarchy.

Example: SPL Seed is a group entry under S. Its members (original, philosophical, engineering) are sections within the group page. If a member warranted its own entry (e.g. a standalone vocabulary), it would appear at its own letter in the A-Z with a pointer back into the group.

## Each SPL language is its own entry

SPL languages (SPL Seed, SPL Engineering, SPL Positioning, HAICC, etc.) are independent languages, not sub-games of each other. Each gets its own A-Z entry. A term from one language (e.g. HAICC is a term in SPL Engineering) belongs in that language's vocabulary — it does not imply a hierarchical relationship between the languages.

## Internal structure is not public

Don't explain the A-Z's structural conventions (hierarchical groups, member pointers, etc.) on the public pages. The structure should be self-evident from the entries themselves. These guidelines are for authors, not readers.
