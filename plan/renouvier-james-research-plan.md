# Renouvier and James — Plan & Research Approach

Working document. Provisional throughout — placement, cuts, and research scope all subject to revision. The scoping research below is a first pass sufficient to target the programme; it is not the research itself. No surface decisions are made here.

## What this plans

A research programme on Charles Renouvier (1815–1903) and his two-way exchange with William James, entered through two interests: **the relational** (Renouvier's own master category) and **the free-will mechanics** — free will not as a metaphysical exemption from causation but as an operation, and specifically as an operation that shapes the reality the subject lives in.

Whether this lands as person page, ring piece, fence page, blog post, or some combination is left open. The research decides it.

## Orienting findings from the scoping research

Six findings that shape what the programme has to do.

**1. The relational is Renouvier's own term, not a rhyme we construct.** In the preface to the *Deuxièmes Essais* he states that the word *être* expresses *le rapport* — being expresses relation — and that a being *is* a function, defined by the functions that constitute it, outside of which nothing real is known or knowable. Mathias Girel's reading is that Renouvier gets to substance-as-function before Cassirer does. If this holds under primary-text scrutiny, it is a much stronger relational position than the secondary literature's framing ("relationship pervades all categories") suggests — it is entity-as-bundled-relations stated outright in 1859.

**2. The free-will mechanics are attentional, and they are the load-bearing find.** Renouvier's definition, as James recorded it in the 30 April 1870 diary entry: the sustaining of a thought because I choose to when I might have other thoughts. That is not a faculty and not a choice-between-actions. It is an operation on *what stays in play* — upstream of action, at the level of which contents remain available. This is the thread the interest is in, and it is markedly less worked-over in the literature than the will-to-believe material that surrounds it.

**3. The transmission runs both ways.** The standard story (James rescued from his 1870 crisis by reading the second *Essais*) is true but one-directional. Renouvier subsequently published James in his own journal, the *Critique philosophique*, and engaged his work. Girel treats it as a two-way relation. The programme should not reproduce the rescue anecdote as the whole story.

**4. *Uchronie* (1876) is the collective-scale version of the same move.** A counterfactual history — Marcus Aurelius names a successor who keeps Christianity from taking root — run as a philosophical instrument rather than a literary diversion, against the organicist and deterministic historical narratives of the period. Renouvier coined the term. Written in the shadow of the Franco-Prussian war and the Commune. This is free-will-shapes-reality at the scale of history, and it is a route into the path-landscape question that the individual-attention material does not by itself open.

**5. Two amputations from Kant, and a substantival late turn.** He drops the thing-in-itself (phenomena are appearances of themselves only) and the actual infinite (the "law of number": every cardinal number is an ultimate, finite, irreducible individual; an infinite number is a contradiction). The finitism is pre-Cantor and mathematically superseded — what survives is the philosophical motive, which needs separating from the failed argument. Late, he ends with the person as ultimate and a *finite* God, "the permanent personality in the world," preferring atheism to an infinite deity.

**6. The source base is thin in English and this constrains the programme.** Logue's *Charles Renouvier, Philosopher of Liberty* is described by its own publisher as the only substantial English introduction. Dunham (*BJHP* 23:4, 2015) is the key peer-reviewed treatment of the James relation. Viney has an essay on James and Renouvier on free will. Girel is a working scholar but the useful piece is blog-form and in French. Primary texts are largely untranslated. There appears to be no SEP entry, which matters for the threshold test on external linking. Any claim about Renouvier's own formulations needs to come off the French primary text or a cited scholarly translation, not off encyclopedia summaries.

## Carried positions

Current standing, to be tested by the research, not asserted results.

**The relational strand is strong and may be stronger than expected.** Being-as-relation, entity-as-function, no substance behind the phenomena. On its face this sits side by side with the seed's relational primitive without strain.

**But there is a representationalism question, and it is the crux.** Renouvier's system runs on *représentation*. Phenomena are given as representations; the categories organise representations. The question the research has to settle is whether that is representation-of-something (a residue of the mirror, even with the thing-in-itself removed) or presentation-without-anything-behind-it — closer to a neutral-monist or Machian reading, where "representation" is a term of art for the given rather than a relation to a hidden original. The whole placement turns on this. If it is the first, the relational strand is fence material despite its surface resonance, and the fence is a substantial one, because the colouring would sit inside the very vocabulary that looks most compatible. If it is the second, the strand is ring material and possibly close to the seed.

**The free-will mechanics may be the sharpest thing here.** Sustaining a thought against alternatives, as the operation, reads as selection among what is available for mixing. Whether that is a real structural correspondence or a surface one is the thing to work out. Note also what it would add: the seed's account of agency runs on the multiplicity available in the cauldron — how many independently combinable sets are in play. Renouvier's operation is different in kind: not how much is available, but the holding of one in play against the others. Those may be complementary rather than redundant, and if so the addition is worth having.

**The grounding move is a genuine problem.** Renouvier argues that freedom is the condition of there being assent, belief, error, and responsibility at all — so the determinist's own affirmation presupposes what it denies. That is transcendental in shape, and the seed declines that kind of grounding. The *pari* — since neither can be demonstrated and one must live, choose freedom, freely — reads as an avowed choice rather than a proof, which would soften it considerably. Which of the two is doing the actual work in his texts is a research question, not a matter of preference, and it is the second thing placement turns on.

**The finitism rhymes with anti-totality.** No actual infinite, no completed whole. The seed-side refusal of the universe-as-totality (no other, therefore not a being) is a different argument reaching a related place. Worth testing rather than assuming.

**The personalism looks like fence material.** The person as ultimate reality is a *what*, and a substantival one, from a thinker whose earlier move was substance-into-function. Whether this is a late reversal, a consistent development, or a theological overlay on an unchanged system is a real scholarly question and the answer changes how much weight it carries.

## What the research needs to cover

Organised by question. Sequencing is not prescribed.

**The relational core.** Primary-text work on the *Deuxièmes Essais* preface and the treatment of the categories: relation as first or master category, being-as-function, what work *représentation* is doing and whether it frames the relation or is framed by it. The Cassirer anticipation claim checked rather than repeated. Cassirer has a page on-site; the substance-to-function line is a natural neighbour.

**The free-will mechanics.** Renouvier's own account of *liberté* across the *Essais* and *Science de la morale* (1869): what sustaining a thought consists in, whether he gives it any mechanism, how it connects to certitude, and how the *pari* is framed in his own words. Separate the transcendental argument from the wager and establish which carries the weight.

**James's inheritance of the mechanics.** The *Principles of Psychology* chapter on Will — effort of attention as the essential act — read as the developed form of Renouvier's definition. This is the thread that runs from "sustaining a thought" to attention constituting the world lived in, and it is where free-will-as-reality-shaping becomes concrete rather than slogan. Distinguish it firmly from "The Will to Believe" (1896), which is the famous and much-attacked descendant and a different argument. "The Dilemma of Determinism" (1884) sits between them.

**The transmission, both directions.** Dunham 2015 as the anchor; Viney; Girel; the *Critique philosophique* record of what James published there and what Renouvier wrote about him. The 1870 diary text and its editorial history — the source is the Houghton manuscript, and the widely circulated version comes through Perry's *Thought and Character of William James*; check whether the quoted text is the manuscript's. Also where the two diverge: James's radical empiricism against Renouvier's phenomenalism, and James's pluralism, which may or may not be Renouvier's finitism carried forward.

**Uchronie and the philosophy of history.** The *philosophie analytique de l'histoire*, the argument against organicist and deterministic historical narrative, his critique of utopianism as licensing despotism, and the Third Republic context that produced it. Whether counterfactual reasoning functions as a method for him or as an illustration.

**The finitism.** The law of number, the pre-Cantor argument, and what remains philosophically once the mathematics is conceded. Cantor is not on-site; whether the argument's failure is load-bearing for anything else in the system is the question.

**The late personalism and the finite God.** Its relation to the earlier functionalism; how the scholarship reads the development.

**The afterlife.** Neo-criticism as a school: Hamelin, Séailles, the Third Republic secular moral-education reception, Durkheim as a student. Renouvier's standing in his century — described as ranking with Comte and Bergson, and as the first Frenchman since Malebranche with a complete idealist system — against his near-total present obscurity. This is tradition-afterlife material and belongs wherever traditions-under-a-name belong, not with the person.

**Counter-sourcing.** The available English material is thin enough that its framings will propagate uncorrected if unchecked. Logue is an advocate; the *Philosopher of Liberty* title announces it. Find the criticism: what the Bergsonians, the positivists, and the later French tradition said against him, and why the system did not survive. A figure this influential who vanished this completely has a reception history, and the reason for the vanishing is content.

## Open decisions

Not settled here.

- **Ring or fence, and on which strand.** The representationalism question and the grounding question are the two that decide it. A split — ring on one strand, fence on another — is architecturally allowed but only worth doing if the strands separate cleanly, which they may not, since the freedom argument and the relational ontology are joined at the certitude claim.
- **Whether the free-will mechanics thread is a Renouvier-and-James piece or a seed-side piece.** The attention-holds-a-thought-in-play material may be more useful worked out on its own terms, with the two of them as sources rather than as the subject.
- **Which register the blog post takes.** The 1870 crisis is ready-made for the named-source biographical mode; the mechanics want a different voice. These are two posts, not one, if both are wanted.
- **"The James post" confirmed (2026-07-27).** It is a blog post — *The Man Who Refused a Finished World*, publishing 2026-07-28 under the positioning series. It names Renouvier and the 1870 wager directly (Renouvier is external-unlinked there; no page exists). It did not surface in the sitemap because it was future-dated at the time of the check. The person page is a separate surface, with the breaches noted above.
