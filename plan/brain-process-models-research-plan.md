# Brain Process Models — Plan & Research Approach

Working document. Provisional throughout — page decisions, subject decisions, and research scope all subject to revision. The scoping below is a site-state audit sufficient to target the programme; it is not the research itself. No surface decisions are made here.

## What this plans

Reference-library backing for the process/brain trio of posts (the survey *Process Models of the Brain*, parked 2030-02-06; the argument *Hierarchy Without Orchestration*, parked 2030-02-08; *Decentralised Cognition Revolution* between them). The survey takes three frameworks — Edelman's Neural Darwinism, Global Workspace Theory (Baars, Dehaene), and Friston's free energy principle — and of the three, only Edelman has any backing on-site. Two load-bearing frameworks stand on nothing, and neither principal is even mentioned anywhere in positioning.

Two moves, kept separate: (1) research the unbacked frameworks to page grade — the two from the post's slate, plus one further candidate the post's own admission criterion admits (complementary learning systems, below); (2) the surface decisions that follow — which persons earn pages (collaborative calls, per the standing rule), whether any subject holds territory, and the link wiring for the trio once pages exist.

A thread runs through all rounds: each framework's standing now includes its reception against modern machine learning. The scoping discussion found that reception sharply uneven — vindication at the level of objective but not mechanism in one case, survival in an unexpected place in another, outright architectural borrowing in a third — and it is treated below as research content per round, to be tested rather than asserted.

**Not a blocker.** The trio can unpark on external links alone (the standing convention: external links until the internal page exists). The gap this plan closes is library coverage, not post readiness. The posts currently carry no internal positioning links at all; they gain them as pages land.

## What the research needs to cover

Organised by framework. Sequencing is not prescribed.

**Global Workspace Theory.** Baars's original statement (1988) — the theatre architecture, and its lineage in AI blackboard systems, which places the theory's engineering ancestry honestly. Dehaene's neuronal workspace as the distinct second contribution: ignition, the late widespread activation around 300ms, the masking paradigm that separates seen from unseen. Then the reception seriously: the dispute over whether the late signature tracks consciousness or merely report (the no-report-paradigm criticism), Block's access-versus-phenomenal objection (the theory is a theory of access), and the adversarial collaboration against IIT — its results were uncomfortable for both sides in places, and getting that right matters more than the promotional literature does. The two-principal structure (originator and neural developer) is itself a finding the surface decisions will need.

**Friston and the free energy principle.** The principle itself: generative models, descending predictions and ascending errors, variational free energy as the quantity minimised. Its lineage — Helmholtz's unconscious inference, predictive coding (Rao & Ballard), the Bayesian brain — kept as lineage (see the boundary note below). The empirical anchors the survey post uses: repetition suppression, mismatch negativity, laminar separation of predictions and errors. Then the criticism track, which is unusually structural: the principle-versus-process-theory distinction and Friston's own concession that the principle is not falsifiable; the triviality objection (a framework that fits everything explains nothing in particular); and the Markov-blanket dispute — whether the blanket is a modelling instrument or a smuggled metaphysics ("The Emperor's New Markov Blankets" is the anchor). Finally the life–mind extension: active inference, self-evidencing, the reach toward autopoiesis — and the enactivist counter-fire, which argues the programme is cognitivism's internalism in new dress. That seam decides where a Friston page sits relative to the built cluster.

*A named pressure on this round: reward prediction error.* The dopamine reward-prediction-error result (Schultz, Dayan & Montague, 1997) — the temporal-difference error signal from reinforcement-learning theory found implemented in dopamine neurons — is a narrower, far harder-evidenced error-correction mechanism, operating on value ("what is worth doing") where the free energy principle operates on perception ("what is coming"), and it is among the most replicated findings in systems neuroscience. On the survey post's own admission criterion — isolates a mechanism cleanly, carries evidence for its core — it outscores the framework currently holding the error-correction seat. The round does not inherit Friston's seat as settled: it examines whether the seat survives the comparison, whether the two are genuinely different questions (perceptual fit vs valuation) that can both stand, and whether the free-energy literature's move of subsuming RPE under the principle is substance or annexation.

**Complementary learning systems.** Admitted to the programme on the same footing as the two rounds above, because it passes the survey post's own test — isolates a mechanism cleanly, carries evidence for its core — and answers a facet the post's slate leaves open. The framework (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly, 1995): the hippocampus learns fast, episodically, one-shot; the neocortex learns slowly, statistically; and consolidation runs through replay — the hippocampus reactivating experience offline, prominently in sleep, so the cortex can interleave it into its statistics without overwriting what it holds. The evidence is two-sided and strong: the amnesic lesion literature (H.M. and successors) on one side, directly recorded rodent hippocampal replay (Wilson & McNaughton onward) on the other. The research covers the 1995 statement and its motivation (it was itself an answer to catastrophic interference in neural networks — the framework was AI-entangled from birth), the replay literature's current state, the revisions (systems-consolidation debates, multiple-trace theory as the standing rival), and the machine-learning reception: replay buffers in reinforcement learning lifted from it explicitly, catastrophic forgetting as the exact failure it exists to solve, continual learning as the frontier where the brain still outclasses every artificial system. Structurally it is the bridge the survey post lacks: the post's three facets run at a lifetime, a moment, and continuously, with the timescales left standing side by side — consolidation is how the moment becomes the lifetime.

**The AI reception, cross-cutting.** Each round checks its framework against the machine-learning record as reception, with the scoping reading held provisionally: for the free energy principle, vindication at the level of objective (self-supervised prediction as the paradigm that won) but not mechanism (backpropagation is not predictive coding; train/deploy is not online inference) — and the engineering failure of active inference to scale, set against the principle's claim of LLMs as confirmation, examined as a claim rather than repeated; for Neural Darwinism, the lottery-ticket resonance (overparameterised networks as repertoire-then-selection) as survival in an unexpected place, against gradient descent as the instructionism Edelman defined himself against; for Global Workspace, outright architectural borrowing (the coordination-among-modules line, the consciousness-indicator rubrics) plus the serial-bottleneck reading of autoregressive generation and chain-of-thought; for complementary learning systems, the most direct inheritance of the four (replay buffers). The deepest shared disanalogy is also content: all four are online models — learning while operating, at watts, without catastrophic forgetting — and that is territory the machine-learning results have not touched.

**The periodised spine (research instrument).** Every parallel above has two dates — when the idea crossed between the fields, and when the crossing became evidential — and they are decades apart in almost every case. The traffic originally ran brain-to-machine: Hopfield's attractor networks (1982), backpropagation through the connectionist movement (1986), complementary learning systems (1995) proposed to fix catastrophic interference in neural networks — and in the free-energy case machine-to-brain, since variational free energy entered Friston's framework *from* the Helmholtz-machine and variational work of Hinton's group in the early-to-mid 90s. Reward prediction error is the lone early convergence, fully evidential already in 1997. For everything else the threshold is 2012 (deep learning working at scale), with the specific parallels crystallising one by one: replay operational ~2015–16 (DQN's buffer; the Kumaran–Hassabis–McClelland update, with McClelland himself in the loop); the prediction-objective reading available 2017–20 and forceful only with 2020s-era capability; the lottery ticket 2019; the modern-Hopfield result 2020; the workspace borrowing 2017–23; the serial-bottleneck reading 2022 onward; the 2024 Nobel as reception-change, not evidence. Underneath the dates sits an inversion: before roughly 2012 brain plausibility legitimised machine-learning methods; between 2012 and 2020 the evidential arrow flipped, and machine-learning success and failure became evidence about brain frameworks — which is also what makes the negative results legible, since a framework could not fail to scale before rivals demonstrably scaled. The spine yields a dating test for the counter-sourcing: a source arguing one of these parallels before its crystallisation date is doing programmatic analogy, not reporting evidence.

**A boundary held, not reopened.** An earlier closure deliberately declined the decoding/routes strand — Helmholtz, Marr, priors-in-perception as an account of how language reaches the child. A Friston round necessarily walks past Helmholtz. The research keeps him as lineage for predictive coding and does not reopen the declined strand by drift; if contact becomes unavoidable, that is a decision to take knowingly, not a side effect.

**Counter-sourcing both.** Both literatures are self-advocating — Friston's especially, with a near-guru reception and profile journalism doing the framing work, and GWT's consciousness-theory marketing not far behind. The working criticism is the counterweight: for the free energy principle, the unfalsifiability and triviality lines from within computational neuroscience and philosophy of science; for GWT, the report-confound line and the access objection. The current standing of the adversarial collaboration should be checked at research time, not carried from memory.

**Edelman, light refresh only.** The page's closing question — whether modern recording techniques vindicated the selectional framework or rendered it unnecessary — could be sharpened with the current state of neural selectionism's afterlife. Nothing else; the page stands.

**The wider survey names stay external — with one fence to restate.** IIT, recurrent processing, attention schema, reservoir computing, the oscillatory framings — the survey post fences these as different-quarry or formal-only, and the research does not chase them beyond what accurate external references require. The exception needing attention is attractor dynamics: the post's "formal rather than mechanistic" fence was fair when written, but the 2024 Nobel to Hopfield and Hinton moved attractor networks into the field's official origin story, and the modern-Hopfield result — transformer attention as a dense associative-memory retrieval step — means the attractor model turned out to sit *inside* the winning architecture rather than losing to it. The fence can hold (attractor dynamics still describes the shape of the dynamics without committing to machinery), but its justification must be restated for the post-Nobel landscape, not carried unexamined. Hopfield — and Hinton — are person-page-calibre questions in their own right, independent of this trio; like Tononi, they are separate later calls, not this round. Also noted and not chased: efficient/sparse coding (Barlow, Olshausen & Field — the redundancy-reduction principle, whose AI afterlife runs indirectly through superposition and sparse-autoencoder interpretability work) and the thousand-brains framework (engineering-motivated, advocacy-heavy, and — like active inference — notable for not having scaled).

## Open decisions

Not settled here.

- **Which persons earn pages — collaborative calls.** Friston is the strongest candidate on his own standing: the field-spanning principle, the citation dominance, the reach into biology and AI. Baars/Dehaene pose the two-principal question: one page, two pages, or a subject page holding both with the principals external. McClelland enters the same conversation via the complementary-learning-systems round (with the replay literature's principals behind him at a further remove). The direct-importance-subject-brings-principals pattern is an example the discussion weighs, not a trigger.
- **Whether a subject holds territory.** Two candidates. *Predictive processing* — a tradition genuinely wider than Friston (Clark, Hohwy, Seth as champions), which would also let a Friston person page stay person-scoped rather than carrying the whole tradition's apparatus. *Global workspace theory* — which would resolve the two-principal question by holding both. "Process models of the brain" is the survey post's own framing, not a public subject name, and is probably not a subject. The territory test applies in every case: a subject page only if it holds territory the person pages can't.
- **Whether the survey post grows a fourth facet.** Consolidation — how the moment becomes the lifetime — is the facet complementary learning systems would add, bridging timescales the post currently leaves standing side by side. A slate decision on a finished post, taken deliberately after the research reports back, not by drift; the post also works untouched, with the framework held library-side.
- **Whether the traffic story becomes a surface.** The spine assembles, as a by-product, a specific and less-well-told story: the two-way traffic between brain science and machine learning — brain as donor through the 90s, the flip, machine learning as evidence since. Whether that becomes a post, feeds other work, or stays scaffolding is decided after the rounds report, not by drift. A general history of AI is declined outright: saturated territory, well-served externally, external links suffice.
- **Which register, if any post falls out.** The expectation is none — the trio already exists and this programme feeds the reference library, with the posts gaining internal links as pages land. If the enactivism-versus-free-energy seam turns out to have its own life, that is a new decision, not a default.
