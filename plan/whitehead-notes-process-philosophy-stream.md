# Notes — process philosophy as a distinct stream

Working notes from a discussion on Whitehead's creativity, the contemporary process-relational cluster, and the architectural question of stream pages. Captures substantive material and editorial/discipline observations to carry forward.

---

## 1. Whitehead's creativity dynamic

Creativity is the ultimate category — "the universal of universals." Not a thing in the world; the principle by which the world advances into novelty. The slogan: *the many become one, and are increased by one*.

The movement:

- A new actual occasion arises by *prehending* the many actual occasions of its past. Prehension is the basic act of taking-account-of — already includes selection and valuation; not passive.
- *Concrescence* unifies the prehensions into one occasion with its own determinate satisfaction.
- The occasion perishes as subject, becomes datum for future arisings, joins the many.
- Repeat. Many+1 is the next many.

Where ordered novelty comes from in Whitehead: the new occasion needs an *initial aim* — a determinate way of integrating the prehensions. Supplied by God's primordial nature, which is the valuation of eternal objects (pure potentials: qualities, structures, forms of definiteness). The occasion modifies the initial aim through its own subjective aim. So: many → prehension → concrescence (guided by initial aim from God's ordering of eternal objects) → satisfaction → perishing → datum.

## 2. The QFT-style reading

Whitehead's apparatus has structural parallels to quantum field theory in metaphysical register. Two possible mappings of the "virtual particle" inclusion:

- *Prehensions as virtual exchanges.* Actual occasions are the on-shell beings; prehensions are the off-shell interaction events. Like virtual-particle exchanges mediating between real particles.
- *Eternal objects as virtual substrate.* Actual occasions are on-shell; eternal objects are off-shell — pure potentialities, not actualised, ingressing into actuals and supplying qualitative determinacy.

Cleaner: both. Prehensions = interactions; eternal objects = field substrate the interactions draw on. Each actual occasion is a concrescence-event that draws on the field and contributes back.

Where the analogy bends: eternal objects are timeless, not short-lived like virtual particles. The ordering comes from God's primordial valuation (pre-given) rather than being intrinsic to the field. The freedom/noise aspect sits differently.

Henry Stapp has worked the Whitehead-QFT terrain explicitly. Stengers, via the Prigogine inheritance, is alive to these resonances.

## 3. The process-relational stream as a distinct movement

Genealogy: Heraclitus (becoming), parts of Leibniz (monads, perspectivism) and Spinoza (substance as activity), Bergson (durée, élan vital), James (radical empiricism, pluralism), Whitehead (the fully systematic version), Hartshorne and Cobb (process theology), Deleuze (claims the lineage in *The Fold*), Latour with affinities, Stengers (direct inheritor), Haraway (acknowledged influence), Massumi, Connolly in political philosophy.

Shared commitments: process over substance, event over object, relation as constitutive rather than derivative, becoming as primary. Anti-representationalism from the being-process pole.

Distinct from:

- Substance metaphysics (Cartesian-Lockean-Humean and analytic descendants)
- Representationalism (mirror picture; primary/secondary qualities; correspondence-truth)
- Analytic linguistic turn (Frege, early Russell, Carnap — language as transparent logical structure)
- Phenomenology in Husserlian register (consciousness-first)
- Continental philosophy of language (Saussure, structuralism, Derrida — different pole)
- Naturalistic reduction (some readings of Quine, eliminative materialism)

Has its own institutional infrastructure: *Process Studies* journal, Center for Process Studies, International Whitehead Conferences.

## 4. The "relational" trap

The everyday word *relational* points at *things in connection* — pre-existing terms linked by relations between them. Network thinking. Relational databases. Relational ethics. The terms are primary; relations are added.

Process-philosophical *relational* is something different — closer to *internal* or *constitutive* relations. The relata don't exist prior to the relations and then enter into them. The relations are what constitute the terms. Each actual occasion *is* its prehensions of past occasions; there's no occasion sitting somewhere first that then has prehensions. The relating *is* the being. Same for Stengers' practices in reciprocal capture: there's no practice existing prior to its constraints and its couplings; the practice is constituted in those couplings.

This is why *particle interaction* might do more accurate work as a differentiator — interaction names the *event of coming-together* that constitutes the terms, not the connection between pre-existing things. The atomistic suggestion needs filtering (occasions aren't atoms in space and time but pulses of becoming), but the event-emphasis is closer to the structural move.

Any deeper treatment of the stream needs to qualify *relational* early. Without that, readers default to network thinking.

## 5. Self-positioning of the contemporary cluster

Stengers, Latour, Haraway, Deleuze (and onward to Viveiros de Castro, Blaser, de la Cadena, Mignolo, Escobar) often present themselves as something new — new materialism, ontological turn, sympoiesis, multispecies thinking, STS, posthumanities — rather than as continuation of process philosophy.

The underlying ontological mechanism is shared across the cluster. The self-positioning varies for both substantive and positional reasons:

- Distancing from Whitehead's heavier apparatus (God, eternal objects, the full speculative system)
- Distancing from process theology and its American-Protestant resonance
- Different national/institutional lineages (Belgian chemistry-Prigogine for Stengers; French sociology-semiotics for Latour; American biology-feminism for Haraway)
- Strategic distancing from "metaphysics" as a label while still doing metaphysics

The working hypothesis — "they are more process philosophy than they like to admit" — has weight, but unevenly across the cluster. Stengers is explicit about her Whitehead inheritance (*Thinking with Whitehead*); Deleuze claims the lineage openly; Haraway cites and acknowledges; Latour is more ambivalent.

## 6. Cosmopolitics on the being-process side

Stengers' work is practice-oriented, but the *reasoning* is process-metaphysical throughout, not language-relational. Her practices have their own modes of existence (Whiteheadian); they enter reciprocal capture (process-relational); they're held together through constraints and ecologies. The medium of analysis is events, occasions, processes — not language games or vocabularies.

She sits on the being-process side of P0, alongside Whitehead. The language-relational refusal of representationalism (the Two-Pronged piece's other half) belongs to Rorty's side. Practice-philosophy ≠ language-relational philosophy. This matters for any future opinion-piece framing.

## 7. Open architectural and editorial questions

- **Stream pages.** Whether philosophical streams need their own page type, or fit as subject pages with a particular shape (genealogical, multi-thinker, contested boundaries). Candidates for stream treatment alongside process philosophy: pragmatism, phenomenology, structuralism, analytic philosophy, hermeneutics.

- **Naming the process-relational stream.** *Process philosophy* is the established label, recognisable, with currency. Alternatives: *process-relational philosophy* (honours the relational dimension as co-load-bearing), *speculative philosophy* (Whitehead's own term), *process thought* (broadest, includes theology). Tension: the classical core sits comfortably under "process philosophy"; the contemporary extensions sit there partly. A page would need to acknowledge multiple-affiliations and the contemporary self-positioning.

- **Opinion piece angle.** Initial framing was a P0 duality reading of philosophy of organism + cosmopolitics — that doesn't quite work, because both sit on the same side of P0. Better framing: the process-relational stream as a distinct movement, doing being-side relational work, recognised as such against the dominant streams. SPLectrum's position relative to it (compatible commitment to anti-representationalism, different medium of analysis) would be one of the load-bearing observations.

## 8. Discipline note

Equal standing (P4) is a discipline in how to think and speak, not just an architectural principle for the site. Holding multiple languages without the comparison reflex — being able to speak Whitehead's apparatus on its own terms, the QFT analogy on its own terms, without continually routing both back to where the seed says what — is part of the discipline.

The instinct toward synthesis-by-translation-into-a-master-framework is precisely what equal standing rejects. Synthesis happens *across* languages (each speaking its own work), not *by reduction* to one. Worth carrying into any future stream-page or opinion-piece work, where the temptation to claim territory is strongest.
