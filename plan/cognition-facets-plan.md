## Psychology facet — three pages

**State: hidden.** The psychology section on the cognition landing is commented out until the facet is built to this plan. An interim developmental-psychology page (Piaget + Vygotsky only) exists unlinked; it is superseded by page 1 below.

### 1. Developmental psychology — the three streams (build first: all backing exists)

One page, three rival answers to the facet's question — how does the acquiring work? — with the language-acquisition dispute as the arena where they met. Mirrors the Human Ways of Sharing post's framing (blog 2026-09-04); post and reference page must not contradict.

- **Usage-based / social-interactionist** — Piaget (acquisition constructive: assimilation/accommodation, structures built by the process's own running), Vygotsky (acquisition socially fed: internalised from shared activity, language and tools first), Tomasello (usage-based language: joint attention, intention-reading). All three have built person pages.
- **Nativism** — the innate endowment unfolding: Chomsky, poverty of stimulus, universal grammar. Built: nativism subject page, Chomsky person page.
- **Behaviourism** — shaping from outside, no inner process worth studying: Skinner, conditioning, reinforcement; the Chomsky review as the hinge. Built: behaviourism subject page, Skinner and Watson person pages.

Held for later entry into this page: **Kelly / personal construct psychology** — a fourth, distinct angle (not how capability is acquired but how the acquired construct system shapes what can be experienced next). Enters when the constructivism close-affinity work opens (the deferred Kelly ↔ von Glasersfeld tie lands on the constructivism subject page then).

### 2. Ecological psychology (build second: needs person-page backing)

Two principals, both currently without pages — the person-page calls are collaborative and still open:

- **J. J. Gibson** — direct perception: information picked up from the ambient optic array, not constructed from impoverished input; affordances (significance relational — a property of the environment relative to the animal's capabilities); perception-action coupling. *The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception* (1979).
- **Eleanor Gibson** — the tradition's account of acquisition: perceptual learning by differentiation — coming to detect distinctions and structure always present in the stimulation but not yet picked up; the visual-cliff work on infant depth perception. National Medal of Science; earns consideration on her own standing.
- The later formalising line (Turvey, Shaw — ecological dynamics) at most a sentence.
- Note: a same-surname collision *with each other* — internal links must disambiguate (j-gibson / e-gibson or similar).

### 3. Memory research (build third: needs the most new backing)

Shape: the **Ebbinghaus–Bartlett axis** — storage versus reconstruction, meaning excluded versus meaning doing all the work — with Tulving as the distinction that speaks directly to the definition.

- **Ebbinghaus** (1885) — nonsense syllables precisely to strip meaning out; the forgetting curve; memory as measurable stored traces.
- **Bartlett** — *Remembering* (1932), the War of the Ghosts retellings: recall systematically transforms toward the familiar; remembering is reconstruction guided by **schemas**, the organised residue of prior experience. What is retained from experience is not the experience but an acquired framework that shapes both recall and new intake — the memory-side twin of Piaget's assimilation. Strong person-page candidate.
- **Tulving** — episodic vs semantic memory: experiences remembered vs knowledge extracted from them and freed of occasion; semantic memory as where the acquiring's residue lives. Likely person-page candidate.
- **Loftus** — the misinformation effect: reconstruction demonstrated live, memories rebuilt to include what arrived after the event; the modern empirical confirmation of Bartlett. One confirming line, not a page.
- Explicitly out: Baddeley's working memory (about the running, not the acquiring); Schacter (refinement of the Bartlett line, not a distinct answer).
