# Working Sketch: Decoherence, Partial Facts, and the Subject — Discussion Capture & Research Programme

Working document. Provisional throughout. Captures a conversation that ran from the absence of decoherence in popular QM history to a facet-wise picture of the subject, and lays out the research programme to build the supporting persons/subjects base for close-affinity pieces, site pages, and blog posts.

## What prompted this

Adam Becker's history of quantum mechanics (Mindscape appearance) contains no decoherence and no RQM. The absence turned out to be structural: a history organised around *interpretations as camps* has no slot for the one development that is shared ground rather than contested ground. Decoherence is a mechanism owned by no camp — which is why it changed what the measurement problem is while vanishing from the story of who won.

Pulling that thread led through Everett's observer, the inversion of the standard ordering, partial decoherence, emergent spacetime, quantum error correction, and the current state of RQM research. The findings bear directly on two open SPLectrum fronts: the precise definition of the subject, and a coherent-regime investigation (seed-side, to be opened as its own document — see the surfaces section).

## The discussion distilled

### Everett's observer — retention without the cauldron

Everett's observer is deliberately deflationary: any physical system with a memory; correlations that persist as records. No consciousness, no special status — a photographic plate qualifies. Nothing triggers branching; linearity of the dynamics forces the multiplicity, and "splitting" is DeWitt's imagery for one state developing internal relative-state structure.

The rhyme with the seed is real and bounded. Everett's observer is *constituted by its records* — close to historicity as accumulated retention. But the memory is passive storage: records accumulate without acting on each other. No cauldron, no directionality, no embryonic teleology. Retention without aliveness — the thermostat-end of the gradient wearing a memory.

The deeper disanalogy: Everett's relative states are relative all the way up to an absolute — the universal wavefunction, the totality with no other. Experience becomes a perspectival residue to be explained away; the subject a bookkeeping entry in a view from nowhere. Determinism is preserved by relocating it to the one level no one occupies ("determinism-in-exile"), while the level that has experiencers recovers its physics only through the contested Born-rule derivations. The perspectival-residue-as-defect pattern is the standing fence diagnostic.

### The inversion, and where the pieces already exist

The question "is there work running the mechanism the other way — multiple subjects each experiencing their reality, decohering into a shared reality?" has a scattered but real answer:

- **Quantum Darwinism** (Zurek): the environment as *witness*; objectivity = information redundantly copied enough that many observers can independently extract it. The mechanism of shared-reality-as-achievement, narrated from the wrong direction (universal state downward).
- **RQM with cross-perspective links** (Adlam & Rovelli 2022): shared facts stabilised pairwise by interaction, out of perspectival facts. The right ordering, with a postulate where the machinery should be. The postulate answers an objection van Fraassen raised in "Rovelli's World" — he certified RQM's consistency, then located the sharing-gap and proposed a weak linking postulate himself.
- **No-go theorems** (Frauchiger–Renner 2018; Brukner; Bong et al. local friendliness 2020): assuming observed facts are absolute leads to contradictions / experimentally violated inequalities. The field's own mathematics clearing ground for facts-relative-to-observers as the conservative option.
- **Full inversions at the fringe**: Müller's law-without-law; QBism (Fuchs) — the ordering without a decoherence-grade mechanism.

Configuration: the ordering exists philosophically, the mechanism exists physically, nobody has bolted them together. Zurek has the machinery with inherited metaphysics; Rovelli has the metaphysics with a postulate. The gap between them is where the decoherence rhyme lives.

### Decoherence as emergent, not mechanism-beneath

In every existing account decoherence keeps its standard status: a consequence of fundamental dynamics, described from a standpoint no subject occupies. In the inverted picture that is not stable — there is no global state for decoherence to be a process *in*. It changes kind: a pattern-level description of what massive mutual coupling looks like; the statistical face of many uptakes at once. Irreversibility as sedimentation seen from inside — practical unrecoverability of a deviation once enough uptakes have taken it up.

This dissolves a standing circularity the field itself worries about (Zeh did, openly): decoherence theory explains classicality while helping itself to a system–environment split put in by hand. If decoherence is emergent from subject-couplings, the split is not an assumption — each subject *is* a cut, and "environment" is other subjects.

### The graded middle — water, clusters, partial facts

Water's route from one molecule to bulk is not a threshold: properties arrive piecewise as the hydrogen-bond network grows; bulk behaviour asymptotes when interior molecules dominate surface ones. No n at which water "happens." Wetness is the pattern-level face of massive coupling — the bond is local and pairwise and all there is; wetness is what enough bonds are, seen at scale.

Decoherence has the same graded middle, experimentally walked (interference with 25,000+ amu molecules; fringes fading progressively with coupling). Zurek's redundancy is explicitly graded: low redundancy = a fact for one or two takers, fragile, revisable; high redundancy = a fact any newcomer can extract, which is what "objective" operationally means.

Mapping: two subjects coupling = flickering dimer; family/workplace = cluster with an interior; language community = bulk. The taken-for-granted world is high-redundancy sediment — same coupling, deep enough that statistics take over from individuals. Bulk-criterion translated: a shared reality reads as objective when most participants couple overwhelmingly to each other; surface never vanishes, so every shared reality keeps an edge — subjects coupled both inward and outward, which is where inter-cluster tension structurally lives.

### Partial decoherence — the facet-wise turn

The physically precise point, underappreciated even in the field: **decoherence is a property of observables, not of systems**. A system decoheres in the bases the environment couples to and stays coherent in the ones it doesn't. What gets witnessed is whatever the interaction is written in (einselection). Position decoheres near-instantly because nearly all ambient couplings are written in position; polarisation stays coherent because almost nothing couples to it.

Entangled photons are the worked case: thoroughly macro-embedded (localised, timestamped, their creation a classical lab fact) while polarisation remains entangled — a public object and a private correlation at once, sorted property by property. Coherence persists where coupling doesn't reach, and can be actively maintained by keeping a degree of freedom away from witnesses.

Carried across: **sharing is facet-wise, not subject-wise**. A subject decoheres per property, per game — deeply sedimented in some facets, coherent (self-held, unwitnessed) in others. Privacy not as withdrawal of the whole subject but as the unshared basis of an otherwise shared one — and partly steerable by construction.

### Monogamy — what sharing costs

Decoherence and entanglement are one event seen from two sides: coherence is never destroyed, it relocates into the joint state. Maximal entanglement between a pair means maximal local mixedness for each member — the correlation is real but resides in the relation, held by neither relatum. Monogamy is the bookkeeping: what is given over to the joint state is subtracted from the local one, exactly.

The gradient: coherence self-held (facet uncoupled, private, pure) → entangled with one other (facet real only between the two; each side locally already surrendered) → witnessed by many (redundant, sedimented, public). The middle rung's hard truth: **sharing a facet with even one other party costs the whole of its self-held purity**. The private ends not at "shared with everyone" but at *shared with anyone*.

### Spacetime as the deepest sediment

The entangled facet has no spacetime standing: no distance-dependence, no signal, no frame-independent ordering, no location. Spatiotemporal vocabulary fails to grip it, while the decohered facets sit in spacetime happily. Cleaner statement: **spacetime is a feature of the decohered layer** — arguably the backbone of the sediment, the redundancy structure par excellence. Measurement = the private facet acquiring spacetime coordinates; merging into the macro *is* becoming locatable.

The it-from-qubit programme (Van Raamsdonk; Maldacena–Susskind ER=EPR; Ryu–Takayanagi) converges on the strong version from the physics side: spacetime connectivity made of entanglement; geometry derived from correlation structure in a theory with no bulk space at all. Caveats held honestly: results live in AdS; extension to our cosmos open. Contrast with LQG: LQG quantises geometry (geometry stays ontologically basic, granular); it-from-qubit emerges it. Only the second is the natural ally of spacetime-as-sediment — though LQG's own finding that entanglement structure determines which spin-network states look like smooth space marks a quiet convergence. Standing irony: Rovelli owns both RQM (dissolves the absolute) and LQG (keeps a granular absolute), never fully cashed against each other.

### Massive entanglement is internal decoherence — and the engineered exception

Closed-system thermalisation (Popescu–Short–Winter; ETH): a large closed system in a pure, highly entangled state looks thermal — decohered — from every inside position. **A sufficiently entangled system is its own environment.** Global purity, local mixedness: the universal state kept pure at the top and the classical world lived in are one object described from nowhere and from somewhere — and only the somewhere is occupied. The decohered macro world *is* massive entanglement seen from inside.

The quantum computer is the engineered exception — a constructed multi-party coherent regime. Three moves: starve the couplings (cryogenics, vacuum, encoding in polarisation-like rather than position-like observables); choreograph the internal witnessing (gates as scripted, basis-selective, switched couplings — the difference from a thermalising lump is not the amount of entanglement but whether witnessing is promiscuous or choreographed); and hide facts non-locally (error correction: the logical qubit lives only in joint correlations; every physical qubit locally fully mixed; the environment's witnessing is irredeemably local, so a fact with no local address cannot be taken up). Threshold theorem: below ~1% physical error, more particles = exponentially less logical decoherence — the naive scaling inverts for facts stored relationally. Demonstrated (Google Willow, 2024).

Two morals for the coherent-regime investigation: coherence at scale is not a resting state but ongoing work against the witnessing default — **sediment is free; coherence has a maintenance cost**. And privacy at scale = the fact having no place — held by the configuration, not by any member; a secret with no single holder.

### The unclosed circle, and RQM's current move

Quantum information did feed foundations (QBism, quantum Darwinism, the no-go theorems all came from it). But working QI culture *uses* the observer without defining it — the "church of the larger Hilbert space" refactors the observer into a modelling choice, silently Everett-shaped by habit (purification always available, availability read as ontology) though the practice is agnostic. The engineering has already committed to answers it won't own: witnessing as local, facts as facts-for-couplings, revisable until redundantly copied, coherent-for-these-couplings vs decohered-for-those — a relational, facet-wise, witness-based picture of fact-formation, implemented in hardware, unclaimed.

RQM is now claiming it: Di Biagio & Rovelli, "Relative Information, Relative Facts" (Oct 2025, rev. Feb 2026) — RQM reformulated in quantum-information terms; **perspectives as commutative subalgebras of observables rather than entire systems** (the facet-wise turn, formalised); relative facts defined via relative information; no addition to orthodox theory, no special role for observers; Everett's own relative-state machinery reclaimed with the top rung removed. Live battlefield: Lawrence–Markiewicz–Żukowski no-go counterattacks; Cavalcanti–Di Biagio–Rovelli defence; Adlam developing the inherent-and-dynamical relationality line independently; Riedel on composite observers and the combination problem; Faglia contra. The fact-nets formalisation project sits under the QISS (Quantum Information Structure of Spacetime) consortium — RQM and it-from-qubit institutionally converging.

### The philosophy triangle

The philosophical literature sorts into three camps, each holding one piece:

- **Metaphysicians of relations**: Candiotto (RQM as ontic structural realism; relations prior to objects, objects as relational nodes), Dorato, Oldofredi (bundle theory), the Ladyman–Ross / French OSR lineage, Esfeld. Relations-first, but taxonomic in instinct — RQM as a specimen to file in a pre-existing ontology. No subject, no dynamics of sharing.
- **Experience-side**: Bitbol (neo-Kantian, phenomenological reading of RQM), QBism's participatory realism (Fuchs, Mermin; Jamesian ancestry), Healey's pragmatism, Barad's agential realism. The subject, with principled austerity about mechanism.
- **Physicists**: Di Biagio–Rovelli, Zurek. The mechanism, bolted to inherited metaphysics.

Nobody runs the ordering: subjects-experiencing first, the shared as sedimented achievement, decoherence/redundancy/cross-perspective links as the mechanism of the sedimenting. Same triangle as in the physics, reproduced in the philosophy — and no one inside positioned to see it as a triangle.

## What this offers the subject-definition question

Candidate learnings, held loosely — each a structure the science hands over, not a derivation:

- **Facet-wise constitution.** The subject is not sorted into coherent/decohered wholesale but per facet, with the boundary set by where coupling reaches. Di Biagio–Rovelli's perspectives-as-observable-algebras is this, formalised. Connects directly to the language-set architecture: a facet ≈ an observable of the subject; a language game couples to specific facets.
- **The cut as self-maintained.** "Each subject is a cut" dissolves the system–environment circularity: the boundary is not an assumption put in by hand but something the subject maintains — an operational-closure shape.
- **Belonging = redundancy grading.** Fuzzy membership gets a quantitative twin: how many independent uptakes carry the facet. Graded resonance-strength as redundancy of witnessing.
- **Privacy as basis-selective and relational.** The private is not a region but the unshared basis; at scale, privacy = non-addressability (the fact having no place). Feeds the coherent-regime investigation its structure: coherent and decohered as conditions distributed across one subject's facets, not regimes a subject is in.
- **The cost structure.** Sharing a facet with anyone surrenders its self-held purity entirely; the fact migrates into the relation. Sediment is free; coherence costs maintenance. This gives the privacy value a structural economics.
- **The boundary case sharpened.** Everett's observer — retention without cauldron — is a precise foil for what the subject-definition must add beyond memory: the retentions acting on each other.
- **Spacetime as sediment.** The apparently absolute container is the deepest shared layer — same status as the rest of the sediment. Radical but load-bearing for interrelational pluralism's claim that THE reality is a widely shared layer.

Open questions carried forward: whether "facet" and "language set" are the same cut at different granularities; whether the redundancy grading maps onto historicity-depth / coupling-breadth / recursion-density or adds a fourth axis; what the subject-side analogue of an interaction Hamiltonian is (what determines which facets a given game couples to).
