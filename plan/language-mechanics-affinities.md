## What this is for

The three metaphysical pillars share a common engine: distinction, encapsulation as language sets, combinatorial mixing in a cauldron, historicity as path-landscape, coupling, and the subject-reality / shared-reality stratification. These are seed-side mechanics, not a sixth pillar. The candidates below are read for where they touch that engine. The likely homes:

- Most sit as **close-affinity pieces** reading the field from the seed's angle (resonance with the mechanics).
- A divergence/convergence thread may **feed the epistemology pillar** (how knowledge stabilises by convergence) without becoming a new pillar.
- One candidate (Berger and Luckmann) carries enough internal machinery to warrant a **subject page**.

## The field, by joint of the mechanics

### Relational primitive — meaning as position, not substance

Distributional semantics and the embedding-space line. Firth ("a word by the company it keeps"), Harris's distributional hypothesis, through to the connectionist / ML reading. Meaning constituted relationally; no atom standing alone. Saussure (on-site) is the structuralist ancestor; this line turns the static structuralist system into something the mechanics can use. Touches the no-neutral-substrate claim. Cross-bleeds with the AI information-field plan.

### Encapsulation — how distinctions bundle into held units

Personal construct psychology (George Kelly). The construct system as the subject's held repertoire of bipolar distinctions, genuinely individual, through which the world is read. The closest psychological statement of *language sets as what the subject is*. Carries the **divergence** face — construct systems genuinely differ between persons. The Umwelt thread (von Uexküll, on-site) sits beside it from the biosemiotic side.

### Combinatorial mixing — new states from combining held units

Conceptual blending (Fauconnier and Turner): two mental spaces combine to yield emergent structure present in neither input — the most direct linguistics parallel to the mixing event. Conceptual metaphor (Lakoff and Johnson) sits one step back: mapping rather than multiplicative combination, same family.

*~~Thinnest coverage in the field.~~ — Resolved by the field research (2026-06-28).* The first-pass "thinnest coverage" read was an artifact of too narrow a test. Mixing is a **family** — mixing, blending, layering, projection/mapping, fusion, recombination, superposition, montage — unified by one signature: start with more than one held unit, get something out that exceeds the parts. On that boundary the joint is heavily populated (Fauconnier & Turner, Koestler's bisociation, Bakhtin/Barthes/Goffman on layering, recombination in genetics and combinatorics, montage in film). The originality is not the operation but its **scope** (whole language-sets, not just concepts) and **status** (a repeatable, constitutive, ungrounded primitive — no external engine). That conjunction is what the broad family, granted in full, still does not collectively assert.

### Historicity and path-landscape — the past shaping possible futures

Dynamic systems theory of development (Thelen and Smith). Development as a landscape of attractors shaped by the system's own activity history, no pre-laid program. The path-landscape in developmental vocabulary, and the learning-in-general parallel — not language-specific, a theory of how any developing system's history constrains its futures. Watch: near-peer to the process sketch itself, so it may read as close affinity shading toward convergence.

### Convergence — how the shared gets into the private

Vygotsky: internalisation; the inter-subjective becoming intra-subjective; private speech as shared speech taken inward. Tomasello: usage-based acquisition, language built from extracted interaction-patterns, no innate machine underneath. Together, the construction-from-use account of how a cauldron gets filled. The **convergence** face that Kelly's divergence face needs to meet.

### Coupling — engagement between subjects without direct transmission

Di Paolo, "linguistic bodies," and the enactivist line: language as coupling of autonomous systems, meaning in the interaction. Couples directly to Varela / Maturana (on-site) and to the coupled-cauldrons account of engagement.

### Subject reality vs. shared reality — stabilisation of the common layer

Berger and Luckmann (subject candidate). The shared reality as stabilised settlement; objectivity as a function of convergence; the dialectic of externalisation / objectivation / internalisation. Rich enough to carry a subject because it has its own internal machinery — the three-moment dialectic itself parallels the mechanics. The lifeworld / phenomenological-psychology thread feeds it from the experiential side.

## Two observations on the shape of the field

**~~The mixing gap.~~ — Closed.** The field research found coverage *even* across all seven joints once mixing is read as a family rather than one narrow "multiplicative" form. The framework's exposed-and-original edge is no longer "nobody mixes" but the *conjunction* the field never makes: mixing of whole **language-sets** + as a **repeatable primitive** + **constitutive and ungrounded**. That conjunction, not the operation, is the original move — and it is the sharper, more defensible edge to write from.

**The divergence/convergence tension.** Kelly (divergence — subject realities genuinely differ) and Vygotsky (convergence — the shared comes in and constitutes the private) pull opposite ways. Every developmental thinker leans one way; the framework needs both, and the meeting point is the coupling account. This tension is the likely organising angle for whatever close-affinity piece gets written — not a tidy thinker-by-thinker walk.
