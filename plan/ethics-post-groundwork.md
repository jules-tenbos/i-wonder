# Descriptive Ethics — Discussion Findings and Post Groundwork

Working notes from a conversation developing the ethics post. Captures where the thinking landed, the structural findings that emerged, and the shape the post is aiming for. Provisional throughout — this is material to work the storyline from, not a settled brief.

Target post: **The Ethics of Language Games** — scheduled 2026-07-20, `[reality]` series.

## What the post is for

A **ground-laying** post, not a verdict-delivering one. Its job is to show the structure of language games within belonging and culture, lay the ground for descriptive ethics, and prepare for specific content-infilling and interpretation later (on the reference surfaces, where the frame can carry it).

It works in the blog register — thinking-in-motion, non-contradiction with the reference pages the only bar. It extends past the current ethics pages rather than contradicting them, so it can go ahead without any page rework.

The line it must hold: **show the reading as a tool the reader can pick up; do not draw the conclusions.** Worked examples make the structure visible, then stop before the verdict on any named living culture. Reaching the verdicts would front-run the very infilling the post is meant to prepare, and would land culture-level judgements before the frame is solid enough to hold them without reading as judgement.

## The core structural findings

### 1. Values are scoped to the language game in play — primarily

This is the ground floor and the primary ethics claim. Values are held in the game in play, full stop. Any wider linking across games is **secondary** — still ethics, but the derived layer, not the ground. The game-scoping itself is the live claim; the cross-game question is downstream *within* ethics, not a departure into another pillar.

The layered reading:

- **Primary:** values scoped to the game in play.
- **Secondary, within a subject:** the multi-membership relations — how one person's games resonate or pull, at the level of separation the subject holds.
- **Secondary, at culture scale:** the culture's game-mix and its primary axis — the "broadly good" as a reading of resonance-and-primacy across the culture's games.

### 2. Back in language land

With language games at the front, ethics is the seed's core language-reading turned to the value register. Reality lives in language; values are a register of that language; reading values is reading a register. So "polarisation," "homogeneous," "heterogeneous" are not sociological labels imported from outside — they describe how the registers of a culture's language sit together. Ethics needs no separate machinery; it is the seed's reading applied at the value layer. This is the consistent base to reason from.

### 3. The static / dynamic divide — ethics : politics :: ontology : aesthetics

Ethics and politics split the same territory the way ontology and aesthetics do: **static** cut and **dynamic** cut.

- **Ethics** reads the standing configuration of values-in-games: that a subject holds several games, at a level it sets; that a culture has this mix and this primary axis; that it is homogeneous, heterogeneous, or polarised.
- **Politics** reads the movement — how the coexistence plays out, shifts, is contested and settled over time; the standing apparatus that settles pulls resonance alone won't resolve.

This re-draws the ethics/politics boundary correctly. The earlier "reading vs managing" line was the wrong axis. The right axis is static/dynamic. The reality page's handoff ("managing them ... is the work of the next pillar") is thinner than it first appears: politics gets the *movement and the apparatus*; the standing configuration — including the natural relations among a subject's games and a culture's mix — stays in ethics.

### 4. Resonance, not imposition — and polarisation as the pathology

The method is to refuse the shoehorn. "The good soldier," "the good judge" try to fit an actor toward a desired position — an imposition dressed as description, because asking "what is the good instance of this role?" already smuggles in a target. (Note also: "good at the game" is a reading of *skill*, not of *values* — a skilled and an unskilled soldier hold the same war-game values. The two readings come apart.)

The alternative reads only internal resonance: across the games a culture holds, how much do they share a base and sit coherently, versus pull apart? This stays resonance-not-imposition because the culture is read against *itself* — its own professed goods — never scored against the reader's primary axis.

The spectrum, with a specific pathology at one end:

- **Homogeneous:** games resonate around a shared primary axis; the broad good is coherent and legible.
- **Heterogeneous:** many games, weak resonance, no settled primary axis; the broad good is thin or plural — but not self-contradicting.
- **Polarised:** two or more games raised to co-primacy that *do not resonate*; the broad good is asserted along axes that pull against each other. The culture contradicts itself at the level of what it calls good.

**Polarisation is not heterogeneity.** Heterogeneity is many games with a thin shared base, not necessarily in tension. Polarisation is contradiction at the level of the primary axis — a culture professing a primary good while simultaneously giving primacy to a game that spends what that good is made of.

### 5. The instrumental / celebrated distinction (load-bearing, flagged as not yet settled)

"Primary axis" is now carrying real weight and needs to be firm before any culture example rests on it. Every culture has a military and a market; that is not the diagnostic. The cut is:

- **Instrumental-and-subordinate:** e.g. war as means to protect the good life — resonant, subordinated, not co-primary.
- **Celebrated-and-co-primary:** e.g. martial glory as itself a pinnacle of the good — co-primary, non-resonant, polarising.

The polarisation reading turns on this hinge. It is flagged as the piece still to settle firmly.

### 6. Agency — setting the level of mixing

Separation between a subject's games (e.g. family orthogonal to professional life) is **not enforced and not required**. It occurs where it occurs, naturally, and its degree is the subject's own call — for some (a family business) there is little or none, and nothing is wrong with that. This is the privacy value read correctly: not a wall the framework mandates, but a degree the subject sets.

The agency the post can carry is therefore: **the subject setting their own level of mixing / non-mixing** — not choosing values from outside a game, but choosing how separate or blended their belongings are held. This is the same faculty the conversation started from ("reaffirm or switch the belonging you were born into"), sharpened.

The pathology (see social media, below) is the **enforced overarching** — a single axis imposed from outside that strips the subject's setting away.

Under the static/dynamic cut, agency sits on both sides under different aspects: the *level a subject holds* is static (ethics reads it as part of the configuration); the *exercising and shifting* of it is dynamic (politics reads it as coexistence in motion). This dissolves the earlier "is agency ethics or politics?" knot — it was never either/or.

## The images (for a multi-image contrast post)

The post may be built around multiple images to contrast, each making one specific structural point. Each earns its place by its job:

- **Family** — received-through-belonging, and orthogonality at the level the subject sets. The warm, universal case: values arrive by belonging; separation from work is natural, not enforced.
- **The soldier** — nested games and the shared game across the line. Three value-sets: *us* (the unit — values held horizontally among co-soldiers, loyalty, not leaving a man; the belonging that carries the values is the unit, not the uniform); *us-and-them* (the war game itself, which both sides co-inhabit — laws and customs of war, treatment of prisoners, "fair warfare," held *across* the enemy line; the enemy is a co-member of the war game, not the pure other outside all shared game); *off-game* (the same person as civilian, where the killing licence is simply absent). The striking point: deny the shared game and the values toward the enemy don't flip, they *vanish* — dehumanisation is the withdrawal of shared-game membership, which is what removes the fair-warfare constraints.
- **Free market / advertising** — polarisation. The good-life-professed-while-spending-what-it's-made-of. Advertising is a sharp instance: a game that manufactures the wants the flourishing game then cannot satisfy.

**Caution on the market/advertising image:** it tips toward verdict fastest and is where the anti-bipolar-framing discipline bites hardest. Keep it doing the structural job — showing what non-resonant co-primacy looks like — and off the "late capitalism is bad" register. The other two images are safer; this one needs the lightest hand.

## Where standard ethics sits (context, mostly offstage in the post)

How the traditions handle role-differentiated ethics (civilian / police / military — same society, incompatible permissions):

- **Consequentialism** dissolves it: one standard, roles as instruments toward it; the role has no independent moral standing.
- **Deontology** feels the strain: reaches for role-based duties / "role morality," but authorises the role from above by a higher universal principle — relativity always on a leash back to one ground.
- **Virtue ethics** handles it most naturally (the good soldier, the good judge, MacIntyre's practices with internal goods) but still wants an overarching account of the good life that ranks and harmonises the roles.

Common thread: every tradition treats role-differentiation as **a problem to be reconciled to a single standard.** The plurality is tolerated, derivative, authorised from above — not fundamental. The drive to reconcile assumes a **hierarchy** among memberships; the observation here is that this hierarchy is largely an afterthought. People switch roles without mapping how the good and bad of each line up; the reconciliation is a theorist's construction, not a thing the person does in the living. Coexistence-without-comparison is the ordinary case; override is the exception theorists over-attend to because it is where the puzzles are.

### Currents that profile ethics as plurality

None routes the plurality through *belonging* the way this position does; each reaches part of it:

- **Value pluralism (Berlin):** many genuine goods, real conflict, no master ranking — but the goods float somewhat free, not routed through membership. A cousin, not a twin.
- **Walzer, *Spheres of Justice*:** different spheres with their own internal distributive logics; social meanings constitute values, read from inside the community's shared understandings; tyranny is one sphere's logic invading another. The closest thing in political philosophy to this position's move; not currently on the close-affinity page and arguably should be considered.
- **MacIntyre:** plural practices with internal goods, but pulled back under one overarching narrative — the figure who sees the plurality and then declines it.
- **David Wong** (already on the close-affinity page): pluralistic relativism — real plurality with a shared floor; the best-placed existing ally, though he sets the floor in universal features of the human condition rather than in whatever two games actually share.
- **Bernard Williams** (already on the page): profiles it by demolition — attacks "morality, the peculiar institution" — good for the negative move, less for the positive structure.
- **Stuart Hampshire:** conflict as the permanent condition; procedure for hearing both sides over a substantive ranking — a bridge to the *politics* pillar (coexistence) more than to ethics.
- **Charles Taylor** (already on the page): plural goods rooted in frameworks of meaning, no God's-eye ranking.

The distinctive move — values received by belonging to a game, membership constituting holding them, the plurality being a plurality of belongings — is the assembly the field hasn't done.

## Social media — the enforced-overarching pathology

The observation: family and professional life are, for most people, **orthogonal** — they don't share an axis, so they don't have to be commensurated, which is what lets the switching between them be frictionless. The separation (where it exists) does protective work — it is privacy at the level of a person's plural belongings.

The harm of social media is **not** that it removes separation (separation was never obligatory), but that it **enforces an overarching frame**: a single axis imposed from outside, onto which all a subject's games are dragged and read, regardless of the level of mixing the subject would set. This connects to the ethics page's soundness condition — a reading is sound where anchored in what is *genuinely shared* between reading context and game read, unsound where it imports non-shared content as though shared. **Context collapse** (boyd, Marwick) is a machine for unsound readings: an utterance made in one game's frame is read by members of a game that doesn't share its base — the family remark judged by the professional game, the decade-old post by today's game. The values don't transfer because the shared base isn't there.

Kept honest: this is not "social media bad" (that would be the bipolar framing the tone guide warns against, and collapsed contexts sometimes do good work — exposing abuse or harassment that orthogonality had hidden). The clean claim is structural: social media enforces an overarching frame where the subject would otherwise set the level, and a plural-belonging subject is destabilised by that enforcement in a specific, diagnosable way.

## On the ethics pages — recommendation

**No page update driven by this post.** The post is ground-laying, in the blog register; nothing in it contradicts the pages, it extends past them. The three findings that surfaced are each not-yet-page-material:

1. **Static/dynamic symmetry** — a meta-observation about how the pillars relate, true of the pages as written, not missing content within the ethics page. If it lands anywhere later, it is on the reality landing page where the pillars are related to each other.
2. **Resonance reading (homogeneous/heterogeneous/polarised)** — additive and consistent; it sharpens the existing common-scale reading (the common game has a texture, a degree of internal resonance). But it is exactly the content-infilling this post is meant to *prepare*, not deliver. It lands on the page later, as settled content, likely with a fuller ethics/politics build — after the blog post has tested it in motion.
3. **Agency as setting-the-level** — suggests the ethics page is slightly incomplete (the subject currently appears only as a locus values are read *off*, with no mention that it holds its games at a level it sets). But this is the **same seam** moving in the metaphysics-of-disclosure post (arriving vs working, received vs set). It is one problem wearing two pillar-costumes and should get **one** answer applied to both, not two answers arrived at separately.

**The connective tissue not to lose:** the agency / active-subject question is the same seam in ethics and in disclosure. Whatever resolves it in the metaphysics-of-disclosure work should be the thing that later resolves it on the ethics page. All three findings point toward a future pages pass — plausibly a joint ethics-and-politics pass that also picks up the disclosure resolution — but that pass wants the disclosure seam closed first, and the decision on it belongs with the architects, not this post.
