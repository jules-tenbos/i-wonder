# Supporting info — the five metaphysics pillars and the aesthetics page

Captures the decisions from the working discussion behind two pieces of work: the Reality-section update to five pillars, and the new aesthetics pillar page. It marks what is **settled**, what is **parked**, and what was raised only as a **candidate** (discussed, not claimed). The candidate/claim line is load-bearing: a candidate placement must not be written into a page as if the project had committed to it.

## The move (settled)

Reality's metaphysics goes from four pillars to five: **epistemology, ontology, aesthetics, ethics, politics**. They remain "one dynamic interrogated differently" — five angles on one act, not five territories. The three core values, freedom-as-resultant, evolution, and discovery are unchanged.

## The five pillars (settled)

- **Epistemology — the process.** Coming to know: private experience and sharing, converged subjectivity, no absolute objectivity. The bare process gets its texture from two sides — aesthetics on the expression side, politics on the sharing side.
- **Ontology — the contents.** Beings settle on language concepts (the same thing); the converged catalogue whose private origin is forgotten — "it could have been different; we created it."
- **Aesthetics — the medium.** (Centre of gravity below.)
- **Ethics — personal.** Integrative, but from the seat of the subject: how I hold the value-tension so none swallows the others, lived as how I relate to the other. The integrative role of the old four-pillar ethics is kept — now as its personal form.
- **Politics — the sharing.** The shared discourse; the language that lives in the double reflection (my expression into my other; another's reading of it into theirs). How shared reality is made and who participates. This gives pillar status to something the framework already carried but had left unstatused.

The cuts: **personal/shared** separates ethics (personal) from politics (shared). **Expression/sharing** are the two sides filling in the learning process (aesthetics / politics).

## Status of the set (settled)

Five is **sufficient and clean, held openly** — not complete and closed. Anti-totality forbids a finished set. The test it passes is gradient-generalisation: process / contents / medium / personal-relating / sharing are core concerns of any subject (a cell, a community, an ML system), with the human philosophical names being the local labels.

## Aesthetics — centre of gravity (settled)

- Aesthetics is the **purity of how content and relations are expressed** — the medium everything passes through. Purity = clean fit; the "resonance between intent and interaction" already on core-values.
- Purity is a **common property of every language**, judged *within* a language by how cleanly it expresses its own content and relations. It does not rank languages and does not judge content — it is content-blind. (A beautiful wrong proof and a clumsy true one come apart on the purity axis.)
- **The leading place.** Tradition keeps aesthetics in the sensory; SPLectrum extends it into abstract thinking — because abstract thinking is made of language, relations, and concepts, aesthetics is present where it is normally assumed absent. The page's weight lands here, on the abstract reach.
- **The strong claim.** The elegant proof's purity is its rightness; the muddy concept's muddiness is the failure. "The right intent poorly expressed diminishes to non-existence" (core-values), generalised from expression to thought.
- **Where the three values meet.** Belonging gives shared language, privacy gives something to express, creativity is the act of expression; the quality of that expression is where all three meet.
- **Prior to ethics and politics.** Aesthetics is the medium they pass through.

**Guard.** Hold purity as *fit* — local and plural, the purity of this concept for what it carries, here — never as a single clean form everything should approach. The second reading ranks languages and cuts against the pluralism.

## Logic (resolved — candidates, not claims)

Logic is **not** a sixth pillar. Three senses were discussed as candidates; none is a committed claim, and none should be written into a page:

- **Logic as a language** — generically true (its own grammar, like mathematics or category theory). Not SPLectrum-specific.
- **Logic as relational pattern** — would belong to the seed-level relational primitive. A candidate, not assigned; and the seed-level relational work is itself still to be done (parked).
- **Logic as validity = aesthetic purity** — a candidate placement, not a SPLectrum claim.

Keep logic off both pages entirely.

## Parked (not for these pages)

- The relational primitive needs **infilling at the seed level** (structural side, not metaphysics). Category theory is the chosen language for it — *chosen, not crowned*: P4 forbids privileging even the language that best articulates relation. "Relational" is currently overloaded (the primitive, identity-as-position, RQM's observer-relative properties, anti-representational use) and wants making explicit.
- By unity-of-sides, that infilling will have a reality-side **representation**. It is not clear yet; "logic" has been the placeholder name. It clarifies *downstream* of the infilling, not before. Off these pages.

3. Later: a **light positioning grounding pass** (politics especially — Arendt, Habermas, Rancière) before any distinctive positioning claims go live.
