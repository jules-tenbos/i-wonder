# Emergence, Sharing and Language Games — Research & Approach

Working document. Provisional throughout — scope, the carried position, and the open questions all subject to revision. States what the research must cover and where the approach is headed. It does not do the research, and it does not prescribe how the work is sequenced or which surfaces carry which part — that is settled once the work is picked up.

## What this is

A direction-of-travel document for a body of work on *emergence*. Two things held together: a wide-sense survey of emergence as it stands in the world (material sciences, philosophy of mind, complexity science, the philosophical debate), and a SPLectrum-side formulation that positions emergence in language. As with the metaphysics work, the aim is to reach a **principled position first**, before entering specifics and details — the position earned from the survey, not asserted ahead of it.

The provisional finding, carried in from discussion and stated as current standing rather than proven result: emergence, located in language, is the crystallisation of new relational connection among relata already in play. It is not a new seed principle but a consequence of the existing apparatus. The research is what tests, grounds, and either firms or revises this.

## The carried position (current standing)

Reached in discussion, held provisionally. The research must test it, not assume it.

- **Emergence located in language.** Reality is experienced in concepts and relations; emergence is therefore a language phenomenon, not a material one that language merely reports. The physical cases (a gas acquiring temperature) become *instances* of a language-phenomenon rather than its home. Locating it in language is what generalises it beyond the material frame to concepts, theories, mathematics, meaning — anywhere language-games evolve.

- **Emergence as new relational connection among existing relata.** A new concept or relational move arises by connecting into the concepts and games already in play. The connections are not evidence that something emerged; they are what the emerging *is*. This dissolves the old puzzle of novelty-without-creation-from-nothing: the components "didn't have it in them" (true of the concept) yet it "came from what was there" (true of the relata) — because emergence is a new relation crystallising among existing relata, not a new substance appearing. Nothing emerges from nothing, because to emerge is to become related.

- **Degree of emergence as density of relation.** Not a kind-distinction but a gradient. A thinly-connected, half-formed concept has emerged weakly; a densely-connected one has emerged strongly. Tractability is what dense relation yields, not a separate criterion.

- **Hard/soft as a mismeasure.** The hard/soft (strong/weak) division does not mark two kinds of emergence. "Hardness" tracks how little a concept has sedimented into shared understanding — a fact about the concept's position between subject-reality and shared-reality, not about the world. Badly-defined concepts (consciousness the standing example) generate the appearance of "hard emergence" because they are vivid on the being-side and thin in the shared game; every relation added softens the claim. The research should establish whether the hard/soft division survives at all, or is best retired.

- **Emergence as derived, not primitive.** The position treats emergence as what the existing seed apparatus already produces — mixing generating new relational handles; sharing (P4/P5) yielding group-level language; the being-side/other-side cut relocating the consciousness case. So the seed-side treatment is a *consequence* page, not a candidate sixth principle. Whether emergence could instead earn primitive standing is left open (see open questions).

## Carried in from the creativity series (2026-07-21)

Discussion findings around the Unwalked Path post (08-16). Provisional like everything here — the survey tests them, not assumes them.

- **The formulation now has a creativity-side twin.** The Unwalked Path post (08-16) states the composition point: a relational path held in potential is composed of constituent paths already walked, in a different configuration — creation as recombination of already-experienced reality, with W. Brian Arthur's combinatorial account of technology (every new technology is a combination of existing technologies) as the outside corroboration. That is the carried position approached from the act side: creativity names the act, emergence names the result — a new configuration *is* new relational connection among relata already in play. The coupling creates a dependency: if the survey revises "new relational connection among existing relata," the post's paragraph inherits the revision.

- **Where "already in play" bottoms out — the constitution/expression floor.** The recombination reading invites a regress (what were the *first* walks composed of?), and three registers terminate it the same way. Arthur: combinations bottom out in captured natural phenomena, which are not themselves technologies. The software-languages post (*Let's Talk Software Languages*, 2026-04-11): languages bottom out in the hardwired instruction set — not itself spawned, but still a language ("a language spawning a language"). The seed: walks bottom out in the medium's relational power (P5). Same shape in all three: the recursion has a floor, the floor belongs to a different game, and the floor is still relational — relations held as *constitution* rather than as *expression*. This says where "relata already in play" ultimately cashes out without leaving language, and it touches both the relation-versus-relatum open question and the being-side/other-side cut.

- **Arthur as a survey candidate.** *The Nature of Technology* (combinatorial evolution — recombination plus capture of natural phenomena) is an emergence account in the technology register; it belongs alongside the complexity-science material.

## What the research must cover

Emergence in its widest sense, on its own terms, conflicts intact. The survey is genuine — not a queue of positions for the seed to knock down. The counter-source discipline applies throughout: a source giving only the tidy arc (the progress-ladder, the settled-consensus version) is incomplete; the debate's live conflicts are part of the subject.

- **The material / physical register.** Emergence as organization arising at scale — symmetry-breaking, "more is different," the many-body transition from insoluble-as-many to tractable-as-new (the statistical-mechanics case). The distinction the physical cases make vivid: non-integrability/unpredictability (must-simulate) versus the arising of a genuinely new descriptive language. These come apart — a fully deterministic system can be unpredictable without acquiring a new language (the many-body / three-body regime), which is diagnostic for what the seed's account is picking out.

- **The philosophy register — the named positions and their live afterlife.** British Emergentism and downward causation; the causal-exclusion critique and the subsequent exchange; weak emergence and the simulation criterion; strong emergence and the "further fact"/hard-problem line; the "more is different" anti-reductionism. Each on its own terms, with its internal conflicts and contested receptions. Research the *afterlife*, not just the founding statements — where each position stands now, who carries it forward, where it is contested.

- **Complexity science.** Emergence as it functions in the complex-adaptive-systems and self-organization traditions — where it is often taken as given rather than analysed, which is itself part of the picture. The relation to (and distinction from) self-organization, criticality, and assembly.

- **The consciousness case specifically.** The strong-emergence claim about phenomenal consciousness, held on its own terms and in full — because it is the standing test case for the "hard/soft" division and for whether "emerges without relation" is even coherent. The research must represent the claim fairly and at strength, not pre-deflate it.

- **The determinism entanglement.** How emergence and determinism get bundled in the literature, and why. The research should surface where the two questions are treated as one and where they are held apart, since the position's claim is that they are orthogonal (a within-game question of state-fixing versus an across-game question of a new game arising).

- **The theory-evolution cases.** Emergence read not only across scales but within the evolution of theories and concepts — classical mechanics arising with new relational tools; relativity from classical (kept as a limit, remixed under a new constraint); wave-particle duality as a concept that is almost pure relation between two prior games. These are pure language-game evolutions with no matter-aggregation, and they are load-bearing for the generalisation claim. Whether the material and theory-evolution cases are genuinely one phenomenon, or two that share a surface, is part of what the survey establishes.

- **Source authority.** Reference-grade sources for the survey (encyclopaedic entries, primary papers and the positions' own statements, the field's own histories); source-level uncertainties surfaced as content. Where the field is itself unsettled (what counts as emergence, whether strong emergence has any instance, the status of downward causation), the work holds the dispute rather than resolving it.

## How we want to go about it (the approach, not the mechanics)

- **Survey before position.** The wide-sense survey is done, and honestly, before the language-native formulation is settled. The position is a finding from the survey, not a lens laid over it.

- **Reformulate in language terms.** The distinctive move is to restate each flavour of emergence in the register of concepts, relations, and language-games — and to see how the flavours relate to each other once they are all spoken in that one register. The working expectation (to be tested, not assumed) is that reformulated this way the flavours stop reading as rival definitions and start reading as different facets or moments of one arc — and that the seed's own contribution sits where the others do not reach: the origin of the shared context in which a group-language forms.

- **Comparing, not competing.** Is-like, side by side. The work explores how the accounts relate and rhyme; it does not score them against each other or stage which-account-wins. No "the seed reaches past X." Where the seed arrives at an insight others already hold (the anti-reductionist "more is different" being the clear case), that is resonance and independent arrival, not surpassing.

- **Sharing as the seed's own station — P4 and P5.** The seed's contribution to the emergence question is an account of *sharing*: how the shared context that a group-language needs comes to be. This is where P4 and P5 do the work. P4 (equal standing of sources) is why the shared is downstream of deviation rather than transmitted from a privileged first speaker — the group-language is sediment-of-many, not dictation-from-one. P5 (coexistence as balance) is why the group-language conserves its subjects rather than dissolving them — the community is a balance struck across its members' deviations, not a higher unity that consumes them. The research and formulation should work out how far P4/P5 carry the account, and settle the two-faces question below.

- **Transforming, not dissolving.** The relation between games is transformation, which conserves both terms — the lower game is not demoted to shadow, the higher is not reduced to the lower. Neither reduction nor its eliminative mirror. This discipline guards against the position quietly reintroducing a privileged layer.

## Open questions to hold

The work must keep these open rather than close them prematurely.

- **The un-sharability residue.** Does being-side (lived-from-inside) content have a floor of un-sharability that keeps it perpetually *slightly* resistant to full sedimentation — such that consciousness softens asymptotically toward weak emergence without ever fully arriving? Or can it, like temperature, sediment completely? This is the residue left by the consciousness case and it bears directly on whether "hard emergence" has any defensible remainder once relocated.

- **Relation versus relatum.** The clear cases are reconnections of visible relata (classical + Maxwell → relativity). But some emergences appear to introduce a relatum that was not previously in play (spacetime as an example). Does every apparently-new relatum resolve into a nominalised new *relation* (states-from-relations again), or can emergence genuinely add a *relatum* and not only a *relation*? This is the test of whether the "never from nothing / always reconnection" account is fully general.

- **Two faces of equal standing.** In the physical case, sources equalise by *washing out* — individual determinacy erased into statistical uniformity, which is what lets the aggregate acquire its own language. In the human-community case, P4's equal standing holds *without* washing out — the persons remain, full, underneath. Same "equal standing," two different fates for the individual. One condition wearing two faces, or two distinct routes to a group-language?

- **The determinism-character across seams.** Emergence and determinism are orthogonal, but there is a real (non-bundled) relation worth mapping: each newly-arisen game carries its own determinism-character (Newtonian point-fixing, quantum distribution, statistical-mechanical ensemble-law), and it need not match the game below. How the fixing-grammar shifts as new games arise is a thread that rhymes with the RQM material already on-site.

- **Emergence: derived or primitive?** The carried position treats it as derived from the existing apparatus. Held open whether it instead earns standing of its own.

- **The colouring cases.** Downward causation (system-level pattern narrated as the whole *acting on* its parts — agent-grammar) and the literal reading of strong emergence (perspectival non-deducibility narrated as ontological further-fact) are both candidates for diagnosis as *colourings* — real observations given a wrong narrative of their pathway. Whether emergence work needs its own dissonance treatment, and which cases belong there, is left for the research to determine.

## Deliverables

The work is expected to produce material across several surfaces — subject page(s) holding emergence as territory on its own terms; person pages for the figures the SPLectrum-side reading leans on; a seed-section formulation of the language-native position; a close-affinity resonance treatment; and blog posts catching the live thinking. Which surface carries which part, in what order, and whether a dissonance (fence) treatment is warranted, is settled by the research and the direction of travel — not fixed here.
