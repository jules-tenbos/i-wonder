## The programme

- **Biogenic approach** (Lyon, "The biogenic approach to cognition," *Cognitive Processing* 7:11–29, 2006): two stances — anthropogenic (start from human cognition, work down) vs biogenic (start from biological facts, work up; "ask psychological questions as if they were biological questions").
- **"Basal cognition"** coined by Lyon c. 2018, partly to escape the loaded connotations of "minimal cognition" (her 2019 *Adaptive Behavior* piece).
- **Convening documents**: the double *Phil Trans R Soc B* theme issue (vol 376, 2021 — "Conceptual tools and the view from the single cell" + "Multicellularity, neurons and the cognitive lens"), guest-edited by **Lyon, Keijzer, Arendt, Levin** (NOT Cheng/Crist — they authored the later "shifting the center of gravity (again)," *Animal Cognition* 2023).
- **Field's working definition** (Lyon et al., "Reframing cognition," 2021): cognition = the suite of capacities by which organisms become familiar with, value, and exploit their environment while avoiding harm — operationalised as a toolkit: sensing, memory, valence, learning, anticipation, decision-making, communication.

## The cases, with replication status

- **E. coli chemotaxis — SAFE (textbook-solid since Berg/Koshland, 1970s).** Runs ~1s, tumbles ~0.1s; the cell compares attractant concentration now vs a few seconds ago via methylation-based adaptation (CheR/CheB) on its chemoreceptors — a genuine molecular record of the recent past. Care: "memory" is technical shorthand for delay chemistry, not psychological retention — frame it so.
- **Physarum polycephalum — SAFE.** Maze shortest-path (Nakagaki, Yamada & Tóth, *Nature* 407:470, 2000; Ig Nobel 2008). Tokyo rail (Tero et al., *Science* 327:439, 2010): oat flakes at 36 station positions, the plasmodium converges on a network comparable to the real rail system in efficiency/fault-tolerance/cost; Ig Nobel 2010; spawned a Physarum-inspired-algorithms literature. Both widely reproduced. **Anticipation** (Saigusa et al., *PRL* 100:018101, 2008): periodic cold-dry shocks; mould slows at the time the next (omitted) shock was due — real but probabilistic (~half of individuals in replications); frame as "many, not all."
- **Mimosa habituation — CONTESTED.** Gagliano et al., *Oecologia* 2014 (drops, response waning, novel-stimulus control, 28-day retention). Critique: Biegler, *Oecologia* 2018 (no proper dishabituation control; fatigue not excluded). **Keep separate from the pea-plant claim:** Markel 2020 (eLife) was a failed replication of Gagliano's *pea Pavlovian learning* (2016), not Mimosa; Gagliano's eLife reply argues Markel's CS was ineffective. Field status: unresolved (cf. *Nature Plants* "Go forth and replicate" editorial, 2020). Only tellable AS a replication-controversy story.
- **Ciliates — SAFE and story-rich.** *Stentor roeselii*: Dexter, Prabakaran & Gunawardena, *Current Biology* 2019 replicated Jennings 1906 — under repeated irritant a hierarchy of escape responses (bend → reverse cilia → contract → detach); discredited for decades by a 1967 non-replication that used the wrong species/stimulus; the 2019 team succeeded with plastic microbeads after carmine powder failed. **Gelber/Paramecium**: Gershman, Balbi, Gallistel & Gunawardena, eLife 2021, rehabilitate Beatrice Gelber's 1950s conditioning experiments — the classic dismissals were flawed, Gelber erased by behaviourist orthodoxy; status is historical re-evaluation plus a call to re-run, not yet a modern replication.

## Counter-voices

- **Adams & Aizawa** (*The Bounds of Cognition*, 2008): cognition requires intrinsic, non-derived content and a "mark of the cognitive"; without one, attribution inflates (the coupling-constitution template, applied to basal claims).
- **Deflationary reading**: the behaviours are real, the vocabulary is metaphor for adaptation chemistry; Physarum-rail is biomimicry-useful without being cognition ("What is cognitive about 'plant cognition'?", *Biology & Philosophy* 2023). Strongest fair objection: if every adaptive homeostatic loop counts, "cognition" ceases to pick anything out — the burden is on the field to show the vocabulary buys predictions the chemistry alone doesn't.
- Mainstream cognitive science: largely treats it as terminological extension; "eternal vigilance is the price of metaphor."

## AI/engineering reception

Xenobots came from an AI collaboration (Bongard's UVM lab evolved the body plans); TAME is explicitly aimed at engineering novel cognitive systems; RL-to-agency bridges exist (BioSystems 2023; Gershman on cell learning). A real but niche "diverse intelligence" community (Templeton-funded), not mainstream ML.

## Levin — person-page material

Born Moscow 1969, emigrated 1978 (Soviet-Jewish visa program) to Lynn, Massachusetts; father a programmer, mother a concert pianist. BS Tufts (computer science and biology); PhD Harvard under Clifford Tabin — the molecular basis of left-right asymmetry (Cell 1995; a Nature "Milestone in Developmental Biology"). Now Vannevar Bush Distinguished Professor at Tufts, director of the Allen Discovery Center, associate faculty at Harvard's Wyss Institute; 400+ publications.

**Programme:** the bioelectric code (resting membrane-voltage patterns across non-neural tissue as instructive signals for growth and form — a computational layer above the genome; *Dev Biol* 2017); pattern memory — the two-headed planaria (brief bioelectric perturbation → worms that regenerate two-headed, and re-regenerate two-headed when re-cut, genome unchanged; Durant et al. 2017, earlier Oviedo 2010); the Xenopus "electric face" (Vandenberg et al. 2011 — a voltage prepattern matching future facial structure before it forms); xenobots (Kriegman/Blackiston/Levin/Bongard, PNAS 2020; kinematic self-replication PNAS 2021); anthrobots (Gumuskaya et al., *Advanced Science* 2023 — motile self-constructing bots from adult human tracheal cells, no genetic edits, promoting neural-wound healing in vitro).

**Frame:** "Cognition all the way down" (Aeon, with Dennett, 2020 — cells have cell-sized goals; larger minds are collective intelligences with bigger goal-spaces); TAME (*Frontiers Syst Neurosci* 2022) — a continuous, engineering-operational scale of agency; the "cognitive light cone" (the size of the largest goal a system can pursue); morphogenesis as collective navigation of morphospace.

**Reception split (the page's honest seam):** the bioelectricity science is mainstream and well-funded (Cell/PNAS/Development, Allen Center); pushback targets the cognitive framing, not the experiments (Pigliucci "Nope, it isn't cognition all the way down," 2020; critiques of the later platonic-space speculation). Large public reach (TED, podcasts).

**Where he stops (his own move):** he operationalises — "is it *really* cognition?" is treated as an engineering question (which point on the agency scale gives the best prediction and control), not a metaphysical yes/no; explicitly disclaims panpsychism-as-doctrine and claims about phenomenal consciousness in cells.

## Lyon — person-page material

Australian; former investigative and science journalist; came to academia late — the biogenic approach developed as a mature-age PhD (ANU). Adjunct/visiting at University of Adelaide. Key works: the biogenic approach (2006); "The cognitive cell" (*Front Microbiol* 2015 — bacterial signal transduction as genuine cognitive capacity); lead framer of the field — lead guest editor of the 2021 theme issues and lead author of "Reframing cognition" (the agenda paper); "Valuing what happens" (valence, 2021). Standing: the field's conceptual architect — later literature traces the lineage through her papers; smaller public profile than Levin. Earlier engagement with Maturana's autopoiesis tradition (an on-site connection).

## Gap-closing round (2026-08-25) — quorum sensing + pre-neural bioelectricity

Two targeted additions for the close-affinity work's meaning-in-the-non-neural angle.

### Quorum sensing — the bacterial interaction language

- **The mechanism (textbook-solid).** Bacteria synthesise, release, and detect small signal molecules (autoinducers); above a threshold concentration — i.e., above a population density — reception flips gene expression population-wide, synchronously. Founding line: Nealson & Hastings's *Vibrio fischeri* luminescence work (1970); the term "quorum sensing" coined by Fuqua, Winans & Greenberg (1994); the standard reviews are Miller & Bassler, "Quorum sensing in bacteria" (*Annu Rev Microbiol* 2001) and Waters & Bassler (*Annu Rev Cell Dev Biol* 2005).
- **Dialects and a common language — the field's own vocabulary.** Species-specific autoinducers (the AHL family in Gram-negatives, oligopeptides in Gram-positives) function as intraspecies signals; **AI-2** (discovered in *Vibrio harveyi*, Bassler et al. 1994) is produced and detected across a large cohort of Gram-negative AND Gram-positive species and is standardly described in the literature as a "universal" interspecies signal — "a common language for bacterial interaction" is the field's phrase, not ours. Receptor diversity (LuxP, LsrB, and a third dCACHE-domain family) means different species read the same molecule through different receptors — the meaning of the signal is fixed on the reception side.
- **What it regulates:** bioluminescence, virulence, competence, motility, antibiotic production, and **biofilm formation** — the last being collectively created structure: population-level meaning-work whose sediment is a shared physical form.
- **Bassler's combinatorial point:** *V. harveyi* runs multiple autoinducer circuits in parallel and integrates them — the cell distinguishes "how many of us" from "how many of them," a genuinely relational readout of social context.
- **For the CA angle:** quorum sensing is the being↔being side at the bacterial scale — signal production, threshold reception, population-wide coordinated response; dialects, a lingua franca, and meaning fixed by the receiver. It slots directly into the "language of interaction" column.

### Pre-neural bioelectricity — the sourcing

- **The claim to source:** neurons are specialised users of an older signalling medium. **Primary backing: Keijzer, van Duijn & Lyon, "What nervous systems do: early evolution, input–output, and the skin brain thesis," *Adaptive Behavior* 21(2), 2013** — excitable epithelia using chemical/electrical signalling as the nervous-system precursor; note **Lyon is a co-author**, so the pre-neural line runs through the field's own conceptual architect, an on-site tie.
- **Levin's statement of it:** "The computational boundary of a 'self'" (*Front Psychol* 2019) — all cells are electrically active; modern neurons evolved from pre-neural precursors already using ion-based signalling for coordination; developmental bioelectricity as the ancestral medium, speed-optimised by nervous systems.
- **Honest breadth:** that neural signalling descends from pre-existing cellular bioelectricity is mainstream; *why* nervous systems arose is disputed — the skin brain thesis (coordination of whole-body movement, not input–output processing) is one proposal in an acknowledged option space (Jékely, Keijzer & Godfrey-Smith, "An option space for early neural evolution," *Phil Trans R Soc B* 2015). The CA piece needs only the descent claim, which is safe; it should not lean on the skin brain thesis specifically.

## Storyline check (for the "cognition without a brain" post)

- **Best safe stories:** Tokyo rail (oat flakes as stations, the mould re-drawing the commuter map overnight, engineers vs a yellow blob, the Ig Nobel); **Stentor** (the best narrative arc in the field: Jennings 1906 → a century of dismissal on a botched non-replication → Harvard 2019, carmine powder fails, plastic beads work — "a single cell has a repertoire, and works through it"); E. coli (the now-vs-three-seconds-ago hook, told as delay-chemistry, honestly).
- **Usable with stated caveat:** Physarum anticipation (many, not all).
- **Great human story, uncertain evidence:** Gelber — a woman scientist erased by behaviourist orthodoxy, rehabilitated 2021; tellable as a story about science, not as an established result.
- **Only as controversy:** Mimosa drops and pea Pavlovian learning.
- **Levin's planaria/xenobots:** safe, spectacular, and carry the "navigating anatomical space" facet of the descent storyline.
