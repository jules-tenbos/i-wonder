# Build plan — meaning territory

Six subject pages and the persons that form their spine. Created 2026-06-21.

These are **outside-context territory**: positioning persons-and-subjects, held on their own terms, full breadth, no SPLectrum vocabulary anywhere on the page (per `tov-positioning-section.md`). None of these pages reads aesthetics the SPLectrum way — that reading lives in the close affinity ring piece, which will cross-link *to* these once they exist.

Why built together: generalising aesthetics to the dynamics of language and meaning surfaces a territory that the philosophical tradition parcelled into separate enclaves. These six are pieces of one dispersed landscape of meaning. Each is a real tradition the site does not yet hold, justified by its own standing, not by what any ring piece needs. The subjects are peers; **sequencing is the editor's call — the list implies no order.**

All six confirmed absent from the current sitemap (checked 2026-06-21).

## Build conventions (settled)

- **Spine-persons first, then the subject.** Build the new persons a subject leans on before the subject page, so its internal links are live rather than external placeholders (no-broken-links rule).
- **Flat files, not directories.** Each subject is a flat `s/semiotics.md` matching phenomenology / structuralism, unless it grows child pages. Trailing-slash URLs work either way via Jekyll.
- **Register on completion.** Every new person/subject page must be added to the A–Z landing index (`/positioning/persons/` or `/positioning/subjects/`) or it does not exist for readers.
- **Drafts for review, no commit until told.** Pages reviewed before committing (plan doc itself excepted — committed on creation per instruction).
- Proposed opener: **Hermeneutics** (richest existing spine, adjacent to the already-built phenomenology subject). Not binding.

## Subjects to add

### [ ] Hermeneutics — `/positioning/subjects/h/hermeneutics/`

The theory of interpretation and understanding. Full-breadth account on its own terms: the movement from biblical and philological interpretation into a general philosophy of understanding; the hermeneutic circle; method versus truth; the reception and the disputes.

Spine: Schleiermacher (general hermeneutics, text-specific → universal), Dilthey (the human sciences, *Verstehen* vs *Erklären*), Heidegger (the ontological turn — understanding as a mode of being), Gadamer (philosophical hermeneutics, fusion of horizons, rehabilitation of prejudice and tradition), Ricoeur (the hermeneutics of suspicion, text and narrative, the detour through explanation). Conflicts intact: the Gadamer–Habermas debate (tradition vs critique of ideology), the Gadamer–Derrida encounter, the methodological-vs-ontological split, Betti's and Hirsch's objections on authorial meaning and validity.

Link internally: Heidegger, Gadamer, Ricoeur. New spine-persons: **Schleiermacher, Dilthey** (required).

### [ ] Deconstruction — `/positioning/subjects/d/deconstruction/`

Derrida's movement and its afterlife, on its own terms. The critique of the metaphysics of presence; *différance*, trace, supplement, iterability; the speech/writing hierarchy. Full breadth includes the fights: the Searle–Derrida exchange over speech acts, the analytic reception (the 1992 Cambridge honorary-degree controversy), the Yale-school literary appropriation (de Man, Hartman, Miller) and the de Man wartime-writings affair, the charge of relativism and the responses, the later ethical/political turn (justice, hospitality, the messianic).

Link internally: Derrida. No new spine-persons required for a first build; de Man is the natural next person if the literary-reception strand wants depth.

### [ ] Semiotics — `/positioning/subjects/s/semiotics/`

The theory of signs as a tradition in its own right — a peer of phenomenology and structuralism (both already held). The two foundational lineages and their divergence: Peirce's triadic sign (sign–object–interpretant; icon/index/symbol; unlimited semiosis) and Saussure's dyadic sign (signifier/signified; *langue*/*parole*; value through difference) feeding into semiology. Downstream: Hjelmslev's glossematics, Jakobson, Barthes (myth, connotation), Eco (the open work, theory of codes, limits of interpretation), Lotman's semiosphere, Greimas. The biosemiotic extension (Sebeok, Hoffmeyer, Uexküll's *Umwelt*) as part of the subject's breadth and current life.

Link internally: Peirce, Saussure. (Eco, Barthes, Jakobson are candidate future persons; not required first build.)

### [ ] Symbolic forms — `/positioning/subjects/s/symbolic-forms/`

Cassirer's philosophy of symbolic forms and the tradition that carries it. Mind constitutes its worlds through symbolic activity, with language, myth, art, religion, and science as distinct, irreducible symbolic forms — each its own mode of world-formation. The Marburg neo-Kantian background; the Davos debate with Heidegger (1929) as a contested reception that belongs to the subject. The afterlife: Susanne Langer's extension into aesthetics and a theory of feeling (*Philosophy in a New Key*, *Feeling and Form*); Nelson Goodman's adjacent but distinct "languages of art" and worldmaking as comparison and dispute.

Link internally: Cassirer. New person (judgment call): **Langer**.

### [ ] Dialogism — `/positioning/subjects/d/dialogism/`

Bakhtin and the Bakhtin Circle, on their own terms. Heteroglossia, the dialogic word, addressivity and answerability, the utterance as the real unit of language, polyphony (the Dostoevsky study), the chronotope, carnival and the carnivalesque (the Rabelais study). The authorship question — the disputed attribution of Voloshinov's and Medvedev's texts to Bakhtin — is part of the subject and should be surfaced as content, not resolved. The vast literary-theory and cultural-studies afterlife as part of its breadth.

Link internally: Bakhtin. New person (judgment call): **Voloshinov**.

### [ ] Rasa — `/positioning/subjects/r/rasa/`

The Indian theory of aesthetic experience, on its own terms — the tradition the *standing of aesthetics* subject already gestures at but does not home. From Bharata's *Natya Shastra* (the *rasa-sutra*, the eight/nine rasas, the *bhavas*) through the classical commentarial development to Abhinavagupta's radicalisation (rasa as an elevated, generalised state of consciousness; *sadharanikarana*, the universalisation that makes private feeling shareable; the *Abhinavabharati* and *Dhvanyaloka-locana*). The relation to *dhvani* (suggestion) theory via Anandavardhana. Internal debates among the classical theorists (Bhatta Lollata, Shankuka, Bhatta Nayaka, Abhinavagupta) as the subject's own contested development. Held as a theory of meaning and aesthetic experience, not as area-studies exotica.

No existing person pages. New persons: **Abhinavagupta** (required); **Bharata** (judgment call).

## Persons to add

### Required spine-persons

- [ ] **Schleiermacher** — `/positioning/persons/s/schleiermacher/` (hermeneutics). Founder of general hermeneutics; regional interpretive rules → a universal art of understanding; grammatical and psychological interpretation; the divinatory method. Full person scope includes the theologian (*On Religion*, the feeling of absolute dependence), not only the hermeneutic contribution.
- [ ] **Dilthey** — `/positioning/persons/d/dilthey/` (hermeneutics). The human sciences (*Geisteswissenschaften*) and their distinct method; *Verstehen* vs *Erklären*; lived experience (*Erlebnis*), expression, understanding; the critique of historical reason. The bridge between Schleiermacher and Heidegger/Gadamer.
- [ ] **Abhinavagupta** — `/positioning/persons/a/abhinavagupta/` (rasa). Kashmiri Shaiva philosopher, aesthetician, and theologian (c. 950–1016). The rasa theory's most influential development; *sadharanikarana*; the synthesis of aesthetics with non-dual Shaiva metaphysics. Full person scope includes the Tantric-philosophical work (*Tantraloka*), not only the aesthetics.

### Judgment calls (the relevant subject build decides — recorded leanings)

- [ ] **Bharata** — `/positioning/persons/b/bharata/` (rasa). Source-author of the *Natya Shastra*. **Leaning: fold into the Rasa subject page as the originating text, no separate person page** — the figure is semi-legendary and the work composite, so a person page would strain the person-and-corpus scope. Rasa build decides.
- [ ] **Voloshinov** — `/positioning/persons/v/voloshinov/` (dialogism). *Marxism and the Philosophy of Language*. **Leaning: add the page** — carries the authorship dispute and the marxist-linguistics strand at person depth. Dialogism build decides.
- [ ] **Langer** — `/positioning/persons/l/langer/` (symbolic forms). Carries symbolic forms into aesthetics (art as the symbolic presentation of feeling). **Leaning: add the page** — the natural bridge the aesthetics ring piece will cross-link to. Symbolic-forms build decides.

Schlegel and Novalis (early Romantics) sit as neighbours to the existing *german idealism* subject and the *counter-current* page; de Man, Eco, Barthes, Jakobson are candidate future persons for deconstruction and semiotics. None blocks any build above.
