# Language-Mechanics Close Affinities — Field Research

Field-mapping output. One pass per joint of the seed-side mechanics, each surveyed wide (well beyond the named candidates) to establish what company the position keeps and where it stands alone. **No organising spine, no placement/coordination decisions here** — this is the field, on the field's own terms, so those decisions can be made later on evidence.

The headline: **all seven joints are heavily populated** — the position keeps serious, named, cross-disciplinary, cross-cultural company at every joint. Combinatorial mixing looked sparse on a first pass, but only under a too-narrow test ("multiplicative combination of the inputs themselves"); read as a *family* (mixing, blending, layering, projection, fusion, recombination), it is as densely companioned as the rest. So the *mixing gap* flagged in the scoping doc **dissolves** — and the most important single finding relocates with it: the framework's original edge is not the mixing operation (which is borrowed and well-attested) but the *conjunction of three moves the field makes separately and never together* — mixing of whole **language-sets**, as a **repeatable primitive**, that is **constitutive and ungrounded**. See Joint 3 and the Shape-of-the-field section.

## Joint 1 — Relational primitive (meaning as position, not substance)

**Verdict: maximally populated. The most-companioned joint of the seven.** This is not a fringe claim anywhere; it is a convergent insight across every relevant discipline and several traditions, and at industrial scale in current ML.

- **Linguistics / distributional:** Firth ("know a word by the company it keeps"), Zellig Harris (distributional structure — "the distribution of an element is all we need to identify it"), modern embeddings (Mikolov/Word2Vec → BERT/GPT — meaning *is* vector position; relational geometry at industrial scale), Goldberg (construction grammar — meaning in the form-role frame).
- **Philosophy of language:** Brandom (inferential-role semantics — content = inferential position), Quine & Davidson (meaning holism — no statement means in isolation), conceptual/functional-role semantics (Block, Harman), Frege (sense as mode of presentation), Kaplan (content as a function of context).
- **The structuralist ancestor:** Saussure ("a system of differences with no positive terms") — *on-site*. This line turns his static system into something the mechanics can use.
- **Formal:** category theory (objects known only by their morphisms), mathematical structuralism (numbers as positions in a structure), relevance logic.
- **Physics / metaphysics:** Rovelli (relational QM — properties only relative to interactions), Ladyman & French (ontic structural realism — "the world just is structure"), Leibniz (space/time as relational order), Spinoza, Whitehead, Hegel (determinate negation), Deleuze (difference is primary; identity is its effect).
- **Human sciences:** Lévi-Strauss (structural anthropology), Emirbayer ("Manifesto for a Relational Sociology"), Latour (actor-network theory), Bateson ("a difference that makes a difference").
- **Non-Western (strong, explicit):** Buddhist pratītyasamutpāda / Nāgārjuna's śūnyatā (no svabhāva — no intrinsic nature, only relational arising), Daoist relational self, Confucian role-constituted personhood, Ubuntu.

**Cross-bleed:** directly with the AI information-field submission (embedding-space-as-language / the cauldron).

**Where it might be missed:** Dewey/James radical empiricism (relations are experienced, not imposed), Kripke's causal-historical reference, Karen Barad's agential realism (intra-action), homotopy type theory, Hebbian "fire together / wire together," Friston's Markov-blanket identity.

## Joint 2 — Distinction-as-primitive + encapsulation into held units

**Verdict: well populated, and it splits cleanly into two camps** — a divergence cluster and a universality cluster. This split is itself a finding (it pre-figures the divergence/convergence tension at Joint 5).

**Distinction as the founding move:**
- Spencer-Brown (*Laws of Form* — the mark is the primitive; everything derives from it), Bateson (the difference that makes a difference), Luhmann (binary codes as system-forming distinctions), Shannon (the bit *is* a distinction), von Foerster (second-order cybernetics — the observer's mark begins observation), Jakobson (distinctive features, marked/unmarked), Saussure (meaning from difference).

**Encapsulation into a held, genuinely-individual repertoire — the closest companions to "language sets as what the subject is":**
- **George Kelly (personal construct psychology)** — *the anchor.* Constructs as bipolar distinctions; the construct system is the subject's held repertoire and is genuinely individual ("persons differ in their construction of events"). Makes both claims explicit and primary. **Carries the divergence face.**
- von Glasersfeld (radical constructivism — the subject builds its own viable model), Barsalou (ad hoc, goal-derived, situated concepts — radical individuation), Bartlett (schemas; reconstruction filtered through individual frames), Schutz (typifications / personal "stock of knowledge" — the bridge to the shared layer), Bourdieu (habitus as an individual-but-socially-patterned repertoire of distinctions/taste; *Distinction*).
- Maturana/Varela (operational closure as a held organisation with a unique coupling history) — *on-site.*

**Universality camp (the contrast):** Kant (universal a priori categories), Saussure (langue, not parole), Rosch (prototypes — universal capacity, varying content), Piaget (universal stages).

**Non-Western parallel:** Yogācāra *vikalpa* (conceptual discrimination as the founding move) + *ālayavijñāna* (the store-consciousness as the individual's accumulated repertoire of imprints) — strikingly parallel to Kelly from an entirely different tradition.

**Where it might be missed:** Polanyi (tacit knowing — are constructs fully articulable, or tacit-grounded?), the marked/unmarked semiotics beyond Jakobson (Eco, Lotman), fuzzy/many-valued/paraconsistent logics (does distinction-as-primitive require *binary* structure?), Garfinkel's breaching experiments, standpoint epistemology.

## Joint 3 — Combinatorial mixing (new states from combining held units)

**Verdict: the operation is densely companioned; the originality is NOT the operation but its scope and status.** A first pass read this joint as nearly empty — but only by applying a narrow test ("multiplicative combination of the inputs themselves") that rejected blending as "emergence-in-a-container." That test was wrong. *Mixing* is not one operation but a **family** — mixing, blending, layering, projection/mapping, fusion, recombination, superposition, montage — unified by a single signature: **start with more than one held unit, get something out that exceeds the parts.** Read on that boundary, the family is heavily populated across eight registers. The sparseness was an artifact of the test, not the field.

**The operation, by register — all dense:**
- **Cognitive linguistics (blend + projection):** Fauconnier & Turner (conceptual blending — input spaces → blend with emergent structure; composition/completion/elaboration), Lakoff & Johnson (conceptual metaphor as cross-domain projection), Coulson (semantic leaps), Per Aage Brandt (tightening which blends are genuinely generative). **Blend is in; projection is in** — projection is the more primitive move, blend is projection-plus-emergence; they are not rivals.
- **Layering (the strand the first pass missed entirely):** Hjelmslev → Barthes (connotation as a second-order system on a first — myth as layered semiosis), Goffman (frame analysis — keying / laminations stacked on an activity), Bakhtin (double-voiced discourse — the word layered with another's accent), the palimpsest as a meaning-model.
- **Combination as the engine of the new:** Koestler (bisociation — the collision of two habitually-incompatible matrices; the sharpest single claim), **Boden (combinational creativity — the field's explicit name for this exact family)**, Poincaré/Hadamard (combinatory play in mathematical invention), Schumpeter (innovation as "new combinations" — *on-site*) / Brian Arthur (technologies as combinations of prior technologies; recombination changes what is combinable) / Weitzman (recombinant growth).
- **Philosophy of novelty-from-combination:** Whitehead (concrescence — "the many become one and are increased by one"; *on-site*), C.D. Broad / British emergentism + Mill's "heteropathic" laws (chemical combination as the model: product unlike the inputs), Deleuze (multiplicities, the assemblage; *on-site*), Simondon (transduction, individuation from the metastable).
- **Formal:** category-theoretic composition / colimits / **pushouts** (literally: glue two objects over a shared base → a genuinely new object; Grothendieck *on-site*), tensor products / quantum superposition (a state not reducible to either input), **vector arithmetic in embedding space** (king − man + woman → a novel position — mixing *in the space itself*; couples to the AI submission).
- **Systems / complexity / biology — the most literal instances:** sexual recombination (two genomes → genotypes novel in both parents), **Margulis's symbiogenesis** (two organisms merge → a genuinely new one; *on-site*), Kauffman (autocatalytic sets, the adjacent possible; *on-site*), Anderson ("more is different").
- **Aesthetics / art practice:** Eisenstein's montage (two shots collide → a third meaning in neither), collage/assemblage, sampling/mashup, jazz recombination, the surrealist "chance meeting of a sewing machine and an umbrella."
- **Cross-cultural — mixing as a first principle:** Empedocles' *krasis* (mixture of the four roots producing all things; *on-site*), the Chinese WuXing generative cycle (phases generating phases — a *repeatable, continuous* generative operation), the I Ching (trigrams combining into hexagrams), yin-yang producing the ten-thousand things, the alchemical *coniunctio* (union of opposites → a third substance), Llull's and Leibniz's *ars combinatoria*.

**So "the mixing gap" as first stated is dissolved.** Blend, projection, and layering are all in-company, and the operation is borrowed and well-attested. **The originality relocates** — from the operation to the *conjunction of three moves the field makes separately but never together:*

1. **The unit is whole held language-sets / distinction-repertoires** — not two *concepts* (blending works on local mental spaces), not two *matrices* (Koestler), not two *technologies* (Arthur). Nobody combines *entire subject-held worlds* as the unit. The nearest gestures — Bakhtin's double-voicing, Bourdieu's habitus, Kelly's construct systems in coupling — name the held repertoire but do not claim multiplicative generation of novel *configurations* from combining two of them.
2. **It is a repeatable primitive of an ongoing system** — the cauldron that *keeps* mixing — not a one-off creative/aesthetic event. Kauffman's autocatalysis + adjacent-possible is the nearest, but pitched at chemical/technological scale; WuXing has the repeatable-generative shape but not the held-language-set unit.
3. **It is constitutive of meaning/reality-formation, and ungrounded** — the ordinary operation by which configurations arise, not a special act, and with *no external engine*. Whitehead has the constitutive register but pays for it with God (the initial aim); Abhinavagupta's rasa has it but with a soteriological endpoint. Strip the external grounding and the human-scale, held-system version has no clear holder.

**Reading:** the framework's exposed edge is sharper and more defensible than "nobody mixes." The operation is densely companioned; **the residual originality is the *scope* (whole language-sets) plus the *status* (repeatable, constitutive, unground­ed).** That is what the broad family, granted in full, still does not collectively assert.

## Joint 4 — Historicity & path-landscape (the past shaping possible futures)

**Verdict: well populated, with one near-peer that needs watching.**

- **Thelen & Smith (dynamic systems theory of development)** — the primary fit. Development as a landscape of attractors shaped by the system's *own activity history*, no pre-laid program. The path-landscape in developmental vocabulary. **Watch:** this is a near-peer to the process sketch itself, so it may read as close-affinity shading toward convergence rather than resonance-across-a-gap.
- Waddington's epigenetic landscape (the original "landscape of canalised paths" image — Thelen's ancestor), Esther Thelen's motor-development work specifically.
- Assembly theory (Cronin & Walker — history written into objects; what can exist depends on what came before) and broader path-dependence (David, Arthur — "history matters," lock-in, increasing returns).
- Evolutionary/developmental: Gould & Lewontin (contingency, the spandrel), niche construction (organisms reshaping the landscape they then inherit), Bergson's *durée* (the past pressing into the present).

**Cross-bleed:** the reality-time submission (assembly theory, historicity) shares this joint directly.

**Where it might be missed:** Markov-vs-history debates, hysteresis in physics, Ricoeur's narrative sedimentation, machine-learning path-dependence (training-trajectory effects on final representations).

## Joint 5 — Convergence (how the shared gets into the private)

**Verdict: heavily populated — the densest joint after the relational primitive.** This is *not* fringe; it is a mainstream, multiply-instantiated, cross-cultural position. It is the **convergence face** that Kelly's divergence face (Joint 2) needs to meet.

- **Vygotsky** — internalisation; the inter-psychological becoming intra-psychological; private speech as social speech taken inward. The canonical statement.
- **Tomasello** — usage-based acquisition; language built from extracted interaction-patterns; shared intentionality; no innate machine underneath. The construction-from-use account of how a cauldron gets filled.
- Mead (the self arising from the social act; the "generalised other"), Berger & Luckmann's internalisation moment (→ Joint 7), Bruner (scaffolding, cultural participation), Bakhtin (the word is half someone else's).
- Peirce (the real as the community of inquiry's final settled opinion — convergence-in-the-limit), Lewis (convention as a convergent equilibrium), Bybee (frequency/usage entrenching structure).
- Distributed cognition: Hutchins (the cockpit/ship holds knowledge no individual holds).
- Searle / Tuomela / Gilbert (collective intentionality, we-mode, joint commitment).
- **Non-Western:** Ubuntu ("a person is a person through other persons"), Confucian *li* (cultivation into the shared order), Buddhist co-arising.

**The whole field leans convergence-heavy.** Where Joint 2's encapsulation literature splits divergence/universality, the convergence literature is one-directional and crowded. The framework needs *both* poles — and the field supplies the convergence pole far more readily than the divergence pole.

## Joint 6 — Coupling (engagement between subjects without direct transmission)

**Verdict: well populated, and built on an explicit, named rejection of the transmission model.** The position has a clear enemy in the literature (the conduit/transmission metaphor) and a clear set of champions.

- **Maturana & Varela** — *on-site.* Structural coupling; languaging as recurrent coordination; consensual domains; the explicit claim that perturbation *triggers but does not specify* the response (structural determinism). The foundational rejection of information-transmission.
- **Di Paolo / De Jaegher / Cuffari** — *Linguistic Bodies*; participatory sense-making; language as the coupling of autonomous bodies. The direct enactivist statement.
- **Reddy** ("The Conduit Metaphor", 1979) — the foundational *naming* of why "transmission" is wrong: it is a linguistic mistake, not a fact. **Shannon's transmission model is the clean contrast case** (the thing being rejected).
- Cybernetics: Bateson (relationship is information; meta-communication), von Foerster (the observer in the system), Pask (conversation theory — coupling two cognitive systems).
- Coordination: Kelso (HKB — coupled oscillators entraining; coordination dynamics), Sebanz & Knoblich (joint action without shared code); Pickering & Garrod (interactive alignment — *assess: closer to convergence than coupling*).
- Phenomenology: Merleau-Ponty (intercorporeity — the handshake), Husserl (*Paarung*), Schutz (the we-relation, "growing older together"), Fuchs/Stern (interaffectivity, attunement, vitality forms).
- Dialogue/meeting: Gadamer (fusion of horizons — a meeting, not a transfer), Buber (I-Thou; "the Between"), Bakhtin (addressivity).
- Empirical: musical improvisation & dance synchrony (entrainment, jamming as coupling); Gallagher's interaction theory (vs theory-theory/simulation); conversation analysis (intersubjectivity achieved turn-by-turn, repair).
- **Non-Western:** Daoist responsive attunement (*ziran*; "the Dao that can be spoken is not the eternal Dao" — it cannot be transmitted), Buddhist co-arising.

**Couples directly** to Varela/Maturana (on-site) and to the coupled-cauldrons account of engagement.

## Joint 7 — Subject-reality vs. shared-reality stratification (the common layer as a stabilised settlement)

**Verdict: heavily populated; a live, defended, well-populated position in contemporary social ontology.** And it carries the most internal machinery of any joint — the externalisation → objectivation → internalisation dialectic.

- **Berger & Luckmann (*The Social Construction of Reality*, 1966)** — *the direct architect.* The three-moment dialectic is theirs: externalisation (activity poured out) → objectivation (it congeals into a facticity that confronts us) → internalisation (the next generation takes it back in via socialisation). Institutionalisation, the paramount reality of everyday life, symbolic universes. **Richest single subject candidate** — its own internal machinery parallels the mechanics.
- **The proto-source:** Hegel (objective spirit — *Geist* externalised in institutions, law, custom; recognition).
- Sociology of knowledge: Durkheim (social facts "exterior and constraining," collective representations — the proto-stratification), Mannheim, Scheler, Marx (the materialist underlay — social being determines consciousness).
- Phenomenology: Husserl (the lifeworld; transcendental intersubjectivity as the ground of objectivity), Schutz (the world taken for granted; finite provinces of meaning / *multiple realities* — supports distinct layers), Merleau-Ponty (the shared world via sedimented embodied practice).
- **Social ontology — the precise companions:** Searle (*The Construction of Social Reality* — "X counts as Y in context C"; institutional vs brute facts; collective acceptance; *objectivity as constituted convergence, explicitly*), Gilbert (plural subjects, joint commitment), Tuomela (we-mode).
- **Objectivity-as-stabilised-convergence specifically:** Peirce (final settled opinion), Putnam (internal realism), Habermas (communicative validity — *mark its convergence-horizon idealism as a stopping point*).
- Wittgenstein (forms of life; agreement-in-judgment as bedrock; *On Certainty* — the inherited world-picture one is socialised into).
- Critical realism: Bhaskar & Archer (stratified social ontology; the morphogenetic cycle — structure inherited, guides agency, reproduced/transformed; "real but dependent").
- **Paradigm instances:** money (pure collective acceptance constituting objectivity), Hart's rule of recognition (law exists by officials' continued practice), Ostrom (commons rule-systems), Halbwachs (collective memory — the shared *past* as inherited construction), Hacking (looping kinds — "making up people"), Castoriadis (the social imaginary; instituting vs instituted), Mary Douglas (*How Institutions Think*), institutional economics (Veblen/Commons/North — institutions as settled habits of thought).
- **Non-Western:** Ubuntu (the shared relational order as the *condition* of personhood, not a layer on top), Confucian *li*.
- **Moffett (added 2026-07-15; see `plan/moffett-research-dossier.md`)** — the *mechanism-and-boundary* account this joint otherwise lacks. Where the joint is dense on the internalisation dialectic (how the shared gets *in*), Moffett is on how the shared *coheres and bounds*: anonymous societies held by shared **markers** (identity badges recognised by all, no individual recognition needed) that gate an in/out boundary and enable unbounded scale — with the boundary itself passing **invariant** through the individual-recognition → marker shift (the medium of recognition changes, the cut it draws does not). Community-scale, empirical, peer-reviewed (BBS 2025). Cast overlap with the map: Tomasello, Henrich, Durkheim, Dunbar are his neighbours too — he is the community-scale wing of the same convergence cluster, not a separate project. Carries the **diversity-of-belonging** thread (the ant's near-absolute chemical gate → strong-but-crossable human markers → soft resonance-strength belonging: one range, many constitutions, none the privileged "real" belonging — reads straight into the interrelational). **Held separately for individual treatment on his *method*** (is-like reasoning, definitions-as-marks-not-essences, vocabulary-analysis — the seed's how-over-what run from the science side); that's a distinct surface, tracked in scheduled-tasks, not this joint.

**Cross-bleed:** Schutz's typifications / stock of knowledge tie this joint back to Joint 2's encapsulation; the convergence mechanism (Joint 5) is how internalisation works; Moffett's markers/boundary account cross-bleeds to the **ethics/politics metaphysics** (community-scale belonging, the flatness→governance threshold) and to the community-scale open question in `scheduled-tasks.md`.

## Shape of the field (observations, not decisions)

1. **The mixing operation is densely companioned; the originality relocated to scope-and-status.** All seven joints sit in dense, named, cross-disciplinary, cross-cultural company — *including* Joint 3 once mixing is read as a family (blend, projection, layering, recombination, fusion) rather than one narrow "multiplicative" form. The framework's exposed edge is no longer "nobody mixes" but the *conjunction* the field never makes: mixing of whole held **language-sets** (not just concepts) + as a **repeatable primitive** of an ongoing system (not a one-off creative act) + as **constitutive and ungrounded** (no external engine). That conjunction, not the operation, is the original move.

2. **The divergence/convergence asymmetry is a field fact, not just a framing.** The convergence pole (Joint 5) is crowded and one-directional; the divergence pole lives mainly in Joint 2's encapsulation cluster (Kelly, von Glasersfeld, Barsalou, von Foerster) and splits against a universality camp. The field hands you convergence far more readily than divergence. The meeting point of the two is the coupling account (Joint 6).

3. **Recurring names span joints** — Bateson (1,2,6), Saussure (1,2; on-site), Schutz (2,6,7), Peirce (1,5,7), Wittgenstein (1,5,7), Whitehead (1,3), the Varela/Maturana corner (2,6; on-site), Ubuntu/Confucian/Buddhist threads (1,5,6,7). The same handful of figures recurs across the engine — a sign the joints are facets of one mechanism, not seven separate fields.

4. **On-site corner already in place** (couples, doesn't rebuild): Varela, Maturana, Merleau-Ponty, Sapolsky (persons); phenomenology, autopoiesis, pragmatism, structuralism, semiotics, biosemiotics (subjects); Saussure, von Uexküll (persons).

5. **Genuinely-absent constellation confirmed** (no existing page; surfaced repeatedly as load-bearing): Vygotsky, Tomasello, Thelen (likely Thelen-and-Smith), Kelly, Di Paolo, Berger, Luckmann; cognitive-linguistics figures (Lakoff, Fauconnier, Bybee, Harris, Firth); and a deeper bench the search surfaced — Spencer-Brown, von Glasersfeld, Barsalou, von Foerster, Searle, Hart, Halbwachs, Hacking, Castoriadis, Ostrom.

6. **Three cross-bleeds with backlog/active work:** the relational primitive *and now the mixing family* ↔ AI information-field submission (embedding space as both the relational geometry *and* the site where vector arithmetic mixes positions — the cauldron); historicity ↔ reality-time submission (assembly theory); and Berger-and-Luckmann's subject candidacy — no existing sociology-of-knowledge / social-construction subject on-site, so no territory clash.

7. **Subject candidates that carry their own machinery** (each holds enough internal structure to stand as territory, on the evidence here): Berger & Luckmann / social construction of reality (richest); personal construct psychology (Kelly); dynamic-systems theory of development (Thelen & Smith); usage-based / developmental linguistics (Vygotsky + Tomasello). Noted as observations — placement is deferred.
