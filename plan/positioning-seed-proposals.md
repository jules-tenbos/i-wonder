## New pages

### The clearest gap — a piece on shared reality / community formation

Working title something like *From Retention to Shared Reality* or *Community and Shared Reality*.

The trajectory across the positioning seed pages currently runs:

```
general relational turn      (Turn in Western Philosophy + Turn in Science)
        ↓
P0 / differentiation          (Being as Tension)
        ↓
individual historicity        (Arrow of Time → From Arrow to Historicity)
        ↓
synthesis / engine            (Interrelational Pluralism)
```

What's missing is the bridge from individual retention to shared reality through language — the trajectory that would support `/seed/human-reality/`. How do *multiple* subjects, each with retention, synchronise their language to form shared reality? The seed page itself argues this clearly, but the positioning support is split between Peirce (currently sitting on IP under "adjacent structural moves") and not much else.

Candidates for a dedicated trajectory piece: Peirce (community of inquirers, truth as long-run convergence), Habermas (communicative action), Brandom (inferential commitments in a community), possibly Mead (social formation of self), Wittgenstein on forms of life (currently on IP under linguistic pluralism), MacIntyre (tradition-constituted rationality, scheduled), Gadamer (fusion of horizons, scheduled).

Some of these already appear on IP, but doing different work there — IP maps thinkers across dimensions of pluralism, while a community-formation piece would trace a single trajectory through how shared reality forms. The two pages can coexist without stepping on each other; the same thinker can do different work in different pages.

This piece would also retrofit cleanly into the existing arc: From Arrow to Historicity could gain a closing pointer toward it, and IP could route some of its current shared-reality material to it and tighten its own coverage to the pluralism question proper.
