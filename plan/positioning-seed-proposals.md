# Positioning seed section — proposed updates and new pages

A review of the six trajectory pieces under `/positioning/seed/` plus the section landing, with proposals for updates to existing pages and consideration of where new pages would help. Companion to the earlier `/seed/` review; the two are best read together.

The positioning seed pages do trajectory work — tracing the historical and intellectual paths leading to the seed's territory. They sit one tier "warmer" than persons/subjects pages (where the thinker speaks for themselves) and one tier "cooler" than on-the-fence pages (where SPLectrum engages as a contender). The voice question for these pages is harder than for either neighbour: enough of a SPLectrum reading to do trajectory work, not so much that the thinkers are claimed for the framework.

---

## Updates to existing pages

### `being-as-tension`

Nothing to flag. `voice-examples.md` already identifies this as one of the working examples — nine formulations in their own vocabulary, the "affinity" closing paragraph earning its synthesis through placement rather than assertion. The page is doing what it should.

### `the-turn-in-western-philosophy`

The page is generally good — the trajectory section (Descartes through Whitehead) is well-paced, and the closing "affinity" paragraph lands cleanly. The places to look at are in the "axis philosophers" sub-sections, where the page does more explicit synthesis.

Two phrasings to reconsider:

- *"The seed makes explicit what Rorty leaves implicit."* This is precisely the construction `editorial-stance.md` flags ("before writing 'X makes explicit', check whether X actually does"). Rorty wouldn't endorse the account — he'd say there's nothing to make explicit. A formulation like *"What the seed names structurally, Rorty practices without naming"* carries the same observation without claiming Rorty for the framework.
- *"[Wittgenstein] chose not to build it."* The implicit framing is that Wittgenstein's project was incomplete relative to the seed. *"He didn't take that step"* is the same observation without the patronising overtone — and it leaves room for the reader to decide whether the step Wittgenstein didn't take was one he should have.

The "Through the seed" sub-sections within the axis treatments are a useful structural device, but they're where the voice slips hardest. They could lean lighter, leaving more of the synthesis work to the closing "affinity" paragraph (which already does it well).

### `the-turn-in-science`

The science trajectory itself is well-told. The synthesis paragraph at the end is where the voice tightens to claim:

*"Different vocabulary, same structural move. Independent arrivals at the same structure."*

The parallel-phrasing device above it (*"Where the seed says X, RQM says Y"*) works because the structural parallels are real. But "same structural move" is a strong claim — the voice doc's distinction between resonance ("a pointer") and convergence ("a claim") puts this on the claim side. *"Shares the structural territory"* or *"converges on the same observation"* would be a softer formulation that respects the same observation.

### `the-arrow-of-time`

Not principally a voice issue — the page is laying scientific groundwork before the philosophical bridge, and it does so cleanly. The structural issue is that the page leans on thinkers who don't yet have positioning entries: Boltzmann, Gibbs, Eddington, Anderson, Shannon, Landauer, Hawking, Penrose, Carroll. `positioning-entries-needed.md` already flags several as load-bearing on this page.

So the page is currently doing its work but pointing outward to Wikipedia rather than the project's own positioning section. As those persons pages land, the link density on this page strengthens without any rewriting. The page itself doesn't need updates — the work to do is around it, on the persons side.

### `from-arrow-to-historicity`

Strong page. Clean conceptual arc: traces → retention → historicity. The closing "open question" framing is appropriately honest about what's unresolved.

One small consideration. The page stops at retention. The seed's own historicity page then introduces *synchronisation of retention* — sharing between subjects — as the next move. If a new positioning piece on shared reality lands (see below), a single sentence at the end of this page pointing forward to where retention becomes synchronisation would complete the handoff. Without that piece, this page closes correctly where it does.

### `interrelational-pluralism`

The most editorially loaded of the six, partly because it has to do the most synthesis work. Two phrasings worth looking at and one structural question.

Phrasings:

- *"Interrelational pluralism recognises what was always one, and applies a unified view across all of them."* The "what was always one" claim is doing real work — these traditions were one thing? The thinkers themselves wouldn't endorse that. A formulation like *"Interrelational pluralism reads these strands together, recognising the structural observation each reaches from its own angle"* respects what is actually visible without claiming a pre-existing unity.
- *"What the existing strands each reach partially, the seed reaches as a single stance."* "Reaches partially" reads slightly as judging each strand against the seed's completeness. *"What each strand reaches from its own concerns, the seed assembles together"* — same point, with the part-of-rather-than-us-vs-them framing that `editorial-stance.md` recommends.

Structural question: coverage. The page currently maps James/Whitehead/Empedocles/Democritus (metaphysical), Feyerabend/Goodman/Kuhn (epistemological), Wittgenstein/Rorty (linguistic), Berlin (value), Arendt/Dahl/Laski (political), Peirce/Rovelli (adjacent structural moves), and Connolly (pluralism vs pluralisation).

`seed-buildup-thinkers.md` has another cluster scheduled: MacIntyre, Gadamer, Hayek, Polanyi, Oakeshott, Cassirer, Bateson, plus non-Western traditions (Nagarjuna, Nishida, Jain Anekantavada). As those land, IP will either need to absorb them or point to where they live. Whether the page expands or stays roughly this size and the new clusters get their own dedicated treatment is a structural choice worth making consciously rather than discovering when the scheduled work arrives.

## New pages

One gap is clearer than the others; one or two further candidates are worth flagging but probably wait.

### The clearest gap — a piece on shared reality / community formation

Working title something like *From Retention to Shared Reality* or *Community and Shared Reality*.

The trajectory across the positioning seed pages currently runs:

```
general relational turn      (Turn in Western Philosophy + Turn in Science)
        ↓
P0 / differentiation          (Being as Tension)
        ↓
individual historicity        (Arrow of Time → From Arrow to Historicity)
        ↓
synthesis / engine            (Interrelational Pluralism)
```

What's missing is the bridge from individual retention to shared reality through language — the trajectory that would support `/seed/human-reality/`. How do *multiple* subjects, each with retention, synchronise their language to form shared reality? The seed page itself argues this clearly, but the positioning support is split between Peirce (currently sitting on IP under "adjacent structural moves") and not much else.

Candidates for a dedicated trajectory piece: Peirce (community of inquirers, truth as long-run convergence), Habermas (communicative action), Brandom (inferential commitments in a community), possibly Mead (social formation of self), Wittgenstein on forms of life (currently on IP under linguistic pluralism), MacIntyre (tradition-constituted rationality, scheduled), Gadamer (fusion of horizons, scheduled).

Some of these already appear on IP, but doing different work there — IP maps thinkers across dimensions of pluralism, while a community-formation piece would trace a single trajectory through how shared reality forms. The two pages can coexist without stepping on each other; the same thinker can do different work in different pages.

This piece would also retrofit cleanly into the existing arc: From Arrow to Historicity could gain a closing pointer toward it, and IP could route some of its current shared-reality material to it and tighten its own coverage to the pluralism question proper.

### Possibly later — a privacy / subject-boundary piece

Would support `/seed/privacy-and-decentralisation/`. Would cover Jonas (organism boundary as metabolism), Nagel (what-it's-like-to-be-X as constitutive of subject), Husserl (phenomenological reduction), plus the decentralisation cluster (Ostrom, Polanyi, Hayek).

I'd hold off. The seed page itself is flagged for review/rewrite, so the positioning trajectory should wait for the seed page to settle. Writing trajectory toward a moving target is wasted work, and the privacy thinkers are not at risk of disappearing.

### Probably not yet

Nothing for `/seed/discovery/`, `/seed/category-theory/`, or `/seed/engineering/`. Discovery is explicitly open territory. Category theory has its own home under `/positioning/subjects/`. Engineering belongs in the engineering section, not in positioning.

## Summary

In rough priority order:

1. **The shared-reality / community-formation piece.** The most useful single addition to the positioning seed section. Closes the gap between From Arrow to Historicity and Interrelational Pluralism, supports `/seed/human-reality/` directly, and lets IP tighten back to the pluralism question. The thinker pool is partly already in the project (Peirce, Wittgenstein) and partly scheduled (MacIntyre, Gadamer).
2. **Voice softenings on the Turn in Western Philosophy.** Two specific phrasings flagged by `editorial-stance.md`'s rules — small but visible to a reader who knows the voice doc.
3. **Voice softening on Turn in Science.** One phrase — "same structural move" → "shares the structural territory" or similar.
4. **Voice softenings and a coverage decision on Interrelational Pluralism.** Two phrasings, plus a conscious choice about whether the page absorbs the scheduled clusters (MacIntyre, Gadamer, Hayek, etc.) or routes them elsewhere.
5. **As persons pages land, Arrow of Time gets denser internal linking.** No rewrite of the page itself; the work to do is on the persons side (Boltzmann, Eddington, Anderson, Shannon, Landauer, Hawking, Penrose, Carroll).
6. **Optional handoff sentence at the close of From Arrow to Historicity** — only worth adding if and when the shared-reality piece exists.
7. **Hold the privacy piece** until `/seed/privacy-and-decentralisation/` settles after its review/rewrite.

Items 1 and 4 are structural decisions for the seed to make. Items 2, 3, and 6 are small editorial moves. Item 5 happens organically as scheduled work lands.
