# Observer Patch Holography

Observer Patch Holography (OPH) is a framework for fundamental physics developed by Bernhard Mueller and collaborators at Floating Pragma. Its starting axiom is that no observer ever sees the whole world. Each observer has access to a local patch of reality; the shared world is what emerges when patches reconcile their overlapping descriptions. From that single condition, OPH attempts to derive what it presents as the standard machinery of modern physics — spacetime, gauge structure, particle content, measurement — as outputs of a consistency architecture rather than as primitives.

## The core architecture

OPH builds reality out of finite local pieces and the rules that govern how they fit together.

A *patch* is the part of reality available to one observer: private state, local observables, and stable records that the observer can read back later. No patch is the whole; every patch is partial. Where two patches overlap, they share a *collar* — a small set of observables that both observers can see. The collar is where the work happens.

If two observers describe their shared collar in the same way, no further action is needed. If they describe it differently, the disagreement registers as a *syndrome* — a compact local error message identifying which shared bits failed to agree. A *repair move* is a local adjustment to data near the disagreement, accepted if it improves the overlap fit, rejected if it makes things worse. Patterns that survive enough repair rounds to be relied on by other patches become *records* — durable traces that constitute shared history.

This is the basic vocabulary: patches, collars, syndromes, repair moves, records. The dynamics of physical reality, in OPH's reading, are what these primitives do when applied recursively across a network of overlapping observers.

## Objectivity as fixed point

Classical physics assumes a world with definite properties that measurements reveal. OPH inverts the assumption. Objectivity is not what the world starts with; it is what the world settles into after enough patches have compared, translated, and reconciled their descriptions.

A measurement, in this reading, is the moment when a local pattern stabilises across observers as a shared record. The transition from many possible outcomes to one definite result — the measurement problem in quantum mechanics — is resolved by placing records and observer synchronisation inside the physics rather than treating them as external bookkeeping.

The phrasing Mueller uses is direct: <cite>"objectivity is the stabilised result, never the starting point."</cite> A particle, a spacetime event, a public fact is what survives translation and comparison among finite views. What does not survive is not real in the shared sense.

## Meaning assignment as fundamental

OPH's broader philosophical claim is that meaning assignment is not a feature added to physics by minds. It is, in OPH's reading, the operation physics performs.

The mapping runs through Peirce's triadic semiotics: sign, object, interpretant. In OPH, the sign-vehicle is a local pattern in a patch. The interpreter is an observer reading that pattern. The code that connects sign to referent is the shared dictionary observers use when comparing across overlaps. Stable meaning is what emerges when many interpreters reconcile their readings of overlapping patterns — the same process, OPH claims, that produces what physics calls objectivity.

The claim runs in both directions. Semiotics asks how meaning comes to exist; OPH claims to answer that question by showing the mechanism. Physics asks what makes a world legible; OPH claims meaning assignment is what makes it legible. Semiotics and physics, on OPH's reading, describe the same process from two angles.

## The wider corpus

The OPH project extends well beyond this philosophical core. The corpus includes attempts to derive the Standard Model gauge group, the fine-structure constant, the cosmological constant, and the dark matter phenomenon from the same consistency axioms; extensions into ethical theory, eschatology, and economic restructuring; and a hardware programme proposing specific physical implementations of the holographic substrate. The full corpus is at floatingpragma.io.
