## PART A — TERRITORY SURVEY

### TL;DR
- Artemy Kolchinsky is a theoretical physicist (PhD Informatics/Complex Systems, Indiana University, 2015; advisor Luis Rocha) working at the interface of nonequilibrium statistical physics and information theory. Career: Santa Fe Institute (postdoc under David Wolpert, 2015–2021) → University of Tokyo/Universal Biology Institute (2022–2023, under Sosuke Ito) → Universitat Pompeu Fabra Barcelona (Marie Curie Fellow, Ricard Solé's ICREA-Complex Systems Lab, 2023–). - Category-theory check: essentially negative. His toolkit is Shannon/Kolmogorov information theory, stochastic thermodynamics, information geometry, and partial-information-decomposition lattices — NOT category theory, sheaf theory, or compositional machinery. The categorical framing of correlational relationalism is the requester's own contribution.

### 2. The Semantic / Meaningful Information Work (central)

**The paper.** A. Kolchinsky & D. H. Wolpert, "Semantic information, autonomous agency and non-equilibrium statistical physics," Interface Focus 8(6): 20180041, 6 December 2018 (arXiv:1806.08053).

**Setup.** Shannon "syntactic" information quantifies correlation with no consideration of what it means. The paper aims to fill Shannon's gap with a definition of semantic information that is (a) intrinsic — grounded in the system's own dynamics rather than an externally-assigned goal — and (b) universal, applying to "a rock, a hurricane or a cell."

**Why not existing approaches (their framing):** utility/objective-function approaches specify the goal exogenously ("meaning for the external scientist who imputes goals to the system, rather than for the system itself"); teleosemantics (Dretske, Millikan) is rejected as selection-restricted and etiological (past-history-defined rather than intrinsic-present); the autonomous-agents literature (Kauffman, Varela, enactivists) had the right intuition but "remained largely informal."

**Formal apparatus.**
- System X, environment Y, jointly distributed p(x₀,y₀), coupled dynamics to time τ.
- **Viability function V**: "the negative Shannon entropy of the distribution over the states of system X" — a "degree of existence," motivated because Shannon entropy upper-bounds the probability of occupying viable states. Connects to thermodynamic (low-entropy, Schrödinger) intuition.
- **Counterfactual interventions**: "scramble" the system–environment channel via information-theoretic coarse-graining, producing intervened distributions with less syntactic information.
- **Viability value of information ΔV**: viability at τ minus viability under the scrambled distribution. Positive = information helps persistence; negative = "pathological" (misrepresentation; e.g. anti-chemotactic bacterium).
- **Semantic information**: syntactic information preserved by the viability-optimal intervention — the minimum needed to lose no viability. Yields a decomposition into "meaningful" and "meaningless" bits.
- **Semantic content** of a state: the conditional distribution over environment states under the optimal intervention.
- **Semantic efficiency**: semantic/syntactic ratio. **Stored vs observed**: stored from initial mutual information; observed dynamically acquired via transfer entropy.

**Worked examples** (rock, hurricane, food-caching bird) illustrate the gradient: rock ≈ zero value of information; hurricane low (passive dissipative structure); caching bird high (genuine agent). Semantic information discriminates agents from passive nonequilibrium structures.

### 3. The Correlation / Syntactic Information-Thermodynamics Work (meaning NOT considered)

The larger, more technically mainstream half. Information is pure correlation, view-free, no viability/meaning/subject.

- **Mismatch cost** (J Stat Mech 2017; PRX 2021; PRE 2021): dissipation depends not only on the computation but on actual-vs-tuned input distribution — "the cost of being wrong."
- **Thermodynamics of computation**: Turing machines (PRR 2020); circuits (NJP 2020); generalized Zurek bound (PRE 2023).
- **Work/entropy under protocol constraints** (PRX 2021): "accessible" information — information convertible to work given constraints.
- **Space–time tradeoffs and hidden states** (Nature Communications 2019; NJP 2019).
- **Tokyo stochastic thermodynamics / information geometry**: housekeeping/excess decompositions (PRR 2023); thermodynamic bounds on spectral perturbations, oscillations, cross-correlation asymmetry (PRR 2024, PRL 2023); generalized free energy (PRR 2026). Uses information geometry (Fisher metrics, optimal transport / Wasserstein-2 gradient flows) — geometric, not categorical.
- **Machine-learning information theory**: Nonlinear Information Bottleneck (Entropy 2019); caveats for IB in deterministic scenarios (ICLR 2019); the skeptical "On the information bottleneck theory of deep learning" (Saxe et al., ICLR 2018 / J Stat Mech 2019), finding no evident causal link between compression and generalization.
- **Partial information decomposition** (Entropy 2022, 2024): order-theoretic redundancy lattices, not categorical.

### 4. Origin of Life / Self-Replication / Thermodynamics of Life

- **"Thermodynamics of Darwinian selection in molecular replicators"** (Phil Trans R Soc B 2025; arXiv 2021): a bound relating fitness, replication rate, and Gibbs free energy dissipated per copy; the critical selection coefficient is lower-bounded by dissipation per replication — a thermodynamic threshold for Darwinian evolution.
- **"Thermodynamic dissipation does not bound replicator growth and decay rates"** (J Chem Phys 2024): direct challenge to Jeremy England's 2013 "Statistical physics of self-replication." Kolchinsky argues no universal relation links dissipation to per-capita growth and decay, because a thermodynamically-consistent replicator cannot undergo both per-capita growth and per-capita decay back into reactants. A genuine, ongoing, named dispute.
- **"Maximizing free energy gain"** (Entropy 2025); **bacteriorhodopsin free-energy harvesting** (PRR 2024); **"Fundamental constraints to the logic of living systems"** (Interface Focus 2024, Solé/SFI, ten authors); **"The Physics of Life"** roadmap (PRX Life 2025); **neutral theory of cooperative dynamics** (PNAS 2025); protocell papers (Phil Trans R Soc B 2025).

### 5. The Philosophical Tensions (surfaced as content)

- **The subject/perspective enters with viability — and they know it.** Section VI: the three framing choices are "'subjective' choices of the scientist." Section IV, on the viability function: "it is important to re-emphasize that, in our framework, the viability function is exogenously determined by the scientist analyzing the system, rather than being a purely endogenous characteristic of the system. At first glance, our approach may appear to suffer some of the same problems as do approaches that define semantic information in terms of an exogenously-specified utility function."
- **The rescue attempts (patches):** universality of form; minimality (viability as the implicit core of nearly all goals); in-principle objectivity ("in principle it may be possible to derive the viability function in some objective way (e.g., in terms of the attractor landscape...)"), plus maximizing semantic information over partitions/timescales to "automatically identify agents." The maximization move is explicitly deferred to future work, and requires a prior choice of which measure to maximize.
- **Agency/autonomy graded, not binary:** "a physical system is an autonomous agent to the extent that it has a large measure of semantic information." Side-steps internal models: "Our framework also side-steps questions of what type of 'internal models' or 'internal representations', if any, are employed." Yet retains representational vocabulary ("semantic content," "represent," "misrepresent") — a tension with strict anti-representationalism.
- **The viability function is one choice with acknowledged defects:** negative entropy may not "maintain identity" (rhino and human both low-entropy); alternatives (viability-set probability, KL/exergy) each have problems (open/driven systems lack an equilibrium reference). Deferred to future work.
- **Boundary/individuation unresolved:** the partition is chosen; the proposed fix (maximize semantic information) makes individuation depend on the meaning-measure, which depends on the partition — a circularity. (Cf. Krakauer et al.'s information theory of individuality, which makes the boundary itself the object of inference.)
- **Neighbours:** teleosemantics rejected while its misrepresentation-desideratum is inherited; autopoiesis/enactivism cited but not engaged (they ground normativity in operational closure and self-produced identity, not an exogenous viability function — the sharpest contrast; Maturana/Varela hold that coding and information-transmission "do not enter in the realization of a concrete autopoietic system"); Friston's FEP cited once, never built on (both share the self-maintaining-far-from-equilibrium intuition; FEP centers internal models, which Kolchinsky avoids); **Rovelli** is the actual origin — "Meaning = Information + Evolution" (2016/2018) proposed meaningful information as survival-relevant correlation, credited and thanked first.
- **Named critiques:** Stuart Bartlett's "Causal Leverage Density" (2024) drops the viability requirement as too restrictive, retaining the scrambling machinery but comparing phase-space trajectory distributions — a friendly-but-substantive generalization. A creationist/ID critique (2018) rejects the whole naturalizing project. Productive applications (chemotaxis, synthetic-cell molecular communication, resource-foraging agents, Daisy-World) show operational traction despite contested grounding.

### Caveats
- Biography/dates/publication map from primary sources; where secondary pages conflict on current title, CV/website is authoritative.
- The 2018 paper is a *proposal*, not a settled result — its most ambitious claims (objective viability derivation, automatic agent-identification, semantic information as a life-criterion) are explicitly speculative and deferred. The formal definitions are delivered; their philosophical adequacy is contested.
- The field is genuinely unsettled (whether meaning is naturalizable, how to handle observer-dependence, the boundary problem). Teleosemantic, enactivist, and biosemiotic traditions each reject key premises; none is decisively refuted by the others.
- The England dispute is technical and ongoing, not a settled overturning.
- Category-theory absence is from a thorough but not exhaustive search; the confident claim is absence from the significant published corpus.

## PART B — SPLECTRUM-SIDE DISCUSSION (exploratory)

*The seed's side of the conversation — thinking-in-motion, not an account of Kolchinsky on his own terms. Preliminary; some threads may not survive.*

### Verdict on importance/usefulness

High value, of a specific kind. Kolchinsky is **not** a philosophical interlocutor the seed must reckon with the way it reckons with Rorty, Whitehead, or Wittgenstein — he supplies no structure the seed lacks. What he supplies is rarer for the purpose: **independent corroboration of the seed's central move, arriving from physics rather than the humanities.** He reaches "meaning is in the relation, not the entity" from nonequilibrium thermodynamics and Shannon correlation — a wholly different substrate — which gives the seed traction in a register it currently barely touches (existing physics footprint: RQM, assembly theory, mineral evolution). Convergence from an unexpected direction outweighs another continental voice restating the familiar; nobody can say he got there by wanting the seed's answer.

The crown jewel: he is a **split figure whose split falls exactly on the seam the seed draws.** His correlational strand (view-free, symmetric, meaning-free mutual information) sits side-by-side with the seed with no friction — pure resonance. His semantic strand carries a *colouring*: it narrates meaning's pathway as something the system objectively has about its environment, viability-grounded, with the observer's frame to be removed "in principle" by future work. That narration mislocates the pathway by a layer — and he concedes it in his own text. That is fence structure, textbook: not two vocabularies clashing on one plane, but a pathway-narrative mislocated by a layer, admitted by the author, patched with a promissory note. Having a *single researcher embody the seam in one corpus* is worth more than two figures on either side — the syntactic→semantic transition can be shown happening inside one program, at the exact point where "matters-to" enters.

Direct tie to the **aliveness sketch**: his viability function *is* self-maintained low-entropy state (P0 with self-maintenance); his hurricane-vs-caching-bird gradient *is* the passive-structure-vs-agent discrimination; his semantic-information measure grades what the sketch grades. On structural shape, Kolchinsky–Wolpert is a closer twin to the self-constituting definition than Ruiz-Mirazo–Moreno, and brings physics formalism where the sketch has prose.

### The relativity structure — local frame vs phantom absolute frame

The sharpest reading of the perspectival tension is a relativity analogy that is structurally exact, not loose. What Kolchinsky wants is a viability function read from nowhere — the physics equivalent of absolute space. What he has is a viability function indexed to a chosen partition, system/environment cut, and timescale. He treats that indexing as a *defect to be removed*; the relativity move treats it as a *feature that was never a defect*. Special relativity did not recover absolute simultaneity by better bookkeeping — it dropped the demand and found the physics fully objective *without* a privileged frame, quantities transforming correctly between frames. His semantic information is exactly this: well-defined *relative to a subject's cut*, and the search for the frame-free version is the search for an ether.

Not even an import from outside his own house. His closest acknowledged source is **Rovelli**, whose RQM holds that quantum states are relative to the observing system — no absolute state, and this is structure, not limitation. Kolchinsky took "meaning = relevant correlation" from Rovelli and left the relationalism on the table. The person he builds on already made the move that dissolves his quagmire, in the exact domain (physics) where he wants to stay respectable. RQM already sits on the site as a subject page; the seam nearly writes itself — *the relationalism you need is already in the source you are citing.*

### Why he doesn't relativise the pov to the system: the silently occupied subject-slot

The viability function needs someone *for whom* persistence matters — a subject-slot, structurally. He cannot not have it; "meaning" requires it. What he has done is **fill that slot with the observer and not notice.** The scientist's frame has not stayed outside evaluating the system; it has climbed into the system's subject-position and is wearing it. "Viable for the bacterium" is really "viable-as-the-analyst-scores-it," spoken from the bacterium's seat.

This is why the perspectival residue will not scrub out. He keeps trying to eliminate the observer from the *description* (better partition, objective attractor-landscape derivation, maximise-to-fix-the-frame) — but the observer is not loose in the description; the observer is lodged in the subject-slot itself, doing the one job the framework cannot run without. Every objectivity-rescue tries to make the occupant invisible while keeping the occupancy, which is why each ends in "in principle, future work." The residue is structural, not sloppy — it is the silent tenant.

This upgrades the relativity analogy: not merely chasing an absolute frame instead of accepting local ones, but **secretly measuring from one frame while claiming frame-independence** — the pre-relativity move of treating the lab frame as no frame at all, the observer's own rest-frame quietly substituted for absolute space.

The **tell** confirming the diagnosis is the representational vocabulary. "Semantic content," states "best fit to *represent*," "*misrepresent* the world" — representation-talk needs a representer, someone for whom the state stands for something. A genuinely system-framed account would have the state *being* a distinction in the system's own carving, no representing required. The recurring representational language is the tenant leaving fingerprints: *the scientist* reads the state as representing the environment, because the scientist is the one for whom it is about anything. (Checkable — worth pulling the actual instances before leaning on it, so the fingerprint is confirmed systematic and not pattern-matched.)

What "objective" is doing underneath: **concealment.** If the viability function is objective, then nobody is sitting in the subject-seat — it is just a fact about the system. The appeal to objectivity is precisely what keeps the occupancy silent. Which is why the RQM twist bites hardest: Rovelli's relationalism makes objective-and-frame-relative *compatible*, dissolving the concealment. You do not need frame-independence to be objective, so you do not need to hide the frame, so the seat's occupant becomes visible — and can be asked whether it is the right one. It is not. **The seed's contribution, made precise: the subject-slot is already there and already filled — evict the analyst, seat the system.** The bacterium's cauldron is the rightful occupant. Nothing in the formalism changes; what changes is who sits in the chair the math already built.

### Meaning read locally is fine — the physics precedent

Kolchinsky's semantic strand is acceptable if interpreted *locally*, from the subject's pov. Having a reference system is not a problem in physics — compare the local reference frame of relativity against absolute Newtonian space. The reinterpretation *rescues* the strand, but he does not authorise it and in places resists it (the objectivity-rescue runs the other way). So on the site this is **fence** material, not resonance: the vocabularies are in tension, and the tension resolves only by relocating the pov to a layer his framework does not grant. The correlational strand is clean resonance; the semantic strand is "here is the colouring, here is where it mislocates the frame, here is the local reading that dissolves it."

### The non-life-to-life seamless transition as proof of concept

His gradient and the seed's gradient are the same gradient, seamless for the same structural reason — but he can only *assert* the seamlessness while the seed can say *why it must hold*. He gets it because viability is substrate-neutral: rock, hurricane, cell all get a viability number, meaning shading up continuously with no line. On his own terms this is slightly uncanny — if semantic information is the scientist's imputation, why does it track a real continuum? The seed answers: the gradient is seamless because *the frame is present all the way down*. There is no layer where the system's own pov switches on. Mineral-evolution stage already has a thin other-side; prebiotic chemistry sharpens it; the cell thickens it. Meaning does not arrive when life arrives — it is the same relational structure (a system's dynamics carving relevance out of its other) getting richer as historicity deepens and coupling broadens. Live-ability everywhere, liveness local. His seamlessness is the seed's gradient viewed from physics, and the seed explains the very continuity he can only report. This is also the strongest independent-corroboration beat: a physicist, refusing a life/non-life line, finding meaning shading up out of geochemistry, arriving from thermodynamics with no anti-representationalist commitment driving him — landing where the aliveness sketch lands from the seed.

### Stochastic thermodynamics as the load-bearing find

More valuable than the semantic paper, arguably, because it gives the correlation-treatment *native to physics* rather than imported into it. Stochastic thermodynamics is already built on the objects the seed treats as primitive: systems whose state is a distribution over micro-configurations, evolving by transition rates, exchanging with an environment across an interface. Its central quantities (entropy production, information flow, fluctuation theorems) are relational and asymmetric-through-coupling — between-quantities, not properties of either side alone. Entropy production is a between-quantity; transfer entropy is directional coupling. The whole apparatus is P0 doing thermodynamic work: a system held in tension with its other across an interface, each shaping the other continuously. The seed's mixing-event structure — a distribution of outcomes shaped by the path landscape — is already the shape of a stochastic-thermodynamic transition. Structural rhyme, tight.

Why enter here rather than through the semantic paper: it is **clean resonance all the way up to the seam.** The correlational strand carries no colouring — no viability function, no occupied subject-slot, no representation-talk. View-free by construction and it *stays* view-free. Semantics is then what happens when the subject-frame is added *on top of* an already-relational physics: physics hands over the correlation layer for free, the seed supplies the frame that turns "correlation" into "matters-to" without changing the physics underneath. The syntactic→semantic transition is not a leap to engineer — it is seating a subject in a structure the thermodynamics already built. **Sosuke Ito** is the person this routes through; if stochastic thermodynamics becomes real territory, Ito crosses from external-citation to person, and information-thermodynamics-as-subject moves from "strong maybe" to spine.

### Concepts and the relational: the entity is its relations

A convergence tightening the process-theory sketch's P0. The sketch has P0 creating "two sides AND the relation that holds between them." What the being-side *consists of*: it is not that P0 makes an entity which then, separately, has relations — **the entity *is* its relations.** The encapsulation is a specific set of relations bundled and held as a unit; the concept is empty of everything except the relations that constitute it. Anti-representationalism landing at the primitive: there is no conceptual "stuff" inside the concept for the relations to be relations *of*. Relations all the way down; the entity is a stable knot of them held closed.

This dissolves the apparent concept-vs-relational tension — one thing from two angles: the concept is the encapsulation-*as-held* (a unit, a what), the relations are the same encapsulation-*as-constituted* (a bundle, a how). A crystallised how, with local standing but no fundamentality — the seed's how-over-what discipline at P0.

"Meaning requires a reference point" is the same structure dynamically: a concept means *by* its relations, so it can only mean *within* the web those relations reach into. Strip the surrounding relations and nothing is left to mean *with*. Context is not added to meaning — context *is* the relations, and the relations *are* the entity. This is why Kolchinsky's viability-read-from-nowhere cannot work: a frame-free meaning would be a concept whose relations reach nowhere — an empty knot, nothing.

The move with teeth: if a concept *is* its relations, then **defining a concept is choosing which relations to bundle** — not labelling a pre-existing thing but cutting a specific relational structure into a held unit. Which cut you make determines what can be expressed downstream, because the concept enters the cauldron as a mixing-unit and its relational content is exactly what is available to mix. Choose the concept, choose the relational reach; choose the reach, set the expressiveness. Concept-choice *is* the expressive capacity of the language. (The same act the framework performs on itself when it chooses its meta-language primitives with discipline, knowing it is a choice.)

### Meaning is subject-tied because the flow is two-way

The essence of meaning's tie to the subject is a **two-way flow**: language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality, *and* the subject has agency over the language. Neither direction alone needs a subject. Pure inflow (reality disclosed through the medium, correlations landing) is Kolchinsky's bacterium — reception without articulation, correlation with nobody home; the subject-slot stays empty because inflow never calls for an occupant. Pure outflow (articulation onto nothing) is noise-generation. **Meaning appears where the loop closes:** the language sets that carve the world are the ones the subject remixes, and the remixing changes what can next be carved. Reception reshapes the cauldron; the reshaped cauldron changes the next reception. Disclosure and articulation are each other's condition, and *that closure is the subject* — the pov is the name for the flows being mutually conditioning.

Why this cannot be scrubbed to a view from nowhere, structurally: an external observer can describe the inflow, but cannot occupy the agency-over-the-medium, because that agency is exercised *from within the very language sets being modified*. There is no outside seat from which to remix a cauldron — remixing is something the cauldron does to itself, with its own contents, along its own path landscape. The loop is closed around the subject's historicity; an observer can watch it but cannot be in it, because being in it *is* being that historicity. One half of the loop (agency over the medium) is only available from inside.

This maps the reception/articulation pair of the mixing-event onto self-constitution: agency-over-the-language is the language-layer face of "actively remixes the products that accumulate inside it." A system that only received would accumulate state (the weathering rock); a system with agency over its medium remixes what it receives *as* it receives it. **The two-way flow is recursive self-mixing seen from the meaning side.** "Meaning is subject-tied" and "aliveness is self-constitution" are the same claim in two vocabularies — meaning is what self-constitution looks like from inside the language; self-constitution is what meaning looks like from outside in the thermodynamics. The two sketches describe one structure through two windows.

At the high-agency end this loops back to concept-creation: agency over the language *is* the power to choose the relational cuts. The outward flow, at depth, is a subject editing the very concepts through which it experiences — deliberate concept-choice as the subject exercising its deepest agency over its own reality.

### Agency as degrees of freedom from facet-complexity

The gradient worry (does "degree of agency" draw a line under which there is no meaning?) dissolves: **agency is not a primitive.** It is the *degrees of freedom available from the complexity of the historicity* — how many facets are in the cauldron available for remixing. A real reduction, not a relabelling: it takes agency off the list of posited things and derives it from what is already there. Few language sets → few facets → remixing nearly forced, outward flow almost degenerate. Pile up historicity → facet-count climbs → combinatorial space climbs. Agency *is* that space. The bacterium is not "a bit agentive"; it is a cauldron of low facet-count, few degrees of freedom, loop closing tightly with little room. The subject-to-agent gradient stops being asserted and starts being *counted*.

This distinguishes **complexity from historicity-as-mere-accumulation**, and the distinction is load-bearing. Two subjects with equal *depth* of retention can have different *facet-complexity*: a deep monotone (much retained along a narrow ridge, few independent axes) versus a shallower but richly multi-faceted cauldron (many independent language sets, high combinatorial reach). The multi-faceted one has more agency despite less accumulation — agency lives in the number of *independently combinable* facets, not the mass retained. Historicity is the source; facet-complexity is the operative quantity. Follows from mixing being multiplicative: what matters is how many independent sets there are to multiply.

Possible unification of the aliveness sketch's "vector not scalar" worry: the three axes (historicity-depth, coupling-breadth, recursion-density) may collapse toward one operative quantity — facet-complexity. Depth feeds facets; coupling-breadth feeds facets (external modulation adds independent axes); recursion-density *is* facets folding back as new mixable units. Three sources feeding one count. Held as hypothesis, not identity — edge cases may diverge.

The **dormant-spore case sharpens rather than breaks the definition:** agency is degrees of freedom available *from* the complexity — facets as *live options*, not inventory. The spore has facets in cold storage; degrees of freedom near zero because nothing is mixing; wakes into high agency when mixing resumes. Agency is dynamic — degrees of freedom *in play* — matching "aliveness is where self-constitution is *active*." The formulation already carries the active/latent distinction the spore demands; it predicted the case rather than needing a patch.

Two threads to keep an eye on (downstream refinement): (i) "degrees of freedom" has a precise meaning in the statistical mechanics being absorbed — gift if facet-count behaves like a phase-space dimension count and the resonance is structural, trap if the term smuggles in unearned rigour; the stochastic-thermodynamics tie could adjudicate. (ii) The *quality* of facets matters, not just the count — facets that resonate barely mix, facets that are orthogonal-but-combinable open genuine new states; "how many facets" may need to become "how many independently combinable facets," a subtler count.

### CT and type theory — layer hypothesis (parked for later work)

A provisional cut, to be developed: CT is relational-structural — built for structure that lives between things without residing in either, composition, what is preserved across mappings. That is the *correlation* layer, the syntactic view-free side; CT is the natural partner for the between-ness. Type theory is about what can be said of what — which distinctions are well-formed, which compositions are licensed. That is closer to the *language-set* layer: encapsulated bundles, what mixes with what, which combinations are admissible. If the cut holds, they serve different layers rather than competing: physics enters through CT (stochastic thermodynamics is compositional, relational, between-quantities); semantic/linguistic structure enters through type theory (what a subject's language sets permit — the typing *is* the frame). The concept-as-empty-knot-of-relations sits on the seam: CT describes what it is made of, type theory describes what it can do. P4 constraint: neither can be *the* privileged meta-language — both are content for the chosen meta-language, tools reached for by use, not enthroned.
