# Building Up the SPLectrum Philosophy from the Seed — Plan

A planning document for a multi-post run starting September 2026. Drafted for external discussion before commitment.

## Context

The SPLectrum seed is a set of six principles about properties common to all languages — language understood broadly, beyond human communication, into all relational activity:

- **P0** — Being implies language. Being and language come into existence together through differentiation; neither prior.
- **P1** — Language is relational. What a language gives access to depends on what it relates to.
- **P2** — Language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality.
- **P3** — Language is where subjects share knowledge about reality. Convergence is the source of shared reality and of the only objectivity available.
- **P4** — Languages are inter-relational and have equal standing in potential. None is privileged in principle, however different in current reach.
- **P5** — Together they form a web of growing complexity.

The blog has been positioning the seed since March 2026 — situating it among historical thinkers (Wittgenstein, Heidegger, RQM, Heraclitus, Spencer-Brown, Nāgārjuna, others), unpacking individual principles (notably P0 in *The Birth of P0 — Being Is Tension*), and applying it to particular domains (human reality, category theory, decentralisation, engineering). The published run closes on **24 August 2026** with *Embracing Anti-Foundationalism* — a preamble piece that ends: *"Anti-foundationalism was the word I took away. For what it opened up, I was still to find out."*

September picks up exactly there.

## The Position to Build

The SPLectrum philosophy has a recognisable but unclaimed shape. The September run names it and sets it on the page. Three load-bearing claims:

1. **Anti-foundationalism, properly understood, is constructive.** The seed isn't rejecting foundations; it's showing what holds without a single foundation: webs, not pyramids.
2. **Foundationalism at the community level is legitimate.** A community can structure itself around foundational commitments inside its own language game — religious, scientific, moral — and that is a legitimate way to be in its reality. What is denied is *meta-*privilege: the claim that one community's foundation has cross-community standing.
3. **Diversity is structural, not optional.** Communities differ because the structure makes them differ. P4 (equal standing in potential) is a property of the picture, not a moral overlay.

The position's centre of gravity is a phrase from the working conversation: **shared subjective objectivity**. Each community arrives at its own objectivity — its own values, its own facts, its own ways of converging on what is real — and these community-bound objectivities interrelate without a meta-view above them. The position avoids both metaphysical realism (no privileged view) and pure relativism (P4 + P5 give the apparatus for inter-community convergence).

**P2 is the cornerstone for the constructive case.** P2 says each subject experiences reality through *their* language. That places foundations inside subjects and communities, in the languages they hold, and rules out a language-free vantage point from which to claim meta-privilege. Without P2, anti-foundationalism either collapses into relativism or smuggles a meta-foundation in by the back door. With P2, foundationalism gets its proper place: legitimate within a language game, illegitimate as a meta-claim.

P0 + P1 remain the structural generative base of the seed. P2 is where the philosophy starts doing the work that distinguishes it.

## Tradition Neighbourhoods

Two clusters of historical company, serving two different layers of the work.

**Pragmatist cluster — the pluralism lineage:**

- **James** — named pluralism as a philosophical programme; radical empiricism; *A Pluralistic Universe*
- **Dewey** — constructive pragmatism; democracy as inquiry; intelligence as social problem-solving
- **Wittgenstein** — forms of life, agreement in judgments, language games
- **Rorty** — ethnocentrism without relativism; conversation replaces epistemology
- **Putnam** — internal realism, multiple true descriptions, no ready-made world

**Tradition and engagement cluster:**

- **MacIntyre** — tradition-constituted rationality; how traditions engage each other
- **Gadamer** — fusion of horizons; understanding across traditions without a meta-framework

**Pluralism and values cluster:**

- **Berlin** — value pluralism, incommensurability of values, no single summum bonum
- **Arendt** — plurality as the human condition; constitutive of political life

**Philosophy of science:**

- **Kuhn** — paradigms as community-bound foundations; incommensurability between paradigms

**Liberal / conservative cluster — decentralisation and practical knowledge:**

- **Hayek** — knowledge problem, spontaneous order, decentralisation without central design
- **Polanyi** — tacit knowledge, personal knowledge, the embodied ground of knowing
- **Oakeshott** — practical knowledge over theoretical, critique of rationalism in politics

**Symbolic forms:**

- **Cassirer** — philosophy of symbolic forms; language, myth, science, art as different ways of constituting reality

**Process-relational / biosemiotic cluster — broader-than-human signposts:**

- **Whitehead** — process metaphysics from atoms upward; explicitly constructive ("speculative philosophy")
- **Peirce** — semiotic system extending below the human; constructive
- **Bateson** — mind and communication across living systems
- **Biosemiotics** (Sebeok, Hoffmeyer, Kull) — Peircean semiotics across all life

The anthropocentric clusters feed the synthesis directly. The process-relational cluster signposts the broader-than-human scope the seed allows but this run doesn't build.

**One known objection to engage:** Davidson's *On the Very Idea of a Conceptual Scheme* (1974) — argues scheme-relativism is incoherent because we couldn't recognise an alternative scheme as a scheme without already sharing enough with it. The seed answers this through P4 (overlap as origin, equal standing in potential, no isolated language games). Folded into the Putnam treatment, where it sits closest.

## Structure of the Build-Up

The site has a positioning section (`docs/positioning/`) with three buckets — close affinity, wider landscape, on the fence. The workflow is established. Each thinker brought in gets:

- A **conversational positioning post** in the blog, non-SPLectrum voice, presenting the thinker on their own strength. Contrasts allowed; equal-standing voice intact.
- A **positioning page** in the ref lib capturing the structural takeaway.

The SPLectrum-voice posts (seed series) then draw what resonates into the philosophy, leaning on the positioning pieces rather than reintroducing the thinkers from scratch.

## The Posts

**1 stall-setting post** (SPLectrum voice, seed series): *From Seed to Interrelational Pluralism* — directly extending the August anti-foundationalism preamble. Names the position: interrelational pluralism. Sets out the movement: what the seed makes possible as a philosophical stance. Sketches what follows across the branches: metaphysics (relational, not substance-based), ontology (beings arise through differentiation, no fixed inventory), epistemology (knowledge as convergence, shared subjective objectivity), ethics (values inside language games, not meta-binding). Signals the anthropocentric starting point. The three load-bearing claims set out plainly; the relativism worry flagged and the apparatus for answering it (P4 + P5) named.

## Phase 2: January 2027

The 2026 buildup builds interrelational pluralism from the human case — because that's where we are, and where the positioning material is richest. The 2027 phase changes perspective: not "human looking out" but life looking at itself, with humans in it.

This is not scope expansion (adding non-human cases to a human-centred position). It is a perspective shift: human as one participant in a broader relational picture, not the reference point. The process-relational signposts (Whitehead, Peirce, Bateson) are thinkers who already had this perspective — they never centred the human in the first place.

The delicacy: we are always human-biased. We cannot step outside our own form of life. The 2027 phase works within that constraint — acknowledging the bias while refusing to mistake it for a boundary.
