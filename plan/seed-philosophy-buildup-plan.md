# Building Out Interrelational Pluralism — Plan

SPLectrum has landed on its position: interrelational pluralism. Named May 20, 2026. The seed unpacking is complete. What follows is post-seed — building out the position across disciplines, mapping its neighbourhood, and letting the blinkers come off. Seed-specific contributions continue, but the site is no longer building toward a naming moment. It is building from one.

## What changes post-seed

The seed series was constrained: what can be said using only six principles. Post-seed, the constraint relaxes. The position is established; now it meets the world.

- **Subject freedom.** Posts are no longer sequenced toward naming the position. Any topic that interrelational pluralism has something to say about is fair game — philosophy, science, politics, engineering, art, everyday life.
- **Positioning research.** The neighbourhood mapping (thinker-by-thinker posts and person pages) was tentatively scheduled Sep–Dec but can start whenever the material is ready. The position exists; the neighbourhood work strengthens it, it doesn't create it.
- **Historical posts.** Posts republished after interrelational pluralism was named need editorial notes placing them in context — they were written before the position landed.
- **New site areas.** Real Life and Tools are signposted on the homepage. These are post-seed areas — they build on the position rather than unpacking it.

## Neighbourhood mapping

Each thinker gets a conversational positioning post (non-SPLectrum voice) and a person page in the ref lib. Clustered by affinity:

**Pragmatist cluster:** James, Dewey, Wittgenstein, Rorty, Putnam (+Davidson)

**Tradition and engagement:** MacIntyre, Gadamer

**Value and political pluralism:** Berlin, Arendt

**Philosophy of science:** Kuhn

**Liberal / conservative — decentralisation and practical knowledge:** Hayek, Polanyi, Oakeshott

**Symbolic forms:** Cassirer

**Process-relational signposts (broader-than-human):** Whitehead, Peirce, Bateson + biosemiotics

**Known objection:** Davidson's scheme-content argument. The seed answers through P4 (overlap as origin, no isolated language games). Folded into the Putnam treatment.

Full thinker list with candidates: `plan/seed-buildup-thinkers.md`.

## Phase 2: January 2027

The 2026 work builds interrelational pluralism from the human case. The 2027 phase changes perspective: not "human looking out" but life looking at itself, with humans in it. Not scope expansion — a perspective shift. The process-relational signposts (Whitehead, Peirce, Bateson) are thinkers who already had this perspective.

The delicacy: we are always human-biased. We cannot step outside our own form of life. The 2027 phase works within that constraint — acknowledging the bias while refusing to mistake it for a boundary.

## Out of scope (for 2026)

- The perspective shift itself — signposted, not built
- Ethics, aesthetics, politics as developed branches
- Philosophy of mind / consciousness as its own branch
- Deeper formal correspondence (category theory beyond the First Encounter post)
- Engineering completion — drafts parked

Touchstone, not comprehensive. The build-out reaches a coherent landing, then leaves the rest as open territory.
