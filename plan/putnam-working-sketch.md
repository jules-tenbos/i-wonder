# Working Sketch: Putnam for SPLectrum

Working document. Provisional throughout — terminology, placements, and open questions all subject to revision. Captures a discussion that began inside the pluralism work and found that Putnam's relevance runs wider than pluralism. Draws on the process-theory-of-language and aliveness sketches and uses the same vocabulary.

## What this is

Putnam entered as a pluralism resonance — internal realism and conceptual relativity sitting close to stratified co-arising realities. The discussion pushed past that. What came out is that Putnam is not one convergence but a small constellation of them, several load-bearing beyond pluralism, and that the one place he strains — the meta-move — is exactly the fork the seed already resolved by taking Rorty's side. This sketch records the headlines the conversation reached and maps the strands, the cast, and candidate placements. It is a planning input, not a finished reading.

The through-line: **Putnam took the right steps and kept one wish.** The steps are the ones the seed also takes. The wish is the residue of a frame he had correctly abandoned.

## The two Putnams, and which converges

Internal-realism Putnam (roughly 1978–1990) is the convergence: conceptual relativity, "how many objects?" having no answer prior to a scheme (the Carnap / Polish-logician count — three individuals or seven, both right relative to what counts as an object), the God's-eye view refused, truth as idealised rational acceptability. This maps almost too cleanly onto plural stratified realities with no privileged *THE* reality.

But it is the phase Putnam *walked out of*. He came to read internal realism as crypto-verificationist — truth-as-idealised-acceptability smuggled too much idealism back in — and moved to a plainer natural realism (James, the later Wittgenstein, direct perception, the "threefold cord" of mind, body, world). So the phase that resonates loudest is the one he disowned, and the phase he landed on may sit *closer* to the seed's anti-representationalism than the famous one does (see strands, below).

The fork worth holding: Putnam spent two decades arguing that the Rortian move — one vocabulary among many, no fact of the matter about getting-it-right — is a mistake ("realism with a human face" was his name for the middle path he wanted). The seed takes its meta-stance straight from Rorty. So this is convergence on first-order pluralism and a *real fight* on the meta-move — ring-shaped below, fence-shaped above.

## The reconstruction: mind-independence emergent from convergence

The move that dissolves Putnam's instability (Herman's, in the discussion, not Putnam's): run the justification bottom-up instead of top-down.

Putnam ran it top-down — assert mind-independence *within* a scheme, then try to recover objectivity after dropping the ready-made structure. It wobbled because he wanted truth to be *more than* ideal-us while being *built from* us: realism-shaped from below, idealism-shaped from above.

Bottom-up: mind-independence *is* the convergence. It is what enough subjects converging *produce*, not a property that survives the convergence and gets pointed back at. The apparent objectivity — the coffee cup being there whether we like it or not — is exactly what a sufficiently stabilised convergence feels like *from inside it*. The seed already says this of *Reality*: not *THE* reality, a widely-shared layer whose objectivity-appearance scales with how many subjects converge in it.

On the seed's terms this *dissolves* Putnam's problem rather than inheriting it, because the seed never tries to keep truth answerable to a ready-made world. There is no bill to pay for "truth outrunning ideal inquiry," because the seed does not want truth to outrun anything behind the convergence.

The ordering correction that came with it: "the shared is downstream of deviation" (from memory) is too flat — it grabs one arc of a loop. The subject *receives* shared tools already-there (reception is prior); *deviates within and against* them in privacy (deviation is downstream of reception); some deviation is *pushed back up* and may enter the shared tools (the shared is downstream of deviation *at that arc*). Both orderings hold; they are segments of one circulation. The feedback-sharing itself is part of the emergent — the coupling is constitutive, not added on. Image: a water molecule is part of water and contributes to properties (wetness) no single molecule holds or perceives; and the relational envelope the molecule is *subjected to* (bulk coordination, hydrogen bonding) comes in main part *from the water* — that received envelope is the equivalent of the shared language tools.

## The fail-against problem, and where it lands

The hard question the emergence reading has to answer: on a bottom-up picture, what is a single subject *wrong against*?

- Not the convergence (the majority): that collapses Galileo into the crank — both are out of step with the standing scheme — and it fights the deviation-first commitment, making every creative deviation an error against the sediment.
- Not a ready-made world: refused throughout.

Where it lands: the molecule was never bare. It is *relationally subjected*, and the relations come in main part from the water. So the fail-against is **tension with the relational envelope the subject is actually inside** — the operative shared language sets, coupling this subject to the others *right now*. Being wrong = acting against the relations you are genuinely subjected to. Not a tribunal, not a headcount, not a world behind the tools.

Galileo and the crank then come apart cleanly. Both deviate from the *stated* convergence. Galileo's deviation *tracks a real relational tension* — relations in the field the standing tools have mis-sedimented (the phenomena, the couplings that are there and badly modelled). The crank's answers to nothing operative. Neither is judged by the majority; they are distinguished by whether the deviation tracks a relational tension that is actually there. The correcting field must therefore be *richer than the tools as currently sedimented* — the shared tools **plus** the not-yet-articulated relational field they are an imperfect sedimentation of. The open seam left here: where that not-yet-articulated tension *lives*, such that it can correct the tools without being a ready-made world. (The decoherence rhyme, next, proposes an answer.)

## Belonging as einselection — the decoherence rhyme

Structural rhyme, not importation. Held under the *relational (RQM)* reading of decoherence only; the Everettian gloss is a trojan horse (see caveat).

The rhyme: a system in isolation holds a superposition — a distribution of live outcomes, none actual. Decoherence is what happens when it *couples to an environment*: phases scramble, interference washes out, the system settles into a stable set of states — the *pointer basis* — and crucially the basis is **not chosen by the system**. It is *einselected* — environment-induced selection of which states survive contact.

Map system → subject, environment → the shared, coupling → the feedback-sharing:

- **Coherent regime (private).** The subject holds its own relational field — internal phase-relations intact, tunings live and superposed, self-consistent, self-held. Real relational structure, *not yet toned by the group*. This is where deviation lives. The einselection apparatus is silent here (it describes what coupling does), so the coherent regime *needs separate investigation* — it is the correct boundary of what the rhyme covers, not a gap.
- **Decoherent regime (belonging).** Coupling to the group scrambles the free private phases and einselects a pointer basis — the shared concept scheme — *across* the group. Belonging *is* the einselection. The shared tools *are* the pointer basis. It is common across the group because decoherence is *mutual*: each subject is environment for the others, so the einselected basis is the shared one. That is the mechanism for "stabilises the concept scheme across the group," and it is a result, not a restatement.

Three things this delivers that the water image could not: reception-arc and contribution-arc in *one* mechanism (the environment selects the basis *and* the subject is environment for every other subject); graded belonging *natively* (decoherence is a rate, not a switch — resonance-strength as coupling-strength, fuzzy membership falling straight out); and a home for the correcting field — the environment always *exceeds* the pointer basis it induces, so the "not-yet-articulated relational tension" is just the environmental degrees of freedom the current basis has not resolved. No ready-made world; more coupling than the sediment has stabilised.

**The A = B collapse (analyse "environment" carefully).** The textbook story casts environment as an external, effectively-infinite *bath* — which quietly imports the totality the seed refuses. Drop the bath: the environment *of* a subject is *other subjects* — other coherent fields, each self-held. Then "coupling to the environment" and "the group's relations toning the subject" were never two things. Every field is coherent-from-within and environment-for-others *simultaneously* — which side is "system" and which "environment" is the perspectival asymmetry over a symmetric cut. **This is P0 exactly.** The anti-totality commitment does the collapsing: keep the bath and the readings come apart; kill the bath and they *must* fuse, because no environment is left that is not itself a coherent subject-field seen from outside.

Consequences that fall out of the collapse:
- The fail-against relocates to its final place: answerability to *the other coherent fields you are actually coupled to* — real, mind-independent-relative-to-you, structured, but themselves subjects, not a world behind subjects. Galileo's tuning einselects under coupling to the phenomena-as-coherent-fields; the crank's couples to nothing that holds. Nothing non-linguistic slips in: everything that can be an environment is itself relational, distinctions-holding, language-in-the-broad-sense.
- **Stability without finality.** With no infinite bath, decoherence loses its standard irreversibility (leak into a reservoir). Phase information is held in *other finite coherent fields*, so recoherence is possible in principle. The shared scheme is stable-*in-fact*, open-*in-principle* — no convergence permanently einselected, every convergence reversible under re-coupling. That *is* the deviation-can-always-re-enter commitment, now derived from there being no totality to make decoherence final.
- **Equal standing, resolved.** The coherent/decoherent cut *is* the potential/actual cut. Equal in coherence (all tunings live, superposed, none privileged — P4 as *potential*); selected in decoherence (the group-coupling stabilises *these* as the shared scheme — *fact*, not principle). The pointer basis is not a standing privilege; it is whatever the current mutual coupling happens to einselect.

**Fence caveat (load-bearing).** The rhyme is the seed's friend *only* under the relational reading — outcomes relative to interactions, no observer-independent collapse. Under the many-worlds gloss decoherence usually travels with, "settling into one basis" is just the view from inside one branch of a still-total wavefunction — which smuggles back the universal wavefunction, i.e. a *THE*-reality, the super-subject the aliveness sketch spent a whole pass refusing. Same math, opposite metaphysics. The A=B collapse actually makes the Everettian reading *unavailable* rather than merely refused: with environment = other subjects and no totality, there is no universal wavefunction to be in a branch *of*. This is fence material — worth stating as the diagnosis, not walked as a competitor.

## The inversion: philosophy and QM, two doors to one nostalgia

The punchline that merges the two threads. Philosophy and science both trip on relative reference frames — and the subject is one such frame — but they trip from *opposite directions*.

- **Philosophy's headache: can't get rid of the privileged frame it doesn't want.** It reads objectivity as frame-independence (the view from nowhere), then cannot find a frame-independent place to stand. The subject keeps showing up as the frame it can't get behind. Putnam's whole struggle is this in the philosophy of language: metaphysical realism *is* the demand for the frame-independent description; internal realism was the attempt to drop the privileged frame while keeping objectivity; the wobble was the residue of still wanting the frame he had correctly abandoned.
- **QM's headache: can't avoid the privileged frame it doesn't want.** Physics made a *virtue* of frame-relativity — Galilean, special, general — the whole triumph is expelling the preferred standpoint. Then measurement walks in and *reintroduces* one precisely at the observer: the single place where the superposition resolves. The field that abolished the privileged frame everywhere else suddenly needs one, at the subject.

Same nostalgia for a view from nowhere, entered by the two doors the two disciplines happened to arrive by. And **RQM is the same cure for both**: make every outcome relative to the interaction, and the privileged observer dissolves into one frame among frames — real *relative to* a coupling, no view from outside. Putnam's dissolution and QM's dissolution are the *identical move*. Conceptual scheme *is* reference frame; "true within a scheme" *is* "outcome relative to an interaction"; Putnam's one unpaid bill (what outruns ideal inquiry?) is exactly the bill RQM tells you not to pay. **Putnam is doing RQM in the philosophy of language and doesn't quite know it.** The seed sits where RQM sits — subject-as-frame, objectivity as cross-frame convergence, no privileged standpoint and none required — and from there both panics read as the same nostalgia, from opposite sides.

## Putnam beyond pluralism — the strands

Pluralism was the door. Behind it, several strands, each placing differently.

- **Conceptual relativity / internal realism.** The pluralism convergence and its meta-fork. First-order → resonance ring; the meta-move (idealised rational acceptability vs. Rortian minimalism) → fence-flavoured. This is the strand that *dissolves* into the emergence-from-convergence reading above.
- **The model-theoretic argument.** Putnam's case that on metaphysical realism reference is radically indeterminate — theories have unintended models, so nothing fixes word-to-world reference from *outside* language. This is anti-representationalist *tooling*: a sharp argument that you cannot fix reference from a standpoint outside language, which is the seed's "no language-free layer" reached by a different route. Candidate for the two-pronged anti-representationalism neighbourhood, or a tools-side entry.
- **Semantic externalism / division of linguistic labour.** "Meanings ain't in the head" (Twin Earth); extension fixed partly by the world plus a *social* division of labour (we defer to experts). This reads two ways and is worth holding as genuine ambivalence. *Friction* with "meaning is subject-tied" if the external world's role is read as reference-fixing from outside. *Resonance* if read as: meaning is in the relation not the entity, and the division of linguistic labour is coupled-cauldrons / shared language sets under another name. Strong **fence candidate** — the ambivalence is the interesting object, not a defect.
- **Fact/value entanglement.** Late Putnam (the collapse of the fact/value dichotomy): thick ethical concepts entangle descriptive and evaluative inseparably; the dichotomy is untenable. Bears on the ethics / values strand — note the cut is *different* from the seed's values-on-the-reality-side separation (that separates values from the *structural* side, not fact from value), so this needs care rather than assimilation. Possible input to the ethics-of-language-games work (values scoped to the game; graded belonging).
- **Natural realism / the threefold cord.** Where Putnam *landed* — direct perception, mind-body-world as one cord, James and the later Wittgenstein. Arguably *closer* to the seed's anti-representationalism and to the enactive neighbourhood (Varela, Merleau-Ponty, autopoiesis, already on-site) than internal realism ever was. The irony worth surfacing: the famous Putnam converges on pluralism-with-a-wobble; the *late* Putnam converges on anti-representationalism-without-the-wobble, and is less cited for it.

## Cast

Almost the entire cast is already built — this is a *linking* opportunity, not a research gap. On-site (per the site sitemap), grouped by thread:

**Conceptual-relativity / reference-frame thread:**
- **Goodman** — *Ways of Worldmaking*, "true-in-a-version." The cleaner statement of emergence-from-convergence than Putnam, because he never strains for the residual answerability Putnam couldn't drop. The sharpest *contrast*: Putnam took the steps and kept one wish; Goodman took the steps and kept nothing. Worth putting side by side.
- **Quine** — ontological relativity, inscrutability of reference. Upstream of both; Putnam's conceptual relativity is Quine's with the naturalism filed off.
- **Carnap** — internal vs. external questions, framework-relativity of existence-questions. The *ur*-text for "how many objects?" having no answer outside a scheme. Under-used and genuinely load-bearing.
- **Kuhn** — paradigm as reference frame, incommensurability. Owns the Galileo/crank problem — what distinguishes the revolutionary from the crank when both are out of step. Felt the "convergence can't be the tribunal" bite hardest and got called a relativist for it, which is the charge the emergence-answer is built to escape.
- **Rorty** — already the seed's meta-source; stands right behind any Putnam piece, at the fork.

**QM-inversion thread:**
- **Rovelli** (RQM), **Bohr** (complementarity, observer-in-the-description — the original of the privileged-frame-reintroduced problem). RQM subject page and RQM blog post already exist.
- **Everett / DeWitt** — the many-worlds gloss, held as *fence* (the position the seed blocks), not adopted.

**Late-Putnam / natural-realism neighbourhood (already on-site):** James, Dewey, Wittgenstein, Sellars, and the enactive cluster (Varela, Merleau-Ponty, autopoiesis).

**The one genuine gap — new-page candidate:**
- **Bas van Fraassen** — constructive empiricism, "empirical adequacy" instead of truth, the explicit refusal to be answerable to the unobservable structure behind the phenomena. The sharpest statement of "stop paying the bill Putnam kept paying," and a missing seat at the QM-interpretation / scientific-realism seam. If any new person page comes out of this, he is first candidate. (Whether he earns a page turns on whether a close-affinity ring piece references him — per the person-page trigger.)

## Open questions

- **The coherent regime.** Einselection is silent on the *private* field. Its content — where a subject's deviations come from, why one subject superposes tunings another never reaches — needs its own account. Candidate: the coherent field *is* historicity — the frozen sediment of this subject's *past* decoherences, held as its private basis, so coherence and decoherence are two *tenses* (coupling-now vs. sediment-of-coupling-before) rather than two populations. Left open: whether privacy is *only* history, or *also* a source of private structure that is not frozen prior coupling.
- **The externalism ambivalence.** Does the division of linguistic labour read as reference-fixing-from-outside (friction with meaning-subject-tied) or as coupled-cauldrons (resonance)? This decides whether the strand is fence or ring, and it may genuinely be fence *because* it is ambivalent.
- **Fact/value vs. the values-side separation.** Putnam's entanglement thesis and the seed's values-on-the-reality-side are *different cuts*. Whether they inform each other or merely look alike wants working out before either is placed near the ethics work.
- **Preferred basis and P4.** The potential/actual reading resolves the tension for now (equal in coherence, selected in decoherence). Worth stress-testing against the ethics graded-belonging account, where "which game" and "which basis" may be the same question.
- **Whether van Fraassen earns a page.** Turns on whether a close-affinity piece actually references him as load-bearing, not on his intrinsic interest.
