# Blog post storyline — the acquisition family (working title TBD)

Post built from the seed affinity page `/positioning/seed/sharing-knowledge/human-ways-of-sharing/`. Blog register, looser and more personal than the page. Not tracked in `scheduled-tasks.md` (blog pipeline is user-managed); this doc is the storyline workspace only.

## Spine

**The acquisition family is the subject of the post.** The developmental / language-acquisition mechanisms — internalisation, usage-based learning, construction, imitation, scaffolding, coupling — share one picture of the subject: **active, engaged, building from the reality it is coming into.** Neither conditioned from outside nor driven from below: a subject doing something itself with what it meets.

**Behaviourism and psychoanalysis are pulled in only for what they lack** — the negative space that throws the family's picture into relief. They are foil, not co-equal thirds. The post does not survey three schools; it walks one family and uses two others to show what the family has that they don't.

- **Behaviourism lacks the inside.** The subject as automaton — action/reaction, stimulus and conditioned response, the inside a black box declared off-limits (Watson, Skinner; the S–R scheme Vygotsky attacked by name). The active subject the family describes *is exactly the box behaviourism refused to open*. Its whole content is the middle term behaviourism threw out.
- **Psychoanalysis lacks the single reality.** The subject driven from a detached internal realm — drives, fantasy, buried history, the inner world privileged over the present one and largely captive to it. It splits reality into internal and external and retreats inward. The family stays engaged with the one world it is coming into.

## The one SPLectrum beat (light — near the psychoanalysis turn)

SPLectrum has no quarrel with drives, fantasy, the inner life — it does not deny them the way behaviourism does. It places them **plainly within the subject's one reality**, rather than in a detached inner realm split off from the outer. Not "there is no inside," but "the inside is not a separate reality." One placing move, stated once. No seed-principle derivation, no P-number walk. The affinity is carried by the post being a positioning piece, not by argument.

## Shape (draft order — to be worked)

1. **Open on the family, on its own terms.** The subject as active constructor / participant — the common thread across the six mechanisms. Concrete, warm, a child coming into language: extracting patterns, internalising, building, coupling. Establish the picture before naming the foil.
2. **Name the picture: the engaged subject.** What unites the six — not a theory to choose among but facets of one stance. (Link the affinity page here for the structural overview.)
3. **The first thing it isn't: the automaton.** Behaviourism. The empty box, action/reaction. The family's inside *is* the box behaviourism refused. Short — it's a foil.
4. **The second thing it isn't: the split reality.** Psychoanalysis. Driven from a detached inner realm; reality split in two, the inner privileged. → the one SPLectrum beat: drives are real, but they belong to the subject's one reality, not a separate inner one.
5. **Close on the family again.** The engaged subject, now seen against both — not conditioned from outside, not captive to the depths, but building from and coordinating with the reality it meets. Land here; the foils have done their work and recede.

## Discipline / cautions

- **Family is the subject throughout; schools are foil.** If a draft spends equal wordcount on all three, the spine has slipped. Behaviourism and psychoanalysis sections stay short and pointed — what they lack, then back to the family.
- **Draw the contrast at the level of the *picture of the subject*** (active-constructor vs. conditioned-automaton vs. driven-from-a-split-reality), NOT total opposition on every axis. Piaget and later behaviourism/information-processing blur at the mechanism level; the clean contrast is the stance toward the subject, not the fine detail.
- **Equal-standing respect for the schools.** The person/subject pages (behaviourism, psychoanalysis, Pavlov/Watson/Skinner, Freud/Jung) treat both honourably. The post characterises the *picture* each carries; it does not dunk on the schools or call them bad science. "What they lack" is a structural observation, not a verdict.
- **Avoid the falsifiability charge as the point.** Psychoanalysis-as-untestable-theory is a different, cheaper essay. The point is the *split reality* picture of the subject, not the theory's epistemic standing. A glancing line at most.
- **SPLectrum stays to the one beat.** No seed vocabulary, no principle derivation. One placing move near the psychoanalysis turn, then out.
- **Post/page non-contradiction.** The page is the neutral structural overview (six mechanisms, two groups); the post is the looser walk with the picture-of-the-subject thesis. Same material, two registers, must not contradict (per `tone-of-voice/post-vs-ref-lib.md`).
