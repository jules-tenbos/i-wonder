# Post A — the acquisition family

**Subject: the family, alone.** The eight mechanisms on the page — internalisation, usage-based learning, statistical learning, construction, imitation, natural pedagogy, scaffolding, coupling — share one picture of the subject: **active, engaged, building from the reality it is coming into.** Even the barest of them makes the point: statistical learning is the subject *extracting* structure from the stream, not being stamped by it.

**No foils.** They belong to Post B. Post A establishes the picture on its own terms; at most a closing gesture that there are other pictures, and they're next.

# Post B — the wider field

## Spine

**SPLectrum finds more value in the acquisition mechanisms, because the other streams took up problematic stances — and then spent their effort keeping the boat afloat rather than contributing insight.**

The charge is about **research posture, not validity.** Not "bad science," not "wrong" — a stance that commits you to defending the stance, so the work goes into shoring up the position instead of into insight. The acquisition family, by contrast, isn't defending a stance: it's a set of findings that accumulated and cohere without anyone having to shore them up. *That* is what "more value" cashes out to — where the insight-per-effort went.

**The three streams and the stance each took:**

- **Behaviourism — no inside.** The subject as automaton; action/reaction, stimulus and conditioned response, the inside a black box declared off-limits (Watson, Skinner; the S–R scheme Vygotsky attacked by name). The active subject the family describes *is exactly the box behaviourism refused to open*.
- **Psychoanalysis — a split inside.** The subject driven from a detached internal realm: drives, fantasy, buried history, the inner world privileged over the present one and largely captive to it. Reality split in two; the family stays engaged with the one world it is coming into.
- **Nativism — a pre-filled inside.** The structure arrives installed; exposure triggers it and sets the switches. The child doesn't build the grammar, it *finds* it.

**The three are NOT parallel — build the asymmetry in, don't smooth it.** Behaviourism and psychoanalysis are historical stances with settled receptions: brisk, pointed, then out. **Nativism is live, defended, and the interesting one** — it gets the substantial engagement. This asymmetry is also what makes one post viable where three foils at equal weight would have been a survey.

## The nativism section — how the boat-keeping charge is made

**Made entirely out of things nativists said.** This is the discipline that keeps the charge compatible with the honourable person page: report the field's own record and the pattern makes itself. No adjudication, no verdict on the science. Everything below is sourced in `plan/chomsky-nativism-dossier.md`.

The record, as the field states it:

- **The theory shrank for sixty years** — rich innate rule schemas (*Aspects*, 1965) → principles + binary parameters (P&P, 1981) → in the limit, **Merge alone** (Minimalism). The field says openly the driver was **evolvability**, not new acquisition evidence: UG must be rich enough for poverty-of-the-stimulus but restricted enough to have evolved. A research program reshaped by what it had to survive. (Dossier §3, §5.)
- **The atoms are conceded a mystery.** Berwick & Chomsky: the origin of human-specific concepts and "the atoms of computation" that Merge uses "remains for us a mystery." Merge combines objects it cannot supply. (§4, §5.1.)
- **Then the lexicon.** Chomsky holds concepts are *triggered*, not learned — explicitly including *carburetor* and *bureaucrat*; only the sound is acquired. Fodor takes it to the limit (*car* is innate) and the field's own summary is that he went too far — but nobody exits the position that forced it. Putnam's astonishment and Dennett's Aristotle-and-the-airplane are the recorded reactions. (§4.)
- **And the tension is held, not resolved:** Minimalism shrinks the endowment for evolvability's sake while concept nativism enlarges it enormously. Chomsky runs both. (§4.)

Each move is locally reasonable; the **pattern** is the point. The effort goes into protecting the commitment.

**What the boat is.** Worth naming lightly, because it explains why the moves were forced: the commitment is **domain-specificity** — that what does the work is dedicated to *language*, meaning utterance-language. Everything awkward follows from there. The atoms must be innate because no general mechanism is available to make them; Fodor needs a whole second private language (Mentalese) because thought plainly happens without words and the theory has nowhere else to put it. (§1, §4b.)

## The decoding touch (light — two sentences, not structure)

Nativism's own best argument is about **decoding**: streams underdetermine, so resolving them needs constraint. That part is right, and it's older and wider than linguistics (Helmholtz; the same argument runs in vision). The trouble starts where constraints-on-decoding get turned into **contents** — a stock of concepts rather than a way of making sense of a stream.

**Discipline: this stays a touch.** The full separation of decoding from relational language is load-bearing structure — it needs the routes-to-the-relational picture, which needs the general/reduced language distinction, which is seed territory the post would be *introducing* rather than referencing. That's the same failure mode that split the post in the first place: a good idea crowding the spine. **The fuller treatment wants a seed-side page to point at — not yet scoped, deliberately** (the decoding strand was closed 2026-07-17; see dossier §9). If the touch can't be carried in two sentences, cut it.

The pivot in: **Chomsky himself slew the first foil** (the 1959 *Verbal Behavior* review) — the man who opened behaviourism's empty box held that what's inside was already there. Then the record: the sixty-year shrinkage, the mystery atoms, the carburetor. The decoding touch lands here.

## The one SPLectrum beat (light — near the psychoanalysis turn)

SPLectrum has no quarrel with drives, fantasy, the inner life — it does not deny them the way behaviourism does. It places them **plainly within the subject's one reality**, rather than in a detached inner realm split off from the outer. Not "there is no inside," but "the inside is not a separate reality." One placing move, stated once. No seed-principle derivation, no P-number walk.

## Discipline / cautions

- **The charge is posture, not validity.** "Problematic stance → effort spent keeping the boat afloat" is the claim. Never "bad science," never a verdict on nativism's truth. Let the field's own record carry it.
- **Nativism is not one position.** Chomsky's saltationism and Pinker & Bloom's gradual adaptationism are *both nativist* and disagree fundamentally; Fodor and Spelke invert each other on concepts. "Nativism says X" is usually false — say which nativism. (Dossier §5, §4b.)
- **The live dispute is domain-specificity, not innateness.** Everyone grants some innate structure — Tomasello included. Don't let the post imply nativists are the only ones with an endowment; that's a straw man and the dossier kills it in §1.
- **Non-contradiction with `persons/c/chomsky.md`** — that page presents poverty-of-the-stimulus honourably and records the usage-based and Everett challenges as *contested*. The post must not read as settling them.
- **Equal-standing for all three schools.** The person/subject pages (behaviourism, psychoanalysis, Pavlov/Watson/Skinner, Freud/Jung, Chomsky) treat them honourably. The post characterises the *stance* each took; it does not dunk.
- **Draw the contrast at the level of the picture of the subject** — active-constructor vs. conditioned-automaton vs. split-realm vs. pre-filled — NOT total opposition on every axis. Piaget and later behaviourism/information-processing blur at the mechanism level; the clean contrast is the stance.
- **Avoid the falsifiability charge** (applies to psychoanalysis *and* to nativism's saltationism — "unfalsifiable single mutation" is a cheaper essay than the one worth writing). The point is the picture and the posture, not epistemic standing.
- **Post/page non-contradiction** — the affinity page is the neutral structural overview (eight mechanisms, two groups); the posts carry the readings. Per `tone-of-voice/post-vs-ref-lib.md`.
- **No affinity for nativism.** Decided 2026-07-17. It is documented territory, not company the seed keeps. The post carries a reading; nothing routes it into a resonance register.
