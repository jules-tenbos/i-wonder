# Building the AI Information Field — Plan & Research Approach

Working document. Provisional throughout — sequencing, cuts, source choices, and research scope all subject to revision. Frontier material grounded as of June 2026 and dates fast; treat the "current high interest" anchor as a moving target, not a fixed point.

## What this plans

A long-running build of AI as an information field across splectrum.world, coupled with a neuroscience strand. The shape follows the site's own architecture: a subject hive held as outside context, then SPLectrum-side reading in the resonance rings and on the fence, then specific angles lifted into language, vocabulary, reality, blog, and real life. The build starts from current high-interest aspects (world models, JEPA, VLA / World Action Models, the LLM-vs-world-model question) and spiders outward to the field's recent history, then its deep foundations.

## Orienting finding from the research

The contemporary field has already converged on the pluralist reading. The 2026 framing is that LLMs and world models do different jobs — language for understanding and goals, world models for physical prediction and planning — and the live direction is hybrid, not replacement; the embodied line has named the union (World Action Models). That leaves LeCun's pure-replacement position as the outlier: he left Meta and raised around $1bn for a JEPA-only bet against LLMs.

Consequence for the build: the field's own current state embodies the seed's pluralism, and the monist replacement bet sits in the open as the contested move. The first fence piece is anchored in live primary material (LeCun's position papers and interviews), not historical reconstruction.

## Design principles

**Two orderings, kept apart.** The spider is a *build sequence* — start at the high-interest node, expand outward by relatedness over time. The resonance rings are a *placement* ordering — relevance to the seed. They are orthogonal. World models is the build-centre but places at the fence; the symbolic/connectionist war comes late in the build but may sit in close affinity. The spider's outward crawl must not be read as a relevance ladder.

**Territory before seed-reading.** Subject and person pages — outside context, on their own terms, no SPLectrum vocabulary, conflicts intact — are the substrate. Ring pieces, fence pages, and cross-bleed all delegate from them through links. Build the ground, then the reading sits on it.

**Input, not spine.** Sources, frames, and the brain are all inputs. Explainer channels (Welch Labs, 3Blue1Brown) build intuition; LeCun's world-model frame is a position to diagnose; neuroscience is a coupled partner. None becomes the organising arc.

**The curation trap.** AI is unusually prone to tidier-than-true narratives — the progress ladder to AGI, the replacement war, the boosterish arc. Research must actively counter-source for the conflicts (symbolic vs connectionist, the winters, the definitional disputes, the current scaling-vs-critics schism), or the territory pages drift into the coloured version.

**Centre of gravity sits seed-side.** AI is not a subject like the others — it occupies the seed's home turf (language, cognition, reality-making). For most subjects the territory page is the bulk and the ring reading a thin overlay; for AI the ring + fence + reality + HAICC load may outweigh the territory page. Plan resourcing accordingly. The discipline still holds: the territory stays clean.

**Neuroscience alignment.** Run a coupled neuroscience strand alongside the AI strand, and borrow its multi-level, no-single-cause method (Sapolsky's human-behavioural-biology, already on-site; Marr's levels). Hold the brain as one region on the aliveness–agency gradient, not the reference point AI approximates — the same input-not-spine discipline turned on the brain. The payoff: the representation-vs-relation fence runs through both fields at once. LeCun's "the world model represents the world" is the colouring of cognitivism's "the brain represents the world"; enactivism is the seed's neuroscience ally, and Varela, Maturana, Merleau-Ponty, autopoiesis, and phenomenology are already built. The world-models fence twins with a cognitivism-vs-enactivism fence — one diagnosis, two domains, half the persons in place.

### Long — the full field and the deep foundations

- The complete historical spine: Dartmouth 1956, the symbolic era, the perceptron and the first winter (Minsky–Papert 1969), expert systems and the second winter, the cybernetic and cognitive-science overlaps, the connectionist revival, the long symbolic/connectionist war, the philosophical critiques (Dreyfus, Searle's Chinese Room, the Turing-test arguments, the AI-effect).

Neuroscience strand:
- The neuroscience history and its own conflicts: localizationism vs distributed processing; the cognitivism vs enactivism/embodied-cognition war (the second front of the representation-vs-relation fence); the consciousness debates.
- The multi-level method made explicit (Sapolsky, Marr) as a structuring template.
- The biological end of the aliveness gradient and the substrate layer of the process-theory sketch (neural code, hormonal and intracellular signalling) — where the language/linguistic cut shades down into biology.

## The type of research we need

### By surface — what each page-type demands

- **Subject and person pages (territory).** Full-breadth, on-their-own-terms research. Reference-grade sources (SEP, Nilsson's *The Quest for AI*, Russell & Norvig, primary papers, the field's own histories), read for the whole subject including its internal conflicts, contested receptions, and mutations. Source-authority noted — which source carries which weight; source-level uncertainties surfaced as content, not as procedural checks. The threshold test applied as names enter: internal link (page exists or is committed) vs external citation.

- **Resonance-ring pieces.** Less fresh external research, more structural reading of territory already built. The research is into the angle, and into where each thinker reaches and stops. Discipline: "resonance with" is a pointer; "converges with" is a claim — the piece reads structurally without claiming the thinkers for the project.

- **Fence pages.** Research the colouring precisely — the narrative-of-pathway distinguished from the observation. Needs the framework's own vocabulary deeply enough to locate where it puts the pathway. For the world-models fence: LeCun's position papers and interviews (the Welch Labs interview is primary), the world-model literature's own self-description, and the parallel cognitivism material on the neuroscience side.

- **Cross-bleed entries (language / vocabulary / reality / real life).** Aspect-specific research — the language-aspect, vocabulary-aspect, or tool-aspect of the same item, each described for its own surface, not repeated across them.

### Cross-cutting research disciplines

- **Counter-source the curation trap.** Actively seek the conflicts and contested receptions; a source that gives only the clean arc is incomplete for territory work.
- **Primary over aggregator.** Favour original sources — lab papers, researchers' own writing, the AI Index, SEP — over secondary summaries. Explainer channels are intuition inputs, not citable territory.
- **Recency on the frontier.** Current material (world models, reasoning, agents, regulation) dates fast — re-verify against live sources; set `lastmod` honestly; apply the threshold-transition discipline when names cross internal/external.
- **Uncertainty as content.** Where the field itself is unsettled (scaling limits, what counts as understanding, AGI timelines), the page holds the dispute rather than resolving it.

### The neuroscience strand specifically

- **Predictive processing / Bayesian brain** (Friston, Clark, Hohwy) — the build-centre's neuroscience twin; primary-paper and review research, cross-linked to world models.
- **Enactivism / embodied cognition** (Varela, Maturana, Thompson, Merleau-Ponty) — largely recombination of on-site material, coupled to the AI fence rather than built fresh.
- **Network neuroscience** — grounds HAICC's network-level emergence claim.
- **The multi-level method** (Sapolsky, Marr) — researched as method, not only as content.

## Open decisions / first move

The load-bearing first decision is the hub's sub-page cut. The research gives a natural seam: build-centre at world models / WAMs, the paradigm map beside it, the conflicts in a contested-receptions page, persons spidering off.

Still open: whether the genuine first artifact is the **fence piece** (where the energy and the live primary material are) or the **thin hub subject** (the territory the fence formally delegates from). The discipline says territory first; the energy says fence first. Worth deciding deliberately rather than by drift.
