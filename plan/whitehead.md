# Whitehead in SPLectrum — plan

A working plan for deepening SPLectrum's engagement with Whitehead. Captures the interpretive findings from the conversation, the pieces to write, and the architectural decisions about where in the site they sit.

## Findings

Three moves changed during the conversation and now drive the work.

**Parsimony framing dropped.** The existing interrelational-pluralism paragraph on Whitehead frames the relationship as "same dynamic, lower metaphysical cost." That presents SPLectrum as Whitehead-lite. P0 is not a cheaper version of creativity-as-ultimate-category — they are different primitives that happen to generate related phenomena. The contrast is substantive, not parsimony.

**Source of ordered novelty is where the divergence sits.** Whitehead's initial aim does two jobs: it stipulates a structured field of possibilities, and it gives each occasion a normative valence (oriented toward best). The genuine divergence with the seed is not on presence/absence of teleology — it is on where ordered novelty comes from. Whitehead locates it in a separate principle (creativity + God). In the seed, ordered novelty arises from the relational structure of language itself. Two different metaphysical strategies for the same problem.

**The deep kinship sits in the Whitehead/Rorty pairing.** Whitehead dismantles the bifurcation of nature from the side of nature; Rorty dismantles representational thinking from the side of language. Two halves of one move. The two together — relations-as-constitutive on the reality side, language-as-relational on the medium side — are what makes SPLectrum what it is. The pairing is what is distinctive, not the parsimony argument and not the absence of God.

## Task list

### Phase 1 — Site pages

**1. Stengers person page** (positioning/persons/s/)

Isabelle Stengers on her own terms. Ecology of practices, cosmopolitics, *Thinking with Whitehead*. Outside-context voice, no SPLectrum vocabulary. The cosmopolitics work is substantial enough that the reference function is real.

**2. Philosophy of organism subject page** (positioning/subjects/p/)

New subject page for Whitehead's specific system. Covers actual occasions, prehension, concrescence, creativity, eternal objects, God. Person/subject page register applies — outside-context voice, no SPLectrum vocabulary on the page.

The reception section names two lines, not one. Hartshorne and Cobb are the theological-American line — process theology, the Claremont school, the contested God concept and its secular critics. Stengers is the continental-scholarly line — *Thinking with Whitehead* made Whitehead live again as philosophy, not only as theology. Both lines are part of the subject's afterlife and both belong on the page.

The existing process philosophy subject page stays bundled (Hegel, Bergson, Whitehead at the level of process-as-primary-over-substance). It names philosophy of organism as the specific Whiteheadian programme and links to it. The Whitehead person page links to philosophy of organism where the depth on the technical system lives.

**3. Seed-ring piece** (positioning/seed/)

Developed structural treatment of the Whitehead/Rorty pairing. Resonance only — names where each reaches and where each stops. Side-by-side, no claiming the thinkers for the project. The two strands held together because the structural observation runs across the bifurcation/mirror line.

**4. Stengers on close-affinity pluralism page**

Add Stengers to the process-relational cluster on her own merits — ecology of practices, cosmopolitics — not just as a Whitehead reception figure. She belongs alongside Whitehead, Bateson, and the others.

Kinship limit: she has no language-centred move. The process-relational pluralism is resonance, but the kinship stops where Rorty picks up. Deep close-affinity territory, not seed-ring. The Whitehead/Rorty pairing piece does not touch her — she extends Whitehead's nature side without bridging to the language side.

### Phase 2 — Blog posts

**5. Pure Whitehead post** (blog, named-source register)

Angle: importance sits in the move to think nature without splitting it. The bifurcation of nature is the diagnosis; the relational ontology is the cure. Everything else — actual occasions, prehension, creativity, eternal objects, God — falls out of the diagnostic move.

Arc: three careers as a diagnosis-cure structure.

- Cambridge mathematician — sees the limits of pure formalism.
- London philosopher of science — diagnoses the bifurcation: the split is not in nature, it is in our theorising.
- Harvard metaphysician at 63 — builds the cure: a speculative system where the split never opens.

The register absorbs Whitehead's own energy: systematic, audacious, vocabulary-coining, polymathic. Architecturally ambitious, not lyrical. The voice of someone willing to invent a vocabulary because the existing one cannot say what he needs to say.

**6. Whitehead/Rorty pairing post** (blog)

Angle: two halves of the anti-representational move. Whitehead from the side of nature, Rorty from the side of language. The intersection is what is distinctive about SPLectrum.

Lighter biographical Whitehead since the pure post precedes it; moves quickly into the structural argument. Conversational register — the kind of post that says "I keep coming back to these two as one gesture" and walks through why.

### Phase 3 — Revisions

**7. Revise site pages in the light of blog post creation**

Both the seed page and the close-affinity pluralism page need the Whitehead paragraph rewritten. Drop the parsimony framing. Name the actual divergence: different metaphysical strategies for sourcing ordered novelty. Other site pages may need revision once the blog posts have sharpened the interpretive position.

## Architectural decisions

The process philosophy page bundles three thinkers at the level of the movement; philosophy of organism gets its own page for the Whitehead-specific system. This is consistent with how positioning handles a thinker whose work has generated its own tradition: the person page covers life and work; the subject page covers the tradition under its name.

The deep kinship between Whitehead and SPLectrum lives in the seed ring (the Whitehead/Rorty pairing piece), not on the Whitehead person page. The pairing is the load-bearing structural reading and the place where SPLectrum-side language belongs.
