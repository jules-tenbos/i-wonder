# Whitehead in SPLectrum — plan

## Findings

**Source of ordered novelty is where the divergence sits.** Whitehead locates it in a separate principle (creativity + God). In the seed, ordered novelty arises from the relational structure of language itself. Two different metaphysical strategies for the same problem.

**The deep kinship sits in the Whitehead/Rorty pairing.** Two language games reject representationalism — Whitehead from the side of being, Rorty from the side of language. From SPLectrum's vantage, the two are seen as two halves of what P0 names.

**Stengers is load-bearing for the Whitehead reception, not optional.** She returned Whitehead to serious continental philosophical reading. Her cosmopolitics project stands on its own as a contemporary contribution to interrelational pluralism. The kinship runs deep on the relational-pluralism side but stops where Rorty's language-side move begins — she has no language-centred move. Deep close-affinity territory, not seed-ring.

## Blog posts

1. **Pure Whitehead post** (named-source register). Angle: importance sits in the move to think nature without splitting it. The bifurcation of nature is the diagnosis; the relational ontology is the cure.

   Arc: three careers as a diagnosis-cure structure. Cambridge mathematician sees the limits of pure formalism. London philosopher of science diagnoses the bifurcation: the split is not in nature, it is in our theorising. Harvard metaphysician at 63 builds the cure: a speculative system where the split never opens.

   The register absorbs Whitehead's own energy: systematic, audacious, vocabulary-coining, polymathic. Architecturally ambitious, not lyrical.

2. **Whitehead/Rorty pairing post**. Two language games, two refusals, co-implicated through P0. Storyline sketch in `plan/whitehead-notes-pairing-post.md`. Conversational register — the recognition moment, not the structural exposition.

3. **Stengers post** (named-source register). Angle: "thinking with" as a method, not a phrase. Each of her major projects is the same move at different scales — the refusal of the god's-eye view. The voice absorbs her character: patient, polemical when needed, deeply collaborative, suspicious of universalism.
