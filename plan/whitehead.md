# Whitehead in SPLectrum — plan (updated)

A working plan for deepening SPLectrum's engagement with Whitehead and the figures who keep his project alive. Captures the interpretive findings from the conversation, the pieces to write, and the architectural decisions about where in the site they sit.

## Findings

Four moves changed during the conversation and now drive the work.

**Parsimony framing dropped.** The existing interrelational-pluralism paragraph on Whitehead frames the relationship as "same dynamic, lower metaphysical cost." That presents SPLectrum as Whitehead-lite. P0 is not a cheaper version of creativity-as-ultimate-category — they are different primitives that happen to generate related phenomena. The contrast is substantive, not parsimony.

**Source of ordered novelty is where the divergence sits.** Whitehead's initial aim does two jobs: it stipulates a structured field of possibilities, and it gives each occasion a normative valence (oriented toward best). The genuine divergence with the seed is not on presence/absence of teleology — it is on where ordered novelty comes from. Whitehead locates it in a separate principle (creativity + God). In the seed, ordered novelty arises from the relational structure of language itself. Two different metaphysical strategies for the same problem.

**The deep kinship sits in the Whitehead/Rorty pairing.** Whitehead dismantles the bifurcation of nature from the side of nature; Rorty dismantles representational thinking from the side of language. Two halves of one move. The two together — relations-as-constitutive on the reality side, language-as-relational on the medium side — are what makes SPLectrum what it is. The pairing is what is distinctive, not the parsimony argument and not the absence of God.

**Stengers is load-bearing for the Whitehead reception, not optional.** She is the figure who returned Whitehead to serious continental philosophical reading after decades of analytic neglect. Without her, the reception section is American process theology only — Hartshorne, Cobb, the Claremont school. Her cosmopolitics project also stands on its own as a contemporary contribution to interrelational pluralism. The kinship runs deep on the relational-pluralism side but stops where Rorty's language-side move begins — she has no language-centred move. Deep close-affinity territory, not seed-ring.

## Pieces, by phase

Pages first, then posts, then revisions on the back of the posts. The loop is tighter that way: pages commit to a structural reading, posts test it against conversational writing, revisions integrate what the writing surfaces.

### Phase 1 — site pages

1. **Stengers person page** — complete. At `/positioning/persons/s/stengers/`. Two contributions (cosmopolitics, ecology of practices) flagged for eventual subject pages; depth on her Whitehead reading anchored to the planned philosophy of organism page.

2. **Philosophy of organism subject page** (`/positioning/subjects/p/`). Whitehead's specific system: actual occasions, prehension, concrescence, creativity, eternal objects, God. Reception covers two lines — theological-American (Hartshorne, Cobb, Griffin, the Claremont school) and continental-scholarly (Stengers as the figure who returned Whitehead to live continental reading; Latour, Massumi, Manning, speculative-realism-adjacent revivals). Contested aspects of the God concept and the secular readings of the system get treatment here. Person/subject page register — outside-context voice, no SPLectrum vocabulary.

   The existing process-philosophy subject page stays bundled (Hegel, Bergson, Whitehead at the level of process-as-primary-over-substance). It names philosophy of organism as the specific Whiteheadian programme and links to it. The Whitehead person page links to philosophy of organism where the depth on the technical system lives.

3. **Seed-ring piece on Whitehead/Rorty pairing** (`/positioning/seed/`). Developed structural treatment. Two halves of the anti-representational move: Whitehead from nature, Rorty from language. Resonance only — names where each reaches and where each stops. Side-by-side, no claiming the thinkers for the project. The two strands held together because the structural observation runs across the bifurcation/mirror line. Stengers can be named once where the piece notes that Whitehead's project still has live carriers, but no more — she is contemporary continuation, not part of the structural argument.

### Phase 2 — blog posts

4. **Pure Whitehead post** (blog, named-source register). Angle: importance sits in the move to think nature without splitting it. The bifurcation of nature is the diagnosis; the relational ontology is the cure. Everything else — actual occasions, prehension, creativity, eternal objects, God — falls out of the diagnostic move.

   Arc: three careers as a diagnosis-cure structure. Cambridge mathematician sees the limits of pure formalism. London philosopher of science diagnoses the bifurcation: the split is not in nature, it is in our theorising. Harvard metaphysician at 63 builds the cure: a speculative system where the split never opens.

   The register absorbs Whitehead's own energy: systematic, audacious, vocabulary-coining, polymathic. Architecturally ambitious, not lyrical. The voice of someone willing to invent a vocabulary because the existing one cannot say what he needs to say.

5. **Whitehead/Rorty pairing post** (blog). Angle: two halves of the anti-representational move. Whitehead from the side of nature, Rorty from the side of language. The intersection is what is distinctive about SPLectrum. Lighter biographical Whitehead since the pure post precedes it; moves quickly into the structural argument. Conversational register — the kind of post that says "I keep coming back to these two as one gesture" and walks through why.

6. **Stengers post** (blog, named-source register). Angle: "thinking with" as a method, not a phrase. Stengers does not replace the thinkers she engages — she works with them, lets their concepts do their own work, declines to ascend to a position from which she would speak about them. Each of her major projects is the same move at different scales: chemistry as a practice with its own validity (the early formation); irreversibility as classical science speaking against its own foundations (the Prigogine collaboration); cosmopolitics as politics that does not need a transcendent arbiter; *Thinking with Whitehead* as the refusal to build a system on top of his system; slow science as a refusal of fast metrics' universal vantage; the idiot as a principled interruption of consensus. The methodological signature is the refusal of the god's-eye view; "thinking with" is its name.

   The voice absorbs her character: patient, polemical when needed, deeply collaborative, suspicious of universalism. Where the Whitehead post is about a diagnostic move (refusing the bifurcation of nature), the Stengers post is about a methodological one (refusing the transcendent vantage). Parallel structure, different refusal.

### Phase 3 — revisions on the back of the posts

7. **Interrelational pluralism revisions.** Seed page: drop the parsimony framing on Whitehead, name the source-of-ordered-novelty divergence. Close-affinity pluralism page: add Stengers to the process-relational cluster as a contemporary contribution alongside Whitehead.

8. **Revisions to the phase 1 pages** as the posts surface what reads as alive in conversation versus what sits on the page. Particular attention to the philosophy of organism page (its reception section should reflect whatever the Stengers post has clarified about her role) and the seed-ring piece (its framing of the structural pairing benefits from whatever the pairing post has surfaced about how the argument lands in looser register).

### Queued

**Cosmopolitics subject page** (`/positioning/subjects/c/`). Cosmopolitics is substantial enough to warrant its own subject page — distinct concept, multi-volume body of work, contested reception (Latour's adoption, the Beck/Stengers distinction, political-ecology and decolonial appropriations). The full apparatus — reciprocal capture, the idiot, critique of tolerance — lives here when it exists. Ecology of practices could be developed within this page as the methodological move underlying cosmopolitics, or split off as its own subject page later.

This is queued behind the immediate cycle. Naming it in the plan licenses the compression of those concepts out of the Stengers person page now — depth on cosmopolitics moves here when this exists, the close-affinity pluralism page treats Stengers' process-relational contribution in passing, and the person page stays at touchstone register.

## Architectural decisions

The existing process-philosophy page bundles three thinkers at the level of the movement; philosophy of organism gets its own page for the Whitehead-specific system. This is consistent with how positioning handles a thinker whose work has generated its own tradition: the person page covers life and work; the subject page covers the tradition under its name.

**Stengers' depth network.** Stengers' contributions spread across three sites by aspect: her Whitehead reading anchors the reception section of the philosophy of organism subject page; her cosmopolitics and ecology of practices appear in the close-affinity pluralism page as a contemporary process-relational contribution; the eventual cosmopolitics subject page carries the full conceptual apparatus. The existing process-philosophy subject page can name her in passing but does not need substantial treatment — she is a Whitehead reader and developer, not a process philosopher in her own right.

The deep kinship between Whitehead and SPLectrum lives in the seed ring (the Whitehead/Rorty pairing piece), not on the Whitehead person page or the Stengers person page. The pairing is the load-bearing structural reading and the place where SPLectrum-side language belongs.
