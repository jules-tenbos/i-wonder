Life to fight another day, wake to think another day
----------------------------------------------------
I've always been fascinated by the tunnel of death, the passage from life to the unknown.
Near my home, to go on riverside walks, I had to pass through a small pedestrian tunnel under the motorway. I always used to image 'walking to the light on the other side'.
We have a similar nightly transition where we move from conscious into sleep. A different outcome though, we usually wake up in the morning!
This transition has turned into a daily meditative session. Feeling the body coming to rest, the mind slowly letting loose. That wasn't always the case. It started with feeling excited - not always happily - about that transition into the unknown. Although here not exactly dying, letting go of the conscious.
I am a lot older now, less impatient, more at peace. Fulfillment in life is the having been able to come to insights - discover perceived clarity. And it has made me a lot happier relinquishing the conscious state and picking it back up in the morning.

The saying: Live to fight another day - Wake to think another day
