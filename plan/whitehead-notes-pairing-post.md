# Notes: Whitehead/Rorty pairing post — storyline sketch

Companion to the Whitehead plan (phase 2, item 5). One angle of several possible.

## Storyline

**Hook:** Two unlikely bedfellows. Whitehead and Rorty don't show up in the same sentence often — process metaphysics and pragmatism keep to their own neighbourhoods. But both refuse representationalism. Worth sitting with the pairing.

**Develop:** Each on their own ground, briefly. Whitehead refuses the split between the world physics measures and the world we experience — builds a relational metaphysics where nothing stands outside its relations. Rorty sets aside the mirror — switches to language, takes truth as solidarity rather than correspondence. Two refusals, two starting points.

**Turn:** The instinct is to read them as parallel attacks on the same target — two people knocking on the same door from different sides. They're not. Whitehead's move is internal to a being-focused language game, Rorty's to a language-focused one. Each does what their game lets them do; neither does the other's work.

**Land:** What brings them together for SPLectrum is P0. Being implies language. The two refusals address the two co-implicated sides of what P0 names. Not parallel — co-implicated. If you only refuse from one side, you've covered half the structure.

## Register

SPLectrum register — P0 anchored explicitly, but conversational rather than structural-expository. Maybe 400–500 words. The ref-lib does the careful structural reading; the post does the recognition — the moment of seeing them as co-implicated rather than parallel.
