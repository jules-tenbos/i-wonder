# The Full Arc

## The Statement

Cosmological evolution, evolution of the earth, evolution of life, and evolution of the mind are expressions of the same underlying principle — increasing relational density. Not separate processes that happen to rhyme, but one process expressing through whatever medium is available: physics, chemistry, biology, cognition.

Cosmos → earth → life → mind. Each is a medium through which relational density increases.

A note of caution: what we know — reality — is a product of the mind, our brain, experience of a shared personal relationship. This observation is itself a language, subject to the same principle — epistemological reality applies to it too. We are inside what we describe. Even the pattern we're seeing is seen from somewhere.

## How This Fits — Research Findings

Three things that have been done separately but rarely together:
1. Unified evolutionary narratives (Chaisson, Teilhard, Big History) — but these typically claim the view from nowhere
2. Relational ontology (Rovelli, Whitehead) — but these typically stay within physics or metaphysics
3. Epistemic situatedness (Kant, Nagel, Maturana) — but these typically do not build unified evolutionary frameworks

This position holds all three simultaneously: a unified evolutionary narrative, grounded in relational thinking, that includes its own epistemic limitation as a structural feature rather than a footnote. That combination is rare.

### Unified Evolutionary Narratives

- **Chaisson (Energy Rate Density / Cosmic Evolution)** — The most systematic scientific advocate. Single metric across cosmos → culture: energy throughput per unit mass increases monotonically from galaxies to societies. Framed within non-equilibrium thermodynamics, no new laws needed. But uses energy, not relations, and presents it as objective description without epistemic caveat.
- **Teilhard de Chardin (Cosmogenesis)** — Geosphere → biosphere → noosphere. Most direct historical precedent for cosmos → earth → life → mind as a single directed process. But explicitly teleological and theological (Omega Point). The position avoids this finalism.
- **Big History (David Christian)** — Unified narrative from Big Bang to present. But descriptive rather than principled, and weak on epistemics.

### Physics and Relational Ontology

- **Rovelli (Relational Quantum Mechanics)** — Quantum states are not properties of systems but relations between systems. Reality at the fundamental level is relational, not substantial. But this is an ontological claim, not epistemically situated.
- **Ontic Structural Realism (Ladyman, French)** — At the fundamental level, all that exists are structures — modal relations, not self-subsistent objects. "Relations are ontologically primitive but objects and properties are not." If taken seriously, "increasing relational density" maps onto increasing structural complexity at every scale.
- **Wheeler (Participatory Anthropic Principle)** — "No phenomenon is a real phenomenon until it is an observed phenomenon." The cosmos produces minds, and minds make the cosmos definite. Resonates with the self-referential caveat, but Wheeler's version is ontological where this position is epistemological.
- **Deutsch & Marletto (Constructor Theory)** — Reformulates physics in terms of which transformations are possible/impossible. Claims to provide "the ultimate unification of thermodynamics, information theory and the theory of living entities."

### Thermodynamics and Self-Organisation

- **Prigogine (Dissipative Structures)** — Systems far from equilibrium spontaneously generate order. Foundational: provides the thermodynamic licence for complexity to increase without violating the second law.
- **England (Dissipation-Driven Adaptation)** — Groups of atoms under energy input spontaneously restructure to dissipate more energy. Life-like organisation as a thermodynamic tendency.
- **Kauffman (Autocatalytic Sets / Self-Organisation)** — Self-sustaining chemical networks arise almost inevitably. Self-organisation as an evolutionary force alongside natural selection. Extends the principle beyond biology.

### Biology and the Life-Mind Connection

- **Maynard Smith & Szathmary (Major Transitions)** — Each transition involves system-level reorganisation with new modes of information storage. Explicitly about system-level change, not species-level.
- **Deacon (Incomplete Nature)** — Traces physics → chemistry → biology → mind through nested levels of constraint. Homeodynamic → morphodynamic → teleodynamic. The closest biological account to "increasing relational density."
- **Maturana & Varela (Autopoiesis / Enactivism)** — "Living systems are cognitive systems, and living as a process is a process of cognition." Dissolves the life/mind boundary. Living is cognising — the step from biology to mind is not a new domain but a deepening.
- **Cronin & Walker (Assembly Theory)** — Complexity measured across physics and biology on a continuous landscape. Biology is one region, not the boundary.

### Earth as Co-Evolving System

- **Lovelock & Margulis (Gaia)** — Earth and its biological systems function as a single self-regulating entity. Geological and biological evolution are inseparable.
- **Smith & Morowitz** — Life as "the fourth geosphere." A planetary process — non-equilibrium phase transitions opening new channels for energy flow.
- **Margulis (Symbiosis)** — The deepest engines of evolution are inter-organismic relationships (endosymbiosis, mitochondria). Cooperation, not just competition.

### Philosophy

- **Whitehead (Process Philosophy)** — Fundamental units are actual occasions of experience — events of relation. Reality constituted by internal relations. Panexperientialism. Probably the closest existing philosophical system — relational, processual, spans cosmos to mind. But builds a full metaphysical system; this position, staying within epistemological reality, is more cautious.
- **Bergson (Creative Evolution)** — Elan vital: a creative impulse driving evolution. Consciousness pervades the creative universe. But elan vital was criticised as explanatorily empty — naming the mystery rather than explaining it.
- **Nagel (Mind and Cosmos)** — "The materialist neo-Darwinian conception of nature is almost certainly false" because it cannot account for mind. Proposes "naturalistic teleology" — a bias of nature. Flags the self-referential problem but does not solve it.

### The Self-Referential Problem

- **Nagel** — Any theory of everything that includes mind must account for the mind that produces the theory. The central unsolved problem.
- **Kant (Phenomena/Noumena)** — We know the phenomenal world structured by our cognitive faculties, not things-in-themselves. The philosophical ancestor of epistemological reality.
- **Lorenz (Evolutionary Epistemology)** — Our cognitive categories are evolutionary adaptations giving partial isomorphism with reality, not total access. Close to epistemological reality.
- **Maturana** — "Everything said is said by an observer." The observer is always inside the system being described. Closest on the self-referential caveat but doesn't extend to cosmic scale.

### Consciousness

- **Tononi (Integrated Information Theory)** — Consciousness is identical with integrated information (phi). Panpsychism: any system with integrated information has some degree of consciousness. If consciousness scales with informational integration, mind is not a latecomer but an amplification. Controversial.

### The Main Dissent

- **Gould** — Would challenge the cosmos → mind sequence as anthropocentric narrative-making. The sequence looks like a ladder only because we stand at the top. The strongest empirical pushback.
- **Mainstream Science** — Operates through disciplinary boundaries. Different domains need different explanatory tools. Attempts to unify often lose explanatory power within each domain.

### Where This Diverges from Mainstream

| Mainstream tendency | This position |
|---|---|
| Physics, biology, and cognition are separate domains with separate frameworks | One process expressing through different media |
| Unified narratives (Chaisson, Teilhard, Big History) claim objective description | Insists the narrative is a product of evolved mind — cannot claim the view from nowhere |
| Relational ontology (Rovelli, Whitehead) stays within physics or metaphysics | Extends relational thinking across the full arc while keeping it epistemically situated |
| Epistemic situatedness (Kant, Nagel, Maturana) doesn't build unified frameworks | Builds the unified narrative but holds the caveat as structural, not footnote |
| Complexity science seeks a universal measure but has no consensus | Proposes "relational density" — would need formalisation |
| Post-Gould biology is hostile to directional narratives | Observed tendency, not purpose — "seems to strive," not "strives" |

### What Makes This Position Distinctive

Most unified evolutionary narratives present themselves as objective descriptions of how reality works. This position insists that the narrative is itself a product of evolved mind and therefore cannot claim the view from nowhere. The epistemic caveat is not a disclaimer bolted on — it is a structural feature of the position. The closest single figures are Whitehead (who lacks the epistemic caveat) and Maturana (who lacks the cosmic scope).
