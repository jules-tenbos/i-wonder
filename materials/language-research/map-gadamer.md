# Gadamer — Map

## Initial phrases

"Language is the universal medium of understanding; all understanding is linguistic."

Understanding occurs as a "fusion of horizons" (Horizontverschmelzung) — always linguistically mediated.

"Being that can be understood is language."

Prejudice (Vorurteil) — not a defect but a condition of understanding. We always approach a subject from within a tradition, with pre-judgments shaped by our historical situation.

Effective-historical consciousness (Wirkungsgeschichtliches Bewusstsein) — awareness that our understanding is shaped by the history that has produced us.

---

## Mapping statement

Gadamer's "being that can be understood is language" — from where we stand, this resonates with how we think, with a wider scope for what counts as linguistic. His claim is that understanding is always linguistically mediated; there is no understanding outside language. The overlap with our principle line 2 (language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality) is direct, in his own terms.

His fusion of horizons describes a specific mechanism for how knowledge emerges. Each subject has their horizon — shaped by tradition, by prejudice (Vorurteil) in his technical sense: the pre-judgments that come with our historical situation. Understanding happens when horizons meet and fuse — not by one absorbing the other, but by both being transformed in the encounter. Shared understanding emerges from the fusion. This is recursive: each fusion opens further horizons to be fused, each encounter generating more to be shared.

From where we stand, this describes the dynamic of principle line 3: language is where subjects share knowledge about reality. Each subject experiences through their language (their horizon). Subjects share (horizons fuse). Knowledge is what survives the overlap (shared understanding emerges). And the overlap generates more to be shared (recursive, opening further horizons). His terms describe the mechanism; ours name the structure.

His prejudice and effective-historical consciousness are worth noting: we always understand from within a tradition, shaped by what came before. This is consistent with how we think about evolution of expression — each thinker works within the language available to them, shaped by what came before. Understanding is never from nowhere; it is always from within a horizon. This is another way of saying there is no view from nowhere.
