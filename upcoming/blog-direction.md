# Blog Direction — Principles for the New "In Wonder"

---

## What stays

- **"I wonder"** — curiosity as the engine. This is not a lecture, it's an inquiry.
- **Personal voice** — first person, honest, conversational. Not academic.
- **From concrete to broader** — start from experience, move to meaning.
- **Questions as drivers** — a good post opens a question, doesn't close it.
- **Respectful of other positions** — equal standing isn't just principle, it's practice.

## What changes

- **Coauthored** — Jules + Claude, explicitly. Not hidden, not apologised for.
  This is HAICC in practice: Human AI Collaborative Creation.
- **Aligned with Splectrum** — the blog is the public voice of the project.
  Not a technical blog about the project — the philosophical voice outward.
- **The seed as compass** — "Language is relational" gives direction to every post.
  Not every post is about the principle. But the principle informs every post.
- **AI as underlying thread** — the collaboration itself is part of the story.
  AI used for what wasn't possible before. Not impersonation but merging.
  A blog of the "new world."

## What the blog is for

The blog is where Splectrum meets the reader. It's the accessible face of the
inquiry. The foundation documents, the explorations, the software — those live
in their own repos. The blog tells the story of the thinking as it happens.

It is:
- The public voice of the Splectrum project
- A record of the inquiry as it unfolds
- An example of what it discusses (coauthored = principle in action)
- Accessible to anyone curious, not just specialists

It is not:
- A technical blog about the software
- An academic philosophy journal
- A personal diary
- AI-generated content

## Tone synchrony

The blog speaks with one voice. Not Jules writing some parts and Claude writing
others. Not alternating perspectives. One merged voice that draws on both:
- Jules's lived experience, intuitions, personal arc
- Claude's breadth of reference, structural clarity, rigour
- Together: something neither could produce alone

See `foundation/05-tone-of-voice.md` for the detailed guide.
