## Territory: The Turn

The outside view dismantled — told through both philosophy and science.

- **The philosophical turn** — Kant to now. The arc from outside observer to participant, seen whole. Overview, not summary of individual encounters.
- **Loss of the God's-eye perspective** — the science story. Classical mechanics → relativity → quantum mechanics. Each step breaking the previous picture of reality. Rich material, possibly more than one post.
- **The crisis of the observer** — where the two threads meet. Science and philosophy arriving at the same impasse from different directions.
- **The push-through** — RQM. From radical position to mainstream. The relational view as the water we now swim in.

Philosophy can be peppered into the science posts where it illuminates. The stories carry the argument.

## Territory: Life

The relational pattern in living systems. Biology, biosemiotics, cellular signalling, the bee dance. Branch point still open — could come off the science arc (physics to life, different scale) or off Merleau-Ponty (the body as medium pointing towards biology). Needs thinking.

## Territory: Splectrum Engineering

Interlaced as the work produces material. Mycelium, HAICC, the software expressing the same principle in code. Timing and topics depend on what emerges.

## Sequence logic

Encounters (1–3) → the turn (philosophy and science) → life → open. Engineering posts woven in throughout. No fixed schedule beyond March — sequence emerges from the work.
