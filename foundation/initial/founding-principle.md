# Splectrum — Founding Principle

Language is relational.
Language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality.
Language is where subjects share knowledge about reality.
Languages are inter-relational and have equal standing in potential.
Together they form a web of growing complexity.

---

## Context

**Language is relational.**
What a language gives access to depends on what it relates to.

**Language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality.**
Experience is always within the reach of a language.

**Language is where subjects share knowledge about reality.**
And language is the source of the only objectivity known: convergence of subjects.

**Languages are inter-relational.**
Languages, as committed ways of expressing relation, are not isolated games. They interact, overlap, and inform each other, all having equal standing in potential.

**Together they form a web of growing complexity.**
Relational density increases as knowledge grows.

---

## Concepts

**Language** — A relational system whose reach is defined by what it relates to.
Resonant positions:
- Wittgenstein: "To imagine a language means to imagine a form of life."
- Heidegger: "Language is the house of being."
- Maturana: Language as coordinated action between organisms — not a thing but an ongoing activity of relating.
- Peirce: Language is semiosis — sign-making. Meaning is always triadic, always relational.

**Relation** — The interaction through which reality manifests.

**Reality** — Each language's reach is only accessible through that language.

**Experience** — The relation between a subject and reality as accessed through a language.

**Sharing** — The overlap of experience between subjects.

**Knowledge** — Lack of surprise. What survives the overlap of experience.

**Objectivity** — Convergence of subjects.

**Inter-relational** — Languages relating to each other. They overlap, interact, and inform each other.

**Equal standing in potential** — Each language stands in its own right.

**Complexity** — Relational density. Increases as knowledge grows.
