# Research Proposal: The Definition of Language

The definition of language is the cornerstone of the founding principle. Everything else depends on it.

## Research approach

### Step 1: Gather

Survey definitions of language across all traditions and periods, with an open mind. Each entry: who, when, where, their core claim about language.

### Step 2: Extract

For each definition, note how it relates to the principle's concepts and the relations between them.

### Step 3: Map

Note resonances and divergences. Let the patterns emerge.

### Step 4: Assess independence

Where convergence appears, could one source have influenced another? Independent convergence carries more weight.

### Step 5: Synthesize

What survives across the widest range? What doesn't converge? Both are valuable.
