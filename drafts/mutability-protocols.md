# Notes

## Storyline

### 1. Open with dirty
- An empty repository knows nothing about its own content
- No mutability metadata, no guarantees — the honest default
- Architecture of absence: what you don't declare doesn't exist as a guarantee

### 2. The three regimes as self-knowledge
- Not lifecycle stages but paradigms
- Immutable: the fabric knows everything, the content is the fact
- Mutable: the fabric knows the history through the queue, the current state is derived
- Dirty: the fabric knows nothing, the current state is all there is

### 3. The subject shapes the landscape
- Mutability metadata placed in contexts, locally shaped, discovered through traversal
- Not configured from outside — the subject declares its own commitments
- Some areas sealed, some controlled, some stay dirty

### 4. The interrogative protocol
- `mycelium.mutability` reads the regime — walks ancestor axis, nearest wins, no declaration means dirty
- Two operators, one value. The protocol reads and declares, it doesn't enforce.

### 5. The mutable mechanism
- A service managing a living surface derived from a sealed queue
- Data change records are immutable records, written through normal data access protocols
- The queue is the truth. The surface is the convenience.
- One-directional dependency: remove the surface, the queue doesn't notice

### 6. Expendability as first-class property
- Rebuild is not emergency recovery — it is a first-class operation
- Schema evolution? Rebuild. New projection? Rebuild. Corruption? Rebuild.
- Truth never lives in the derived state

### 7. The transition
- Dirty to immutable is a one-way creation act — a snapshot, a commitment
- What happened in dirty before that moment is gone
- Not a conversion — a birth

### 8. Close with the landscape
- The fabric itself has no opinion about change
- The landscape is the sum of declarations — cautious or adventurous, sealed or open
- The mutability protocols read whatever the subject has declared about itself

## Key theme
Self-knowledge. What the fabric knows about its own content. The subject shaping its own reliability landscape through declarations.
