About two years ago I had a fortuitous car crash - if something exists of that sort.

## The Blue Screen of Death

I got what could be described as the equivalent of a Windows blue screen of death while driving on the motorway. I lost consciousness while driving and was knocked around a bit by other vehicles. 

When I woke up I was stuck at the central reservation facing oncoming traffic. I wondered how I got there as I had and still have no memory of what happened when the lights went out.

## The Recovery

I can't complain - my system rebooted and I had hardly a scratch on me. I recovered physically and no hard cause was ever found for the incident. 

My poor central computer took a bit longer though to find its mojo back. I had no functional damage but I seemed to be thinking in slow motion and was tired as hell.

## The Realization

It rubbed my nose in it how much I valued thinking and studying and how important this type of mental activity is to me. I really got set on retraining my brain to its former glory...

## The New Course

So I picked up my interest in philosophy again and decided to pursue studying it seriously. I decided to refresh my foreign language knowledge and extend it so I could read works in their original language. 

I decided to strain my brain and hopefully make some more sense of what life is about. And in doing so, I discovered that this crash, as traumatic as it was, had put me back on a course of learning and growth that I might never have found otherwise.

Sometimes our greatest setbacks become our most important redirections.
