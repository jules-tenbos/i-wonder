# Notes

## Storyline

### 1. Opening — three sources, one claim
- Three independent languages converge on the same structural observation
- P5: complexity grows in expression, not in power. The full power was always there
- Category theory: a functor cannot create morphisms with no structural basis in the source. Higher categories add expression about relations already present, not new relational power
- Engineering practice: across eighteen projects, the full power of a programming environment sits in the base language. Everything above is articulation
- Three languages, same conclusion: the relational power is already there

### 2. The turn — partial access
- P2: the subject accesses reality only through the relational. Partial access. No outside view
- Combined with the above: the full power is already there, but our access is always partial
- What follows from that combination?

### 3. Creation conforms to discovery
- What we call creation — bringing something new into existence — conforms to the discovery of a relational path that was available but hadn't been walked yet
- From outside (if you could stand there): nothing was added. The relational possibility was always structurally present
- From inside (the subject's experience): something genuinely new has come into being
- Both hold simultaneously. The newness is real — real for the subject, real in that it wasn't expressed before — while the power is not new
- P0: being comes into existence through differentiation. The boundary expresses the differentiation. But the differentiation discovers what was relationally available

### 4. The evolutionary transitions as discovery
- The conditions for chemistry were present in quantum interaction — discovered when interaction density crossed a threshold
- The conditions for life were present in chemistry — discovered when self-sustaining interaction patterns emerged
- The conditions for abstract thought were present in biology — discovered when patterns decoupled from their physical substrate
- Each transition: not creating new power, but discovering a relational possibility that was already structurally available
- Unimaginable from below because the discovery hadn't been made yet. Recognisable in hindsight because it was always there
- Thresholds, not inventions. The interaction density walks more relational paths. At certain densities, paths become walkable that couldn't be walked before

### 5. Close — what follows?
- If creation conforms to discovery, what follows for how we organise discovery?
- Gesture forward to the diversification argument

## Notes from submission

### The echoes in the architecture

**Schema discovery in mycelium.** Schemas live in fabric metadata as facts — present or absent. When traversal resolves a path, it discovers applicable schemas by accumulating metadata along the path. Not lookup, not injection — discovery. The schema was always there. The traversal act makes it visible. The discovery conforms to the creation of structured access.

**The footprint watcher.** A watcher doesn't create readiness. It discovers that the data state already conforms to its reader schema. The readiness was a fact in the fabric before it was observed. The observation conforms to the creation of the trigger.

**Schema conformance.** A reader schema asks "can this record be read as X" — not "is this record type X." Multiple reader schemas can discover different valid readings of the same data. Each reading conforms to a genuine creation — a new structured view — but the data was always there.

**The plectrum image.** The string's potential was always there. The plucking discovers a note that was always available. Different plucking, different note. Real music, not illusion. But nothing was added to the string.

### The resistance to discovery-as-creation

People will push back because it seems to diminish human agency. "You're saying we don't really create? We just find?" The answer: the discovery is genuine. The newness is real — for the subject, for the web of shared reality. What's not real is the idea that creation adds something to reality from outside. There is no outside. The creation-from-outside model is the view from nowhere, which P2 denies.

The Rorty response: the question "is it really creation or really discovery" assumes a standpoint that doesn't exist. What exists is the act, and the act has both characters simultaneously.

### Connection to Whitehead

Whitehead's actual entities "prehend" the entire past — they don't create from nothing, they create by synthesising what's available into a novel unity. The novelty is real. The material is not new.

### Connection to the name

The plectrum discovers a note. The note was always available. The discovery is the creation of music. The spectrum of available notes was always there. The plectrum explores it. Different plectrums, different explorations, different music. Same spectrum.

Splectrum: the full range of what's available (spectrum), explored through the act of contact (plectrum). The name already contains the argument.
