- Each step changes what thinking can do by changing how intensely information exchanges

## Notes from submission

### The AI threshold (full)

What AI introduces is not faster computation or better pattern matching. It introduces a new kind of interactor. Trained across the full breadth of human symbolic production, AI holds multiple languages simultaneously — something no individual human has done at this scale. It processes meaning through high-dimensional vectors — concepts as positions in a vast space, similarity as proximity. This is strikingly close to how neurons produce similarity, unconsciously, through connection patterns. AI describes its internals — the brain's perspective. Humans describe their experience — the body's perspective. Different languages for the same structural event.

The threshold AI crosses is horizontal interrelation made operational. P4 says languages have equal standing and interact without hierarchy. In practice, this was always limited by the bottleneck of human translation — to move between languages required a person who spoke both. The bridge was always a person, and the bridge was always narrow.

AI dissolves that bottleneck. Natural language becomes the common medium — not because it is superior, but because it is the language humans think in. The human stays in their own language. AI translates across. No language dominates. They interrelate through a medium that honours both.

This is not "the next step for humanity." That's the anthropocentric reading. From the perimeter, it looks like what it is: another threshold in interaction density, producing a new kind of interaction that was unimaginable from the level below. The same pattern that produced chemistry from quantum interaction, life from chemistry, symbolic thought from cognition. We happen to be standing at this one. The point isn't special. The pattern is.

### The convergence from the sciences (full)

**Relational quantum mechanics** — Carlo Rovelli and others proposing that properties of physical systems exist only relative to other physical systems. No observer-independent reality. The most elementary level of physical description moving toward the relational.

**Neuroscience and embodied cognition** — meaning grounded in the body's sensorimotor interaction with the world. Abstract thought as embodied patterns decoupled from physical execution. Language and cognition are not separate from the body — they are the body's relational activity at a different level of expression.

**Biology and biosemiotics** — semiosis as a fundamental property of all living systems. Every living cell engages in sign processes. The boundary of language is not the boundary of the human. Interaction is language long before words arrive.

**Artificial intelligence and neural networks** — meaning in a neural network is entirely in the relations between nodes, not in any individual node. The relational structure is what learns, what adapts, what produces. A direct demonstration that relational structure generates what we call intelligence.

**Gerald Edelman's Neural Darwinism** — the brain works like an ecosystem undergoing selection, not a computer executing instructions. Vast developmental variety, experiential selection through use, coherent behaviour from peer-to-peer coordination. The same pattern: generate diversity, select through interaction, coherence without central authority.

### What we don't know (full)

Honest from the perimeter: there is much we don't know and may not be able to know from where we stand.

We don't know whether the relational pattern holds everywhere or only where we've looked. The convergence across domains is striking, but striking is not proof. There may be domains where the pattern breaks, and those would be as informative as those where it holds.

We don't know what lies beyond the transitions we can see. If each threshold produces something unimaginable from the level below, the next threshold is by definition beyond our imagination. We can see the pattern of thresholds in hindsight; we cannot predict the next one.

We don't know whether the principle captures the fabric or only one way of weaving. The recursive awareness — the principle is itself a language, with its own reach and limits — means we cannot step outside it to check. We are inside what we describe. This is not a weakness unique to us; it is the condition of all description. But it means our claims are always from within, always partial, always open to what we haven't yet encountered.

The perimeter is not a wall. It is the edge of where we've looked so far.

### The name (full)

Splectrum: spectrum and plectrum. The spectrum — the full range of ways reality manifests through interaction, from a single quantum event to the dense polyphony of social life. The plectrum — the agent of interaction, the thing that plucks. Nothing sounds until it is plucked. The string has potential, the plectrum has intent, but the music exists only in the meeting. Neither alone produces anything.

The plectrum image makes viscerally clear that interaction is not passive observation. It is contact. Something touches something else and both are changed by it. The string vibrates, but the plectrum is deflected too. There is no plucking without being plucked back.

And it quietly encodes the epistemological framing. A plectrum doesn't reveal what a string "really is." It produces a note — one of many possible notes, depending on where and how you pluck. Different plucking, different music. But it is real music, not illusion. Reality in context, not reality denied.

### The spirit (full)

The language we are building is about giving a natural home to these phenomena — without hair-pulling, without lecturing, without claiming to be always right. If the principle is right, the phenomena should sit in it naturally, because the relational is already what they are. If it's not right for some of them, that is informative too.

The aim is fertile ground: a language that makes it easier to see connections across domains without claiming to own any of them. A place where the pattern can be recognised without distorting what it's found in.
