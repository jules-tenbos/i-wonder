# Notes

## Storyline

### 1. Setup — the most applied angle
- The seed is abstract, structural. But it speaks to us directly — to how we experience the world, how we share, how communities form.
- This is the oldest human question: making sense of existence. The seed offers a foundation for it.

### 2. P2 as keystone — experience from the inside
- We are bodies, sensing, always already in language.
- Everything that reaches us comes through the languages we hold.
- Retention is the key move: without a lasting imprint, interaction leaves no trace. Experience requires it.
- Reality as accumulation — the world as it has disclosed itself to me. Personal, from within.
- Connect to the fire example from "Language, Experience and Reality" — the flames already labelled, the body absorbing before words arrive.

### 3. P3 — how the shared realm forms
- It follows from P2 that we never interact directly.
- The mechanism: one of us acts on the environment, another extracts similar information from the effect.
- What we experience as conversation, being together — emergent from this relay.
- Speaking the same language = matched pairs of producing and receiving.
- Sharing is synchronisation of language. Knowledge is what transfers given the level of synchronisation.
- The more aligned, the more can pass through.

### 4. From sharing to community — the P0 callback
- Through synchronisation, a shared realm forms — shared reality, shared values.
- The group that has synchronised enough is a community.
- Its formation is an act of creation — P0 at the human level.
- Differentiation draws a boundary: us and them, baked in.
- Not hostility — structural. No "us" without what is not us.

### 5. P4 — language creates language
- Once sharing brings language to life, overlap becomes origin.
- Horizontal: peer communities influencing each other. Vertical: one spawning another.
- Every overlap is a potential starting point for new language.
- Equal standing — none privileged in principle.

### 6. P5 — the evolutionary cycle
- Knowledge transfers, new languages emerge from overlap, those synchronise with others.
- The human web grows through this cycle.
- Complexity in the relating, not in any single community.
