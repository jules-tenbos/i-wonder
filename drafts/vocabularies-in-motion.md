# Notes

## Storyline

### 1. The review that found it
- Cold reads under two external anchors: Google (E-E-A-T, Page Quality) and Diataxis (content type). UK Gov content-design standards considered; review-in-motion decided they didn't carry weight for explanatory writing on a framework like SPLectrum.
- Four mature ref-lib pages scored 2–3 out of 5 on explicit vocabulary.

### 2. The flag was unsolicited
- Not a criterion set in order to find this. Came up because the external frame asked about reader service and the gap showed.
- From self-set criteria the finding would have read as self-confirmation. Coming from the frame itself, it was evidence — and that changed the weight.

### 3. Two venues, already structural
- SPLectrum-internal terms → `/vocabulary/splectrum/`.
- External terms (différance, logos, śūnyatā) → authoritative sources (SEP, Britannica).
- Not an invention — a discipline to adopt.

### 4. Choosing the in-prose instrument
- Inline link-on-first-use would push the voice toward Wikipedia-register. Essayistic sites don't do this; it breaks flow.
- Stay with the voice's existing discipline: "no jargon without immediate unpacking."
- Add a pointer to a meta-page per content page.

### 5. The shape — a shadow site
- `/vocabulary/site/` mirrors content paths. Each shadow page catalogs its content page's vocabulary with commentary on usage.
- Opt-in: a shadow page exists only when the content page points to it. No obligation to mirror the whole site.
- Content stays content; the vocabulary conversation lives next door.

### 6. Writing the first shadow surfaced more than expected
- **Hot and cold extraction caught different things.** A cold pass (agent with no site context) picked up face-value terms the page itself weights — roadmap, AI-driven cognitive revolution, consequences-from-the-seed. A hot pass (embedded in the site's prior material) picked up compression-drift (pillars used in two senses), cross-site idiom (lives-in), origin-and-borrowing patterns. The two-pass shape earned itself — cold for breadth, hot for depth.
- **Concepts + relations = language.** Language decomposes. Concepts are entities — bounded beings with their own contents. Relations are the interactions between them, of many kinds. Vocabulary is the set of concepts within a bounded context: the node set, before the relations.
- **Scale-free structure.** A concept is a being with contents. A vocabulary is a being with concept-contents. A language game is a being with a vocabulary in use. Same ontology at every scale; what varies is size, function, relation. The seed's own `Being → Entity` translation is the type across all of them.
- **Borrowing without appropriation.** "Language game" is Wittgenstein's. When SPLectrum uses it, it borrows. Borrowing doesn't co-own the term, however deeply the concept gets operationally adopted. Lineage stays legible.

### 7. Category theory lands naturally
- Objects and morphisms map cleanly onto concepts and relations. Vocabulary is Ob(C), the object set of a category, per bounded context. Functors map between contexts — the structural description for how one language game's terms translate into another's.
- Not imposed. The working decomposition is pre-categorical; category theory is what it invites. Formal treatment stays as an open research direction, `submissions/language-games-category-theory.md`.

### 8. Triple purpose earned in practice
- **Writing quality.** A shadow page is a concrete object during tone-of-voice reviews. Drift becomes visible — not only in scores but in commentary.
- **Language research.** The emerging tree is a concrete handle on how SPLectrum's own language games interrelate. The structural decomposition (concept / vocabulary / language game as scale-free beings) surfaced from writing page one.
- **Translation.** Each shadow page gives translators a scoped glossary for the content page they're working on — pre-positioned alignment with the multilanguage-site direction.

### 9. Achievable shape — organic growth
- No upfront spec. Shadow pages exist where a content page opts in.
- Procedure doc writes itself as pages accumulate. No legislation of form before form has emerged.
- Scope for now: site areas with clear language structure. Topnav, blog, positioning have different voice structures — not what the shadow captures.

### 10. Closing
- Collaborative writing with external-standard review in the loop surfaces its own gaps — and produces instruments for its own refinement.
- The shadow-site move came out of one review. The structural decomposition (concepts and relations, scale-free being, category-theoretic alignment) came out of writing one shadow page.
- Serves the site (consistency), the research (language interrelation), and the translation track. One artefact, three payoffs.
