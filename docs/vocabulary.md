# Splectrum Vocabulary

Terms as used within Splectrum. This vocabulary grows as the project develops.

---

**Relational unit** — entities and the languages between them. The basic structure of relation.

**Subject** — the point-of-view entity of a relational unit. The perspective from which experience, knowledge, and interaction are described. Not necessarily human — any entity that participates relationally.

**Persona** — an interaction role a subject exposes. A language game configured for a specific engagement — with its own vocabulary, rules, autonomy, and participants. Not a mask. A way of being present in a context. A subject may have multiple personas.
