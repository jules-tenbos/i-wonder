# Seed vocabulary — Engineering

The seed vocabulary as it appears in [the applied seed - Engineering](/seed/engineering/). Translations and extensions of [original-seed terms](original).

**Protocol** — Engineering term for [language](original).

**Shared data state** — Engineering term for [shared reality](original).

**Subject reality** — The data picture of an owner's subject matter — the dynamic data state Mycelium makes visible to the owner.
