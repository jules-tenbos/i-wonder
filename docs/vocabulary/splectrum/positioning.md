# Positioning vocabulary

This page contains SPLectrum's positioning vocabulary — terms for engaging external thought languages without imposing on them.

<span id="close-affinity">**Close affinity**</span> — [Positioning pieces](#positioning-piece) with close affinity and high influence.

<span id="on-the-fence">**On the fence**</span> — [Positioning pieces](#positioning-piece) with a mixture of [resonance and dissonance](#resonance-and-dissonance), of interest because of that mixture.

<span id="outside-voice">**Outside voice**</span> — The voice of the [positioning](#positioning) area. SPLectrum stays at a distance, letting external material speak on its own terms. Contrast: the narrator's inside voice in the core areas.

<span id="positioning">**Positioning**</span> — Outside work that puts SPLectrum in context. Engages external thought languages (thinkers, frameworks, traditions) on [equal standing](seed#equal-standing), without imposing SPLectrum's framing.

<span id="positioning-piece">**Positioning piece**</span> — The unit of content in the [positioning](#positioning) area. Each piece engages one thinker, framework, tradition, situational piece, or research angle.

<span id="resonance-and-dissonance">**Resonance and dissonance**</span> — The canonical pair for the relation between a [piece](#positioning-piece) and SPLectrum. Resonance: structural agreement. Dissonance: structural disagreement. SPLectrum takes part when a piece carries a mix of both.

<span id="wider-landscape">**Wider landscape**</span> — [Positioning pieces](#positioning-piece) at some distance from the centre, carrying considerable influence.
