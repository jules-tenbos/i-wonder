# Seed vocabulary

The vocabulary of the seed — SPLectrum's foundational language. Sourced from the seed's own pages: [the original principles](/seed/original) and [the engineering language mapping](/seed/engineering/language-mapping).

## Words from the seed

| Seed word | Engineering word | What it is |
|-----------|------------------|-----------|
| Being | Entity | A unit of encapsulated being — the inside is being, the surface is language |
| Language | Protocol | A language game unit — one specific interaction pattern |
| Subject | Owner | The entity that owns the interior of its being — like a microservice |
| Reality | Data state | The data as it is — a snapshot |
| Shared reality | Shared data state | The data state held in common across subjects |

## Additional engineering vocabulary

| Engineering word | What it is |
|------------------|-----------|
| Data world | The totality of data a subject can potentially relate to |
| Subject reality | The data picture of an owner's subject matter — the dynamic data state Mycelium makes visible to the owner |
| Persona | A structured set of protocols — a language game role |

## Structural terms

Other seed words — equal standing (P4), convergence (P3), relational density (P5) — shape structural commitments rather than naming specific artefacts. They live in [the three pillars](/seed/engineering/three-pillars) as design principles.
