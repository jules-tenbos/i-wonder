# From Clockwork to Open Questions — vocabulary

The vocabulary of {% include pending-link.html date="2026-04-26T00:00:00Z" text="[From Clockwork to Open Questions](/blog/2026/04/how-science-became-relational/)" fallback="*From Clockwork to Open Questions* (publishes 2026-04-26)" %}.

| Term / phrase | Language | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| the turn | philosophy | Used for the philosophical pivot (Kant, Descartes) and extended to science. See: [the turn in Western philosophy](/positioning/seed/the-turn-in-western-philosophy/), [the turn in science](/positioning/seed/the-turn-in-science/) |
| mind-body split | philosophy (Descartes) | The move that licensed a purely mechanical physical world. |
| mechanism / mechanistic world | philosophy of science | Reality as machinery, no purpose inside. Narrowed from the everyday "how something works". |
| deterministic | physics | Fixed outcomes from initial conditions; defining property of the classical picture, later contrasted with probabilistic QM. |
| absolute space / time / spacetime | physics (Newton) | The classical stage; said to "disappear" with relativity. |
| clockwork universe | philosophy of science (Laplace) | Total predictability given full state knowledge — title source. |
| classical mechanics / classical physics | physics | Newton-through-Maxwell tradition; baseline for the QM contrast. |
| relational view (of space) | philosophy (Leibniz) | Space as the ordering of coexisting things, not a container. |
| special / general relativity | physics (Einstein) | Space and time coupled and tied to a local observer; gravity as geometry. |
| ultraviolet catastrophe | physics | The Rayleigh-Jeans prediction failing at high frequencies; trigger for quantisation. |
| quanta / discrete packets | physics (Planck) | Energy not smooth — the "grain" in nature. |
| wave-particle duality | physics (de Broglie) | Particles also behave as waves. |
| Heisenberg uncertainty | physics | Position and momentum cannot both be definite — feature of nature, not of instruments. |
| wave function | physics | The central QM object whose status is in dispute on the page. |
| probability amplitude | physics (Born) | Born's reading of the wave function. |
| wave function collapse | physics | The unresolved measurement event. |
| superposition | physics | What the equations describe before measurement yields a definite outcome. |
| observer | philosophy / physics | Ranges from the everyday "person reading off properties" to the QM role tied to measurement. |
| complementarity | physics (Bohr) | Bohr and Heisenberg's response to the measurement question. |
| hidden variables | physics (Bohm) | Bohm's response: restored determinism at the cost of non-locality. |
| branching worlds | physics (Everett) | All outcomes happen, in branching universes. |
| Bell's theorem | physics | Quantum correlations exceed any classical (locally deterministic) explanation. |
| separability | physics | "The whole is the sum of the parts" — said here to fail. |
| quantum field theory | physics | Frame in which three of the four fundamental forces are unified; gravity still outside. |
| worldview / picture (of reality) | general English narrowed (Wittgenstein-flavoured) | Recurring — the thing classical physics had and modern physics is said to lack. |

## Observations

- **Heavy on inherited vocabulary, no SPLectrum coinage.** The post does positioning work — locating SPLectrum within the science arc — and the table reflects that: every row sources from physics, philosophy of science, or general English. The framings the post uses that recur across the site (the turn, relational view, separability) all come from the conversation the post is participating in, not from the project.
- **Two senses to watch.** *Classical* slides between the historical era ("classical mechanics", "classical physics") and the narrower local-realist sense ("any classical explanation" in the Bell paragraph). *Observer* slides between the everyday "reads off properties" and the measurement-coupled QM role. Both are common physics-philosophy ambiguities rather than page defects, but worth flagging on review.
- **Cohesion landings with the ref lib.** "Separability fails" matches the closing line of [the turn in science](/positioning/seed/the-turn-in-science/). "the turn" extends the site motif from philosophy into science. The Leibniz container-vs-ordering pair matches its handling on the same ref lib page.
