## Observations

- **SPLectrum's own vocabulary grows.** Where the first post (P0–P2) mostly arranges inherited philosophical terms, this post introduces more of SPLectrum's own: indirect transmission, equal standing, interrelational, potentiality, the web of growing complexity. The social core is where SPLectrum starts speaking in its own voice.
- **P3 vocabulary is sparse by design.** P3 touches an emergent level — subject-subject interactions through the other — that isn't ready to be pinned down with defined terms. The sparseness reflects the principle's still-in-formation status, not careless writing.
- **Post split.** This page covers the second half (P3–P5). Terms for P0–P2 are on the companion page for [SPLectrum and First Principles](/blog/2026/05/splectrum-and-first-principles/).
