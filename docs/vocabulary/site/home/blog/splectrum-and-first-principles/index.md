## Observations

- **Inherited vocabulary, structural distinctiveness.** Most load-bearing terms come from philosophy / phenomenology / Wittgenstein. SPLectrum's own column is small (the seed, equal standing, interrelational). The post's distinctiveness lives in *what it arranges*, not *what it names* — coherent with the seed-as-apparatus framing on `seed/human-reality.md`.
- **P3 vocabulary is sparse by design.** P3 touches an emergent level — subject-subject interactions through the other — that isn't ready to be pinned down with defined Things. The sparseness reflects the principle's still-in-formation status, not careless writing. Don't read the asymmetry as a defect to fix.
