## Observations

- **Inherited vocabulary, structural distinctiveness.** Most load-bearing terms come from philosophy / phenomenology. SPLectrum's own column is small (the seed). The post's distinctiveness lives in *what it arranges*, not *what it names* — coherent with the seed-as-apparatus framing on `seed/human-reality.md`.
- **Post split.** This page covers the first half (P0–P2). Terms for P3–P5 (equal standing, interrelational, language game) belong to the companion post [First Principles — the Social Core](/blog/2026/05/first-principles-the-social-core/).
