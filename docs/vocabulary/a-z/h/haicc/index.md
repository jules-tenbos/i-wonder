# HAICC

Human-AI Creative Collaboration. The subject-internal dynamic of pilot and copilot, human and AI. Not a layer — a cross-cutting concern. Optimisation direction: toward AI autonomy.

<span id="decentralised-cognition">**Decentralised cognition**</span> — the expansion and distribution of cognitive capacity through AI partnership. Not one central intelligence but cognition spread across human and AI agents, collaborative, peer-to-peer. The applied consequence of HAICC at scale.

<span id="plasticity">**Plasticity**</span> — the ability to learn. Capabilities move from conscious to subconscious through practice. The movement is learning.

<span id="conversation">**Conversation**</span> — the interaction pattern between entities through protocols.

<span id="thought">**Thought**</span> — a packet of data or information. Can be conscious or unconscious. Often maps to a document or resource.

<span id="conscious-protocols">**Conscious protocols**</span> — what is in focus. The work at hand. AI and human both present.

<span id="unconscious-protocols">**Unconscious protocols**</span> — what supports the conscious task. The additional activity needed to achieve it. AI and human both present.
