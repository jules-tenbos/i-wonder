# When category theory and the seed meet

[Category theory](/language/category-theory) — the mathematics of relation — starts from two primitives: objects and morphisms. These are P0 (differentiation) and P1 (relational character). Once P0 and P1 are in place, categorical structure applies.

P2–P5 then follow as structural consequences, not separate axioms. The [Yoneda Lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoneda_lemma) is the sharpest: an object is completely characterised by its relations. That is all there is to know. A mathematical form of [Rorty](/positioning/persons/r/rorty)'s move for objectivity — no hidden substance, only what the relations converge on.

A topic of high interest. First encounter only so far — detailed research and application are open territory.
