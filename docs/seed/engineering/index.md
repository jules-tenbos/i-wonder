# The applied seed - Engineering

How SPLectrum's engineering creates fertile ground for the seed — the environment where it can blossom. Active support, not control. The work is directed at the ground the seed grows from, not at the seed itself.

The engineering language game at seed level has two sides: a vocabulary that carries the seed into engineering speech, and a structural starting position — three pillars that together form the supporting ground. One of the pillars carries the AI integration directly — human and AI as collaborative peers in the work. The other two provide enhanced support for that integration.

**[Engineering Seed Language](language-mapping)**<br>
A core vocabulary that suits the engineering environment. The vocabulary will be expanded upon as engineering moves into more detail.

**[Three pillars](three-pillars)**<br>
A fertile environment means that components can evolve with minimum friction. This is the structural setup the three pillars aim to achieve — a separation into data, language and interaction.
