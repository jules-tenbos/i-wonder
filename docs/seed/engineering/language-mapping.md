# Engineering Seed Language

The vocabulary of SPLectrum's engineering language game at seed level. Words that carry the seed's concerns into engineering speech.

Engineering shifts away from the individual POV toward interactions across a data state, and this base vocabulary reflects that.

## Words from the seed

| Seed word | Engineering | What it is |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Being | Entity | A unit of encapsulated being — the inside is being, the surface is language |
| Language | Protocol | A language game unit — one specific interaction pattern |
| Subject | Owner | The entity that owns the interior of its being — like a microservice |
| Reality | Data state | The data as it is — a snapshot |
| Shared reality | Shared data state | The data state held in common across subjects |

## Additional vocabulary

| Engineering | What it is |
|-------------|-----------|
| Data world | The totality of data a subject can potentially relate to |
| Subject reality | The data picture of an owner's subject matter — the dynamic data state Mycelium makes visible to the owner |
| Persona | A structured set of protocols — a language game role |

Protocol and persona are two levels of a language game: protocol is the unit (one interaction pattern), persona is the role (the structured set enacting it). SPLectrum engineering splits where Wittgenstein leaves "game" ambiguous.

Other words from the seed — equal standing (P4), convergence (P3), relational density (P5) — shape structural commitments rather than naming specific artefacts. They live in the [three pillars](three-pillars) as design principles.
