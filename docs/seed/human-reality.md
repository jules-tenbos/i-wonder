# The seed and Human Reality

From P2 — experience is always within the reach of a language — reality is accessed from inside, not viewed from nowhere. From P3 — language is the source of the only objectivity known: convergence of subjects — what we call reality is what a community of subjects converges on through shared language. No absolute metaphysics separable from the community whose language expresses it.

A single person lives inside many such realities at once — family, village, profession, religion, ethnicity. Each with its own vocabulary, its own relational patterns, its own ways of saying what is. Not one reality per subject, but many, overlapping in the subject who moves through them.

The same reading extends beyond the human. Every form of life inhabits a reality shaped by its own interactions — what [Uexküll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umwelt) called the *Umwelt*, the lived-world proper to a species. Further down still — chemistry, quantum contexts — "reality" becomes a question of which interactions stabilise into which forms. The structural frame reaches across all of it.

## The apparatus

That all of this can be said on one surface, across such different domains, comes from the seed together with the [universal definition of language](/language/what-is-a-language). Concepts and relations — the structural minimum holds for a family, a forest floor, a quantum measurement. They differ entirely in content. They share the form.

This is the SPLectrum distinctive. The *position* — reality as community-constituted, no view from nowhere — has deep roots: Wittgenstein on forms of life, Kuhn on paradigms, Uexküll on *Umwelten*, multinaturalism. What the seed adds is the *apparatus* that lets the position hold across domains without leaving the relational frame. Not another voice in the relativism debate — a way of putting disciplines, forms of life, and non-life on the same comparative surface.
