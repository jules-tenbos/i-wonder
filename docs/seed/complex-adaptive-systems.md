# The seed and Complex Adaptive Systems

Reading the seed principles, CAS-agent characteristics come into view. The substantive recognition sits at P2 — the subject-and-experience level, where the interaction of shared language and personal historicity creates the agent and its landscape. The mappings below make the recognition visible, principle by principle.

For the developed CAS vocabulary, see the [CAS subject page](/positioning/subjects/c/complex-adaptive-systems/).

## P0 — Being implies language
## P1 — Language is relational

P0 produces a being — tension across an interface, two sides in one act, neither privileged. P1 gives that interface relational character. These are prior conditions; no specific CAS rhyme lives at this level.

## P2 — Language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality

Experience requires language. There is no language-free experience; the subject experiences reality through its language. The language a subject has typically comes from shared reality — the community supplies the language sets the subject inherits.

Historicity is necessary for experience. Historicity weights inherited language sets through unique personal accumulation. Without that weighting, the subject has language but no subjective experience through it. Historicity gives teleology — it pushes experience in a specific direction, from within, not imposed from outside.

The interaction of these two streams — shared-language inheritance and unique-personal-historicity — creates the agent and the landscape. The agent: the distinctively shaped output-producer. The landscape: path-shaped terrain favouring some continuations over others. CAS names this dynamic: agent operating in a dynamical landscape, with self-organization as the name for the internal teleology.

For the seed-side exposition, see [the seed and Historicity](/seed/historicity/).

## P3 — Language is where subjects share knowledge about reality

The subject shares back the shape historicity made of inherited language. This sharing-back feeds into shared language, modifying it over time. The loop: shared language supplies subjects; historicity-weighted output modifies shared language; modified shared language supplies the next subjects. This is language evolution. The mechanism is the seed's; the agent dynamics from P2 drive it.

## P4 — Languages are interrelational and have equal standing in potential
## P5 — Together they form a web of growing complexity

P4 and P5 follow from P2 and P3. Once the agent-driven adaptive loop is in place and the feedback into shared language is happening, languages interact, carry equal standing in potential, and the web's relational density grows.

## The recognition and where it leads

The mappings make visible that the seed's dynamic — at the subject-and-experience level — has CAS-agent characteristics. CAS carries a well-developed, computationally tractable vocabulary for these dynamics; the [CAS subject page](/positioning/subjects/c/complex-adaptive-systems/) is where to explore it.
