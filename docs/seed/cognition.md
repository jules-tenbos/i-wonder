# The seed and Cognition

This page focuses on P2 — the medium, the subject, experience — and names the process P2 quietly presupposes.

**P2 — Language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality.**

[Heidegger](/positioning/persons/h/heidegger/)'s observation stands at the entrance: we are always already in the world. There is no moment before immersion — no subject that first exists and then acquires a meaningful world. The observation states a fact and leaves a question standing: how?

P2's gloss carries the beginning of the answer. Experience requires accumulation; a subject's reality is the totality of what has been retained; knowledge is what the subject comes to *recognise* within that accumulation. Retaining, accumulating, recognising — none of this happens by itself. For P2 to stand, a process must be running that makes something of what arrives. Without it there is interaction but no experience: a medium executing, every signal delivered, nothing made of any of it. The name for that process is cognition — *the process of acquiring knowledge and understanding through experience* — held wide: not confined to minds, reaching as far as subjecthood reaches. And it is also the answer to the *how* question at the entrance. Cognition is an essential part of being a subject.

Cognition is internal to the dynamics of the language game: it extracts from the medium and it feeds the medium — it does not stand above the medium, and it does not precede it. Being in language is P2's fact; cognition furnishes that fact from within rather than founding it from outside. Load-bearing in function, subordinate in rank.

How the making-sense runs in any particular subject — in brains, in machines, in whatever else holds the position — is contested territory with many accounts and no owner; the seed does not pick among them. That there is some process is all P2 requires.
