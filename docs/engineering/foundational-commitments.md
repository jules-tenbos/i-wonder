# Foundational Commitments

The engineering-wide commitments that inform every SPLectrum design choice. Taken before the SPLectrum architecture is reached — they explain why it has the shape it does. They apply within the SPLectrum design space; components wrapped in from outside (substrate, infrastructure) retain their own design integrity.

---

**Software engineering is the entry point.** Software is the way in — automation, paired with AI integration and decentralisation for private deployments. The broader aim: an integrated automated creation environment.

**Data first, not process first.** The natural unit of software engineering is data. Data state is primary; processes act on it. Hence the *data X* vocabulary family — data state, data entity, data owner, data world.

**No hidden state.** Whatever exists in the system is visible in some owner's data world view. Nothing lurks behind interfaces. Known by its imprint on the fabric, not by looking inside.

**Functional elements join the data state.** A logical extension of *no hidden state*: if state isn't hidden, then functional elements — protocols, schemas, metadata — also live in the data state. Facts in the fabric, not behind APIs.

**Activity is human-AI collaboration.** SPLectrum's foundational stance: human and AI as collaborative peers, no central controller. Activity is organised accordingly.

**The design lives in the logical space.** Architecture describes how a system must look, not how it must be physically built. The separation is strategic: physical implementation is the natural place for AI to take maximal autonomy, advancing the human-AI collaboration.

**Decentralisation is constitutive.** Solutions are built within a private context. Decentralisation is not an afterthought — no central database, no single source of truth, no central controller.

**Natural language as carrier.** Natural language carries meaning across the system. AI makes this operational. The carrier must be the most fluid medium available — anything narrower would close down what the language fabric can express.

**Ambiguity is generative.** The boundary is never perfectly sealed — and that is generativity, not failure. Rigid formal languages close down possibility. Natural language keeps the system open at the fringes, where new meaning enters.
