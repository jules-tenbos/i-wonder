# Engineering - The Technology

SPLectrum engineering starts from a set of [engineering commitments](engineering-commitments).

Three tracks at present, each addressing a distinct engineering concern. More tracks will appear, implemented on top of the SPLectrum platform.

---

**[Infrastructure](infrastructure/)** — external software maintained by third parties or in-house. Runtimes, platforms, and standalone modules.

**[Language Substrate](substrate/)** — the committed languages: AVRO, Git, Kafka. Each plays a specific structural role in the architecture.

**[SPLectrum](splectrum/)** — the platform itself. Logical and physical design.
