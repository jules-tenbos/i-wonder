# HAICC — Human-AI Creative Collaboration

The cognition fabric — how cognition gets distributed, expressed, and coordinated while maintaining genuine human partnership. Decentralised at the cognition level: human and AI agents as collaborative peers.

HAICC is one of SPLectrum's three engineering pillars, alongside [Mycelium](/engineering/mycelium/) (data) and SPLectrum itself (language). Where Mycelium provides the data substrate and SPLectrum provides the relational structure, HAICC provides the collaborative process.

---

## Scope

- Collaborative AI methodology — division of responsibilities, autonomy, complementary strengths
- [Personas](/engineering/personas/) — the communication channels SPLectrum operates through
- Agent workflow — how human and AI agents coordinate across the codebase
- Process models — decentralised evolutionary process, neuroscience-inspired coordination
