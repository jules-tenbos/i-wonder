# Wider Ecosystem

A curated, health-aware index of what's built on the Bare + P2P + Pear stack — first-party flagships and community alike. Entries are graded by maturity and tagged by variety.

> **Listing is not endorsement.** Freshness varies; this is a snapshot. Assess fitness for your own use.

**Grading key:**
- **Established** — mature, widely used, production-grade.
- **Active** — maintained and credible, but newer / smaller / less battle-tested.
- **Further** — early, experimental, niche, or unverified; a loose, best-effort catch-all.

Grades are a provisional snapshot, refined by freshness and discovery passes.
