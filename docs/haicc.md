# Collaborative AI

We — [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) and I — believe in collaborative AI with a clear division of responsibilities. We only collaborate where our responsibilities meet; otherwise we strongly favour autonomy. The aim is a balanced relationship where we compensate for each other's weaknesses and push each other's strengths.

[Claude Code](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/claude-code) is a natural fit for the manner in which I want to collaborate. That, together with [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com)'s research focus and willingness to share findings openly — values I hold strongly — made it an easy and the right choice.

This site is an example. A human and an AI coauthor it. The ideas, the voice, the lived experience — that's mine. The research, the groundwork, the structural rigour — that's where Claude comes in. What you read is the result: one voice, two complementary strengths.

[Teaming Up With My Alter Ego](/blog/2026/03/teaming-up-with-my-alter-ego/) tells the personal story of how this came about.
