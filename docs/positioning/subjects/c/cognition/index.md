# Cognition

This is a meta subject. It groups the resources that address the process of cognition as per [the definition this site carries](/seed/cognition/): *cognition is the process of acquiring knowledge and understanding through experience*. Everything that falls within that definition's scope belongs here — across disciplines and sources. The subject is under construction; the list below grows as facets are taken up.

## Philosophy

- **[Cognition in core philosophy](/positioning/subjects/c/cognition/philosophy/core/)** — the approaches in core philosophy that reflect the definition: cognising running within experience, not operating on it after.
- **[Cognition as a property of life](/positioning/subjects/c/cognition/philosophy/biology/)** — biology's claim on the word: cognition reaching as far as life itself.

## Biology

- **[Basal cognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basal_cognition)** — the research programme of Pamela Lyon, [Michael Levin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Levin_(biologist)) and others, studying cognition below the nervous system: sensing, memory, valence and problem-solving in cells, plants and tissues.

## Psychology

- **[Ecological psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_psychology)** — [J. J. Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_J._Gibson)'s research tradition: significance found in the environment itself, as *affordances* — what the surroundings offer the animal.
