# Cognition

This is a meta subject. It groups the resources that address the process of cognition as per [the definition this site carries](/seed/cognition/): *cognition is the process of acquiring knowledge and understanding through experience*. Everything that falls within that definition's scope belongs here — across disciplines and sources. The subject is under construction; the list below grows as facets are taken up.

## Biology

- **[Basal cognition](/positioning/subjects/c/cognition/biology/basal-cognition/)** — the research programme studying cognition below the nervous system: sensing, memory, valence and problem-solving in cells, plants and tissues.

## Neuroscience

- **[Process models of the brain](/positioning/subjects/c/cognition/neuroscience/process-models/)** — three frameworks read for the insight each brings to cognition: selection building capability over time, workspace competition settling the moment, a generative model correcting anticipation against arrival.

## Philosophy

- **[Cognition in core philosophy](/positioning/subjects/c/cognition/philosophy/core/)** — the approaches that hold cognising as running within experience, not operating on it after: Kant, the phenomenologists, the pragmatists, the process and Eastern angles.
- **[Cognition as a property of life](/positioning/subjects/c/cognition/philosophy/biology/)** — the wide reading stated philosophically: cognition reaching as far as life itself, from Uexküll's Umwelt to the Santiago theory.
