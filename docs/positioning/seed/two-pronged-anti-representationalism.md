# Two-Pronged Anti-Representationalism

[Whitehead](/positioning/persons/w/whitehead/) and [Rorty](/positioning/persons/r/rorty/) are not typically read together. Whitehead lives in process metaphysics and the continental revival; Rorty in pragmatism and post-analytic philosophy. What follows is a structural reading — not a report on how the literature pairs them, but how, from SPLectrum's point of view, the two dismantle representationalism from opposite sides of one gesture.

---

## The shared diagnostic

Representationalism is the view that there is a way the world is, independently of any vocabulary or experience of it, and that the task of thought is to mirror that way accurately. Since [Descartes](/positioning/persons/d/descartes/), this picture has held Western philosophy in two registers. A metaphysical register: the world has primary, mind-independent qualities that science describes; secondary qualities are derivative effects on observers. An epistemological register: the mind represents the world by mirroring it; truth is correspondence.

Anti-representationalism refuses both registers. Whitehead refuses the metaphysical register. Rorty refuses the epistemological register. Each refusal is necessary; neither alone is sufficient.

---

## Whitehead's half: from the side of nature

The diagnostic move is in *Science and the Modern World* (1925). The bifurcation of nature names the split, inherited from Galileo and Descartes, between the nature physics measures and the nature we experience. For modern thought the first is real; the second is derivative. Whitehead refuses the split.

The cure is the [philosophy of organism](/positioning/subjects/p/philosophy-of-organism/) — a speculative metaphysics in which the split never opens because relations are constitutive of what anything is. Actual entities, prehension, concrescence: the apparatus is built so that nothing in the world stands outside its relations to other things. There is no primary substrate of mind-independent qualities for science to mirror; there are only actual occasions, each one constituted by how it takes account of others.

What Whitehead reaches: relations as constitutive at the level of nature itself. The metaphysical register of representationalism is refused at the foundation. This is resonant with what P1 articulates — the relational character of being, where what something is depends on its relations, not on a hidden inner nature.

Where Whitehead stops: the linguistic dimension of the anti-representational move. His system is metaphysical; he does not develop the dimension where what dismantles the mirror is not the structure of nature but the structure of language. The philosophy of organism makes language one element within a metaphysics; it does not make language the medium in which the dismantling happens.

---

## Rorty's half: from the side of language

The diagnostic move is in *Philosophy and the Mirror of Nature* (1979). The picture of mind as a mirror of nature — the picture that has held Western philosophy captive since Descartes — is not a theory to be refuted but a framework to be set aside. No vocabulary occupies a privileged outside position from which it reports the world as it really is. Truth is not correspondence but something closer to solidarity — what holds up under continued conversation among people who take each other seriously.

Rorty's [pragmatist](/positioning/subjects/p/pragmatism/) inheritance — [James](/positioning/persons/j/james/), [Dewey](/positioning/persons/d/dewey/), the later [Wittgenstein](/positioning/persons/w/wittgenstein/) — carries this further. *Contingency, Irony, and Solidarity* (1989) develops the position into a constructive pragmatism: vocabularies are tools, not pictures; final vocabularies are contingent — shaped by history and circumstance, not by access to the way things really are. The philosophical task is not to find the right vocabulary but to keep the conversation going.

What Rorty reaches: language as relational at the foundation. The epistemological register of representationalism is refused because the very picture of mind-as-mirror is refused. This rhymes with P3 — shared reality through shared language — and with P4's equal standing, where no vocabulary ranks another.

Where Rorty stops: the gradient below human conversation. Rorty works within the human linguistic field; he does not extend the move to non-human or non-symbolic relations. His pluralism is a pluralism of vocabularies among speakers. Objects are what we talk about, not what speaks.

---

## Two halves joined within the seed

Two halves, two refusals, one gesture. Whitehead delivers relations-as-constitutive on the side of nature — the bifurcation refused at the metaphysical level. Rorty delivers language-as-relational on the side of medium — the mirror refused at the epistemological level.

Within the SPLectrum seed, both halves are naturally positioned. P1 articulates the relational character of being — what something is depends on its relations, partial in reach and contingent in character. P3 names the medium: shared reality through shared language. P4 names the structure of the medium: languages with equal standing, none ranking another. Whitehead without Rorty has the relational ontology but not the linguistic medium. Rorty without Whitehead has the linguistic medium but not the relational ontology that extends below human conversation.

The principles go beyond what either half names. Beyond Whitehead, the language side is load-bearing — the medium of relation is named explicitly (P3). Beyond Rorty, the gradient extends below human conversation — P4's languages with equal standing include non-human and non-symbolic ones. The conversation Rorty keeps going is one slice of a wider relational structure.

The creative mechanism is the dynamic that P4 and P0 together name. When inter-relational languages with equal standing overlap (P4), the overlap is where differentiation produces new being and language again (P0). The anti-representational move clears the ground; the creative dynamic is what grows in the cleared space.
