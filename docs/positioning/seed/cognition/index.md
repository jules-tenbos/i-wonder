# Cognition held close

The seed holds cognition as the process within P2 — running *as* the subject experiences reality, not after it. The position is stated in full at [the seed and Cognition](/seed/cognition/). Around it gathers a current: thinkers across several traditions — never one school, and never under one name — who hold cognition close to experience. No second process working on what arrives; no arrival that is not already loaded. This bundle reads that current from the seed's side.

**[Cognition and core philosophy](/positioning/seed/cognition/cognition-and-core-philosophy/)** — the positions in core philosophy that hold cognition fused with the experiencing of reality, stated in their own terms with their resonance to the seed read alongside.

The bundle admits by criterion, not topic: positions that move cognition into experience, life and activity — members of the current. It is not a survey of the field, and it does not take up the field's process models. The field on its own terms — every cut, including those this bundle would never hold — is mapped in the [cognition subject](/positioning/subjects/c/cognition/).
