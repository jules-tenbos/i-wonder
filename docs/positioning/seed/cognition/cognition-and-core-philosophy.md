# Cognition and Core Philosophy

The seed holds cognition as the process within P2 — running *as* the subject experiences reality, with no second process working on what arrives ([the seed and Cognition](/seed/cognition/)). Core philosophy holds a family of positions that arrive at the same closeness, each in its own vocabulary. This page states those positions and reads their resonance with the seed's, current by current. The depth on each thinker lives on their page; the resonance is what is added here.

---

## The constitutive origin

[Kant](/positioning/persons/k/kant/) argued that cognition is constituted by the mind's own activity: the understanding's synthesis runs in all experience — intuitions without concepts are blind — so there is no experiencing first and cognising after. *The resonance:* the fusion's first modern statement, and the seed shares the constitutive claim entire. The parting is additive: where Kant gave every rational being one fixed apparatus of categories, the seed has each subject running its own process, historicity-laden — the direction the traditions after him also took.

## The phenomenological current

[Husserl](/positioning/persons/h/husserl/) found sense-constitution running beneath active thought — passive synthesis: experience organised before the ego does anything. [Heidegger](/positioning/persons/h/heidegger/) placed the subject always already in a meaningful world, the detached knower a derivative stance. [Merleau-Ponty](/positioning/persons/m/merleau-ponty/) put the work in the body: perception arrives whole and significant, nothing waiting for interpretation. [Gadamer](/positioning/persons/g/gadamer/) grounded all understanding in pre-judgement delivered by tradition — the enabling ground, not a contamination. *The resonance:* this current holds the experiencing itself — P2's dynamic in phenomenological vocabulary: the medium delivering reality already meaningful, the process never not running. Gadamer reaches furthest into the seed's territory: effective history is priming by belonging — tooling delivered, carrying understanding already acquired.

## The analytic refusal

[Ryle](/positioning/persons/r/ryle/) showed the inner-act picture regresses: intelligent performance cannot require a prior act of cognising — knowing-how is not knowing-that applied. [Wittgenstein](/positioning/persons/w/wittgenstein/) told philosophy to stop thinking of understanding as a mental process at all: understanding shows itself in use, against a background of training. [Peirce](/positioning/persons/p/peirce/), earlier, dissolved the line from the other end — perceptual judgment as extreme abduction. *The resonance:* the refusal of the second act is the seed's no-separation stated in analytic key; language games are the medium met as practice; and training is belonging's delivery mechanism seen from the community's side.

## The process current

[Bergson](/positioning/persons/b/bergson/): no perception which is not full of memories — the unloaded percept never occurs, and perception selects, carving what matters from a richer whole. [James](/positioning/persons/j/james/): the relations are given in the stream of experience, not supplied afterwards. [Dewey](/positioning/persons/d/dewey/): the spectator retired; knowing as a mode of doing within the situation. [Whitehead](/positioning/persons/w/whitehead/): every actual entity takes up its world — experience-like process all the way down. [Hegel](/positioning/persons/h/hegel/) stands behind them: experience as self-transforming, the running reshaping the runner. *The resonance:* this current carries the process side of the seed's position — cognition extracting from the medium and feeding it, the subject's reality accumulating through the run. Bergson's selection is the extraction; James's given relations are meaning already in the medium; Whitehead's reach anticipates the seed's — the process wherever history enters response, well past the human case.

## The Eastern statements

[Yogācāra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogachara) analysed experience as arising with no split between grasper and grasped, each perception conditioned by stored seeds of past experience. The Madhyamaka of [Candrakīrti](/positioning/persons/c/candrakirti/) made dependent origination basic: the knowing arises with its conditions and has no essence apart from them. [Abhinavagupta](/positioning/persons/a/abhinavagupta/)'s Pratyabhijñā school held manifestation and reflective awareness strictly inseparable — awareness fused with what appears. [Zhuangzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhuangzi_(book))'s cook cuts with nothing deliberative behind the knife — the knowing in the doing. *The resonance:* the fusion stated outside the Western trajectory entirely, which is its own evidence that the closeness is not one tradition's artefact. Two meetings are close enough to name: Pratyabhijñā — the school of *recognition* — meets the seed's own anchor word, knowledge as what the subject comes to recognise; and the conditioned arising of the knowing meets each subject running its own process, differently conditioned, differently knowing.

---

The family gathered here spans traditions that rarely cite one another, and it never organised under one name. The seed stands among these positions, not above them — and goes on where they leave off: the priming of a subject's process is not only its own past but others' deposits, delivered by belonging; and the process reaches wherever history enters response. Those extensions are the seed's own. Everything up to them, this family already holds.
