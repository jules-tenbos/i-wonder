# Sharing Knowledge

**P3 — language is where subjects share knowledge about reality.** A creation starts as private experience; for it to have shared existence it must be added to shared reality through language — and what is added is the expression, not the creation itself. Sharing happens by different mechanisms at different scales, from the copying of a repertoire to the coupling of two minds. These pages read those mechanisms across the fields that study them.

- [Human ways of sharing](human-ways-of-sharing) — developmental psychology and language acquisition on convergence, divergence, and coupling
- [Tools shared, use private](tools-shared-use-private) — convergence and divergence as two directions of one fact, read across Kelly, Vygotsky, Tomasello, Piaget and Di Paolo, meeting in the coupling of minds
