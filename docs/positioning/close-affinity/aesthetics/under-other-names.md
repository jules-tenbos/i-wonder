# Under other names

A step further out, the dynamic of meaning is just as visible but goes by other words. In the traditions of interpretation and reception it runs as *understanding*, as *disclosure*, as *relish* — never filed under aesthetics, and no less the generative motion of meaning for that. Look at where meaning is described as *arriving*.

---

**[Hermeneutics](/positioning/subjects/h/hermeneutics/)**, in [Heidegger](/positioning/persons/h/heidegger/): language is not a tool laid over a finished thought but the medium through which meaning discloses — the element in which anything comes to show itself at all, with poetry the keeping-open of that disclosive power. Here the dynamic runs under the name of disclosure. (The depth behind the name — Schleiermacher's general art of understanding, Dilthey's method of the human sciences — is carried on the hermeneutics page.)

**Hermeneutics**, in [Gadamer](/positioning/persons/g/gadamer/): understanding happens as a *fusion of horizons* — meaning arrives in the meeting of interpreter and text, and neither party is left unchanged. Convergence is the source of shared sense, the same motion SPLectrum reads. Gadamer carries that meeting on to *truth*; whether and how the convergence bears on truth is a question the surface holds open rather than settling here — the meeting is the resonance, its reach left in play.

**The [rasa tradition](/positioning/subjects/r/rasa/)**, in [Abhinavagupta](/positioning/persons/a/abhinavagupta/): the sharpest statement on the site of meaning arriving across the interface between expression and reception. In *sadharanikarana*, generalisation, the depicted emotion is lifted free both of the particular character and of the spectator's own ego, so that a feeling that is privately one person's becomes a relish that can be shared — the most shared and the most private of things meeting in a single act, belonging and privacy at once. Held as a peer theory of meaning, not as exotica. The rasa tradition follows the dynamic into reception and on toward a contemplative end beyond art; SPLectrum carries the same line on to the making side as well.

---

Two traditions, three faces, and in none of them the word *aesthetics*. The motion runs here as understanding, as disclosure, as the relish of a shared feeling — interpretation and reception, meaning described as arriving in the meeting. This is the region of the scatter where the dynamic kept its work and changed its name.
