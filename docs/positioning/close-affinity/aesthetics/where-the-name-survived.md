# Where the name survived

This is the region of the scatter nearest the name — the traditions that kept the word, working openly as aesthetics, the philosophy of art, poetics. The dynamic of meaning runs close to the surface here, and it appears under each tradition's own term: as expression, as metaphor, as symbolic form. Set them side by side.

---

**Expression theory** — [Croce](/positioning/persons/c/croce/), then [Collingwood](/positioning/persons/c/collingwood/) — held that art and language are at bottom one activity. Croce: to intuit something fully is already to express it, and "the philosophy of language and the philosophy of art are the same thing." The expressive act is named, and made the first form of knowing — the dynamic, here, called expression. SPLectrum reads the same identity of art and language and carries it on, to the shared medium the act draws on.

Collingwood worked the same identity and moved its centre. The artist begins not from a finished intuition but from "a vague perturbation" that becomes articulate only *through* the act of expressing it — meaning made in the making, not transcribed from a thought already complete. It is the clearest single statement in the cast of the generative core SPLectrum puts first: uniqueness formed in the working, reality added rather than read out. And where Croce kept the spark within the individual, Collingwood socialised it — the audience re-performs the expressive act, so understanding is itself a re-attunement, the meeting of expression and reception.

**The theory of metaphor**, in [Ricoeur](/positioning/persons/r/ricoeur/): metaphor is no ornament but "an event of thought." A living metaphor "redescribes reality" — holding two senses in tension to make a meaning that was not there before, rather than relabelling an old one — the dynamic at its smallest visible unit, one turn of phrase enlarging what can be said.

**[Symbolic forms](/positioning/subjects/s/symbolic-forms/)**, in [Cassirer](/positioning/persons/c/cassirer/): language, myth, art, religion, science are each a distinct symbolic form, each forming a world by its own logic, none reducible to another — register-plurality at world-forming scale, the many shared codes a subject lives among holding equal standing. His forms are forms *for a subject*, the transcendental seating that surfaced in the Davos debate with Heidegger; SPLectrum reads the same plurality of codes without that seating — a difference in reading, not a fault to fix.

---

Four enclaves, and in each the same generative motion of meaning, present under the tradition's own name — expression, metaphor, symbolic form. This is the region of the scatter nearest the label, where the dynamic runs closest to the surface. Whether aesthetics ranks above or below philosophy's other branches is a separate argument — the [counter-current's](/positioning/subjects/a/aesthetics-in-philosophy/), held on its own page — and not this surface's fight.
