# Dialogism, pragmatism, deconstruction, cybernetics

In linguistics, social theory, the philosophy of language, and cybernetics, the dynamic of meaning does core work with no aesthetics anywhere in view. Here it is not even a department; it is the load-bearing thing, going by names no one would shelve under art. Look at what is doing the work.

---

**[Dialogism](/positioning/subjects/d/dialogism/)**, in [Bakhtin](/positioning/persons/b/bakhtin/): a single utterance is already plural — *heteroglossia*, the many social voices alive in one tongue, and *double-voiced discourse*, two accents in one word. "The word in language is half someone else's" — the shared register and the private charge contending inside one sentence. (Behind Bakhtin, Voloshinov's account of the social sign is carried on the dialogism page.)

**Pragmatism**, in [Dewey](/positioning/persons/d/dewey/): in *Art as Experience* the aesthetic is the quality of a *transaction* between a living being and its environment — meaning made in the encounter, not deposited in an object and read off later. Here the dynamic shows as relation itself — the meaning living in the transaction, not in either pole.

**[Deconstruction](/positioning/subjects/d/deconstruction/)**, in [Derrida](/positioning/persons/d/derrida/): *différance* — meaning differential and endlessly deferred, for Derrida never coming fully to rest. That it never quite closes is the resonance here; how far meaning nonetheless settles is taken up on the SPLectrum side, not closed in Derrida.

**Cybernetics**, in [Bateson](/positioning/persons/b/bateson/): "a difference that makes a difference" — his definition of information, and of meaning, as purely differential, now at the scale of whole systems. The same differential pole runs in Derrida's *différance* and the [structuralists'](/positioning/subjects/s/structuralism/) sign; here it turns up from a wholly different ground, with no aesthetics in sight.

---

Four grounds — dialogism, pragmatism, deconstruction, cybernetics — and on none of them is the dynamic called aesthetics; it does core work here as the dialogic word, as transaction, as *différance*, as the difference that makes a difference.
