# Hermeneutics and rasa

In the traditions of interpretation and reception, the dynamic of meaning is just as visible but goes by other words — it runs as *understanding*, as *disclosure*, as *relish*, never filed under aesthetics and no less the motion of meaning for that. Look at where meaning is described as *arriving*.

---

**[Hermeneutics](/positioning/subjects/h/hermeneutics/)**, in [Heidegger](/positioning/persons/h/heidegger/): language is not a tool laid over a finished thought but the medium through which meaning discloses — the element in which anything comes to show itself at all, with poetry the keeping-open of that disclosive power. Here the dynamic runs under the name of disclosure. (The depth behind the name — Schleiermacher's general art of understanding, Dilthey's method of the human sciences — is carried on the hermeneutics page.)

**Hermeneutics**, in [Gadamer](/positioning/persons/g/gadamer/): understanding happens as a *fusion of horizons* — meaning arrives in the meeting of interpreter and text, and neither party is left unchanged. Gadamer carries it on to *truth*; whether and how the meeting bears on truth is a question the surface holds open rather than settling here — the meeting itself is the resonance, its reach left in play.

**The [rasa tradition](/positioning/subjects/r/rasa/)**, in [Abhinavagupta](/positioning/persons/a/abhinavagupta/): the sharpest statement on the site of meaning arriving across the interface between expression and reception. In *sadharanikarana*, generalisation, the depicted emotion is lifted free both of the particular character and of the spectator's own ego, so that a feeling that is privately one person's becomes a relish that can be shared — the most shared and the most private of things meeting in a single act. Held as a peer theory of meaning, not as exotica. The rasa tradition follows the dynamic into reception, and on toward a contemplative end beyond art; the making side is taken up elsewhere.

---

Two traditions, three faces, and in none of them the word *aesthetics*. The motion runs here as understanding, as disclosure, as the relish of a shared feeling — interpretation and reception, meaning described as arriving in the meeting.
