# Robert Sapolsky

Stanford neuroendocrinologist and primatologist whose work on human behavioural biology traces behaviour across layers — genetics, hormones, neurobiology, ecology, evolution.

Sits in wider landscape: the seed is structural — how language and relation work. Sapolsky's contribution is language content — what we come to say about behaviour and its biological substrate. Different layers, adjacent subject matter.
