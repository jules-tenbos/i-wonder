# Views on the Boundary

Frameworks and narratives with structural parallels to the seed. They arrive from different starting points, use different vocabularies, and reach different conclusions — but the parallels are worth examining.

Equal standing means taking these views seriously on their own terms. Not endorsement, not dismissal. Where parallels with the seed appear, they are noted. Where the frameworks diverge, the divergence is examined — what structural commitment produces the difference, and what that difference reveals about both sides.
