# The 9-Box Homepage

The SPLectrum homepage presents a 3×3 grid of nine boxes. Read as a structural artifact rather than a layout choice, the grid shows a coherence worth setting out. This page describes what the grid is doing.

## The grid

![The 9-box homepage grid](/assets/images/9-box-homepage.png)

## Reading the rows

The rows describe three layers of how the project relates to its material.

The **top row** is *applied* — where insights are put to work, in three registers. [In Wonder](/in-wonder/) is conversational application: dialogue with thinkers and ideas, in small units. [Real Life](/real-life/) is structural application: substantial pieces on real-world domains. [Engineering](/engineering/) is technological application: the software substrate.

The **middle row** is *framework* — the project's conceptual structure. [Positioning](/positioning/) situates SPLectrum in the wider intellectual context. [Seed](/seed/) sets out the core principles. [Reality](/reality/) is the knowledge-construct built on top of the seed.

The **bottom row** is *machinery* — the material handlers. [Language](/language/) is the relational medium. [Vocabulary](/vocabulary/) is the concepts. [Tools](/tools/) is what one reaches for, defined by use.

The bottom is the machinery, the middle is the framework, the top is where both are put to work.

## Reading the columns

The columns surface a coherence the rows alone do not show.

The **left column** (In Wonder, Positioning, Language) runs along an engaging/relational spine. Each box is about engagement with what is outside the project: thinkers in conversation, the wider intellectual landscape, the relational medium itself.

The **centre column** (Real Life, Seed, Vocabulary) runs along a what-is spine. Each box names substantive content: lived structural reality, the principles at the core, the concepts deployed.

The **right column** (Engineering, Reality, Tools) runs along a constructed/instrumental spine. Each box is what is built or wielded: applied technology, the knowledge-construct, the toolkit.

Seed sits at the centre. Read column-wise, it anchors the substantive spine. Read row-wise, it anchors the framework. Read as the centre of a 3×3, it is the point the other eight boxes express from different angles.

## A reflexive observation

The homepage is itself a Real Life artifact — a structural application of the SPLectrum worldview to the question of how the project presents itself in public. The grid is not a neutral layout; it is a substantive structural choice.

That makes the grid self-including: it contains a Real Life box, and it is itself a Real Life thing. The grid does not sit outside the structure it organises. This is consistent with the project's anti-foundationalism — nothing stands outside. The framework that organises the work is itself part of the work, produced by the same worldview it expresses, available to the same scrutiny.

## What follows

The grid is treatable as content. The same material can be approached as a blog post (conversational register) and as a Real Life piece (this page). Both hold without contradiction; each does what the other format cannot.

The columns are usable as navigation logic. The engaging column, the what-is column, and the constructed column each carry their own register, and pages within them benefit from being read against that register.

The grid will continue to shift as the project grows. What is set out here is what the grid is currently showing; future revisions may make it show more, or differently.
