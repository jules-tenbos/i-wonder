# The Home of Interrelational Pluralism

A [foundational philosophy](/seed/) about the structure of reality, grown from seed — six principles on language, subjects, and relation. No discipline privileged, no meta-language above the rest. Three core values — belonging, privacy, creativity — recalibrating the pillars of [metaphysics](/reality/).

This site doesn't present a finished product but documents SPLectrum as a process of disclosure — a process where diverse voices meet and mix, held in pluralism rather than resolved.
