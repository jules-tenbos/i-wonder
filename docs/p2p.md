# P2P

Peer-to-peer is not a technology choice. It is the structure of experience itself.

Every experience of reality belongs to a subject — personal, situated, irreducible. Reality is not experienced centrally and then distributed. It is experienced locally and then shared. Two subjects sharing their experience — that is already a peer-to-peer network. The most fundamental one.

SPLectrum takes this seriously. If the [seed principles](/seed) hold — being implies language, language is inter-relational, languages have equal standing in potential — then decentralisation is not a design preference. It conforms to how reality is constituted.

## Why it matters

The centralised internet concentrates control over data, identity, and computation. Someone else holds your data. Someone else decides what you can run. Someone else owns the infrastructure your digital life depends on. This is not a technical problem — it is a structural one. Centralisation assumes a view from above. The seed says there is no view from above.

P2P reverses the direction. Data lives where it belongs — with the subject. Identity is self-sovereign. Computation happens at the edge. No intermediary decides what is allowed. The architecture matches the structure of experience rather than contradicting it.

## P2P in SPLectrum

SPLectrum builds on [Holepunch](https://holepunch.to) — the P2P platform behind [Bare](/engineering/bare/), [Pear](https://docs.pear.holepunch.to), and the distributed infrastructure that makes peer-to-peer practical. Not theoretical P2P. Working P2P.

[Mycelium](/engineering/mycelium/) — the data fabric — is decentralised by design. The git repository is the hard boundary. There is no central database, no single source of truth. Only subject realities, shared through exchange. HAICC — the cognition fabric — distributes cognition the same way. Human and AI as peers, not master and servant.

The centralised internet is not the enemy to be dismantled. It is the substrate. Decentralised ecosystems emerge on top of what already exists. Not revolution, but emergence. Not replacement, but transcendence.

## The deeper point

Decentralisation is often argued on ethical or political grounds — freedom, privacy, resistance to power. Those arguments matter. But the seed goes deeper. Decentralisation is not primarily about what we should build. It is about what reality already is. Every subject constitutes its own reality through its own language. Shared reality is the overlap, not the imposition.

Building P2P is building in alignment with that structure. Building centralised is building against it. Both work. One is swimming with the current.
