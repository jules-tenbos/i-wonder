A note arrives. Not from nothing — it *prehends* the notes that came before it, feels them as its given past. The prior phrase, the silence around it, the listener's attentive state — all taken up, all entering this moment as data. Pitch, timbre, duration *ingress* as pure potentials, giving the note its qualitative feel. These take-ups integrate through phases of becoming, each one narrowing what this note will be. Whitehead calls this process *concrescence*: the self-constitution of a determinate event from the many it inherits.

The note reaches its *satisfaction* — its achieved definiteness, the moment it is fully this and nothing else. Then it *perishes*. It is no longer becoming. But it has not vanished. It enters what Whitehead calls *objective immortality*: ceased becoming, but living on as a datum available for the next note to prehend. The melody moves forward because each note takes up the perished satisfaction of its predecessors. The melody itself is not a single occasion but a *society* of them — a pattern sustained across many actual occasions, each prehending its predecessors, each contributing its own satisfaction forward.

This is the basic unit of Whitehead's philosophy of organism: the *actual occasion*. Not a substance, not an enduring thing — a determinate event of self-constitution. Everything else in *Process and Reality* is built from it. What we call persisting things — a stone, a person, a melody — are societies: patterns of occasions, not things that endure unchanged through time.

The vocabulary is demanding. Prehension, ingression, concrescence, satisfaction, objective immortality — each term does specific, non-substitutable work. This is not obscurity for its own sake. It is the cost of two methodological commitments that run through the entire system.

The first: a real primitive doing genuine work. The actual occasion is not a placeholder or an abstraction. It is the fully real unit from which the system builds. The concrete event is what the system builds from. The note is not a metaphor for becoming; it is a becoming.

The second: a structured process flow built from the primitive. Concrescence is not a vague "things become." It is phased, directional, and determinate. The occasion prehends, integrates, achieves satisfaction, and perishes into objective immortality — and that perishing is the link to subsequent occasions. The flow is not added on top of the primitive; it is how the primitive works.

These two commitments — real primitive, structured flow — are what give *Process and Reality* its architectural weight. They are also what make it difficult. The system asks you to follow a single event through its entire process of self-constitution before you see what "process" means at the level of the whole. The reward is a picture where nothing is assumed that has not been built, and nothing is built that does not do work.

The seed takes up the same methodological discipline — real primitive, structured flow — with [category theory](/blog/label/category-theory/) in spirit. That story is for a later post.
