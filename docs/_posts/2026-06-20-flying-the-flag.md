Free, at last. That is how I feel. Why? Because writing this post I can speak the SPLectrum voice with freedom. I won't be talking about the seed and the common structure where belonging to a wide shared base is important. I can speak freely for myself, my voice as part of the pluralistic conversation.

And to start I want to lay out in broad lines what will drive the SPLectrum metaphysics. In the transition post [Building from the Seed](/blog/2026/06/building-from-the-seed/) I introduced the parameters: the subject point of view and the three core values - belonging, privacy and creativity. Whatever happens around us, whatever is claimed about it - it is always witnessed and then shared if it wasn't personally witnessed. Our metaphysics is not going to be given to us on stone tablets as coming from out there, it will take shape through disclosure.

I find this really empowering, my reality starts with its disclosure to me. When I open my eyes it is always privacy first, belonging second. The same for everyone else, what a beautiful structural equality! And yes there is plenty that is shared, where I belong to a community of like-minded but it is mine to decide.

There is a price to pay: there is nothing and nobody to tell us 'this is true and that is false, or this is right and that is wrong'. It is up to us who belong to figure that out - together. From individual and across communities, that is where diversity makes us stronger.  

That straight away gives a strong direction for SPLectrum epistemology. Absolute does not exist, be it objectivity or truth. [need to expand]

Where epistemology is about process, how do I get to what I (can) know, ontology is about the things I know, beings. Because of the process how we come to knowledge, the experience through language, the sharing through language, in ontology the beings settle strongly on language concepts - they are basically the same thing. However, nobody or nothing is giving them to us, we create and share them. And even despite the diversity of language we converge on a common catalog, forgetting in the process that everything starts within the privacy of personal experience. It could have been different, we created it.

[paragraph about creativity]

[on the importance of aesthetics] aesthetics, traditionally being concerned with sensory experience takes on an important role in SPLectrum since there is no reality without sensory experience. And since experience is mediated by language, aesthetics can be seen as dealing with the beauty, purity, effectiveness of language, with the means and tools we use to share and create belonging, eventually how well we function as communities.

[paragraph on ethics] How well we function - this lands us into ethics as the last metaphysics pillar, not because it is the least important but - in SPLectrum view - it gets its strength from the other three, and most importantly of aesthetics as the high resonance between intent and interaction, between the aim and how it is conveyed.

The absence of absolute values seems to easily instill fear - objectivity as subjectivity converging is often pushed away. In fact it should be embraced. If objectivity is where our subjectivities converge, then we all have an equal stake in shaping what counts as real.
