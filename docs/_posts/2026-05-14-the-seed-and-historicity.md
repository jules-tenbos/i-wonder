- Trace vs retention: subject is a being whose activity turns trace into retention
- The chain historicity enables: experience, reality, shared reality, knowledge
- Self-creation through language accumulation — chemistry to human life
- First movement: how we see reality through our languages
- Classical science angle: time-symmetric laws, entropy, Rovelli's memory work ("is like," not "is")
