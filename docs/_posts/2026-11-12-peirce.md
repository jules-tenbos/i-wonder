Positioning post. Non-SPLectrum voice, outside voice. Signpost: semiotics below the human.

**Storyline points:**

- Triadic semiotics: sign, object, interpretant — any system that interprets signs is semiotic
- Categories: Firstness, Secondness, Thirdness — structural, not anthropocentric
- Pragmaticism — his term, distinguishing from James/Dewey pragmatism
- The bridge to biosemiotics: Peirce's semiotics is what biosemiotics builds on
