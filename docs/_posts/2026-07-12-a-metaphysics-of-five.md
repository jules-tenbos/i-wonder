Life is about ups and downs. In Flying the Flag I felt free as a bird, and I ended up having to deal with a headache in SPLectrum's Aesthetics. And now I am on the up again. In this post I continue the journey into metaphysics, having walked from epistemology and ontology into SPLectrum's aesthetics the totality of SPLectrum's metaphysics is starting to dawn on me. 

I don't work in academics, what I do here is my hobby - you can call me an amateur. I especially enjoy the freedom of thought it allows me - not being tied into any of the tribal setups. My interest is to study wide, and on purpose not that deep (on detail) - for our knowlegde, wisdom to have real value it must survive when crossing the boundary of our local tribe, anthropocentrism.   

But now I landed myself in the midst of philosophy, forced to apply more rigeur and study deeper. A lot of the blog posts is the conversation of me dealing with it. A reason a lot of posts are about a small step forward. This post is no exception. After realising how important the dynamic of expression of maening is to SPLectrum, I thought it makes sense to position the body of SPLectrum's metaphysics before proceeding with ethics and politics. 

SPLectrum’s metaphysics takes shape through disclosure: reality shows itself to the subject through the language medium, rather than being derived from first principles or mirrored from a world standing behind experience. What it discloses is not left as description but built into a positive account — a reality that is relational and in motion, made from the subject’s seat rather than from any standpoint outside it. 

Three resonance areas: disclosure (Heidegger, Merleau-ponty, gadamer), the relational and the process (Whitehead, Bergson,Bateson, Luhmann), anti-representational (created from within, not offered for description from outside)

That account rests on five pillars: framed here from the position of a human subject, though they do not depend on it — a widening of the subject will illustrate this at a later stage.

I covered three of them, and these are internal facing: how do I learn, what entities exist and how do I work with the relational. SPlectrum add two more, and these are outward looking: ethics - what are the values embedded in the way that I interact with what is around me - and politics - the intereactions of coexistence between an individual and its communities and between communities.
