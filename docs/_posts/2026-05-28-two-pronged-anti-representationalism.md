Whitehead and Rorty don't show up in the same sentence often. Process metaphysics and pragmatism keep to their own neighbourhoods. So it takes a moment to notice that both of them refuse representationalism. Worth sitting with the pairing.

Whitehead does it on the side of being. He refuses the split between the world physics measures and the world we experience — the bifurcation of nature — and builds a relational metaphysics where nothing stands outside its relations. The actual entity *is* its prehensions; nothing is a self-contained thing first and secondarily related. There is no inside-the-mind that represents an outside-the-mind, because the inside/outside line gets dismantled at the metaphysical level.

Rorty does it on the side of language. He sets aside the mirror — the picture of mind as a reflector of nature — and switches the conversation. Knowledge isn't accurate representation; truth isn't correspondence. Both are what survives in our practices of justification. Truth as solidarity. He doesn't argue against representation so much as walk out of the room where representation was the only game.

Two refusals, two starting points. The instinct is to read them as parallel attacks on the same target — two people knocking on the same door from different sides. They're not. Whitehead's move is internal to a being-focused language game: he changes how being is structured so representation can't get a foothold. Rorty's move is internal to a language-focused one: he changes how language is used so representation stops being asked of it. Each does what their own game allows. Neither does the other's work.

What brings them together, for SPLectrum, is P0. Being implies language. The two refusals address the two co-implicated sides of what P0 names — the being-side and the language-side of one structure. Not parallel attacks. Co-implicated halves of the same job.

This matters because if you only refuse representationalism from one side, you've covered half the structure. Whitehead alone leaves the language-side untouched — and his relational metaphysics can quietly re-import representational form, because the prehensions inside entities still behave like internal contents about external things. Rorty alone leaves the being-side untouched — brilliant at refusing the old questions, but he doesn't give you a way of seeing relations as constitutive rather than expressive. P0 puts the two refusals in the same frame, where they finish each other.
