The two-pronged post closed with a question: take Rorty's minimal position in language and add Whitehead's process and rigour — how? The Whitehead post showed where the limit sits: process theory ends up as one language among many in a crowded room, not the structure beneath. This post accepts that limit and works with it.

Keep Whitehead's primitive-plus-structure approach — a real primitive doing genuine work, a structured process flow built from it. Drop the meta-status claim. The result is a minimalist process theory that doesn't pretend to be the structure beneath everything. It is a chosen meta-language, deliberately one among many. The minimalism is Rorty's, applied at the meta-level. The choice is made with discipline, not arbitrarily — it has to serve structural articulation — but the choice is made knowing it is a choice.

The substantive articulation of the dynamic lives in [interrelational pluralism](/seed/interrelational-pluralism/). What follows here is a parallel expression of the same dynamic, in a form friendly toward formal and process methodologies. The dynamic stays what it is; the angle changes.

The minimum the dynamic needs to run. A relational primitive: distinction-with-relation, symmetric binary creation, content-agnostic. Any "thing" that shows up — physical, mental, factual — is the same structural operation: a cut made, the relation across it holding in the same act. Subjects: loci constituted by historicity, accumulated retention of differentiations. Language sets: encapsulated bundles of distinctions — a small one (hot/cold, wet/dry), a larger one (natural language), a specialised one (law vocabulary, sport concepts).

Mixing: the operation that combines language sets combinatorially within a subject. Hot/cold combined with wet/dry produces four states, none of which existed in either input alone. P0 iteration: the same operation reappearing at scale — a community is a new being differentiated from its context, P0 firing again at the community level. Nothing imported. Small set, Rorty-clean.

Complexity of language is the rule, not the exception. A subject does not carry one language game. It carries many language sets, interacting combinatorially in the cauldron. On this picture, subjecthood is constituted by this multiplicity in motion. Language sets are not tools the subject reaches for — they are what the subject is.

Historicity isn't a line. It's a path landscape — terrain shaped by accumulation, with valleys, ridges, basins. The past doesn't lay down a single track; it shapes the topology across which future paths can move, favouring some routes and making others harder. The directionality is not pre-conceived, not deterministic — but it is real. The past favours specific futures. This is embryonic teleology, living in the landscape's contours. Agency as we recognise it is this embryonic teleology developed further — same kind, different degree. The subject-to-agent transition is developmental continuation, not categorical leap.

At each mixing event, a distribution of outcomes is possible — the subject's path landscape shapes the distribution. Across events, path-dependent crystallisation: each move sets up conditions for the next, the trajectory accreting without being pre-laid. Subjects engage indirectly. There is no direct subject-to-subject channel. Each subject has its own "other" — its environment-from-its-perspective. One subject's expression modulates its own other; another subject reads that as a modulation of its own other. Engagement reduces to coupled cauldrons mutually modulating through what they share. The mechanism is one — cauldron mixing — operating in coupled instances.

P0 iterates. Communities become subjects when they develop enough to lead their own life — same cauldron mechanism at the next scale. Communities of communities can in principle follow. The structure is fractal. Each mixing produces new units that can themselves be mixed, recursively. Subjects with deepening historicity and broader coupling evolve toward agentive behaviour — the web of growing complexity that P5 names.

A structural shape friendly toward formal and process methodologies — category theory, assembly theory, complex adaptive systems, relational quantum mechanics — without binding the framework to any of them. They are interlocutors where the building blocks happen to be tractable. The distribution-of-outcomes shape rhymes with relational quantum mechanics. The path landscape rhymes with assembly theory. The developmental gradient rhymes with complex adaptive systems. Structural rhymes, not importations. A parallel expression alongside the philosophical one in interrelational pluralism — the formal-friendly angle on the same dynamic.
