When [SPLectrum was born](/2026/03/splectrum-is-born.html) the seed had only five principles about language and the relational. Then after having done the initial [positioning](/search/label/positioning) posts the SPLectrum seed was ready for a bit of unpacking. I named that post [SPLectrum from First Principles](/2026/05/splectrum-and-first-principles.html). While preparing for the post I ran into a bit of bother: where does language come from? My thoughts drifted Heidegger's way - *being is always already disclosed* - and if that is the case, so must language, the relational. Ok, *being implies language* but I don't think I can say more. Since this principle is of a different nature from the other ones, I decided to call it P0 — the principle of language origin.

As it was still early in the life of SPLectrum, just out of neonatal, I decided to add the P0 to the original SPLectrum was Born post as well. The SPLectrum from First Principles is the first post in SPLectrum's infancy period, when the seed principles start to be unpacked and the identity of the framework starts to take shape. And P0 isn't playing a minor role in this. The framing of P0 with [Fichte](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/johann-fichte/) and [Heidegger](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/) is proving to be very powerful. Fichte's starting point: the I posits itself by distinguishing itself from the not-I — being comes into existence through the act of differentiation. There is no being without the other. Heidegger takes it further: being is not something that exists and then gets disclosed — being is always already in a world, always already relational. Together they give P0 its depth: being implies the cut (Fichte) and the cut is always already relational (Heidegger).

P0 started as a simple statement — a bare acknowledgment — but has since become a genuine creational principle. The act of placing a boundary *is* the act of creation. You don't create something and then put a boundary around it. The boundary is the creation. Before it, no thing. After it, something. Every instance of anything — a cell, a thought, a data unit, a self — repeats the same cut. P0 is not one principle among six. It is the ground from which the other five become possible. This deepening happened during the preparation of another first principles post: SPLectrum engineering from first principles, due for publication on May 12th. And it has left me with a lot of research to be done solely on the positioning of P0 and how the creational manifests itself in the inter-relational character of languages.

It just comes to show. It was the search for a low-friction toplevel engineering design that has proved to be the strongest catalyst for P0 and its evolution. And why? Because the language capabilities of AI make it paramount that the framework seamlessly translates into engineering solutions. And a description of language alone doesn't suffice in that case.
