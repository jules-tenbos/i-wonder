# Notes — aesthetics blog post

The post delivers the material for the reality-side aesthetics page (post first, page after). One argument: earn the move in the post; the page states it later. Reality side throughout — value-terms, no P-language.

## The one move

Aesthetics is not the philosophy-of-art appendix that arrives after truth, being and goodness. It is the medium those three pass through — **the resonance between intent and interaction**, how well the language fits what it is trying to carry. The post makes that *noticed*, not asserted.

## Leading image

**Sympathetic resonance.** Strike a note and a tuned string across the room answers; strike the wrong one and the room stays dead. It carries the three relata without forcing them — the pitch you mean (intent, the inner), the air and the other body that ring or don't (interaction, the shared), the ringing-or-thud itself (the quality). "Resonance" stops being a term because the image *is* resonance.

Way in: the lived version — the word that finally fits versus the one that's almost-right. Everyone knows the click when a sentence lands and the dull miss when it doesn't. Open in the body, then let the image name what happened. (A light nod to the plectrum — strike, ring — only if it earns its place; don't lean on the signature image to carry it.)

## Arc

1. The lived click — reaching for the word that fits; the felt difference when it lands.
2. Name it: that difference isn't decoration — it's whether the thing got carried at all. Intent vs. what lands in the shared air.
3. Widen, quietly: not only for poets. No private experience without sensory experience; sensory experience runs through language. The quality of the medium sits upstream of everything we know and share.
4. The turn: so aesthetics is not last among the four but first — the medium the others pass through. Resonance between intent and interaction.
5. Leave it open. One line toward where it goes, not a summary.

## Material to carry (this feeds the page)

- Privacy → sensory → language → aesthetics: no private experience without sensory experience, sensory experience language-mediated, so the quality of that mediation is upstream even of the private.
- The working definition, in lived form: how well the language fits what it is trying to carry.
- Medium, not decoration — what everything passes through.
- The ethics edge, good closing pressure: the right intent poorly expressed diminishes to the point of non-existence — which is why this isn't niche.

- Storyline → page by non-contradiction, not transcription. The post does the one move via the arc; it does *not* restate the four-pillar scaffold. That stays the page's job. Warmer and partial is correct.
