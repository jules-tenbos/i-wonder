A new series begins here, and it begins with a value. SPLectrum holds [three values at its core](/reality/core-values/) — belonging, privacy, creativity. The first two are the poles of being a subject: we always already belong, are part of communities, their language ours before we could have refused it; and what we experience is ours alone — no one else sits in my seat. This series is about the third, and the third is not a third thing standing beside the other two. Creativity is the motion between them.

[Creativity is in all of us, in our own way. Yes, people's names get attached to specific creations that are deemed important. But the importance lies in what they stand for, the concept, not the people. The fact that it is a specific person is contingent. Don't forget all those that are not mentioned, contributions that make a difference but remain not named.] Creativity is like playing sheet music and giving it your own interpretation. There is the instrument, the sheet music, examples of execution but always the individuality of the musician. This can happen in private, can be shared in concert, may be copied and persisted. A clear example of creativity as where the individual differs from the shared. 

The motion works like this. At the start everything I have for making sense of the world comes from belonging — words, concepts, ways of seeing, all primed into me. Everything I do with it, the way I use it happens in private, in the seat only I occupy. The common tools, used personally. That difference between the common ground and the private use is not a rounding error to be corrected. It is a workshop. What is shared arrives in my seat, gets lived, and comes back not quite the same — a label shifted, a structure bent, a connection made that was not in the handover. Most of it stays small and stays home. Some of it gets shared, and the shared ground grows. Newness is added to reality, not read out of it.

Notice what this makes creativity: structural, not special. Not a talent rationed to the few, not the art department of life. But a motion that is present in every subject, every day, every subject working the shared tools in a private seat — there is no other way to hold them. The painter and the physicist run it conspicuously. The child bending a word until it fits, the cook who stops following the recipe, the friend who describes your situation in terms you had never put to it — same motion. Where there is a subject, the workshop is open.

A subject can incorporate those changes into its own toolbox, add new tools or change existing ones. Incorporated like that, the changes never leave the private realm. However, when the subject shares — feeds a change back into the community it belongs to — it spreads across that community and is there to create change for all. This dynamic is inherently social. It not only converges on shared realities, but moves those realities on.

Take the cook. The recipe arrived as shared ground — a grandmother, a book, the back of a packet. It gets cooked in one kitchen, to one set of tastes, in the seat only that cook occupies. And one evening it bends: the sugar halved, an hour added, a spice borrowed from a different cuisine. Nobody authorised it; the recipe just came back from being lived not quite the same. Most of those bends stay in that kitchen — the cook's own version now, a tool changed in the toolbox. But hand it on — cook it for guests, write it down for a daughter — and the bend leaves home. The community's recipe is now slightly different, because one seat lived it differently. That is the whole motion at kitchen scale, and it is running in every kitchen, every workshop, every conversation, all the time.

And the motion feeds itself. The more that is shared, the richer the common ground; the richer the ground, the more there is to differ from; the more to differ from, the more every private seat has to work with. Belonging feeds privacy feeds belonging. Shared reality grows because the seats keep adding to it, and every addition is raw material in everyone else's workshop. The loop compounds — which is a quiet hint that it might not stop at the scale of persons.

That is the generative value, whole. Two poles and the motion between them: belonging hands the tools in, privacy bends them in the only seat it has, and what gets handed back moves the shared ground on. It asks for no talent and waits for no permission. It is simply what a subject does — and reality has never once come out of a day the same as it went in.
