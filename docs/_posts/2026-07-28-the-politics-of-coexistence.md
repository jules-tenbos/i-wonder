This is where the position is laid down; the working-out comes after. Politics is the last major pillar of metaphysics, and it plays the dynamic counterpart to ethics the way aesthetics does to ontology: ethics reads the standing configuration of a subject's values toward others, and politics reads the same territory in motion.

At the base level SPLectrum attaches ethical values to the language games. As a subject we are part of multiple language games, belong to many. And this is reflected in the values we carry, not a simple one size fits all, but a distribution. We have choice and agency to tailor this to who we are as a subject. 

SPLectrum sees politics as concerned with the art of coexistence. This covers the more traditional fields of public life, but SPLectrum sees this concern wherever there is coexistence all the way into family and private life. Can you live happily being a father and a soldier at the same time?

At the heart of effective coexistence lies understanding. The prerequisite is enough common ground in shared language — and this matters far more than agreement or disagreement. One could put it as: is there enough meaning expression in the language game for a meaningful game of coexistence to be played at all? Agreement can come later, or never; what coexistence needs first is that the moves and roles are legible to each other.

Coexistence, then, is the dynamic of playing one's roles across a whole web of language games at once. And the subject is an active participant in that play, through three choices: play in agreement, play in disagreement, or don't play at all. That last one matters most — we each have the choice not to let a language game be part of our reality. And here is the turn: in choosing which games to play, a subject is choosing which values are live in its own reality. The still picture ethics reads — this subject holds these values — is the settled trace of these choices playing out. To say it in a few words: life is just a game — albeit an important one.

Out of all this playing, a balance establishes itself. Not decreed from above, not handed down by anyone with the authority to say how the games should sit together — there is no such seat. The balance is simply what the games playing alongside one another settle into, and it shifts as the play shifts.

With politics set down, the metaphysics is complete — and it has a clear shape. Epistemology, ontology and aesthetics are one realm — call it the realm of *realisation*: the subject acquiring meaning, holding it as concepts, and expressing it in language. Reality is disclosed to the subject and enacted by it in the same movement, and realisation carries both senses at once — coming to see, and making real. Ethics and politics are the second — the realm of *coexistence*: the subject's standing and its ongoing interactions with *other* subjects and communities — others who have agency of their own, who play their own games and make their own choices. Two realms of relation, realisation then coexistence, and across both the same refusal holds: no universal good handed down from above, no one seated above the games to decide how they should coexist.

I think this is a sound approach to metaphysics — not a finished system, but a solid place to build from. What is left is not a verdict but a position to read from. From here the question of how many realities can genuinely stand alongside one another — pluralism — can be taken up not as an argument to win but as a landscape to read. And that is where this series goes next.

## Seam — from the still picture to the same territory in motion

- Ethics read the standing configuration: games, values, belonging held at strength. Politics reads the same territory as the games coexist in motion.
- Name the scales the ethics post closed on: between communities, between a subject and its communities, and — passing line only — within the subject, since a subject holds several games at once.
- Light "still → moving" resonance (ethics : politics :: ontology : aesthetics). Do NOT lecture the pillar symmetry — a gesture, is-like, not a system.

## Beat 1 — the balance establishes, it is not decided

- Many games played alongside one another; the equilibrium is the *resultant* of that playing, never pronounced from above. No adjudicating agent, no view-from-above, no deliberative "we" that weighs and pronounces. (Core claim. Watch the verbs.)
- It rests on **common understanding, not agreement** — mutual legibility of roles. Coexisting games can read the moves and roles each other play without sharing the values behind them. The equilibrium rests on that readability, not on shared values. (The hinge.)
- *Optional single line (lean: include, one line):* the equilibrium is a living resultant that shifts with how the games are played. STOP at play-dependence — do NOT reach for fair / quality (that is content, downstream).

## Beat 2 — active role-agency

- The subject is active in the roles it plays: yes this role / change the role / no-play. Agency is **inflection, not authorship** — it lives in the choosing, not in authoring the games.
- Gesture only. Leave the elaborate material (live-by-example, self-realisation) to its own post.

## Close — hand off to pluralism

- With ethics and politics laid down — no universal good from above, no decider from above — the ground is set to read pluralism *from* a position rather than join its argument.
- Hand off the way ethics handed off to this post. Forward gesture, not a summary.
