## Seam — from the still picture to the same territory in motion

- Ethics read the standing configuration: games, values, belonging held at strength. Politics reads the same territory as the games coexist in motion.
- Name the scales the ethics post closed on: between communities, between a subject and its communities, and — passing line only — within the subject, since a subject holds several games at once.
- Light "still → moving" resonance (ethics : politics :: ontology : aesthetics). Do NOT lecture the pillar symmetry — a gesture, is-like, not a system.

## Beat 1 — the balance establishes, it is not decided

- Many games played alongside one another; the equilibrium is the *resultant* of that playing, never pronounced from above. No adjudicating agent, no view-from-above, no deliberative "we" that weighs and pronounces. (Core claim. Watch the verbs.)
- It rests on **common understanding, not agreement** — mutual legibility of roles. Coexisting games can read the moves and roles each other play without sharing the values behind them. The equilibrium rests on that readability, not on shared values. (The hinge.)
- *Optional single line (lean: include, one line):* the equilibrium is a living resultant that shifts with how the games are played. STOP at play-dependence — do NOT reach for fair / quality (that is content, downstream).

## Beat 2 — active role-agency

- The subject is active in the roles it plays: yes this role / change the role / no-play. Agency is **inflection, not authorship** — it lives in the choosing, not in authoring the games.
- Gesture only. Leave the elaborate material (live-by-example, self-realisation) to its own post.

## Close — hand off to pluralism

- With ethics and politics laid down — no universal good from above, no decider from above — the ground is set to read pluralism *from* a position rather than join its argument.
- Hand off the way ethics handed off to this post. Forward gesture, not a summary.
