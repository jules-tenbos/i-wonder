# "Foundational" — three senses

When SPLectrum calls itself a "foundational project", the term carries three specific senses, not the loose gloss of "basic" or "important":

1. **Axiomatic base with historical support.** The seed is a set of axioms. They aren't arbitrary — mainstream historical thinking (the positioning work) supports them.
2. **About the dynamics — the essence of language in evolution.** Not "starting from" first principles as a one-time ground floor. The framework is *about* the living, evolving relational structure itself. Subject matter, not method.
3. **Equal standing, cross-disciplinary.** P4 applied at the discipline level — philosophy, physics, engineering, arts as languages with equal standing in potential. Cross-disciplinary reach isn't incidental breadth; it's a structural claim.

## Why

The word does specific work. Loose readings lose the weight.

## How to apply

Use "foundational" with these senses in mind when writing about SPLectrum. Don't slip into "starting from" or "first principles" framings for sense 2 — that misreads dynamics as static ground. When unpacking the term (on pages, in vocabulary, in responses), preserve all three senses; they work together. The homepage grid implicitly unpacks them: Seed + Positioning maps to sense 1, Language + Vocabulary to sense 2, Reality + Engineering to sense 3.
