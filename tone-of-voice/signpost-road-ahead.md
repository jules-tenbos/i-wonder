# Signpost the road ahead

When something is planned ahead or under active development, the ref lib page can say so. Don't treat "more coming" as a defect to hide behind silence. The World of SPLectrum is explicitly a continuation of thought and conversation — pointing at the road ahead is part of the site's honesty about what it is.

## Why

Complementary to the touchstone principle and the organic-ecosystem stance. A touchstone page that stays silent about open questions can read as if it believes it has the answer. A page that signposts ongoing work reads as a current position in a living conversation — which is what SPLectrum actually is.

## How to apply

- Use concrete forward pointers where they land: "more coming in the [series]", "open territory", "research in progress", "developing in [blog label]".
- Link to the conversation surface, not the private working material. Blog labels (e.g. `/blog/label/discovery`), upcoming posts once scheduled, or specific follow-up pages are fair targets. Don't expose `submissions/` working files.
- Keep the signpost light — one sentence, not a roadmap. The touchstone page is still a touchstone.
- Use this for genuine open territory, not for filler. If a page is simply incomplete, finish it; the signpost is for material that's under active development.
