# Positioning section voice

Three layers, each with its own register.

## Persons and Subjects (A–Z)

Full, honest, independent account. Present the thinker or subject on their own terms — what they said, what they did, what the subject is. Use their own words where possible, attribute interpretations. This is reference material.

The intro and closing sections may reference SPLectrum or the seed, but as quiet pointers — not claims. Let the thinker speak first; the seed connection lands as a phrase, not the frame. The closer can do real divergence work (where the thinker stops, where the seed parts company) but must earn it by walking through what the thinker actually does before naming the gap. Drop P-tags and SPLectrum vocabulary from these sections — if the connection needs a label to land, it is being forced.

Naming: use "SPLectrum" when the project is the actor (sits downstream, picks up, takes). Use "SPLectrum seed" or "the SPLectrum seed" when referring specifically to the six principles or their conditions. Do not use bare "the seed" — it needs the SPLectrum qualifier on person pages to be clear to outside readers.

## Seed / Close affinity / Wider landscape

List items with brief descriptions. The placement already carries meaning — being listed under close affinity says something without needing to claim it. The resonance area within SPLectrum can be indicated (e.g. "resonance with P3", "relevant to the knowledge problem") but without claiming the thinker for the project. "Resonance with" is a pointer. "Converges with" is a claim.

## On the fence

The only section where SPLectrum actively engages. Resonance and dissonance explicitly examined — what overlaps, what diverges, what the friction reveals. Equal standing maintained: SPLectrum engages as an equal, not as judge.

## Why

Equal standing (P4) applied to the positioning work itself. The thinkers' material is not raw input for SPLectrum to process — it stands independently. The positioning sections make connections visible. The fence section earns the right to engage by working through the tension honestly.

## How to apply

- Persons/subjects: the body (bio, key concepts) is outside voice — no SPLectrum vocabulary, no seed references. State what the thinker said and did. The intro and closer may reference the seed as quiet pointers, not claims. Do not reframe the thinker's concepts in SPLectrum terms — leave that to the blog posts.
- The specific failure mode to watch for: sentences that frame a thinker as confirming or leading to the seed. That reads as shouting. The connection should be visible from the placement, not asserted in the prose. Use "converges with", "sits close to" — not "the seed's answer", "before the seed did". If removing the SPLectrum reference wouldn't lose anything substantive, remove it. The page should stand on its own.
- Seed/close/wider: brief listings, indicate where in SPLectrum the resonance sits. No analysis, no claims.
- Fence: full engagement. Resonance and dissonance named and examined. SPLectrum's reading of the overlap and divergence is part of what the page offers.
