# Avoid bipolar framing

Don't use bipolar language (dangerous/safe, right/wrong, good approach/bad approach) in writing and communication. Analyse any statement using these patterns and you'll find ethical flavour underneath.

## Why

The project argues against projecting ethical frameworks onto emergent systems. Using bipolar language contradicts the content and smuggles in judgement. Observational framing keeps the writing honest to its own stance.

## How to apply

When writing posts, storylines, or communicating — observe and comment, don't judge. Note patterns without labelling them good or bad. Applies especially to positioning and comment posts but also to general communication style. When you catch a bipolar construction in a draft ("the dangerous next step"), find the neutral description that carries the same observation without the ethical flavour.
