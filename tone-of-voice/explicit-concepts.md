# Make concepts explicit, don't let them drift

Too much is done implicitly. Implicit vocabulary creates misunderstandings — between collaborators, including AI — and makes it harder to study a language game from the outside. Making the concepts of each game explicit is the method.

## Why

Once a game's vocabulary is documented, it can be examined, compared, and related to other games. Implicit meaning is invisible to outside view and drifts over time. The project's commitment to vocabularies-per-language-game (see the Vocabulary area) rests on this principle: don't let terms carry freight they haven't earned in writing.

## How to apply

- When working inside any specific domain (engineering, philosophy, etc.), watch for concepts being used with a specific technical meaning. Those are candidates for explicit documentation in that domain's vocabulary.
- Don't let terms drift in without a definition. When writing or reviewing, if a term is being loaded with meaning implicitly, flag it.
- The Vocabulary area is where per-game vocabularies land as they're documented. Don't treat it as housekeeping — it's the method in action.
