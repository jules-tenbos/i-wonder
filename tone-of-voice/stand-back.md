# Stand back when the content speaks

Let the content do its own work. The stronger the material or the placement, the less commentary it needs around it. Trailing paragraphs that tell the reader how to read the foregoing through SPLectrum's lens usually subtract rather than add.

## Why

When material already carries its own meaning — through the thinkers named, the arc traced, the placement in a category — adding SPLectrum commentary on top claims the material for the project and weakens the presentation. The reader doesn't need to be told what to see; they can see it. Commentary earns its place only when it adds something the content doesn't already show.

This is also the operational edge where the AI training pull toward authority shows up most clearly (see `feedback_ai_authority_reach.md` in live memory): "if the seed holds: …" patterns, convergence claims, inclusion-as-fact framings. They feel natural to write and almost never improve the page.

## How to apply

- **Don't write trailing SPLectrum-commentary paragraphs.** On ref-lib pages where the content does its own work, stop when the content stops. No summary-through-the-lens paragraph, no "if the seed holds:" pivot per thinker, no concluding synthesis that frames the material for the project.
- **Don't write per-item hooks that claim the item.** "Wittgenstein arrives at…", "Rorty converges with…" — the verb claims the thinker for the framework. Leave the material on its own terms.
- **Remove on review.** The first-draft pull is strong; catch it on the pass.

## Applied per page type

Positioning pages have three registers by placement:

- **Close-affinity pages stand back completely.** The placement in the close-affinity category already says SPLectrum holds the affinity; the content — thinkers, arc, shape — speaks for itself. No closing paragraph about how SPLectrum relates.
- **On-the-fence pages can speak more.** These pieces straddle in and out of alignment with the project. The friction is the point, and the friction often has to be named to be visible. SPLectrum's reading of where the overlap and divergence sit is part of what the page offers.
- **Wider-landscape pages** — follow the material. How much commentary belongs depends on what ends up there and how it relates to SPLectrum's work.

The unifying rule: don't comment where the placement already carries the meaning.
