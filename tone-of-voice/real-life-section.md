# Real Life section advice

## What the section is for

Structural-scale applied analysis. SPLectrum's framework brought to bear on real-world domains — science funding and diversity, education, governance, communities, and whatever else earns its place. The developed, curated home for material the blog may have surfaced first.

Distinct from the blog's working stream. A blog post tries out an idea in conversation; a Real Life piece is the substantial, reference-shape treatment. Both can coexist on the same domain without contradiction (per `post-vs-ref-lib.md`).

## Register

SPLectrum is the speaker, but applied — the voice is structural observation, not philosophical exposition. The register is closer to positioning (engaged, learning, on equal standing with the material) than to the seed pages (definitional, foundational).

Non-claiming throughout. "This suggests…", "what follows is…", "the structural observation is…" — not "SPLectrum shows…" or "the seed proves…". The analysis should demonstrate the framework's usefulness by doing the work, not by asserting that it does the work.

Stand back when the content speaks. Real-world domains carry their own weight. If the analysis is good, no trailing paragraph is needed to tell the reader what to make of it.

## Shape

Tentatively the same `a-z / concepts / types` shape as the bottom row, with A-Z entries holding life-domain entries (science funding and diversity, education, governance…) and types holding scale-cuts or kind-cuts. The shape can deviate if the content asks for it — start with one or two pieces and let the section find its form.

## The reflexive case

The homepage grid is itself a Real Life artifact — structural-scale applied analysis of the project's own public manifestation. The section contains itself. This self-inclusion is consistent with SPLectrum's anti-foundationalism: nothing stands outside the structure it organises.

## How to apply

- Each piece should be substantial enough to stand on its own — not a blog post with headers, but a developed structural treatment.
- The framework is the lens, not the subject. Write about the domain; let the framework show its usefulness through the analysis.
- Cross-link to the blog working stream where a post covers the same domain in conversational register.
- Avoid magisterial claims about what the analysis "reveals" or "proves." State what is observable; let the reader draw conclusions.
