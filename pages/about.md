# About

I'm Jules ten Bos. I wonder about things — how reality works, how we relate to it, how language shapes what we see. This blog is where those questions get explored in the open.

The thinking centres on a principle I call the SPLectrum seed — six lines about language, relation, and reality. Not a theory of everything. A starting point. [SPLectrum in a Nutshell](/seed) is where that unpacks.

SPLectrum is also an engineering project — collaborative AI applied to software. Apart from the language and meaning work, it focuses on research into an AI-friendly repository called Mycelium and a human-AI collaborative creation methodology (HAICC). The engineering side lives in the <a href="https://splectrum.world/engineering/">engineering area of the reference library</a>.

We practice what we preach — this blog is created under an explicit [human-AI partnership](/haicc), openly acknowledged.
