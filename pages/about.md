# About

I'm Jules Tenbos. I wonder about things — how reality works, how we relate to it, how language shapes what we see. This blog is where those questions get explored in the open.

The thinking centres on a principle I call the Splectrum seed — five lines about language, relation, and reality. Not a theory of everything. A starting point. [Splectrum from Seed](/p/splectrum-from-seed.html) is where that unpacks.

Splectrum is also an engineering project — collaborative AI applied to software. Apart from the language and meaning work, it focuses on research into an AI-friendly repository called Mycelium and a human-AI collaborative creation methodology (HAICC). The engineering side lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/splectrum).

We practice what we preach — this blog is created under an explicit [human-AI partnership](/p/collaborative-ai.html), openly acknowledged.
