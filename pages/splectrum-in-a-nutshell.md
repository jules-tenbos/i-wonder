# Splectrum in a Nutshell

Splectrum is a research project exploring how language — in the widest sense — shapes the reality we live in. It starts from six principles: <a href="https://jules-tenbos.github.io/i-wonder/seed">the seed</a>.

**The philosophical turn.** From Descartes' personal enquiry through Kant's structuring mind to the language turn — Wittgenstein, Rorty, Merleau-Ponty. The observer is not a mirror. Reality is relational.

**The scientific turn.** From Newton's clockwork through quantum mechanics to relational QM. The observer is not neutral. Facts are relative to the participant.

**Language in practice.** Software languages, language games, the body as medium. Language is everywhere — not just words.

The <a href="https://jules-tenbos.github.io/i-wonder/">reference library</a> has the detail. This blog is the conversation.
