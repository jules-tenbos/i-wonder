# Carrier Language

Substantial submission. Footprint not yet defined.

---

P0 says being implies language. P1-P5 describe what that language does. The carrier language is the formal structure in which P1-P5 are expressed — the relational DNA that all spawned languages inherit.

Every spawned language carries the structural primitives: binding (P1), subject-reality mediation (P2), shared knowledge convergence (P3), interrelation and equal standing (P4), growing complexity (P5). The carrier encodes what every instance must honour.

The being of relation itself may be static — the grammar of relation doesn't change. Being, other, interface, binding — these are invariants. What changes is what gets expressed through them. The formal language captures the invariant structure. The living languages are what flows through it. The seed IS the static formal language. Six lines. The rest is expression.

The candidate family for this formal carrier sits near category theory — the mathematics of relationships. Morphisms, compositions, functors. Structure-first, content-agnostic. But it needs the generativity of natural language — a formal language that can spawn other formal languages. A meta-language for relational structure.

This is the Mycelium layer. Not a specific language but the relational substrate from which all specific languages grow. The engineering project (DSL framework, collaborative AI) and the philosophy are the same thing approached from different directions. The engineering builds the carrier. The philosophy understands what it carries.

## Pattern-first as modus operandi

Not about how you train AI, but about what the carrier language could enable: reasoning across disciplines using relational patterns rather than domain-specific vocabulary. P0 as a structural primitive (being/other/interface), P1 as the binding operation, P4 as the equivalence principle across domains. Not words to predict but relational patterns to work with.

Like physics: you don't teach gravity by showing a million falling objects. You give the structure and it generates the instances. The patterns are universal where the data is specific.
