# John Vervaeke — considered and declined (2026-09-05)

**Decision (Jules): NOT included on the site.** The baggage the inclusion would bring weighs too heavy, especially for a living contemporary figure — the surrounding online ecosystem (the sensemaking / "liminal web" dialogue circuit, the Peterson adjacency, the two-track reception: a modest peer-reviewed corpus against a much larger lecture-series-and-podcast public). Not a verdict on the ideas.

## The fence line, if ever revisited

- **Separable, ring-adjacent:** the science — relevance realization (the 2012 anchor paper with Lillicrap and Richards), transjectivity, the agent–arena relation, the four kinds of knowing. Resonances noted in discussion: meaning as connectedness (relational, not propositional); agent–arena ↔ being-and-its-language; salience/mattering ↔ disclosure vocabulary; four knowings ↔ the knowledge/understanding held-operative split; lineage through Gibson (affordances), enactivism, predictive processing — all already on site.
- **Where the objection lands:** the meaning-**crisis** framing. It carries a normative decline premise — a baseline of health located in the past, its loss evidenced by felt deficit — and belongs to the perennial diagnosis-of-decline genre (Nietzsche's nihilism, Weber's disenchantment, 1950s anomie…). When a crisis recurs in every era, it is the standing condition noticed again in that era's vocabulary.
- **Jules's counter-position:** evolution in action. Humans show an innate *appetite* for problems, not an aversion; today we deal with the problems of the day. Stated in Vervaeke's own machinery: opponent processing never settles — perpetual retuning is the design, not a malfunction; the dissolution of old orders is the machinery succeeding (explore over exploit at civilisational scale), and the felt turbulence is what mid-retuning is like. There has never been a moment that wasn't mid-retuning. Vervaeke needs an extra normative premise to get from "the orders dissolved" to "pathology," and his science doesn't supply it.

## A wider observation worth keeping: "the static mind"

From the same discussion: it is astonishing how static many thinkers hold human thought — how few factor in evolutionary/cumulative effects, building on the fruits of previous labour. Descartes's reason, Kant's categories, Chomsky's endowment: a fixed instrument specified once, timelessly. The analytic habit of testing against "our intuitions" assumes the intuitions are not themselves sediment. The minority who do factor it in: Tomasello (the ratchet — the mechanism named outright), Vygotsky (tools), Gadamer (tradition as ground), Bartlett (schemas — retention as acquired framework shaping new intake), Hegel (at civilisational scale, at the price of a destination); off-site, Merlin Donald, Cecilia Heyes (cognitive gadgets — the most direct modern statement), Popper's World 3. The seed holds it as a principle: the always-already is socially funded; there is no fixed instrument outside the accumulation, because the instrument is made of accumulation. "The static mind" names a fence running through many thinkers at once — a natural cross-cutting observation if the disciplines phase (Putnam, multiple realizability) wants one.
