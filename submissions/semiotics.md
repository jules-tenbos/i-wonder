# Semiotics

Semiotics as the study of sign processes — from Saussure's structural system through Peirce's generalization and evolution of signs, Uexküll's Umwelt, and biosemiotics. The field makes two moves that map directly onto the seed: generalization of language beyond linguistics (P0), and the evolutionary character of sign processes (P5). Uexküll's Umwelt parallels P2: each organism experiences reality through its own world of signs.

## Research needed

### Key figures and their moves

**Saussure** — already in philosophical trajectory. System of differences, signifier/signified, meaning as differential. The structural/synchronic side. What we have is enough for positioning; may need expansion for a dedicated semiotics page.

**Peirce** — the double move: generalization (signs beyond human language) and evolution (semiosis as unbounded process, interpretants generating new signs). His pragmatism context. The triad: sign/object/interpretant. How this differs from Saussure's dyad. The openness of semiosis — no final interpretant. Needs proper research for positioning.

**Uexküll** — Umwelt as the organism's own world of meaningful signs. A tick's reality is butyric acid, temperature, hair density. Each subject lives in its own semiotic world. The parallel with P2 (language as medium of experience) is structural and independent. Needs research — how Umwelt relates to the seed, whether it extends to non-biological subjects.

**Sebeok** — connected Peirce and Uexküll, coined biosemiotics, argued semiosis is coextensive with life. The bridge figure. Needs research — what exactly did he add beyond connecting the two?

**Biosemiotics as field** — Jesper Hoffmeyer, Kalevi Kull, the Copenhagen-Tartu school. How far does biosemiotics extend semiosis? All life? All systems? Where does it stop and why?

### Structural questions to resolve

- Where does semiotics sit in the seed? P4 (inter-relation of languages) is the home territory, but it touches P0 (generalization), P2 (Umwelt/experience), P5 (evolution of signs). Map this properly.
- Peirce's generalization move — is it the same move as P0 or a step toward it? P0 says being implies language. Peirce says signs are everywhere. Are these the same claim or does P0 go further?
- Saussure's system vs Peirce's process — is this the same distinction as langue/parole? Or something deeper?
- Umwelt and P2 — structural parallel or structural identity? Does Uexküll's Umwelt carry the same weight as P2, or is P2 broader (all beings, not just organisms)?
- Where does biosemiotics stop? Sebeok says semiosis is coextensive with life. The seed says being implies language — which extends beyond life. What is the boundary and does it matter?
- The sign as relational — Saussure says the sign is purely differential (no content in itself). This is P1. Peirce's triad is relational (sign only functions through relation to object and interpretant). Same structural observation, different vocabulary.

### Category theory connection

- Peirce's semiosis as morphism chain — interpretant of one sign becomes sign for the next. This is composition of arrows. Does this formalise naturally in category theory?
- Yoneda and Peirce — an object is known entirely by its relations (Yoneda). A sign is known entirely by its interpretants (Peirce). Same structural claim?
