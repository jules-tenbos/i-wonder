# Symbiotic Intelligence — Wild & Wachsmann

Working notes for a future **on-the-fence** positioning piece.

**Paper:** Wild, J. & Wachsmann, S. (2026). *Symbiotic Intelligence: Rethinking AI with Mycelium*. Arts, 15(4), 69. [DOI: 10.3390/arts15040069](https://doi.org/10.3390/arts15040069).
**Full text extract:** `symbiotic-intelligence-paper.txt` (same directory).

Surfaced via a Google search for *mycelium* + *mutable* — their paper appeared alongside SPLectrum's Mycelium pages on the first page of results. The name collision is the starting point and the productive tension.

## What the paper argues

Practice-led art research. Four installations (*Interference Spores*, *Interference Spores 2.0*, *Forest*, *Symbiogenesis*) co-working with living Grey Oyster mycelium whose electrical signals drive lights, sound, Wi-Fi jammers. Methodology: "thinking-through-making" / "critical unknowing" — knowledge emerges from material engagement, not prior theory.

The argumentative spine is a **genealogy of "intelligence"**:

- Galton → Pearson → Spearman invented modern "general intelligence" / g-factor as a social-Darwinist, eugenic, colonial project.
- Goertzel & Pennachin (2007) explicitly ground AGI in Spearman's g-factor — direct terminological inheritance.
- So contemporary AGI discourse (Bostrom, Hendrycks, Schmidhuber, Andreessen's *Techno-Optimist Manifesto*, Kurzweil) reactivates hierarchical, competitive, extinction-inflected narratives by inheriting the vocabulary.
- Counter-narrative drawn from Margulis (symbiogenesis), Haraway, Malabou (epigenetic intelligence), Bridle (planetary intelligence), Hayles (prosthetic cognition). Intelligence as relational, distributed, more-than-human, care-bound — emerging through encounter, not possessed by individuals.
- Narratives are "world-making" (Haraway, Simondon's associated milieu): intervening on imaginaries is a legitimate intervention on AI.
- Installations as "sites of aesthetic rupture" (Rancière) — material enactments, not propositional arguments.

## Resonance with SPLectrum

- Intelligence/cognition as **relational, distributed, non-atomistic** — direct overlap with P3 (language inter-relational) and the subject-reality view.
- Rejection of ranked hierarchies — aligns with P4 (equal standing in potential).
- Meaning/intelligence arising *between* subjects — overlaps with shared-reality / convergence structure.
- Narratives as world-conditioning — resonates with the language-centered view of reality.

## Dissonance with SPLectrum

- **Methodologically opposite.** Practice-led, speculative, non-propositional, explicitly anti-framework. SPLectrum is an explicit structural framework with seed axioms and an engineering fabric.
- **Ethics-first.** Care, anti-capitalism, anti-"corporate intelligence" are load-bearing. SPLectrum treats decentralisation as structural, not ethical.
- **Intent imported into biology.** "Cooperation", "reciprocity", "mutual flourishing" applied to fungal networks — the same move the existing mutualism piece pushes back on.
- **Anti-engineering posture.** Technical domain is something to be *ruptured aesthetically*, not built. SPLectrum is unashamedly engineering.
- **"Mycelium" is pure metaphor** for biological symbiosis. SPLectrum's Mycelium is a committed engineering substrate (AVRO + git + Kafka). The word collision is real.

## The productive tension: two Myceliums

The name collision is the piece's anchor. Same word, two senses:

- **Wild/Wachsmann Mycelium** — biological, metaphorical, ethical. Intelligence as fungal symbiosis; AI should be more like a mycelial network.
- **SPLectrum Mycelium** — engineering substrate, committed, structural. The data fabric through which a subject accesses the data world.

Both gesture at the same structural intuition: interconnected, distributed, relational systems. They diverge sharply on whether the move is *ethical* (how AI should be imagined and designed) or *structural* (how a data fabric actually works).

## Relation to the existing mutualism piece

Substantial overlap with `/positioning/on-the-fence/mutualism.md`. Wild/Wachsmann are a concrete, named, contemporary instance of exactly what mutualism critiques — anthropomorphic cooperation-vocabulary applied to fungal networks, ethics imported into emergence. But they extend it in two ways:

- They make the metaphor a **design program for AI**.
- They share the word **Mycelium** with SPLectrum engineering.

The mutualism piece is abstract; this paper is its named exemplar. A positioning piece on Wild/Wachsmann should refer out to mutualism for the structural-not-ethical argument, and extend it specifically to the AI-design-as-symbiosis move.

## Angle for the positioning piece

1. Acknowledge the g-factor / AGI genealogy as a genuine contribution. It's citable and sharp regardless of the ethical framing.
2. Affirm the relational turn.
3. Mark the name collision on Mycelium — use it to sharpen the two senses (biological metaphor / engineering substrate).
4. Part company on the ethics-first framing; refer out to the mutualism piece for the structural-not-ethical argument.
5. Note what's preserved in SPLectrum's engineering Mycelium that the metaphor can't carry: commitment, specificity, accountability for the artefacts that realise it.
