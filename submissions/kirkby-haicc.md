# Kirkby, Baumgarth & Henseler — Welcome, new brand colleague

Working notes for a future **wider-landscape** positioning piece.

**Paper:** Kirkby, A., Baumgarth, C., & Henseler, J. (2025). *Welcome, new brand colleague! A conceptual framework for efficient and effective human–AI co-creation for creative brand voice.* Journal of Brand Management, 32, 385–399. Published online 14 May 2025. [DOI: 10.1057/s41262-025-00387-y](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41262-025-00387-y). The Author(s) 2025, open access.
**Full text extract:** `kirkby-haicc-paper.txt` (same directory).

Surfaced via a Google search for *HAICC* — the paper appeared near the top and brought with it a startling coincidence.

## The name collision — the anchoring hook

Kirkby et al. use the acronym **HAICC**, spelled identically to SPLectrum's. Their expansion is **Human-AI Co-Creation** (vs SPLectrum's **Human-AI Creative Collaboration**). Independent use, on closely related territory. Two live expansions for the same four letters, surfaced by the same searches.

This is not a contest — neither side knows about the other. It is overloading. The acronym now has multiple expansions in the literature, and that calls for naming discipline on SPLectrum's side:

- Cite Kirkby et al. on the first HAICC mention in this positioning piece.
- Never let "HAICC" stand alone on first mention in any future public piece; keep the full expansion prominent.
- Add a disambiguation note to `/vocabulary/splectrum/engineering/haicc.md`: *"Not to be confused with HAICC = Human-AI Co-Creation (Kirkby et al. 2025), a brand-management framework."*
- "Creative" in SPLectrum's expansion does real disambiguation work — keep it load-bearing.

The collision is also the piece's entry: *"Another HAICC arrived in 2025. Here is what they mean, and why we mean something else."*

## What the paper argues

Conceptual (non-empirical) piece in Journal of Brand Management proposing a three-level framework for organising human-AI co-creation specifically in the domain of **creative brand voice** — the textual, visual, and sonic expression of a brand's identity.

**Individual level (brand professional + AI) — operational processes.**
- **AI task roles** mapped onto Wallas' (1926) four-stage creative process (preparation → incubation → illumination → verification). Roles include *coordinator, definer, creator, evaluator, second opinion, expert consultant, automator, doer*. Drawn from Siemon 2022, Rezwana & Maher 2021/2022, Bader et al. 1988.
- **Knowledge and skills** — Zirar et al.'s (2023) triad: technical, human, conceptual.
- **Co-creation teaming** — five modes on a spectrum: teaming human → human lead + AI assist → hybrid → AI lead + human assist → teaming AI. Their default recommendation for complex creative work: human lead + AI assist.

**Organisational level (brand management) — interaction management.**
- Infrastructure and a **brand voice database**; argument for tailored/proprietary models trained on brand-owned corpora.
- **AI socialisation** via HRM, using Makarius et al.'s (2020) framework (sense → comprehend → act). AI treated like a newcomer employee.

**Societal level (brand environment) — external norms.**
- Responsibility and accountability (CDR, EU AI Act, bias avoidance, data ownership).
- AI transparency — Methnani et al.'s (2021) decomposition: trust, verifiability, fairness, contestability.
- Brand voice copyright — authorship ambiguity, training-data IP lawsuits (Anthropic, Midjourney, Stability, DeviantArt, LAION).

Grounded in TOE (Tornatzky & Fleischer 1990), Bronfenbrenner, socio-technical systems (Feldman 2023), brand management literature. Managerial synthesis, not foundational theory. Methodological license from Vargo 2023 for conceptual-only work.

## Resonance with SPLectrum HAICC

- **Name and domain framing**: identical acronym, adjacent problem space.
- **AI task roles are structurally persona-like** — coordinator, creator, evaluator, definer, etc. Each role is a bounded set of responsibilities invoked at a stage of a process. The closest substantive hook to SPLectrum's personas.
- **"Welcome, new brand colleague"** — title framing of AI as a team member with induction. Rhymes with SPLectrum's persona-as-role stance.
- **Tailored brand-voice databases / proprietary model training** — faint echo of JIT materialisation (capability built where the work is).
- **Teaming spectrum** recognises leadership can vary across a process — compatible with SPLectrum's optimisation-toward-AI-autonomy-within-persona.
- The **inherited claim from Liapis et al. 2014** that creativity emerging from human-AI interaction cannot be credited to either alone — both frames share this.

## Dissonance

- **Flat dyad, not decentralised cognition.** One human + one AI system; no distributed field of subjects and agents.
- **Hierarchy, not peer partnership.** AI is "onboarded," "monitored," "trained," constrained by HRM. SPLectrum's equal-standing collaboration (P4) is absent; their default is "human leads, AI assists."
- **Brand-consistency-first, outputs-first.** The goal is preserving an already-established brand voice; creative novelty is hedged. SPLectrum treats the collaboration itself as generative.
- **No philosophical substrate.** No Wittgenstein, no language games, no protocols as primitives. Creativity is a cognitive-process variable, not a linguistic/communal act.
- **No JIT, no fabric thinking.** Infrastructure is a prerequisite, not a materialisation that follows capability need.
- **Efficiency/effectiveness as master metric** — a brand-management frame SPLectrum does not share.

Nowhere do the references include Wittgenstein, language games, decentralised cognition, Clark, Hutchins, persona theory in the design/philosophical sense, or JIT/fabric literature. The absence is itself positioning — they arrive from a different intellectual watershed.

## Submission angle

Two moves in one piece:

1. **The collision as entry.** "Another HAICC arrived in 2025. Here's what they mean, and why we mean something else." Use it to name SPLectrum's HAICC distinctly and cite Kirkby et al. as the neighbour.
2. **Role-based AI task model and 'new brand colleague' motif as the specific points of contact.** Persona-thinking is emerging independently in brand management; SPLectrum's version is the deeper, philosophically-grounded generalisation — decentralised cognition, protocols, language games, JIT.

Not close affinity — they do not arrive at a kindred structural territory; they arrive at a managerial checklist.
Not on the fence — the dissonance is clear-cut, not productively mixed.
Not skip — the name alone guarantees search-discovery adjacency for years.

## Relation to other pieces surfaced this cycle

- **HAI-Co² (Dutta et al. 2025)** — a parallel positioning piece, also surfaced by HAICC-related searches. HAI-Co² overlaps on the symmetric-cooperation axis (dyadic, search-over-construction-space); this Kirkby piece overlaps on the domain-application axis (managerial, brand voice). The three together — SPLectrum HAICC, HAI-Co², Kirkby HAICC — form a productive triangle: structural/philosophical, formal/computational, managerial/applied. Worth referencing across the three positioning pieces.
- **COFI (Rezwana & Maher 2021/2022)** — directly cited by Kirkby et al. as a source for AI task roles and interaction-component thinking. Already retrieved (same directory). Background material, not a standalone positioning piece — the 2022 TOCHI extension surveys 92 co-creative systems and finds 82.6% have no AI-to-human channel and 95.7% collect no consequential information. That "pleasing turn-takers with no AI voice" finding is the deficit HAICC's persona construct addresses; useful as an empirical foil in the HAI-Co² piece and as an HCI counterpart to Kirkby's managerial framing here.
