# The Addition of P0 — Comment

Comment submission. To be published shortly after Splectrum from First Principles as a historical note.

---

## Background

Splectrum originally launched with five seed principles (P1–P5), published in the "Splectrum is Born" post. P0 did not exist in the initial formulation.

## Draft comments

### Comment 1 — A note on P0

The original "Splectrum is Born" post has been updated to include P0, but it wasn't part of the initial formulation — Splectrum began with P1 through P5. P0 emerged while writing the follow-up, "Splectrum from First Principles." In unpacking the seeds from their foundations, a deeper question surfaced: not just *that* language exists, but *how and why* it comes into existence at all. What began as a gap turned out to be a creational principle — far more significant than initially expected, carrying its own rationale that goes well beyond a simple precondition for the other five.

### Comment 2 — How P0 grew

P0 started as something almost trivial — a bare acknowledgment, close to "let there be language," a simple statement of being. But it didn't stay there. As the framework deepened, P0 revealed itself as a genuine creational principle — not just the fact that language is, but a rationale for *how* and *why* it comes into being. It moved from a quiet precondition to the most foundational claim in the entire framework. What looked like a footnote turned out to be the foundation.
