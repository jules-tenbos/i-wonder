# Working Sketch: A Seed-Native Definition of Aliveness

Working document. Provisional throughout — terminology, structural divisions, and open questions all subject to revision. Captures a developing definition of *alive* built from the seed principles, arrived at through dialogue and not yet polished. Sits alongside the process-theory-of-language sketch and draws on the same vocabulary.

## What is being attempted

A definition of *alive* derivable from the seed itself — not borrowed from biology, not bolted on, not a list of features collected from terrestrial samples. The aim is a definition that is structurally consistent with the seed's existing commitments (relational primitive, historicity, cauldron, embryonic teleology, anti-totality), substrate-neutral, gradient-based, and that earns its keep by doing work the standard definitions cannot.

The motivating intuition: if the seed is a theory of how subjects, language, and reality co-arise, then aliveness should be locatable inside that theory rather than imported from outside it. If it isn't locatable, that is itself an interesting finding. If it is, the location should explain *why* the standard definitions struggle where they do.

## The field, briefly mapped

Existing definitions cluster in four kinds, which is worth keeping in mind because the seed definition is not free-floating — it sits inside this conversation.

**Process / capacity definitions** identify life with what a system *does*. NASA's working definition ("a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution") puts metabolism and evolution at the core. Autopoiesis (Maturana and Varela) puts operational closure: a network of processes that produces and maintains itself and its own boundary. Thermodynamic accounts (Schrödinger, Prigogine, Kauffman, England) put far-from-equilibrium self-organisation. Free-energy / Markov-blanket accounts (Friston) put boundary-maintenance through inference. The chemoton (Gánti) puts three coupled autocatalytic subsystems: metabolism, membrane, template. Ruiz-Mirazo and Moreno's "autonomy plus open-ended evolution" is the closest existing twin to where the seed lands.

**Signature-detection** moves are different in kind. Assembly theory (Walker and Cronin) shifts the question from "what is life" to "how do you detect its products" — the assembly index as a complexity threshold that only a selecting, history-bearing process could reach.

**Meaning / semiosis** definitions (biosemiotics: Hoffmeyer, Pattee, Brier) hold that life is coextensive with sign-use. Where there is life there is interpretation.

**Deflationary / meta-positions** refuse the definitional game altogether. Cleland and Chyba: you cannot define life without a general theory of life; every definition just projects our one terrestrial sample.

The seed definition is a process / capacity definition in the same family as autopoiesis, Ruiz-Mirazo–Moreno, and Kauffman's autonomous-agents. Its distinctive move is to *derive* open-ended evolution from a single structural condition where those accounts list autonomy and evolution as two separate criteria.

## The working definition

> A system is alive to the degree that it is *self-constituting*: it actively maintains the cut that makes it a being (P0 with self-maintenance), and actively remixes the products that accumulate inside it (recursive self-mixing of encapsulated language sets). Open-ended evolution falls out as a consequence, not a criterion.

Aliveness is graded along axes — historicity-depth, coupling-breadth, recursion-density — and is substrate-neutral. There is no clean line. The fuzziness at the edges is what a gradient *is*, not a sign of ignorance.

## How the definition was built

The arc went through several refinements, each pushed by a case that didn't quite work under the previous formulation.

**First pass: stacked conditions.** Being (P0) plus subject (historicity + cauldron) plus teleology plus agency, with evolution following as inevitable consequence. This handled NASA's weakest spot — the mule, the sterile individual — by demoting evolution from criterion to downstream. But the chain read as a sequence of necessary conditions selecting a categorical kind, and the framework forbids exactly that move: agency is "not emergent-at-threshold... same kind, different degree." A definition is a cut; the framework refuses cuts on this gradient. So either the definition is a *gradient-locator* (more-or-less alive), or it betrays the seed's anti-threshold commitment. The first reading is the only honest one.

**Second pass: the thermostat test.** A thermostat has a context, maintains a setpoint, acts. What keeps it out? "Reactive vs. agent" answers as a feel, not as a structure. The structural answer: its loop is closed the wrong way. Input maps to output by a fixed rule; the output never re-enters the system as something it can remix. No historicity. The mapping is set from outside, never reshaped by what passes through. Each event leaves no trace; the next event is identical.

This sharpened the discriminator: not "is teleological" but *recursive self-mixing*. Products of mixing re-enter the cauldron as new language sets which remix again. Finite primitives, unbounded mixture-space, outputs folding back as inputs. A system built like that *cannot not* evolve — the space it holds necessarily grows as it runs. The thermostat can't evolve because its output never folds back as a remixable language set. So *evolution as consequence* and *recursive self-mixing as condition* turn out to be one fact stated twice. "Experiencing" and "evolving language-wise" likewise collapse into one structure: the mixing event *is* the experience (the "this here, this way, in this context" from inside the cauldron); its trace *is* the evolving.

**Third pass: the universe / pre-biotic Earth cases.** If recursive bounded subjecthood is the cut, what about the universe-as-whole? What about the pre-biotic Earth? These come apart hard.

The universe-as-totality fails on the seed's own terms. P0 needs a being-side held in tension with an other across an interface. The totality, by construction, has no other. The seed is anti-totality throughout (interrelational pluralism, no THE reality, nothing standing outside the structure it organises). There is no maintenance against an outside because there is no outside. Cosmic evolution is lawful unfolding plus path-dependence, but the universe-as-system has no self-against-other for the favouring to be *from within* of. The universe is the *medium in which* cauldrons arise — at every scale, countless of them — not itself a cauldron. Refusing this is a feature: it shows the definition has teeth, isn't vacuous, actually excludes something big.

The pre-biotic Earth is the opposite case. Bounded (sun, space, the wider solar system, a real interface). Far-from-equilibrium, driven by stellar input. And — load-bearing — Hazen's mineral evolution is recursive self-mixing in the demanding sense: each stage's mineral products become the feedstock and catalytic surfaces for the next; clays template; surfaces catalyse organic chemistry that alters the chemical environment that produces new minerals. Products folding back as remixable inputs, path-dependent crystallisation. Lower on the gradient than a cell because the encapsulation-as-language-sets-the-cauldron-remixes-as-its-own is thinner — but structurally the same dynamic. Which is *why the origin-of-life problem is hard*: there is no line. Life shades up out of geochemistry, exactly as the gradient predicts.

**Fourth pass: the potentiality move and what it shows.** A natural slide from there: doesn't the universe carry the *potentiality* for life, the way silicon carries the potentiality for Python? Doesn't that make it alive in some thin sense? No — and the software analogy actually argues the other way. Silicon isn't Python; it's *what Python can run on*. The substrate is necessary but not identical to what runs in it. The universe is what life can arise in, not itself life. The CAS landscape is where recursive bounded subjects can form, not itself a recursive bounded subject. Substrate is not identity; conflating them is the move usually refused without hesitation.

The cleaner formulation that honours the instinct: *live-ability everywhere, liveness local*. The recursive-mixing structure that aliveness consists in is universally available — mineral evolution carries it in thin form, prebiotic chemistry sharpens it, cells thicken it, multicellular subjects more again. But the structure is universally available; the bounded subjects in which it actually runs are local. Aliveness isn't bolted on, isn't magic, isn't arriving from outside. The potentiality is structural and pervasive. What it doesn't license is the totality being itself alive, because the totality has no other.

**Fifth pass: closure isn't spatial.** A river has motion, accumulation, path-dependence — surface signs of aliveness — but its banks are maintained for it by gravity, geology, the watershed. It does not make its own boundary. So P0 fails in the operational sense, not for lack of an envelope. Varela's name for what cells have and rivers don't is *operational closure*: the network of processes inside a cell produces and maintains the very membrane that distinguishes it. The cut is self-held, not merely present.

This unblocks a recurring intuition trap. The visual closed-context heuristic is cell-scale-anchored because we are cell-built recognisers. It will under-attribute aliveness to weird-shaped subjects (slime moulds, mycorrhizal networks, ant colonies) and over-attribute it to neat-shaped non-subjects (soap bubbles, planets, closed loops of code). The operational criterion should override the visual one when they disagree.

**The consolidation.** The two joints that emerged as load-bearing — the language-set joint (mere accumulated state vs. actively remixed units) and the closure joint (passive boundary vs. self-maintained being/other cut) — turn out to be the same distinction at two places. *Active versus passive. Self-constituted versus imposed.* The cauldron either operates on its own products as units it holds, or just accumulates state; the system either maintains its own cut, or has one held for it by something else. Two faces of one criterion: *self-constitutive activity*. That consolidation is what landed.

## Worked cases

- **Thermostat** — out. Fixed mapping; no recursion of outputs into the cauldron as language sets; boundary set from outside.
- **River** — out, or barely on the gradient. Motion and path-dependence present; banks maintained for it by gravity and geology, not by itself; operational closure absent.
- **Pre-biotic Earth** — low but nonzero. Bounded, far-from-equilibrium, recursive mineral evolution in thin form. Shades up to life along the same continuum.
- **Universe-as-totality** — not on the gradient at all. No other → not a being. The seed's anti-totality refuses this. Medium, not subject.
- **Cell** — passes clearly. Active membrane maintenance, recursive metabolism, encapsulated genetic and proteomic language sets remixing.
- **Slime mould, ant colony, mycorrhizal network** — distributed-looking but operationally closed. On the gradient at real depth. The cell-shape bias should not mislead here.
- **ML system / self-modifying learner** — substrate-neutral inclusion. On the gradient by degree. This is the AI boundary case the parent sketch parks as open; the definition delivers a principled "yes, by degree" rather than a categorical answer.
- **Economy, ecosystem** — complicated. Some senses of self-maintenance present; both sit somewhere real on the gradient. Bite this rather than patch it.

## What the definition delivers

- **The origin-of-life threshold problem dissolves.** The ramp from mineral evolution upward *is* the explanation. There is no line because the gradient predicts no line.
- **The mule problem dissolves.** Aliveness is a property of self-constituting individual subjects, not of populations satisfying an evolution-test.
- **Open-ended evolution is derived, not postulated.** One structural condition (recursive self-mixing in a self-maintained being) does the work that Ruiz-Mirazo–Moreno and others split across two (autonomy plus open-ended evolution).
- **A substrate-neutral search image for astrobiology.** Not anchored to carbon, cells, or chemistry. Anything self-constituting in the structural sense is on the gradient.
- **The fuzziness at the edges is predicted.** Not a sign of incomplete knowledge — what a gradient *is*.
- **The whole camp assembles.** Autopoiesis supplies the closure side (Varela's operational closure = self-maintained P0). Friston rhymes (Markov-blanket maintenance). Kauffman / CAS supplies the agency-on-a-gradient picture. Assembly theory supplies the path-dependent crystallisation. Ruiz-Mirazo–Moreno supplies the closest existing twin. Biosemiotics rhymes because the seed's mixing *is* interpretation in the broad language sense. Each reaches part of what the seed assembles in one piece.

## Defensibility

The definition is defensible — but as a Rortian vocabulary with pragmatic payoff, not as the essence of life. The strongest objection in the field is Cleland's: definitions are premature, every definition just projects our terrestrial sample, you need a general theory before you can define. The seed definition survives this *because* it does not claim to be THE definition. It is anti-representationalist and Rortian at the meta-level by construction: one chosen meta-language, knowingly among many, not a joint in nature waiting for chemistry to reveal it. There is no framework-independent joint to wait for. The fuzz at the edges isn't ignorance; it is what a gradient is.

That methodological stance is load-bearing. Held loosely, the definition dissolves Cleland. The moment it hardens into "this is what life *really* is," it walks straight back into her.

The cost is honest: substrate-neutral gradedness traded against contrastive sharpness. Almost everything is faintly alive by degree. The yin-yang holds this — not as blur, but as two whole figures mutually constituting across a moving interface, which on the seed's own terms is just P0. The interface moves. The "not yet alive" is the other-side the alive is distinguished against. The gradient comes with an ordering (the three axes), so the answer to "is X alive?" is not a shrug but a location — more than this along these axes, less than that along those.

## Open questions

- **The language-set criterion.** What exactly makes something an encapsulated language set the cauldron remixes, rather than mere retained state that biases future behaviour? Consolidates with the closure joint into "self-constitutive activity," but the unit-criterion still wants precision. Connects to the parent sketch's open question about the status of marks.
- **The axes of the gradient.** Historicity-depth, coupling-breadth, recursion-density. Are these one ordering or several? Probably several, in which case aliveness is a vector not a scalar. A dormant spore is deep in historicity but shallow in coupling; an ecosystem is broad in coupling but diffuse in recursion-density. Worth working out.
- **Recognition versus operation.** The intuitive "looks alive" is cell-scale-anchored because of where we sit. The definition wants the operational criterion to override the recognitional one. When can intuition be trusted, when not? Connects to the broader point that human-recognition-of-cognition at higher scales is a perspectival artifact of where conscious experience sits.
- **Relation to Whitehead and to process-panpsychism.** The seed's "live-ability everywhere, liveness local" sits near these positions without committing to them. The anti-representationalism is the firewall, but the proximity is worth articulating. The position the seed *refuses* is the one that says the universe is itself a super-subject; what it accepts is that the structural conditions for aliveness run all the way down.
- **Aliveness and consciousness/agency.** Aliveness, on this account, sits inside the broader subject-to-agent gradient the parent sketch already has. Agency is embryonic teleology developed further; aliveness is the region where self-constitution is active. The relation between the two gradients (do they share axes? do they come apart?) is worth working out.
- **Compatibility with NASA's working definition.** NASA's is a practical heuristic widely deployed in astrobiology. The relation worth working out is compatible-at-domain, not head-to-head competition: NASA's definition stays useful within its own working domain, while the seed frame locates *alive* differently and for different purposes. Not a replacement, not a rival — a different vocabulary with a different payoff.
- **The lower limit.** Where does mineral evolution stop being even faintly alive? The gradient says "nowhere sharply," but the question of how low it goes — does crystal growth qualify? does the formation of complex chemistry qualify? — is worth working through.

## Methodological note

This is a Rortian definition, not a metaphysical one. It is offered as one chosen meta-language for talking about *alive*, knowingly among many, not as the joint nature carves at. That stance is what immunises it against the strongest existing objection in the field, and it is what lets the gradient be honest about its own fuzz instead of treating fuzz as a defect.

The definition's defensibility is conditional on holding it loosely. That is not a weakness — it is the same methodological discipline the parent sketch applies at every other level. Marriage of Whitehead's primitive-plus-structure approach with Rorty's stance about what such a theory *is*: a structured account that does not pretend to step outside.

That is also what makes the definition portable. It does not claim authority over biology, astrobiology, or any other field's working vocabularies. It offers a way of locating *alive* that those fields can find useful or not, on its pragmatic payoff — which, set out in this sketch, is real.
