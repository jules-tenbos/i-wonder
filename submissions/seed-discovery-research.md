# The seed and discovery — research submission

The `/seed/discovery` incarnation lands on "what we experience as creation conforms to the discovery of unwalked relational paths". Behind this is a deeper question the touchstone page doesn't carry: **is drawing a boundary a discovery of potential, or a reorganisation of what was already there?**

Cross-principle territory — P0 (creation by differentiation) and P5 (full power was always there) meeting at the same point.

## Angles to develop

- **Full power theme.** The [Let's Talk Software Languages](/blog/2026/04/lets-talk-software-languages/) post already carries this: the full power is in the base language; additions are articulation, not added capacity. Discovery of new expressions rather than creation of new capability. How does this generalise beyond software?
- **Language concepts as beings.** Each concept creation is a boundary-drawing — P0 at work. Evolution of language = evolution of thinking. Knowledge accumulates as walked relational paths.
- **Contingent concept creation.** New relational paths come into existence through the drawing of new distinctions. Were those paths always there in potential, or does the distinction create them?

## The central question

Is drawing a boundary a discovery of potential, or a reorganisation of potential?

- **Discovery** — the relational structure is complete from P0; we walk paths that were always there. Current SPLectrum framing leans this way ("the full power was always there").
- **Reorganisation** — differentiation changes the space of possibility. New boundaries open paths that weren't available before. Creation in a stronger sense.
- **Both** — some paths are discovered (given enough interaction density, they surface); some are created (the distinction itself opens the new path).

## Accessible examples

The "full power was always there" pattern shows up across domains. Candidates to develop alongside software languages (already on `/seed/discovery`):

- **Music** — twelve notes, infinite music. Every new genre or composition is an insight into expressing what the notes always permitted.
- **Chess** — fixed rules, infinite games. Every new opening or strategy unlocks what the rules always allowed.
- **Natural numbers** — the full power sits in the Peano axioms. Every theorem is an insight into something that was always true in the system.
- **DNA / genetic code** — four bases, twenty amino acids, all of life. Evolution articulates what the foundations can express. The sharpest example — and the one that opens the creation-vs-discovery question most acutely.

Music and chess are the most intuitive for a general reader; mathematics works but is more technical; biology is powerful but loaded.

## Philosophical anchors

The potentiality/actuality tradition:

- **Aristotle** — *dynamis* / *energeia*. Potentiality pre-exists its actualisation.
- **Bergson** — virtual and actual. The virtual is fully real but not yet articulated; creative evolution is its unfolding.
- **Deleuze** — extends Bergson. The virtual as a field of differences that actualises through repetition, with actualisation not preserving the form.

Aristotle and Bergson lean toward potentiality as real-but-latent (discovery side). Deleuze pushes toward actualisation as genuinely productive (creation side).
