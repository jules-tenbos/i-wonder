- Ethics / economics / politics (Capitalism as zero-sum game; Paine on economic justice; Effortocracy as meritocracy critique; Rights & Liberties; Globalisation; Win-Win-Win Ethics: reconciling consequentialism, virtue ethics, deontology; Both-Sidesism).

**Operating mode.** Weekly cadence. Hosts guest writers (Jaiveer Singh, Andrew Tane Glen). Cross-posts with other blogs (ToSummarise). Illustrated throughout — animals, simple visual figures. Embeds podcast episodes. Operates in a network rather than alone.

## Intellectual neighbourhood

He is in conversation with — but critical of — the rationalist orbit. The "what Yudkowsky gets wrong" piece, the Moloch lore (Scott Alexander), the AI alignment focus, the game-theoretic vocabulary all locate him there. The critical distance is real: he is not reproducing rationalist orthodoxies. He is reading them, taking what is useful, and pushing back where he disagrees.

The Moloch framing for coordination failures, the emergence-and-substrates ontology, and the cooperation-as-central-problem framing are the load-bearing pieces.

## Resonances

Where the languages sit close:

- **Substrate / layered ontology.** His "On the Shoulders of Substrates" framing — one phenomenon as the foundation for the next — does work similar to the layering between the relational dynamic and the language games that sit on it. Whether the cuts line up requires reading the piece; the title alone signals a recognisable move.
- **Coordination as central object.** Cooperation, defection, trust, harmony — how relational dynamics actually work — is his persistent subject. Adjacent to P5 territory.
- **Pattern recognition across domains.** Game theory in nature, emergence in physics, cooperation in economics, alignment in AI, ethics across frameworks. Cross-domain structural reading is his deepest method, and it rhymes with the project's.
- **Critical of rationalist orthodoxies.** Adjacent-to-but-not-of the rationalist scene, with explicit critique. Locates him in a neighbourhood the project itself occupies.
- **Games and simulations as tools to think with.** He builds things to understand connections. That is itself a tool-pattern of interest to the Tools section.
- **Mutualism-adjacent.** The non-zero-sum framing puts him close to the mutualism subject page already in positioning.

## Dissonances and tensions

Where the languages do not sit on the same layer, or carry different colourings:

- **Normative tilt.** "Non-zero-sum" does two jobs at once: as structural description (outcomes where total value is not fixed) and as normative goal (cooperation = good). The project's editorial principles flag bipolar framings; this is one. His stance is interventionist ("world-help"); the project's is structural-observational. Not a contradiction, but a real stance difference worth naming.
- **Moloch as personification.** "Moloch is ruining your life" personifies coordination failure as an intentional villain. The project's no-personifying discipline applies to the seed and extends to not anthropomorphising emergent dynamics. The Moloch framing may be deeper than register — it invokes an intentional-agent explanation for what is system-level emergence. Whether this is fence territory or just register-level vividness needs a closer read.
- **Pre-given agents in game theory.** Game theory assumes agents with stable preferences existing prior to the interaction. P1 says the agent and the situation co-constitute — what an agent *is* in an interaction is not prior to the interaction. Same direction of thinking, different ontological layer. Game theory can be one vocabulary used for one cut of the relational dynamic; the layering question is real.
- **"More than the sum of our parts."** A holism / emergence move. P1 articulates something stronger and quieter: the parts are not fully there as independent things prior to the relations they sit in. Adjacent pointing, different ontology.
- **Interventionist vs observational stance.** His project is world-help, action-oriented, aimed at making more non-zero-sum games happen. The project's stance is structural observation. Compatible aims, different shapes.
