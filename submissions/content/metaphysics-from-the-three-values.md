# Reality-side metaphysics from the three values — synthesis and plan

Working document. Provisional throughout — terminology, structural divisions, and open questions all subject to revision. Captures an exploratory discussion that built a content-side metaphysics by *disclosure* from the three reality-side values: belonging, privacy, creativity, with freedom as their resultant. Written to feed two builds — the Reality section, and a blog series. Sits alongside the process-theory-of-language and aliveness sketches and draws on the same vocabulary.

## What this is — and the register it lives in

A working synthesis, not site copy. It moves between registers freely because it is a planning artifact: it discusses the seed/structural side and the content/reality side together in order to plan. The pages and posts it feeds do not. Reality-section pages speak in value-terms, subject-pov, no P-routing; the structural articulation stays on the seed side and is cross-linked, never imported.

One methodological result governs everything below.

**The values do not found a metaphysics; they disclose one.** They are not axioms a structure is derived from. They are the stance a subject lives from, and the metaphysics is the world that shows up to that stance. "Build from" and "underpin" both carry derivation grammar — a foundation laid beneath — and derivation is the outside (structural) view. The content-side move is the description of a disclosed world, not the derivation of a grounded one. The test the approach had to pass: it held under exactly the pressure that would have broken a derivation.

**The anchor is solid but floating.** A reference point firm enough to give insight, with no privileged direction of dependence — you can enter at the values and reach the world, or stand in the world and read the values back, and neither is the bottom. A foundation is solid but *fixed* (one entry, dependence in one direction); relativism is *fluid and unanchored* (no entry, nothing to lean on); this is the third thing. The reversibility is not a weakness to manage — it *is* the pluralism, the no-THE-reality showing in the method itself. Solid-but-floating is the content-side face of the project's rigid-structure / contingent-content position.

## The world the three values disclose

Three words: **thick, porous, unfinished.**

- *Privacy → thick.* There is an inside to a subject that is not given to the outside, not readable off behaviour, not exhausted by its relations. The world has depth. This already rules out the flat ontology where a thing just *is* its relations.
- *Belonging → porous.* "What is mine living also in others." Not a shared object we both face, but my content present *in* you. Interiors leak into one another without dissolving into a common substance. Doors between insides, not a common room they all report to.
- *Creativity → unfinished.* New states come into being that were not folded into the old. Reality is added to, not read out. The future is made, not unpacked.

**Freedom** is not a fourth value but the resultant — what it is to be a subject in a thick, porous, unfinished world: own ground, real connection, real making.

## By branch — one dynamic under different questions

The branches are not separate territories. They are the one dynamic interrogated differently: ontology asks what is, epistemology how it is known, ethics how the tension is held, aesthetics how a good mix feels, politics the same at community scale.

### Epistemology
- *Thick* → no exhaustive outside knowledge of anyone; every subject is partly opaque, including to itself. Humility is structural, not a virtue added on.
- *Porous* → to know is for content to come to live in you. Learning is uptake. "Objectivity" stops being correspondence and becomes *breadth of belonging* — what looks objective is what lives in many. Truth as the widely-held layer, not the matching one.
- *Unfinished* → knowing is making. Inquiry reorganises the field by articulating it; new vocabularies create new things to know. Epistemology as poetics, not mirror-polishing.

### Ontology
The "what is" question is the **concept/being hinge** — and content-side, there is no hinge to narrate, only an identity to live: **being is concept.** We have being as concept, because language is the medium and there is no getting behind the medium to a bare being it would express. To say what something is just *is* to give its concept. The cloth is all thread; there is no cloth behind it.

"Concept and being arise in the same act" is genesis grammar — the outside view. Any *arising* needs a vantage on the act, and only the structural register has acts as its objects. The living subject never watches its own being arise; it lives in being-already-given. Content-side there is no act and no two faces, only the lived identity.

What there is: *selves with insides* (privacy), *the shared / interpenetration* (belonging), *the new / becoming* (creativity). No bare objects as the base — inert things are the thin end of a gradient, what you reach by thinning the inside, not the ground floor (the lower limit is open; this is not panpsychism). No totality. Becoming over being. Described, not grounded.

Vocabulary lock: *subject reality* = the experience of reality within being (contains shared and non-shared aspects). *Ontology* = the held-as-object layer — my catalogue of what is. *My reality* = the field that impacts me, which exceeds my ontology, since things impact me that I do not hold. The **gap between my ontology and my reality** — what impacts me but is not mine — is the site of creativity: the unheld presses, the cauldron mixes in response, a new being-as-concept forms and my ontology stretches to take up my version of it. Reality always exceeds ontology, so the loop never closes. Unfinished, structurally.

Overlap-but-not-equal runs at two depths. Some of another's ontology is not in mine at all; and even where they "overlap," nothing crosses directly — I get my reading of how the other modulates the field we both touch, and that reading lands as *my* version. The shared/different cut runs *through* each item, not only between items. No perfectly shared entry anywhere — belonging exactly as defined, never a jointly held object.

### Ethics
Not a rule-system handed down — the art of holding the three in tension so none swallows the others. Each, maxed, kills the rest: belonging maxed suffocates (the cult, the total ideology); privacy maxed isolates until there is no other to mix with; creativity maxed destabilises so nothing decoheres into a shared floor. **Freedom is the resultant that appears when the tension is well-held and collapses when one value wins.** Health is the lit rim staying lit, the floor staying shared, the within staying held.

The seductive failure has a clean criterion. Communion and suffocation are the same decoherence at different totalities; the line is *uptake*. Belonging taken up freely is communion; belonging forced over a refusing private is suffocation — imposition requires uptake, futile when the private refuses, suffocating when forced anyway. The **shock** — discovering a difference you had taken as shared — is the ethical hinge: the moment you either crush it back into the floor or let it teach and extend you. Equal standing, lived.

## The decoherence dynamic

Embedded in the world, in the other: **what is shared disappears; what differs remains as tension at the interface.** Salience tracks difference. The more fully something is shared and absorbed, the less it shows — it drops out of the held-as-object and becomes the transparent floor you operate on without seeing. Lived subject reality is mostly dark floor — a vast invisible commonality — with a narrow bright rim of difference. Experience over-weights separation: you are embedded mostly in the shared but feel mostly the differences, because the sharing has gone transparent. The commonality is the dark matter.

Two sources of interface tension: standing difference (never taken as shared), and collapsed sameness (the shock — taken as shared, then broke; the sharpest tension, because a transparent medium becomes an opaque obstacle right where you leaned your weight; also creativity-forcing). Their mirror is *utter unity* — the rim shrinking toward zero, the floor swallowing almost everything. Same dial: run it one way, communion; snap it the other, the shock; widen it across many subjects, the solid layer that carries fact-languages.

**Decoherence is a structural property of belonging, not of any one content language.** It emerges from sharing once participation crosses thresholds — steep but continuous, not a switch (the off-diagonal terms decay fast but continuously, the truer rhyme, and faithful to the gradient). It is most *striking* in science, because science's forcing is the most rigorous — reproducibility, instrumentation, formalism are convergence-forcing technology, the lab a decoherence factory — but it is the same property running everywhere. It can be as strong in religion, where it can suffocate the private.

Consequences for objectivity: objectivity is breadth of belonging decohered into definiteness. Science gets a solid reality to state facts against without that reality being THE reality — stablest-and-most-shared, not truest; the authority of a fact is the rigour of the forcing, not contact with the real. A scientific revolution is the floor re-cohering — the interference terms you had settled coming back, a new definiteness having to settle (Kuhn, in this vocabulary).

## Abstraction, creation, and the reflexive fold

The creative loopback, read from the two branches, is one act under two names. The being side calls it **creation** — the fabric getting larger. The knowledge side calls it **abstraction** — the lifting-into-conceptual-hold. Each register names the aspect it is positioned to notice. The old hierarchy falls out as an artifact: "abstract = thin, derived, less real" is the knowledge side mistaking its partial view for the whole. On the fused view the abstract is fresh cloth, as real as any.

Because abstraction is the loopback running, it **permeates the living and non-living** — it is not the human differentia. The human special part is *reflexivity*, but guarded against a slide: reflexivity is not a faculty beyond abstraction (that would be the emergent-at-threshold move the gradient refuses), but abstraction *folded onto its own source* — the loop taking its own operation as the thing to mix. Same kind; the fold is the degree. "Stepping outside ourselves" is the outside view, and it is non-absolute because a fold does not leave the cloth, it doubles it. There is no being-without-concept to step out onto. Reflexivity runs down the gradient too — the human is the deep-fold region, not where a new power switches on.

Even the structural/outside view lives fused. It does not escape the fusion; it makes its medium transparent — looks *through* the common layer at "structure" and forgets it is looking through anything. The forgetting is what lets it pose as an outside view, and also why it spills: a stance that has stopped noticing its medium leaks the medium without noticing. Register discipline is therefore an *authorial* discipline, not a fact about the world — the vocabularies are held apart on the page because the lived world fuses them and will not do the separating for us. The structural register's work is to un-fuse what living runs together.

## Interrelational pluralism as the world the three disclose

The immediate purpose. **IRP is the name for the world the three values disclose** — not a thesis the values are laid under as a foundation. The expansion is a reality-side companion to the structural IRP home (seed section), cross-linked to it, written in value-terms.

The values sort onto the term, and the mapping happens to track the lived (waking-up) order, neither encoding rank:

- *Belonging → interrelational.* The overlap-not-equal, mine-living-in-others, the "inter" that keeps the plurality from being mere scatter. The load-bearing move is following belonging *past the human*: belonging is the coupling wherever subjects co-modulate, at any scale, down to where the gradient thins into the barely-living. That reach turns mere plurality into a web — windowed interiors, not a dust of sealed monads — and is what makes this a metaphysics rather than a social theory.
- *Privacy → the plural.* Distinct interiors, so the realities are genuinely many, not one world seen from angles. The pluralism, shown rather than asserted.
- *Creativity → the co-arising.* The loopback, new realities forming, the floor decohering and the rim staying lit. Plurality in motion, not a static catalogue.

"There are many realities, related, in motion" stops being doctrine and becomes the lived dynamic above — the floor, the shock, the suffocation. IRP gets a texture, not just a claim.

A reflexivity worth keeping: a home that offers a solid-but-floating anchor is itself an interrelational-pluralist artifact — the way it presents IRP is already doing IRP. Form carrying content, as the homepage grid does.

## Toward the blog

The blog is the working stream — conversational register, thinking in motion, ~600 words, openings not resolutions, aphoristic image leading. A short series can carry the disclosure approach: the values as entry; the world they light up (thick/porous/unfinished); the branches one at a time; the decoherence dynamic; IRP as the world they disclose.

**First post — "Flying the flag of belonging, privacy and creativity."** Worth doing, with one eye on a tension. A flag *planted* reads as a fixed banner — a creed — which is the opposite of the solid-but-floating anchor and the shown-not-asserted stance. The rescue is to read the flag as it actually works: a marker visible from afar, a where-we-stand you navigate *by*, not the ground under your feet. Flown that way, the flag *is* the floating anchor. So: keep the image, let the body disclose the world the three light up rather than declaring allegiance, and close on the reversibility — the flag flies, but you could come at it from the other side — so it does not harden into a foundation. Use the lived order (belonging, privacy, creativity), unnumbered, and let the sequence carry itself.

One housekeeping flag: this likely overlaps the values-introduction post already in progress (belonging, privacy, creativity as the value foundation, value-numbers stripped, waking-up narrative). Decide whether "Flying the flag" *is* that post under a punchier title, or a distinct IRP-anchored opener that the values-intro then follows — to avoid two posts doing the same work.
