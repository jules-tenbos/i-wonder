# Reality section — structure and content plan

Working document. Captures the structural decisions for the Reality section of splectrum.world and outlines content for the landing page and core-values & metaphysics page. Provisional throughout.

## Design principles

**Subject-centred throughout.** Everything in the Reality section is from the seat of the subject — disclosed, not derived. The values are the subject's situation; the metaphysics is what the subject sees from there; evolution is the subject-centred view generalised across the gradient. No outside view, no stone tablets.

**Content-side register.** Reality-section pages speak in value-terms, subject point of view. No P-routing (P0, P2, etc.), no seed-structural vocabulary imported. The structural articulation stays on the seed side and is cross-linked, never imported. The two sides of the SPLectrum coin stay apart on the page.

**Disclosure, not derivation.** The values do not found a metaphysics; they disclose one. They are the stance a subject lives from, and the metaphysics is the world that shows up to that stance. The anchor is solid but floating — firm enough to give insight, with no privileged direction of dependence. You can enter at the values and reach the world, or stand in the world and read the values back.

**Subfolders as future structure.** Each block on the landing page maps to a subfolder. Content starts as summaries on the landing page; when depth arrives, it moves to subfolder pages without URL changes.

## Landing page — `/reality/index.md`

General introduction: SPLectrum's reality section speaks the content language — the other side of the coin from the seed's meta-language of structure. Reality is disclosed from the seat of the subject through three core values: belonging, privacy, creativity. Freedom is not a fourth value but the resultant — what it is to be a subject when the three are held together.

### Block: Core Values

Introduction to the three values as drivers of reality. Link to the core-values & metaphysics page.

- **Belonging** — experience is shaped by shared language, the toolset of perception given by species and community. What is mine living also in others. The interrelational — the coupling that keeps plurality from being mere scatter. Follows belonging past the human: wherever subjects co-modulate, at any scale.
- **Privacy** — what I experience is internal to me; no one can look through my eyes. Distinct interiors, so the realities are genuinely many. The plural — shown, not asserted. The world has depth: things have an inside not given to the outside.
- **Creativity** — the lived tension between what is shared and what is privately mine. New states come into being that were not folded into the old. Reality is added to, not read out. The co-arising — plurality in motion.
- **Freedom** — make, own, share. Not three goods rationed against each other but one self-creation showing three faces. The resultant when the tension is well-held.

No deeper links yet; the value subfolders are future structure.

### Block: Metaphysics

Introduction: the four pillars are not separate territories — they are one dynamic interrogated differently. The metaphysics is disclosed by the values, not derived from them. Equal standing among the pillars.

- **Epistemology** — how we come to know. No absolutes — neither objectivity nor truth in the absolute sense. Objectivity is where subjectivities converge: breadth of belonging, not correspondence with an external real. Knowledge is always perspectival, provisional, earned through community. Every subject is partly opaque, including to itself — humility is structural.
- **Ontology** — what we hold as beings. Being is concept — language is the medium and there is no getting behind it. We create and share our ontological catalogue through language, converging despite diversity, forgetting in the process that it started in the privacy of personal experience. It could have been different; we created it. Reality always exceeds ontology, so the loop never closes.
- **Aesthetics** — the quality of language. Since there is no reality without sensory experience, and experience is mediated by language, aesthetics is the discipline of how well the language fits. Beauty, purity, effectiveness of the means and tools we use to share and create. Not a subordinate to the other three — the medium everything passes through.
- **Ethics** — how we hold the tension between the values so none swallows the others. Gets its strength from the other three, and most importantly from aesthetics: the resonance between intent and how it is conveyed. An ethical intent poorly expressed diminishes to the point of non-existence. Placed last not because least important, but because it needs the other three to already be working.

No deeper links yet; the pillar subfolders are future structure.

### Block: Evolution

Introduction: the subject-centred view generalised across the gradient of reality.

**Bidirectional approach:**

- **Backward from humans.** Start where the subject-centred view is clearest — human experience, human language, the three values as we know them — and generalise down the gradient. How far back does belonging go? Where does privacy thin out? Does creativity have a seat wherever there is tension at an interface? This direction keeps us honest about what we're projecting and what we're discovering. It is the natural direction from the core values: we know what they feel like here; the question is how far they stretch.
- **Forward from the beginning.** The traditional evolutionary arc — major transitions, thresholds in interaction density, expression growth — but read through the values rather than through complexity-accumulation. Not progress toward us, but a web where each level continues alongside the others. The SPLectrum reading: the power was always there, what changes is expression.

The two directions meet somewhere — and where they meet is the interesting question. The meeting point is where the generalisation from above and the observation from below converge or show their limits.

Existing evolution content (the curve, thresholds not breaks, the thinking side, shared structural territory) stays and gets reworked into the bidirectional frame.

1. The three core values — what they are, where they come from (the subject's situation), what world they disclose (thick, porous, unfinished).

3. Freedom as the resultant — the three composing, not competing.
4. The solid-but-floating anchor — this is not a foundation (fixed, one direction of dependence) and not relativism (fluid, unanchored). It is a vantage point firm enough to give insight and honest enough to know it is one stance among many.

Cross-links to the seed section (structural side) and to the IRP home. The page does not import structural vocabulary — it is the content-side companion, cross-linked, not merged.

## Positioning pages to create

New pages needed to support the reality section's positions. These are the natural link targets from the core-values & metaphysics page.

### Persons

- **Kuhn** — paradigm shifts map directly onto the decoherence dynamic: "the floor re-cohering." Essential reference for the epistemology pillar's objectivity-as-convergence position. A scientific revolution is the widely-shared layer losing its definiteness and having to re-settle.
- **Schiller** (Friedrich) — *Letters on the Aesthetic Education of Man*. The closest historical precedent for the aesthetics-before-ethics ordering. Argued that aesthetic development is the precondition for ethical freedom — the same structural claim SPLectrum makes.
- **Buber** — I-Thou. The relational encounter as primary. Belonging as meeting, not merging — directly speaks to the belonging value. "All real living is meeting."
- **Nagel** — "What Is It Like to Be a Bat?" The philosophical anchor for privacy as a structural fact. The irreducibility of first-person experience is not a limitation but the condition — the same move the reality section makes.
- **Dewey** — *Art as Experience*: aesthetics as the quality of lived experience, not a fine-arts discipline. Also pragmatist epistemology: knowing as making, not mirroring. Touches both the aesthetics and epistemology pillars.

### Subjects

- **Phenomenology** — the tradition behind "from the seat of the subject." The subject-centred method itself. Not one person but the approach — Husserl, Heidegger, Merleau-Ponty each take it somewhere different. SPLectrum's disclosure method is phenomenological in spirit.
- **Pragmatism** — the epistemological family where "objectivity as convergence" lives. James, Dewey, Rorty are already on site as persons, but pragmatism as a subject — the tradition, its variants, where SPLectrum's epistemology sits within it — is missing.
- **Aesthetics** — the discipline in its own right. SPLectrum takes a specific position (quality of language, precondition for ethics) that needs the landscape: from *aisthesis* (sensation) through Kant's judgment of taste to the expressivist tradition (Croce, Collingwood) and phenomenological aesthetics.

### Affinity readings

- **Aesthetics-before-ethics** — a close-affinity or seed-ring piece. The distinctive ordering. Schiller is the main historical ally. Wittgenstein's "ethics and aesthetics are one" (Tractatus 6.421) touches it differently. This is where SPLectrum says something most readers would push back on — worth a positioning piece.
- **Objectivity as convergence** — where this sits relative to pragmatism (James, Dewey, Rorty), to Kuhn's paradigm model, and to the correspondence theory it replaces. Probably a wider-landscape or close-affinity piece.
