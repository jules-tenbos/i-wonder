## Topic

A piece on time, sitting in the Reality section alongside discovery, evolution, and relational reading. Time as another aspect of reality the SPLectrum framework articulates, with historicity as the anchoring thread.

## Centre of gravity

Time as material and/or relational rather than backdrop or illusion. SPLectrum's historicity reads time as accumulated retention that is materially constitutive of the subject and relational to its interactions. The piece develops this through two main external interlocutors who reject the Newtonian backdrop and the Einsteinian illusion frame from different angles.

## Key interlocutors

**Assembly theory (Cronin and Walker).** Time as a material property of complex objects; construction history is a measure of an object's size in time. The strong claim from "Time is an object" (Aeon, 2023): time is the stuff out of which things in the Universe are made. The theory identifies time with memory, information, causation, and selection.

**Rovelli on time.** "The Order of Time" (2018) and related work: time as relational, perspectival, emergent from thermodynamics. The thermal time hypothesis (with Connes). Loop quantum gravity treating time as not fundamental at the deepest level. Distinct from but related to his RQM work.

The two diverge on what replaces the Newtonian/Einsteinian conception — Rovelli: relational/emergent; assembly theory: material in objects. SPLectrum's historicity sits between them productively.

**Developmental-historicity thread (migrated from the language-mechanics field map, 2026-07-19; that map is retired).** The path-landscape reading of historicity has a developmental-biology wing worth considering as a third register alongside assembly theory and Rovelli: **Thelen & Smith's dynamic systems theory of development** — development as a landscape of attractors shaped by the system's *own activity history*, no pre-laid program (Waddington's epigenetic landscape is the ancestor image; Esther Thelen's infant motor-development work the empirical base), and broader **path-dependence** (Arthur, David — "history matters," lock-in, increasing returns; Gould & Lewontin on contingency; niche construction; Bergson's *durée*). **Caution carried over:** Thelen is a *near-peer* to the SPLectrum process sketch, so a resonance reading risks shading toward convergence rather than resonance-across-a-gap — handle in the reality-position outward register if used. Not committed to this piece; noted so the candidate isn't lost with the field map.
