# Category theory on SPLectrum's language games

Once SPLectrum's language registers are documented explicitly — each with its vocabulary, patterns, stance — they become objects that category theory can operate on. Not as philosophical parallel this time, but as an operational tool. This opens a research program: using the mathematics of relation to study how the project's own languages relate across each other.

## The move

Explicit registers are the prerequisite. Implicit patterns don't admit categorical analysis — you can't apply a functor to a habit. The discipline of making language games explicit (in `tone-of-voice/`, `process/`, vocabulary pages, positioning pages) creates the foundation. Each register becomes an object; relationships between registers become morphisms; patterns of relationship become natural transformations.

## What categorical analysis enables

- **Mappings between registers.** How does the voice of a seed incarnation map to the voice of an engineering page? What's preserved, what's lost? Which concepts map cleanly, which require reinterpretation?
- **Transformations between mappings.** When multiple routes exist between registers (direct, or via an intermediate language game), natural transformations compare them.
- **Overlaps as structural fact.** Not "these registers feel similar" but "here's the subcategory they share".
- **Divergences named precisely.** Where registers can't map cleanly, the obstruction is a structural fact to articulate.
- **Quantification via the categorical apparatus.** Limits, colimits, adjunctions, functors between language-game categories — specific tools for specific structural features.

## Why this matters

The move turns SPLectrum's documentation discipline into something that produces **relational insight** — understanding how the project's own languages relate across each other. This is closer to what the seed's P1 actually claims: language is relational. Applied to SPLectrum's own registers, that claim becomes operationally actionable, not just philosophically resonant.

The three interdisciplinary pillars lock together here:

- **Philosophy** — the seed's relational stance.
- **Science / mathematics** — category theory as the formal instrument.
- **Engineering** — language registers as explicit, documented objects.

HAICC may contribute at scale: AI collaboration helps do relational analysis across a growing corpus of registers, especially where human intuition alone struggles to hold the full picture.

## Why explicit registers are non-negotiable

The discipline of making language explicit isn't just for readers or for consistency across the ref lib. It's the precondition for the categorical research programme. An implicit register can't be a morphism's source or target. A drift-loaded habit can't be studied categorically. Only explicit objects support the apparatus.

This reframes what `tone-of-voice/`, `process/`, and the future principle-themed positioning pages *are*: not housekeeping, but the **corpus** the analysis will work on.
