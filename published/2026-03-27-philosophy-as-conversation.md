## Notes

Rorty as the deliberate bridge-builder. He fused Wittgenstein, Heidegger,
and Dewey and declared the analytical/continental split artificial.
*Philosophy and the Mirror of Nature* (1979): philosophy is not a mirror
reflecting reality but a conversation between traditions. Anti-foundationalism
made explicit. Solidarity over objectivity.

Close to the seed's "languages are inter-relational with equal standing" —
no single tradition owns the truth, knowledge emerges from the overlap.
Rorty did consciously what Wittgenstein did implicitly: he walked through
the door and said the border wasn't real.

This post gives us philosophical standing to work across traditions with
confidence. Together with Wittgenstein it forms the axis from which the
seed can expand into other territory (biology, evolution, engineering)
without having to justify itself philosophically each time.
