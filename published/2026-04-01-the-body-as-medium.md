## Notes

Merleau-Ponty grounds language in the body. Walk through the door
Wittgenstein opened, now with Rorty's permission to cross freely.

The lived body as "a third genre of being between the pure subject and
the object." Perception as bodily, pre-reflective — the body knows
before the words arrive. The private relocated, not denied: private
because of the relating, not despite it.

Where the seed agrees: the subject's experience is real and irreducible.
Where it goes further: beyond the human body — any subject, any language,
including non-biological.

Merleau-Ponty's heirs: Dreyfus (embodied AI critique), Varela/Thompson
(enactivism — cognition as enacted through engagement, not computation).
The enactivist line is close to "language is relational" — knowing through
engaging, not representing.

An encounter, not an adoption. The seed draws from Merleau-Ponty but
isn't bounded by his human-body anchor.
