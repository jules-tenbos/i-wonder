## Notes

### What this post does

Goes in search of the simplest language. Programming languages as languages; how stripped-down can a language get and still be a language? Opens the door to a wider scope without needing to assert it.

### Key threads

- Programming languages are languages — formal, structured, relational
- Languages demonstrably evolve from simple to complex
- The question: how simple can a language get?
- Elementary/simple languages — what are the minimum structural properties?
- This naturally leads to: if something this simple counts as a language, then...

### Tone

- Exploratory, curious — genuinely going in search, not presenting conclusions
- Concrete examples, not abstract argument
- The bold claim is not made here — just the ground prepared
