## Notes

### What this post does

The science parallel to the philosophical turn. One post covering the full arc: how the outside view was built, and how it was dismantled.

### The arc

- Aristotle — the world understood through qualities, purposes, final causes
- Scientific Revolution — Copernicus, Galileo, Kepler: from "why" to "how", from qualities to quantities
- Descartes/Newton — the outside view perfected. Mind/body split liberates science to study matter without consciousness. Deterministic, observer-independent, reality as clockwork
- The mind/body split — first liberating, then a detached headache. Science built on excluding the observer
- Relativity (Einstein, 1905/1915) — first crack. No absolute frame. What you measure depends on where you stand
- Quantum mechanics (1920s-30s) — the observer is in it. Measurement affects outcome. Properties don't exist independently of observation

### Key tension

The Cartesian split was a brilliant move — it freed science to work. But the price was built in: if mind is separate from matter, the observer is separate from the observed. That assumption holds until relativity and QM force the question.

### Tone

- The science story told as a turn, not as a history of physics
- Philosophy peppered in where it illuminates — Descartes as hinge
- Not technical — the structural pattern is the point
