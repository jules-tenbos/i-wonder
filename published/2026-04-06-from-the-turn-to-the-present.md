## Notes

### What this post does

Bridge post. Steps back from the individual encounters (Wittgenstein, Rorty, Merleau-Ponty) to show the arc as a whole. Not a summary of what came before — what becomes visible when you see the full trajectory at once. Then pivots to science: physics was making the same move independently.

### The philosophical arc

The outside view dismantled, traced from Kant to the present:
- Kant — the observer is not a passive mirror, actively shapes experience
- Hegel — made that structuring historical and social
- Husserl — turned to lived experience itself
- Heidegger — placed understanding within the activity of living
- Wittgenstein — meaning in use, language games, forms of life
- Rorty — no mirror, vocabularies as tools, conversation replaces epistemology
- Merleau-Ponty — the body back in the picture

Not coordinated. Independent arrivals at the same structural turn.

### The pivot to science

Physics made the same move:
- Classical mechanics — ultimate outside view. Deterministic, observer-independent, reality as clockwork.
- Relativity — first crack. No absolute frame. What you measure depends on where you stand.
- Quantum mechanics — the observer is in it. Measurement affects outcome.
- RQM (Rovelli, 1996) — properties don't belong to objects, they belong to interactions.

Two independent traditions, different vocabularies, same structural pattern. The convergence is the point.

### From the Rorty post closing

"But the dam was breached. The relational view of reality started to gain traction across different disciplines, albeit often still as minority views. This can be clearly illustrated by a discussion in physics: the story of relational quantum mechanics."

This post picks up that thread.

### Tone

- More assertive than the individual encounter posts — this is where you can say "from where I stand, this is what I see" with the full weight of the encounters behind it
- Not a retrospective — a runway for what comes next
- Philosophy and science as two independent paths arriving at the same place — that IS the seed in action, without needing to say so
