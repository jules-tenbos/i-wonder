# Exploration 003: The Extract

Step 2 of the [language research proposal](../../foundation/language-research-proposal.md): for each definition, note how it relates to the principle's concepts and the relations between them.

## Method

Two contexts must be held simultaneously:
1. **The entry's own context** — what the thinker is saying, in their language, within their historical situation
2. **The principle's context** — how this relates (positively, negatively, partially, from a different angle) to the principle's specific concepts

Entries may support, challenge, or complicate a principle statement. The categorisation is about *relevance*, not agreement.

## Categories

Six files, one per principle concept. Each contains the entries pre-categorised under that concept in Step 1 (the Gather), ready for extraction.

| File | Concept | Entries | Principle statement |

## Approach

We will decide on an order and a top slice — which entries to extract first, how deep to go before passing material to Step 3 (Map). Not all 117 positions need full extraction; enough to let patterns emerge.

## What feeds Step 3

Extracted positions with notes on how they relate to each principle concept — supporting, challenging, complicating, or extending it. The remaining positions stay parked, available if the patterns call for more material.
